## Ordering note (deliberate deviation from the operator's step order)

The operator's plan placed this fix in **step 3** (with F-GOV-005) and the approval-reachability work in **step 2**.
It was landed **before step 2** instead, for a concrete safety reason: once an `:approved` verdict is reachable
(F-GOV-006), a decision with `risk_assessment > 0.8` would reach the rule engine instead of the `high_risk` policy —
because that policy is wildcard-scoped and the wildcard was broken. The result would be a window in which a
high-risk decision can be auto-approved while the policy that is supposed to park it for human review is dead.
Landing the wildcard first removes that window. The operator's explicit ordering constraint (F-GOV-007 verified
before F-GOV-006/F-GOV-023 makes `:approved` reachable) is respected: F-GOV-007 was closed at `5dcc9ebd` with CI
evidence before this change.

**Residual scope (not covered here):** F-GOV-005 (`permitted?/1` still treats `:conditional` as a machine grant) and
F-GOV-006/F-GOV-023 (an approved verdict is still unreachable) remain **OPEN**.
