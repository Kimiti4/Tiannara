## Behaviour preserved, behaviour changed (explicit)

| Behaviour | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| A decision requiring human review | parked (`:requires_human`) | parked (`:requires_human`) — unchanged |
| A human approval | recorded only; the decision stayed parked forever | recorded **and** the decision re-evaluated; the result is stored against the request |
| A human approval for a *different* decision of the same type | no effect | **still no effect** (the lookup binds to the decision type *and* the payload) |
| A rejection | recorded only | recorded; the decision stays parked and unauthorised |
| `CEL.Kernel.restart_service/2` | unreachable (parked on human review) | reachable once the human approval has been resumed |
| `CEL.Kernel.restart_service/2` grant test | any `:conditional` decision was accepted | only a full approval or a **satisfied** conditional grant (the audited predicates) |
| `CEL.Kernel.halt/2` grant test | any `:approved` decision; a conditional was refused | a full approval only |
| `CEL.Kernel.transition/1` | structural transition table only | **unchanged** — recorded as D-ARC-014, not improvised |

## Decisions recorded (not improvised)

- **D-ARC-014 — open:** `CEL.Kernel.transition/1` still validates the transition against the runtime-state table alone;
  the *constitutional* authorisation of a state transition is not implemented. Implementing it would require a decision
  type and a policy for state transitions, which is new constitutional machinery — deliberately not improvised inside a
  stabilization batch. Recorded as an open decision with the options.

**Residual scope (not covered here):** F-GOV-010 (certificate signature, subject binding and expiry) and F-GOV-025
(the two-signal evidence contract) remain **OPEN**.

## Evidence gaps (recorded, not hidden)

- No application was booted; the proof is structural and test-bound, not runtime-bound.
- The resumption is asynchronous by design (the approval call must not block on the Council while the Council may be
  blocked on the queue), so a caller that retries before the resumer has run still sees the parked decision. The
  Council re-evaluates inline in that window, so the outcome is the same, but the timing is not synchronous.
- `HumanApprovalQueue.approve/3` and `reject/3` still have **no production callers** anywhere in the repository: the
  operator or service that grants a human approval is still to be built. The mechanism is now real; its caller is
  not.
