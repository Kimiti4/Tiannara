## Behaviour preserved, behaviour changed (explicit)

| Behaviour | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `permitted?(:approved)` | true | true (unchanged) |
| `permitted?(:conditional)` | **true** | **false** (the defect) |
| `permitted?(:requires_human)` / `(:rejected)` | false | false (unchanged) |
| Minting from a conditional decision whose conditions are satisfied | allowed | allowed (through the audited conditional-grant path) |
| Minting from a conditional decision with an **unmet** condition | **allowed** | **refused** (the defect) |
| Delete effect from a conditional decision with satisfied conditions | allowed | allowed (explicit conditional grant) |
| Delete effect from a conditional decision with unmet conditions or a violated principle | **allowed** | **refused** |
| `WorldMutationEngine.conditions_met?/1` semantics | structural + no unmet conditions | identical (now delegated to the canonical Council implementation) |

## Ordering note

Landed **before** the operator's step 2 (F-GOV-006/F-GOV-023). Reason: once an `:approved` verdict becomes reachable, a
`:conditional` verdict that is still treated as a machine grant would let an evidence-poor decision become an
authorised effect without its conditions ever being checked. The operator's explicit constraint (F-GOV-007 verified
before F-GOV-006/F-GOV-023) is respected — F-GOV-007 was closed at `5dcc9ebd` with CI evidence.

**Residual scope (not covered here):** F-GOV-006/F-GOV-023 (an approved verdict is still unreachable through the
production path) and F-GOV-004/F-GOV-017 (no human-approval resume path) remain **OPEN**.
