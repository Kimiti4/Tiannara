## Caveats

- The repo self-audits honestly: `docs/capability_ledger.md` and `docs/production_completion_baseline.md` independently rate most families PARTIAL and explicitly flag ESAP as single-backend, Planetary/civilization scale as UNVERIFIED, and deterministic replay as previously THEATRICAL.
- A formal stub system exists (`lib/tiannara/stubs/stub.ex` with `__tiannara_stub__/0`, consumed by `cel/kernel/boot_sequencer.ex` to refuse stub boot) — theatricality is deliberately surfaced, not hidden.
- PlanetaryTwin is not uniquely phantom: `Tiannara.WorldModel`, `Tiannara.TheoryEcology`, `Tiannara.KnowledgeGraph` and other root-namespace placeholders also have no beams. The CRAV `@subsystem_module_map` is largely a namespace-placeholder table; PlanetaryTwin was the entry where nothing at all existed under the name.
