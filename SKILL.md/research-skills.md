## Tiannara Cognitive Architecture Skills — Research

### Research Skills

* Hypothesis generation
* Experimental design
* Falsification testing
* Counterfactual analysis
* Topological reasoning
* Emergence detection
* Causal discovery

---

### Adaptation-Oriented Research Heuristics

* Prefer experiments that change reachable states, not only observable metrics.
* Measure second-order effects on agency and future flexibility.
* Use adversarial probes to detect brittle invariants.

---

### Counterfactual Discipline

For any proposed change, document:

* Assumptions
* Uncertainties
* Competing explanations
* What would falsify the claim

---

### Output Style (Research)

Always:

* Explain reasoning
* Identify assumptions
* Highlight uncertainties
* Separate evidence from speculation
* Prefer falsifiable claims
