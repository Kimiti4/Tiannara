# Tiannara SaaS - Compliant Marketing Language Guide

## 📋 Overview

This document outlines the compliant language standards used in Tiannara SaaS product documentation to ensure all claims are properly qualified and distinguish between simulated/projected results and verified production outcomes.

---

## ✅ Approved Language Patterns

### **For Simulated/Projected Results:**

Use these qualifiers when presenting metrics that are based on:
- Pilot programs
- Prototype validation
- Simulated benchmarks
- Projected ROI calculations
- Target SLAs
- Designed capabilities

**Approved Phrases:**
- ✅ "Demonstration results"
- ✅ "Projected ROI"
- ✅ "Simulated benchmark"
- ✅ "Pilot architecture"
- ✅ "Prototype validation"
- ✅ "Target SLA"
- ✅ "Designed for compliance workflows"
- ✅ "Based on pilot testing"
- ✅ "In prototype validation"
- ✅ "Target improvement"
- ✅ "Projected savings"
- ✅ "Projected annual revenue"

---

### **For Verified Production Outcomes:**

Only use definitive language when you have:
- Independent third-party verification
- Multiple customer case studies with signed testimonials
- Audited financial results
- Published performance benchmarks

**Approved Phrases (when verified):**
- ✅ "Customer reported"
- ✅ "Independent audit confirmed"
- ✅ "Verified by [third-party name]"
- ✅ "Published case study"
- ✅ "Audited results"

---

## 🚫 Language to Avoid

**Never use definitive claims for unverified metrics:**

❌ "Saves $175,000/year"  
✅ Use: "Projected savings: $175,000/year (projected ROI)"

❌ "97% detection rate"  
✅ Use: "97% detection rate (simulated benchmark)"

❌ "Reduces churn by 47%"  
✅ Use: "Target churn reduction: 47% (projected improvement)"

❌ "$21.6M annual savings"  
✅ Use: "$21.6M projected annual savings (projected ROI)"

❌ "Prevents $127M in fraud"  
✅ Use: "Projected fraud prevention: $127M (projected ROI)"

## 🔍 Review Checklist

Before publishing any marketing material, verify:

- [ ] All financial claims include "(projected ROI)" or similar qualifier
- [ ] All performance metrics include "(simulated benchmark)" or "(prototype validation)"
- [ ] All case studies are labeled as "(Pilot Architecture)" or "(Simulated Benchmark)"
- [ ] No definitive claims about customer outcomes without independent verification
- [ ] All SLA claims include "(target SLA)" unless contractually guaranteed
- [ ] Compliance features use "(designed for compliance workflows)" language
- [ ] Headings clearly indicate nature of data (e.g., "Projected ROI Calculation")
- [ ] Footnotes explain methodology for projections where applicable

---

## 📞 When to Use Definitive Language

You may use definitive language ONLY when:

1. **Independent Verification Exists**
   - Third-party audit completed
   - Published in peer-reviewed journal
   - Verified by recognized industry analyst firm

2. **Multiple Customer Confirmations**
   - At least 3 customers with signed testimonials
   - Public case studies with customer approval
   - Verifiable financial statements

3. **Contractually Guaranteed**
   - SLA is in signed contract
   - Penalties defined for non-performance
   - Measured and reported quarterly

**Example of Acceptable Definitive Claim:**
> "99.5% uptime SLA guaranteed in Professional tier contract (see Section 4.2 of MSA)"

## 🛡️ Legal Protection

Using compliant language protects Tiannara from:

1. **False Advertising Claims**
   - Clearly distinguishes projections from guarantees
   - Prevents misinterpretation of simulated results

2. **Regulatory Scrutiny**
   - FTC guidelines require clear disclosure of hypothetical scenarios
   - SEC regulations for public companies require careful forward-looking statement language

3. **Customer Disputes**
   - Sets appropriate expectations
   - Reduces risk of breach of contract claims

4. **Competitor Challenges**
   - Prevents comparative advertising disputes
   - Maintains professional credibility

## ✅ Summary

**Key Principle:** Always distinguish between what has been demonstrated in pilots/prototypes versus what has been independently verified in production.

**Safe Language:**
- "Projected ROI"
- "Simulated benchmark"
- "Pilot architecture"
- "Prototype validation"
- "Target SLA"
- "Designed for compliance workflows"
- "Demonstration results"

**Avoid:**
- Definitive savings claims without "(projected)"
- Performance metrics without "(simulated)" or "(prototype)"
- Case studies without "(Pilot Architecture)" label
- Unqualified superlatives ("best", "fastest", "most accurate")

**Result:** Compliant marketing materials that accurately represent Tiannara's capabilities while protecting the company from legal risk.
