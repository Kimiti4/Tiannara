## Key Architectural Decisions Required

### Decision 1: Centralized vs. Distributed Telemetry

**Option A:** Centralized telemetry aggregator
- Pros: Simpler, easier to query, consistent view
- Cons: Single point of failure, scalability limits

**Option B:** Distributed telemetry with eventual consistency
- Pros: Resilient, scalable, matches CIS philosophy
- Cons: Complex, harder to query, potential inconsistencies

**Recommendation:** Hybrid - local aggregation per domain, global eventual consistency

---

### Decision 2: Synchronous vs. Asynchronous AEO Decisions

**Option A:** Synchronous routing decisions (block until AEO decides)
- Pros: Consistent, predictable latency
- Cons: Bottleneck, reduces throughput

**Option B:** Asynchronous routing (fire-and-forget with feedback)
- Pros: High throughput, scalable
- Cons: Potential misrouting, harder to debug

**Recommendation:** Synchronous for high-stakes decisions, async for routine tasks

---

### Decision 3: Hard Constraints vs. Soft Preferences in CIS

**Option A:** Hard constraints (violation = immediate block)
- Pros: Safe, predictable, clear boundaries
- Cons: Inflexible, may block legitimate edge cases

**Option B:** Soft preferences (violation = warning + reduced trust)
- Pros: Flexible, adaptive, handles nuance
- Cons: May allow harmful behavior, harder to reason about

**Recommendation:** Tiered - hard constraints for safety-critical, soft for performance
