## Key Findings

### 1. Strategy Genomes Work ✅

Five distinct research strategies successfully implemented:
- **Aggressive Explorers**: High exploration (0.9), low validation (0.2) → 20% conversion
- **Conservative Validators**: Low exploration (0.2), high validation (0.9) → 92.5% conversion
- **Replication First**: Minimal exploration (0.1), very high validation (0.95)
- **Cross-Domain Synthesizers**: Balanced approach, high cross-domain (0.9) → 50% conversion
- **Anomaly Hunters**: High anomaly sensitivity (0.95) → 40% conversion

**Insight**: Conservative validators produce highest quality (92.5% conversion) but fewer discoveries. Aggressive explorers generate more candidates but validate fewer (20% conversion). This trade-off creates natural selection pressure.

### 2. Knowledge Production Scales Linearly ✅

Tier 4 produced **38.4 million validated discoveries** across 100k ticks:
- Generation rate: ~384 validated/sec
- Consistent 54.8% conversion rate throughout
- No degradation despite 5 major shocks

**Insight**: Active knowledge production (6.5B) vastly outperforms passive recovery (6.5A). The system doesn't just heal—it creates new validated knowledge.

### 3. System Extremely Resilient ⚠️

All shocks recovered in **1 tick** across all tiers. This suggests:
- Recovery mechanism may be TOO effective
- Cannot measure adaptation (recovery time improvement) because baseline is already optimal
- Confidence drops from shocks but immediately bounces back

**Interpretation**: This is actually a GOOD problem—the epistemic substrate is so robust that shocks cause only transient damage. However, it means we cannot test "adaptation" (improving recovery speed) because recovery is already instantaneous.

### 4. Strategy Diversity Maintained ✅

Tier 4 maintained 4 distinct strategy types with no monoculture:
- 50 aggressive explorers (20%)
- 100 conservative validators (40%)
- 50 cross-domain synthesizers (20%)
- 50 anomaly hunters (20%)

**Insight**: Diverse strategies coexist because each has different strengths. No single strategy dominates, creating evolutionary stability.

## Limitations Identified

### 1. Recovery Too Fast

All shocks recover in 1 tick, making adaptation measurement impossible. This suggests:
- Shock severity may need to be higher (>30%)
- Or recovery mechanism needs tuning (slower passive recovery)
- Current design achieves resilience but not measurable adaptation

### 2. Antifragility Not Yet Demonstrated

Cannot measure antifragility (post-shock velocity > pre-shock velocity) because:
- Velocity tracking implementation incomplete
- Recovery happens too fast to distinguish pre/post phases
- Need longer recovery periods to measure improvement

### 3. Funding Allocation Simplified

Knowledge capital calculation works but funding allocation uses simple formula. Future work:
- Implement proportional funding based on capital share
- Add competition for limited resources
- Create selection pressure through funding constraints

### 4. No Cross-Program Interaction

Programs operate independently. Missing:
- Discovery Exchange (sharing between programs)
- Cross-pollination mechanisms
- Methodology asset creation
- Import/export between worlds
