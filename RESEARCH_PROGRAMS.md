# Phase 15 Research Programs

## RP-001 Constitutional fundamentals

- Hypothesis: ownership, provenance, and replay constraints reduce invalid governance transitions.
- Design: 100,000 preregistered experiments across governance dimensions.
- Primary endpoints: invalid-transition rate, replay divergence, recovery latency.
- Gate: statistical power at least 0.99 and independent replication.

## RP-002 Long-horizon governance

- Hypothesis: explicit invariant enforcement bounds governance entropy.
- Design: 1,000 simulated 100-year histories with ablations.
- Endpoints: entropy slope, institutional survival, irreversible failure rate.

## RP-003 Civilization stability

- Hypothesis: distributed recovery paths outperform single-authority recovery under correlated failure.
- Design: 10,000 counterfactual histories with matched shocks.
- Endpoints: divergence, time to recovery, resilience, constitutional violations.

## RP-004 Replication and archaeology

- Hypothesis: exported ledgers and frozen manifests are sufficient for blind reconstruction.
- Design: independent executable that imports no runtime modules.
- Endpoints: artifact availability, state-root equality, claim reconstruction rate.

All programs require fixed seeds, logical clocks, environment manifests, raw results, exclusions, and negative results. They are planned; the repository contains no evidence that the required scales have run.
