## Common Questions

### Q: Why is Layer 6.5 necessary if Layers 1-6 already passed?
**A**: Layers 1-6 verify JTMS++ works under normal conditions. Layer 6.5 verifies the CIVILIZATION can survive catastrophic disruption. Real civilizations face paradigm shifts, funding crises, and scientific revolutions. The system must prove resilience, not just correctness.

### Q: What's the difference between "repair" and "adaptation"?
**A**: Repair returns system to previous state. Adaptation evolves system to a better state. Original plan only had replications (repair). Enhanced plan adds hypothesis generation, program creation, and funding allocation (adaptation), enabling possibility of antifragility.

### Q: Why three shock types instead of just random?
**A**: Random shocks test general robustness. Paradigm crisis tests targeted attacks on core knowledge. Funding crisis tests economic resilience. Real crises are rarely random—they target specific vulnerabilities. Multiple shock types provide comprehensive resilience assessment.

### Q: What if Layer 6.5 fails?
**A**: Failure identifies specific weaknesses (which criterion failed, when recovery stalled, what mechanisms missing). Action: analyze failure mode, strengthen weak mechanisms, adjust shock parameters if unrealistic, rerun with improvements. Cannot proceed to Phase 12.0 Step 3 until Layer 6.5 achieves ≥ Resilience classification.

### Q: How long will Layer 6.5 take to implement?
**A**: Estimated ~46 hours (5.75 days) including implementation, testing, debugging, and optimization. First run at reduced scale (10 worlds, 20k ticks) for debugging, then full scale (50 worlds, 100k ticks).

### Q: What's the most important metric in Layer 6.5?
**A**: Recovery time. Two systems might both recover to 80%, but one takes 10k ticks and another takes 80k ticks. Speed of recovery is critical for civilization sustainability. Secondary: Classification level (aiming for Resilience minimum, hoping for Antifragility).

### Q: Can we skip Layer 6.5 and go straight to Phase 12.0 Step 3?
**A**: Not recommended. Layer 6.5 is the bridge between infrastructure verification and true emergence. Without proving resilience, integrating with Civilization Scheduler risks building on fragile foundation. Better to verify now than discover fragility after full integration.
