Looking at the evolution of Tiannara from your Phase 13 and Phase 14 work, I think **Phase 14 completes the Constitutional Operating System**, not the entire project. Everything after that shifts from **building the operating system** to **building the civilization that runs on it**.

I would organize the remaining roadmap into six major eras.

---

# Phase 15 — Scientific Civilization

**Objective**

Turn Tiannara from a governance system into an autonomous scientific civilization.

Instead of running isolated simulations, Tiannara develops long-lived scientific institutions.

Core systems include:

* Scientific Councils
* Theory Lifecycle
* Discovery Pipeline
* Research Programs
* Research Funding
* Scientific Capital Markets
* Competing theories
* Evidence accumulation
* Replication studies
* Peer review
* Confidence evolution
* Discovery archaeology

Deliverables might include:

* ScientificTheory
* TheoryGenome
* DiscoveryLedger
* ExperimentLedger
* ReplicationEngine
* TheoryReplay
* ScientificCivilization

The key idea is that science becomes an ecosystem rather than individual experiments.

## One recommendation

I would avoid planning beyond **Phase 20** in implementation detail. Phases 15–20 already represent several years of work. A practical approach is to define each future phase at a high architectural level, then apply the same discipline you've established:

**Architecture → Ownership → Freeze → Implementation → Validation → Certification → Evolution**

That keeps the roadmap flexible while preserving the constitutional principles that now underpin Tiannara.
