# Phase 15 — Constitutional Scientific Discovery

## Objective

Transform Tiannara's constitutional experimentation platform into a research engine that discovers constitutional laws, governance invariants, and civilization patterns through large-scale scientific discovery.

No constitutional principle remains unexamined. Every governance phenomenon becomes data.

Every experiment becomes a research paper.

## Core Principle

The Phase 14.2 experimentation platform is the infrastructure.

Phase 15 is the science.

Phase 15 asks:

- What are the fundamental laws of governance?
- Which constitutional changes produce measurable improvements?
- What invariants hold across all valid governance models?
- Which governance patterns emerge from first principles?
- How do civilizations achieve long-term stability?
- What is the relationship between constitutional complexity and governance entropy?
- Can governance be optimized for civilization survival?

## Acceptance Criteria

The phase is complete only if all of the following are satisfied:

- Every major governance phenomenon is explained by discovered laws or patterns.
- Discovered invariants hold across all valid constitutional configurations.
- Large-scale discovery campaigns produce statistically significant results.
- Knowledge graph is fully replayable and auditable.
- Independent scientific audit validates all discoveries.
- Governance science readiness is at least GSR-4.
- Constitutional laws guide governance decisions.
- Long-horizon predictions match experimental outcomes.
- No contradictions exist between discovered laws and observed governance.
- The complete scientific discovery system passes constitutional certification.
