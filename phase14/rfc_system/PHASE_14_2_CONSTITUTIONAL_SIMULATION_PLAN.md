# Phase 14.2 — Constitutional Experimentation Platform

## Objective

Transform the RFC system from a constitutionally certified governance architecture into a scientifically validated constitutional experimentation platform capable of proving constitutional changes before they are ever allowed to exist.

No constitutional change may reach ratification without surviving scientific experimentation.

Every constitutional proposal must become an experiment.

## Core Principle

The platform is not merely a simulation subsystem. It is Tiannara's scientific method for constitutional change.

It must support:
- simulation
- benchmarking
- adversarial testing
- optimization
- evolutionary search
- A/B constitutional testing
- statistical experiments
- real-world deployment experiments
- shadow deployments
- digital twins
- replay experiments

Every RFC becomes:

Observation

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Experiment

↓

Simulation

↓

Evidence

↓

Statistical Validation

↓

Institution Review

↓

Ratification

↓

↓

Freeze

## Acceptance Criteria

The phase is complete only if all of the following are satisfied:

- Every constitutional proposal is executed as a scientific experiment before ratification.
- Simulations are deterministic and fully replayable from ledger artifacts alone.
- Counterfactual governance histories can be generated and compared.
- Statistical validation accompanies every constitutional claim.
- Evidence is content-addressed, independently reproducible, and cryptographically verifiable.
- No bypass exists around the simulation or certification pipeline.
- Long-horizon simulations produce measurable governance, entropy, fitness, and institutional health metrics.
- Civilization readiness is evaluated using explicit, versioned metrics.
- The complete experimentation platform passes independent audit and constitutional certification.
