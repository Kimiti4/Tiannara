| 10 years | Short-term: proof complexity growth, theorem discovery rate, KG density |

| 100 years | Extended: survivability of mathematical foundation, scalability under growth |

---

## Metrics

- Entropy — diversity and distribution of mathematical artifacts
- Fitness — utility of theorems for downstream verification
- Complexity — proof step count, dependency depth, symbolic expression size
- Scalability — KG node/edge count, registry registration count
- Robustness — replay divergence rate, validation campaign failure rate
- Survivability — fraction of artifacts that remain valid after time horizon
