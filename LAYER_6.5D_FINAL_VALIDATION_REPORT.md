# Executive Summary

The 100,000 tick validation run represents the first successful large-scale ecological civilization experiment within Tiannara.

The objective of Layer 6.5D was to determine whether institutional entities could survive, compete, reproduce, specialize, and evolve under economic and ecological constraints without centralized control.

The experiment successfully demonstrated:

* Long-term simulation stability
* Economic sustainability
* Adaptive improvement
* Population turnover
* Ecological differentiation
* Multi-world divergence
* Institutional selection pressure
* Capability accumulation
* High-volume knowledge production

However, the experiment also exposed a fundamental architectural limitation that prevents open-ended evolution.

The civilization ecology successfully evolved.

The civilization knowledge architecture did not.

The simulation ultimately became constrained by a hard capability ceiling, resulting in evolutionary stagnation despite continued discovery production.

This report concludes that Layer 6.5D successfully validated ecological evolution while simultaneously revealing the next major architectural bottleneck: capability saturation.
