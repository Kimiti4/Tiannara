# Discovery Capital Flows

Scientific capital is derived from evidence, never assigned directly from a claim.

`observation -> evidence quality -> replication -> validated discovery -> capital delta`

Eligible dimensions are discovery, evidence, knowledge, theory, prediction, engineering, innovation, and reproducibility. Each delta must contain an owner, capital type, signed amount, source hashes, policy version, computation proof, confidence, and reversal link when applicable.

Rules:

1. Novelty without validity earns no capital.
2. Independent replication and useful refutation earn reproducibility/evidence capital.
3. Retractions emit compensating deltas; history is not rewritten.
4. Aggregate balances must replay exactly from the append-only delta stream.
5. Self-citation, duplicate evidence, and circular provenance are excluded.

The current report documents the contract. Production issuance remains blocked until campaign evidence and an independent audit exist.
