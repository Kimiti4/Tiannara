# Decision Engine (Phase 20.9)

## Purpose

The Decision Engine is the central intelligence of the Self-Integration layer. It evaluates improvement proposals from Phase 20.6 (Engineering), Phase 20.7 (Experimentation), and Phase 20.8 (Optimization), assesses their evidence chains, risks, and scope, and makes deterministic integration decisions.

## Inputs

- Improvement proposals from 20.6/20.7/20.8 (each with complete evidence chain)
- Current constitutional state snapshot
- Historical integration records
- Active conditions/constraints

## Outputs

- IntegrationProposal records (content-addressed)
- Decision outcomes: Approve / Reject / Defer / Conditional
- Risk assessment documents
- Evidence chain verification reports

## Decision Criteria

1. **Evidence Completeness** — Proposal must include complete evidence chains
2. **Constitutional Compliance** — Must not violate existing constitutional principles
3. **Impact Assessment** — Scope must be well-defined and bounded
4. **Risk Evaluation** — Risk must be acceptable with mitigation plan
5. **Dependency Resolution** — All dependencies must be satisfiable
6. **Archaeology Requirements** — Must include archaeology preservation plan
7. **Replay Verification** — Must be independently replayable

## Process Flow

```
Proposal Received
  → Evidence Chain Verification
  → Constitutional Compliance Check
  → Impact Scope Analysis
  → Risk Assessment
  → Dependency Resolution
  → Decision Computation (deterministic)
  → IntegrationProposal Record Created
  → Decision Published
```

## Determinism

All decisions are computed deterministically from the proposal content and current constitutional state. The decision function is: `decision = f(proposal_hash, state_hash, evidence_chain_hashes)`.

## Integrity

- Every IntegrationProposal is content-addressed by its fingerprint
- Evidence chains are verified against their root hashes
- Decision rationale is recorded for audit
- All records feed into the replay and archaeology subsystems
