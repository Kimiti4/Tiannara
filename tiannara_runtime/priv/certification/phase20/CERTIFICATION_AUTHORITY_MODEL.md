# Certification Authority Model (Phase 20.999)

## Purpose

Define the authority model for constitutional certification. The authority model establishes who has the power to certify, sign, and declare the Constitutional Operating System.

## Authority Hierarchy

```
Human Authority (ultimate)
    ↑
Constitutional Council
    ↑
Constitutional Architect
    ↑
System (self-certification)
```

## Authority Roles

### Human Authority
- Ultimate authority over the Constitutional OS
- Right to override any decision
- Right to veto certification
- Right to approve constitutional amendments
- Represents human oversight

### Constitutional Council
- Governance authority
- Approves certification decisions
- Validates governance continuity
- Represents collective constitutional governance

### Constitutional Architect
- Design authority
- Confirms architecture completeness
- Validates constitutional principles
- Represents technical authority

### System
- Self-certification capability (optional)
- Automatically verifies constitutional compliance
- Provides additional evidence
- Does not replace human authority

## Authority Requirements

For certification to proceed:
1. Constitutional Architect must certify architecture
2. Constitutional Council must approve certification
3. Human Authority must confirm certification
4. System self-certification is optional but recommended

## Authority Verification

- Each authority's signature is cryptographically verifiable
- Authority identities are content-addressed
- Authority decisions are permanently recorded in archaeology
- Authority delegation is documented and traceable
- Authority changes require constitutional process
