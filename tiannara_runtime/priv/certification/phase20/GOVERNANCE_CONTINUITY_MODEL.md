# Governance Continuity Model (Phase 20.97)

## Purpose

Define the model for verifying governance continuity across generations. Ensure that constitutional invariants are preserved, policy remains consistent, override history is traceable, decisions are deterministic, and human authority is maintained throughout evolution.

## Governance Continuity Domains

| Domain | What Is Preserved | Verification |
|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| Constitutional invariants | All constitutional principles | Invariant check at each generation |
| Policy consistency | Policies remain coherent | Policy hash chain comparison |
| Override history | Complete override record | Override chain traversal |
| Decision traceability | Every decision has evidence chain | Evidence chain verification |
| Human authority | Human override capability preserved | Authority record check |
| Council integrity | Council decisions recorded and replayable | Council record chain |

## Governance Drift Detection

Governance drift occurs when:
- Constitutional invariant changes without documented amendment
- Policy becomes inconsistent with constitutional principles
- Override records are incomplete or missing
- Decisions lack evidence chain support
- Human authority is circumvented or lost
- Council decisions are not recorded or replayable

## Governance Drift Categories

| Category | Severity | Description |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| Constitutional drift | Critical | Constitutional invariant violated |
| Policy drift | Major | Policy inconsistent with constitution |
| Override drift | Major | Override history incomplete |
| Decision drift | Major | Decision evidence chain broken |
| Authority drift | Critical | Human authority compromised |
| Council drift | Major | Council record integrity lost |

## No Governance Drift Requirement

Phase 20.97 requires that governance shows no constitutional drift across all generations. This means:
- Constitutional invariants remain identical from Generation 1 through Generation 100,000
- Policy decisions remain consistent with the constitution
- Override records form a complete, continuous chain
- All decisions are traceable to evidence
- Human authority is preserved and documented
- Council records are complete and replayable
