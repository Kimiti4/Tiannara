Validate the entire Tiannara Constitutional Operating System as a single integrated constitutional organism.

Prove that every subsystem behaves constitutionally under deterministic replay, adversarial pressure, long-duration evolution, independent reconstruction, and scientific validation.

## Validation Gates

Every subsystem must pass:
1. **Structural** — Schema conformance, data model integrity
2. **Behavioral** — Input → output correctness
3. **Deterministic** — Identical output for identical input
4. **Replay** — Full replay from archaeology
5. **Knowledge** — Knowledge preservation and consistency
6. **Scientific** — Scientific method compliance
7. **Mathematical** — Proof and rewrite integrity
8. **Engineering** — Engineering artifact integrity
9. **Governance** — Constitutional invariant preservation
10. **Integration** — Cross-subsystem coherence
11. **Long-duration** — Stability over millions of cycles
12. **Adversarial** — Resilience under attack
