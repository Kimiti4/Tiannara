# Certification Evidence Chain (Phase 20.999)

## Purpose

Define the complete evidence chain for certification. Every claim that the system is constitutionally certified must be backed by verifiable evidence — from Genesis through the final certificate.

## Evidence Types

| Level | Evidence | Verification |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| 1–7 | Process evidence | Phase completion certificates |
| 8 | Validation evidence | Campaign results, gate status |
| 9 | Audit evidence | Subsystem verdicts, findings |
| 10 | Long-horizon evidence | Generation history, continuity |
| 11 | Readiness evidence | CRI computation, thresholds |
| 12 | Signature evidence | Cryptographic signatures |
| 13 | Freeze evidence | Immutable freeze record |
| 14 | Certificate evidence | Final certificate hash |

## Chain Verification

- Walk the evidence chain from Genesis to certificate
- Verify every link is continuous
- Verify every hash matches
- Verify every certificate references valid prior evidence
- Final chain root must match certificate hash
