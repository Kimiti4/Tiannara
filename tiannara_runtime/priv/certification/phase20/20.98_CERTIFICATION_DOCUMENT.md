## Certification Statement

Phase 20.98 (Constitutional Readiness Index) establishes a comprehensive quantitative framework for evaluating Tiannara's preparedness for constitutional certification. The CRI measures 10 readiness dimensions across 20 research domains using deterministic scoring, with 6 progressive levels from CRI-0 (specification only) through CRI-6 (certification candidate). The framework maintains a strict separation between readiness evaluation and final certification — the CRI measures readiness but does not issue certification.
