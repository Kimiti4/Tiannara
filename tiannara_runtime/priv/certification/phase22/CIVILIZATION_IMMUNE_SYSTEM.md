# Civilization Immune System

## Purpose

Define constitutional immune functions that detect threats, monitor health, classify risks, contain damage, coordinate recovery, integrate learning, and verify constitutional compliance — without violating constitutional invariants.

## Immune Functions

### Threat Detection
- Continuous monitoring across all civilizational subsystems
- Pattern recognition for known and novel threat types
- Anomaly detection against baseline behavior
- Early warning indicator tracking

### Health Monitoring
- Vital sign measurement across subsystems
- Trend analysis for early degradation detection
- Comparative health assessment across institutions
- Systemic health index computation

### Risk Classification
- Threat severity assessment
- Risk probability estimation
- Impact scope determination
- Urgency classification

### Damage Containment
- Isolation of affected subsystems
- Propagation barrier activation
- Critical function preservation
- Secondary damage prevention

### Recovery Coordination
- Recovery priority determination
- Resource allocation for recovery
- Cross-institution recovery coordination
- Recovery progress tracking

### Learning Integration
- Post-event analysis and lesson extraction
- Resilience model updates
- Knowledge artifact creation
- Future detection improvement

### Constitutional Verification
- All immune actions checked against constitutional bounds
- No immune action may violate constitutional invariants
- Human authority maintained throughout
- Full transparency and audit trail

## Immune Response Levels

- **Level 0 — Monitoring**: Normal operation, routine surveillance
- **Level 1 — Alert**: Anomaly detected, investigation initiated
- **Level 2 — Response**: Active containment and recovery
- **Level 3 — Crisis**: Civilizational emergency protocols
- **Level 4 — Constitutional**: Constitutional emergency provisions

## Constraints

- No immune action may violate constitutional invariants
- Immune actions must be fully replayable
- Immune records become constitutional artifacts
