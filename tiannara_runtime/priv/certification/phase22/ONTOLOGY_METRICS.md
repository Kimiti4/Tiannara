# Ontology Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the coverage, quality, consistency, and utility of the Constitutional Civilizational Ontology.

## Metrics

- **Ontology Coverage**: Fraction of civilization domains represented
- **Entity Completeness**: Completeness of entity attributes
- **Relationship Density**: Number of relationships per entity
- **Knowledge Connectivity**: Cross-domain knowledge links
- **Infrastructure Connectivity**: Infrastructure dependency mapping completeness
- **Resource Coverage**: Resource types and flows represented
- **Institution Diversity**: Variety of institution types represented
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Extensibility**: Ease of adding new entity types
- **Semantic Consistency**: Absence of contradictions in ontology

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ontology Coverage | 0.70 | 0.90 | 0.99+ |
| Entity Completeness | 0.80 | 0.95 | 1.00 |
| Relationship Density | 2.0 | 5.0 | 10.0+ |
| Knowledge Connectivity | 0.50 | 0.75 | 0.95+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
| Semantic Consistency | 0.95 | 0.99 | 1.00 |
