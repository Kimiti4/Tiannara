# Systemic Risk Engine

## Purpose

Model and track systemic risks across all civilizational subsystems, with explicit risk propagation pathways.

## Risk Domains

### Scientific Ecosystem
- Research institution fragility
- Critical experiment single points of failure
- Knowledge monopoly risk
- Scientific methodology erosion

### Engineering Infrastructure
- Critical infrastructure single points of failure
- Infrastructure interdependence cascades
- Maintenance deficit accumulation
- Technology obsolescence

### Energy
- Energy source concentration risk
- Grid fragility and cascade potential
- Storage inadequacy
- Transition disruption risk

### Economy
- Economic concentration risk
- Supply chain critical dependencies
- Financial contagion vectors
- Resource depletion risk

### Governance
- Governance authority concentration
- Constitutional violation cascade
- Decision paralysis risk
- Institutional capture risk

### Education
- Educational infrastructure fragility
- Knowledge transmission interruption
- Curriculum obsolescence
- Research training pipeline fragility

### Healthcare
- Healthcare system capacity
- Pandemic and epidemic risk
- Medical supply chain fragility
- Health infrastructure resilience

### Communication
- Communication network fragility
- Information cascade risks
- Disinformation vulnerability
- Communication monopoly risk

### Manufacturing
- Manufacturing concentration risk
- Critical component dependencies
- Supply chain fragility
- Manufacturing capacity adequacy

### Environment
- Environmental system tipping points
- Climate cascade risks
- Biodiversity loss impacts
- Resource scarcity risks

### Knowledge Systems
- Knowledge archive fragility
- Knowledge monopoly risk
- Knowledge loss cascade
- Knowledge fragmentation risk

## Risk Properties

Each risk has: source, probability, impact, propagation pathways, mitigation options, confidence, trend.

## Constraints

- Risk propagation must be explicit
- Risk records become constitutional artifacts
- Systemic risks are tracked across all domains
