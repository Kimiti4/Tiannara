# Type II Transition Planning

## Purpose

Plan the transition from Type I to Type II — stellar-scale energy capture through advanced space-based infrastructure.

## Type II Characteristics

- Total energy capture: ~10^26 W
- Dyson swarm or similar stellar capture structures
- Comprehensive space-based infrastructure
- Interplanetary energy grid
- Advanced energy conversion technologies

## Transition Requirements

### Scientific Prerequisites
- Advanced materials science (nanostructures, metamaterials)
- Space-based energy conversion physics
- Autonomous construction at scale
- Orbital and planetary mechanics

### Engineering Prerequisites
- Self-replicating construction systems
- Large-scale space manufacturing
- Orbital infrastructure deployment
- Autonomous maintenance systems

### Infrastructure Prerequisites
- Space launch and transport infrastructure
- Orbital construction facilities
- Planetary surface bases
- Deep space communication network

### Institutional Prerequisites
- Multi-planet governance framework
- Space resource rights and allocation
- Interplanetary scientific coordination
- Constitutional extension to space

## Transition Phases

1. **Space Access**: Reliable, affordable launch capability
2. **Orbital Infrastructure**: Permanent orbital facilities
3. **Planetary Development**: Multi-planet presence
4. **Stellar Capture**: Dyson swarm construction begins
5. **Completion**: Full stellar energy capture achieved

## Milestones

Each phase has verifiable milestones for energy capture, infrastructure deployment, and capability development.

## Constraints

- Transition must be constitutionally governed
- Planetary civilization continuity maintained
- Transition records become constitutional artifacts
