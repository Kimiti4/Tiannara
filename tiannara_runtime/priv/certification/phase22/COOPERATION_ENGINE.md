# Cooperation Engine

## Purpose

Manage and coordinate inter-civilization cooperation across scientific, engineering, infrastructure, emergency response, knowledge sharing, education, technology transfer, and joint exploration domains.

## Cooperation Domains

### Scientific Cooperation
- Joint research programs and agendas
- Shared laboratory and facility access
- Distributed simulation and computation
- Collaborative data collection and analysis

### Engineering Cooperation
- Large-scale engineering projects
- Shared manufacturing capacity
- Joint technology development
- Infrastructure co-investment

### Infrastructure Sharing
- Shared energy grid interconnections
- Joint communication infrastructure
- Shared transportation networks
- Cooperative computational infrastructure

### Emergency Response
- Cross-civilization disaster response
- Shared emergency resources
- Mutual aid agreements
- Joint recovery operations

### Knowledge Sharing
- Open knowledge repositories
- Educational exchange programs
- Translation and knowledge accessibility
- Cultural and scientific exchange

### Technology Transfer
- Technology licensing and sharing
- Cooperative R&D programs
- Technology adaptation and localization
- Joint intellectual property frameworks

### Joint Exploration
- Space exploration collaboration
- Deep ocean and extreme environment exploration
- Scientific frontier expeditions
- Shared exploration infrastructure

## Cooperation Mechanisms

### Voluntary Cooperation
- Civilizations choose to participate
- Cooperation terms are negotiated
- Exit provisions are included

### Constitutional Cooperation
- Cooperation respects constitutional principles
- Human authority is preserved
- Transparency and accountability maintained

### Agreement-Based Cooperation
- Formal cooperation agreements
- Clearly defined rights and obligations
- Dispute resolution mechanisms

## Cooperation Structure

Each cooperation artifact contains:

- **Cooperation ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Type**: Cooperation domain and mechanism
- **Participating Civilizations**: Involved parties
- **Agreements**: Formal cooperation agreements
- **Shared Resources**: Resources allocated to cooperation
- **Cooperation Terms**: Rights, obligations, duration
- **Expected Outcomes**: Anticipated cooperation benefits
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Cooperation must be voluntary and constitutional
- Cooperation records become constitutional artifacts
- Cooperation does not compromise civilizational sovereignty
