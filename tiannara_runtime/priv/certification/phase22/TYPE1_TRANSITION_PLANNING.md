# Type I Transition Planning

## Purpose

Plan the transition from current Kardashev level to Type I — complete planetary-scale energy capture.

## Type I Characteristics

- Total energy capture: ~10^16 W
- Full utilization of planetary energy resources
- Global energy grid integration
- Comprehensive renewable and advanced energy portfolio
- Climate and environmental energy management

## Transition Requirements

### Scientific Prerequisites
- Advanced fusion energy science
- High-efficiency energy conversion
- Large-scale energy storage
- Global climate and energy modeling

### Engineering Prerequisites
- Global energy grid architecture
- Large-scale renewable deployment
- Fusion power plant engineering
- Energy storage at planetary scale

### Infrastructure Prerequisites
- Global transmission network
- Distributed generation infrastructure
- Energy storage facilities
- Smart grid management systems

### Institutional Prerequisites
- Global energy governance framework
- Energy transition coordination
- Constitutional energy provisions
- International scientific collaboration

## Transition Phases

1. **Foundation**: Current energy systems optimized and expanded
2. **Transition**: Gradual replacement of fossil fuels with advanced sources
3. **Integration**: Global energy grid interconnection
4. **Completion**: Full planetary energy capture achieved

## Milestones

Each phase has verifiable milestones with completion criteria, timeline estimates, and resource requirements.

## Constraints

- Transition must be constitutionally governed
- Energy resilience maintained throughout transition
- Environmental impact minimized
- Transition records become constitutional artifacts
