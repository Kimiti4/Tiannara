# Architect Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive reports on the state, recommendations, and evolution of civilization architecture.

## Report Sections

- Executive Summary
- Architecture Vision
- Requirement Coverage
- Recommended Architecture
- Evaluation Results
- Constitutional Alignment Certification
- Multi-Century Roadmap
- Alternative Architectures
- Risk Assessment
- Replay and Archaeology
- Architect Recommendations

## Frequency
- Standard: Per architecture recommendation cycle
- Comprehensive: Per 10 generations
