# Reality Alignment Engine

## Purpose

Track and measure alignment between the digital twin and observed reality, documenting agreement, divergence, missing observations, uncertainty, and confidence.

## Alignment Dimensions

### Agreement
- Twin state matches observed reality within uncertainty bounds
- High confidence, low divergence
- Twin is well-synchronized

### Divergence
- Twin state differs from observed reality beyond uncertainty bounds
- May indicate missing synchronization or model error
- Divergence triggers investigation

### Missing Observations
- Civilization dimensions with insufficient observational coverage
- Twin relies on projection rather than observation
- Observation gaps documented for prioritization

### Uncertainty
- Confidence bounds on all twin state values
- Uncertainty sources explicitly documented
- Uncertainty reduction prioritized

### Confidence
- Aggregate confidence in twin fidelity
- Dimension-specific confidence scores
- Confidence tracks synchronization history

## Alignment Metrics

- **Reality Alignment Score**: Overall twin fidelity (0.0–1.0)
- **Dimension Alignment Scores**: Per-dimension fidelity
- **Observation Coverage**: Fraction of dimensions with recent observations
- **Divergence Magnitude**: Total divergence across all dimensions
- **Uncertainty Bounds**: Average uncertainty across dimensions

## Alignment Management

- No forced convergence — twins may legitimately diverge
- Divergence triggers synchronization, not mutation
- Uncertainty increases with divergence
- Confidence decreases with staleness

## Constraints

- Alignment measurements must be deterministic
- Alignment records become constitutional artifacts
- Reality always has authority over the twin
