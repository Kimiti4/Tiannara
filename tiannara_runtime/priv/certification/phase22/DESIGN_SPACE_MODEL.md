# Design Space Model

## Purpose

Define the complete search space for civilization optimization, with each dimension having constitutional bounds.

## Design Dimensions

### Institution Design
- Institution portfolio composition
- Institution architecture (centralized, distributed, federated)
- Inter-institutional interface design
- Governance structure per institution

### Infrastructure
- Infrastructure portfolio composition
- Capacity allocation per domain
- Geographic distribution
- Redundancy configuration

### Scientific Portfolio
- Research domain distribution
- Basic vs. applied research ratio
- Research institution configuration
- Scientific capital allocation

### Engineering Portfolio
- Engineering domain distribution
- Infrastructure project portfolio
- Engineering capability targets
- Technology development roadmap

### Education
- Education system architecture
- Curriculum breadth vs. depth balance
- Research training integration
- Lifelong learning infrastructure

### Governance
- Governance structure design
- Decision authority distribution
- Constitutional enforcement mechanisms
- Inter-branch coordination

### Energy
- Energy source portfolio
- Infrastructure investment allocation
- Transition pathway selection
- Storage and distribution architecture

### Economy
- Economic system architecture
- Resource allocation mechanisms
- Capital flow design
- Market vs. planning balance

### Manufacturing
- Manufacturing capacity distribution
- Automation level targets
- Supply chain architecture
- Quality assurance systems

### Automation
- Automation level by domain
- Human-automation interface design
- Autonomous system governance
- Safety and reliability requirements

### Space Capability
- Space infrastructure targets
- Launch capability requirements
- Orbital infrastructure plan
- Planetary surface capability

### Environmental Systems
- Environmental management approach
- Resource renewal mechanisms
- Ecological preservation targets
- Climate management strategy

## Constitutional Bounds

Every dimension has constitutional bounds that define the feasible region:
- Minimum and maximum values per dimension
- Constitutional guarantee constraints
- Resource budget constraints
- Feasibility constraints
- Preference constraints (soft, recorded)

## Constraints

- Design space must be fully deterministic
- Constitutional bounds are non-negotiable
- Design space records become constitutional artifacts
