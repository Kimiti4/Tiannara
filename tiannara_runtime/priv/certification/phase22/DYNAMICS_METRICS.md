# Dynamics Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the accuracy, coverage, and utility of civilizational dynamics models.

## Metrics

- **Growth Rate**: Civilization growth rate across dimensions
- **Innovation Rate**: New innovations per unit time
- **Knowledge Growth**: Knowledge accumulation rate
- **Scientific Productivity**: Discoveries per scientific capital unit
- **Engineering Productivity**: Infrastructure per engineering capital unit
- **Resilience Index**: Composite civilizational resilience (0.0–1.0)
- **Collapse Probability**: Probability of collapse within forecast horizon
- **Recovery Rate**: Speed of recovery after disruption
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Dynamic Model Completeness**: Coverage of civilizational dynamics domains

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resilience Index | 0.50 | 0.70 | 0.90+ |
| Growth Rate | >0% | >2%/yr | >5%/yr |
| Innovation Rate | >0 | Sustainable | Accelerating |
| Scientific Productivity | 0.50 | 0.75 | 0.95+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
