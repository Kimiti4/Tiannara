# Institution Ontology

## Purpose

Formally represent all institution types within civilizations, including their structure, purpose, capabilities, dependencies, and lineage.

## Institution Types

### Scientific Institutes
- Research organizations and laboratories
- Observatories and experimental facilities
- Scientific academies and societies
- Research funding organizations

### Universities and Education
- Higher education institutions
- Research universities
- Training and vocational schools
- Knowledge dissemination organizations

### Government Institutions
- Legislative bodies
- Executive agencies
- Judicial systems
- Regulatory authorities
- Civil service organizations

### Industrial Organizations
- Manufacturing enterprises
- Technology companies
- Energy producers and distributors
- Infrastructure operators

### Healthcare Systems
- Medical research organizations
- Healthcare providers
- Public health agencies
- Biomedical institutions

### Financial Systems
- Banking and finance
- Investment and capital allocation
- Insurance and risk management
- Economic governance

### Cultural Institutions
- Museums and archives
- Cultural preservation organizations
- Media and communication
- Arts and humanities

## Institution Properties

Each institution possesses:
- **Institution ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Purpose**: Constitutional purpose and mission
- **Responsibilities**: Specific duties and obligations
- **Capabilities**: Available skills, knowledge, and resources
- **Dependencies**: Required inputs from other entities
- **Resources**: Allocated capital and assets
- **Knowledge**: Domain-specific knowledge holdings
- **Lineage**: Institutional heritage and predecessor relationships
- **Governance**: Internal governance structure
- **Interfaces**: Connection points to other institutions

## Institutional Relationships

- Hierarchical (superior/subordinate)
- Collaborative (peer partnerships)
- Dependency (provider/consumer)
- Regulatory (oversight/compliance)
- Successor (lineage-based replacement)

## Constraints

- Institutions must preserve complete lineage
- Institutional records become constitutional artifacts
- Institutions may be composite (containing sub-institutions)
