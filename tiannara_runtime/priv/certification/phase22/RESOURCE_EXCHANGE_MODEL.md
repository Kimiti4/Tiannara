# Resource Exchange Model

## Purpose

Represent and govern exchanges of resources between civilizations, ensuring provenance preservation, fair exchange, and deterministic tracking of all resource flows.

## Exchangeable Resources

### Energy
- Electrical power exchange
- Fuel and energy carrier transfer
- Grid interconnection capacity
- Energy storage sharing

### Materials
- Raw materials and minerals
- Manufactured components
- Specialized materials
- Waste and recycling flows

### Knowledge
- Scientific knowledge and data
- Engineering knowledge and designs
- Technical documentation
- Procedural knowledge

### Technology
- Hardware and equipment
- Software and algorithms
- Patents and intellectual property
- Technical specifications

### Manufacturing Capacity
- Production capacity allocation
- Specialized manufacturing services
- Tooling and equipment sharing
- Quality assurance services

### Computation
- Computing capacity allocation
- Specialized computation services
- Data storage and processing
- Algorithm access and execution

### Scientific Data
- Observational data sets
- Experimental results
- Simulation outputs
- Validated measurements

### Engineering Assets
- Infrastructure access rights
- Facility sharing agreements
- Equipment lending programs
- Maintenance and support services

## Exchange Provenance

Every exchange preserves complete provenance:

- **Origin**: Source civilization and resource identifier
- **Destination**: Receiving civilization
- **Exchange Terms**: Conditions of transfer
- **Timeline**: Exchange schedule and duration
- **Value Assessment**: Relative value of exchanged resources
- **Obligations**: Ongoing commitments arising from exchange

## Exchange Structure

Each exchange artifact contains:

- **Exchange ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Resource Type**: Type of resource being exchanged
- **Source Civilization**: Providing civilization
- **Destination Civilization**: Receiving civilization
- **Resource Description**: Detailed resource specification
- **Quantity**: Amount of resource exchanged
- **Value**: Relative exchange value
- **Exchange Terms**: Conditions and obligations
- **Provenance Chain**: Complete transfer history
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- All exchanges preserve complete provenance
- Exchange records become constitutional artifacts
- Exchanges must be mutually agreed and constitutional
