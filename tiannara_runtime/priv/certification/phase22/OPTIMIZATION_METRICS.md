# Optimization Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the effectiveness, coverage, and quality of civilization optimization.

## Metrics

- **Design Space Coverage**: Fraction of feasible design space explored
- **Constraint Satisfaction Rate**: Fraction of candidates satisfying all constraints
- **Pareto Frontier Size**: Number of non-dominated solutions discovered
- **Optimization Efficiency**: Pareto solutions per unit search effort
- **Tradeoff Diversity**: Range of tradeoff profiles in Pareto frontier
- **Objective Balance**: Distribution of objective scores across frontier
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Optimization Confidence**: Confidence in Pareto frontier completeness (0.0–1.0)
- **Civilizational Optimization Readiness**: Overall optimization maturity index

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constraint Satisfaction | 95% | 99% | 100% |
| Pareto Frontier Size | 5 | 20 | 100+ |
| Tradeoff Diversity | 0.50 | 0.75 | 0.95+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
