# Engineering Evaluation Model

## Purpose

Evaluate civilization designs across multiple dimensions — ensuring no single metric dominates and evaluations remain evidence-based.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### Scientific Productivity
- Knowledge generation rate per scientific capital unit
- Discovery rate relative to investment
- Research institution efficiency
- Knowledge integration rate

### Engineering Productivity
- Infrastructure output per engineering capital unit
- Engineering project completion rate
- Technology deployment velocity
- Engineering knowledge reuse rate

### Institutional Stability
- Institution survival rate over time
- Institutional adaptation velocity
- Governance continuity
- Institutional knowledge retention

### Infrastructure Efficiency
- Infrastructure output per resource input
- Capacity utilization rates
- Maintenance cost as fraction of total
- Infrastructure lifecycle performance

### Innovation Rate
- New innovations per time unit
- Innovation adoption velocity
- Cross-domain innovation transfer rate
- Breakthrough innovation frequency

### Knowledge Growth
- Knowledge accumulation rate
- Knowledge diversity index
- Knowledge integration index
- Knowledge loss rate

### Resilience
- Civilizational Resilience Index
- Recovery speed after disruption
- Adaptive capacity score
- Redundancy adequacy

### Sustainability
- Resource renewal vs. consumption ratio
- Environmental health trend
- Long-term capability trajectory
- Inter-generational equity

### Human Flourishing
- Health and longevity metrics
- Educational attainment
- Economic opportunity
- Cultural and scientific participation

## Evaluation Process

- All dimensions evaluated for each design
- Scores aggregated with explicit trade-offs
- No single metric dominates evaluation
- Evaluation records become constitutional artifacts

## Constraints

- Evaluations must be evidence-based
- Multi-dimensional scoring required
- Evaluation records become constitutional artifacts
