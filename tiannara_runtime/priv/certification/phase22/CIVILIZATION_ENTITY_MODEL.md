# Civilization Entity Model

## Purpose

Formally represent civilizations as first-class entities within the Constitutional Civilizational Ontology.

## Model Structure

Every civilization contains:
- **Civilization ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Name**: Human-readable designation
- **Epoch**: Current developmental epoch
- **Population**: Population characteristics and distribution
- **Knowledge State**: Total accumulated knowledge representation
- **Scientific Capital**: Research and discovery capacity
- **Engineering Capital**: Infrastructure and construction capacity
- **Energy Profile**: Energy production, consumption, and type distribution
- **Economic Profile**: Economic system, output, and characteristics
- **Governance Profile**: Governance structure and institutions
- **Infrastructure Profile**: Infrastructure systems and capacity
- **Technological Capability**: Technology levels across domains
- **Education Capability**: Education system capacity and reach
- **Industrial Capability**: Manufacturing and industrial capacity
- **Environmental State**: Environmental conditions and resource base
- **External Relationships**: Relationships with other civilizations or entities
- **Replay Root**: Content-addressed replay identifier
- **Archaeology Root**: Content-addressed archaeology identifier

## Civilization Identity

Each civilization has a unique identity derived from:
- Origin planet and location
- Founding epoch
- Constitutional charter hash
- Lineage (parent civilization if applicable)
- Cultural and historical identifiers

## Epoch Classification

- **Formative**: Early development, basic infrastructure
- **Developmental**: Growing complexity, expanding capabilities
- **Mature**: Stable systems, advanced capabilities
- **Transcendent**: Multi-planet or multi-system presence
- **Legacy**: Historical or successor civilization

## Constraints

- All civilizations must have complete provenance
- Civilization records become constitutional artifacts
- Civilizations may have successor or predecessor relationships
