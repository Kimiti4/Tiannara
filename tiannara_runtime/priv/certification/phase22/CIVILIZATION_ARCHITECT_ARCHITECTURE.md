# Constitutional Civilization Architect Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Civilization Architect is the highest layer of civilizational intelligence. Instead of asking "How do we optimize Tiannara?" it asks "What is the optimal civilization architecture for humanity over the next several centuries?" It synthesizes all prior phases — ontology, dynamics, twin, engineering, optimization, resilience, evolution, Kardashev planning, and simulation — into comprehensive civilization architecture proposals.

## Constitutional Principle

The Architect proposes. Humanity decides. Constitutional governance is the non-negotiable framework. The Architect's role is to integrate all civilizational knowledge into coherent, evidence-grounded architectural visions for humanity's long-term future.
