# Twin Consistency Engine

## Purpose

Verify consistency across all digital twin representations — logical, mathematical, causal, institutional, resource, engineering, and cross-layer.

## Consistency Dimensions

### Logical Consistency
- No contradictory state values within a layer
- All relationships respect defined constraints
- Entity identifiers are unique and content-addressed

### Mathematical Consistency
- Quantitative relationships balance (e.g., conservation laws)
- Resource flows satisfy mass-energy accounting
- Growth and decay rates respect mathematical bounds

### Causal Consistency
- Causes precede effects in time
- No backward causality in state transitions
- Intervention simulations respect causal ordering

### Institutional Consistency
- Institution capabilities match their responsibilities
- Resource allocations respect institutional boundaries
- Dependencies are satisfied before outputs are produced

### Resource Consistency
- Total resources equal sum of allocations
- Resource flows satisfy conservation
- Resource transformations preserve substance

### Engineering Consistency
- Engineering capabilities match infrastructure requirements
- Infrastructure capacity meets demand
- Engineering projects respect resource constraints

### Cross-Layer Consistency
- Knowledge layer enables science and engineering layers
- Infrastructure layer supports economy and population layers
- Governance layer regulates all layers consistently

## Consistency Verification

- Automated consistency checks on every synchronization
- Periodic comprehensive consistency audit
- Inconsistencies preserved, never silently corrected
- Inconsistency records become constitutional artifacts

## Constraints

- Consistency verification must be deterministic
- Failures are preserved, not hidden
- Consistency records become constitutional artifacts
