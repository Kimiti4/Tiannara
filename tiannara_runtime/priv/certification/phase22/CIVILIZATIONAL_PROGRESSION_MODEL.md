# Civilizational Progression Model

## Purpose

Track and evaluate multi-dimensional civilizational progression across constitutional stages, capturing the full breadth of advancement beyond mere energy capture.

## Progression Dimensions

### Scientific Discovery
- Fundamental knowledge accumulation
- Scientific methodology maturity
- Research institution capability
- Cross-domain knowledge integration
- Discovery rate and acceleration

### Engineering Capability
- Infrastructure complexity and scale
- Manufacturing precision and capacity
- Automation and robotics advancement
- System integration capability

### Governance Quality
- Constitutional governance maturity
- Institutional effectiveness
- Coordination capability
- Transparency and accountability

### Resilience Capacity
- Systemic risk management
- Recovery capability
- Redundancy adequacy
- Adaptive capacity

### Sustainability
- Environmental health
- Resource renewal rates
- Long-term viability
- Inter-generational equity

### Energy Capture
- Total energy capture
- Energy efficiency
- Source diversity
- Energy infrastructure maturity

### Global Coordination
- Global research coordination
- Global infrastructure coordination
- Knowledge distribution
- Planetary-scale collaboration

### Collective Intelligence
- Educational attainment
- Information processing capacity
- Computational capability
- Knowledge accessibility

## Progression Assessment

1. **Measure Current State**: Evaluate all progression dimensions
2. **Compare to Stage Criteria**: Match against constitutional stage definitions
3. **Calculate Stage Score**: Weighted multi-dimensional stage score
4. **Identify Gaps**: Dimension-specific gaps to next stage
5. **Determine Trajectory**: Direction and rate of progression
6. **Generate Assessment**: Complete progression artifact

## Progression Structure

Each progression assessment contains:

- **Assessment ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Civilization ID**: Civilization being assessed
- **Current Stage**: Current constitutional stage (0, I, II, III)
- **Dimension Scores**: Score per progression dimension
- **Overall Progression Score**: Weighted composite score (0.0–1.0)
- **Stage Transition Readiness**: Readiness for next stage per dimension
- **Progression Rate**: Rate of progression over time
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Progression assessment must be multi-dimensional
- Assessment records become constitutional artifacts
- Progression is measured, not optimized
