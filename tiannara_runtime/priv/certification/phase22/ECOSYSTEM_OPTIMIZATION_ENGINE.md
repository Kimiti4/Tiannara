# Ecosystem Optimization Engine

## Purpose

Optimize multi-civilization ecosystem outcomes while respecting civilizational autonomy, constitutional constraints, resource limits, scientific priorities, engineering priorities, and long-term sustainability.

## Optimization Constraints

### Autonomy Constraint
No civilization is optimized at the expense of its constitutional sovereignty.

### Constitutional Constraint
All ecosystem optimizations must satisfy constitutional principles.

### Resource Limit Constraint
Optimization respects the resource limits of each civilization.

### Scientific Priority Constraint
Scientific integrity and discovery capacity are preserved.

### Engineering Priority Constraint
Engineering capacity and capability development are preserved.

### Sustainability Constraint
Long-term ecosystem sustainability is never compromised for short-term gains.

## Optimization Objectives

### Knowledge Growth
- Maximize knowledge creation and exchange across civilizations
- Optimize scientific collaboration network structure
- Balance exploration and exploitation in research

### Capability Development
- Accelerate capability maturation across civilizations
- Balance specialization and diversity
- Optimize technology transfer and adaptation

### Resource Efficiency
- Minimize resource waste through exchange
- Optimal allocation of shared resources
- Reduce redundant infrastructure

### Ecosystem Resilience
- Maximize ecosystem robustness to disruptions
- Optimize redundancy while minimizing waste
- Balance efficiency and resilience

### Coordination Effectiveness
- Maximize value from inter-civilization coordination
- Minimize coordination overhead
- Optimize communication and decision-making networks

## Optimization Methodology

1. **Define Objective Function**: Multi-objective function with weights
2. **Identify Decision Variables**: Coordination parameters to optimize
3. **Apply Constraints**: Constitutional, autonomy, resource, sustainability constraints
4. **Search Optimization Space**: Deterministic optimization search
5. **Evaluate Pareto Frontier**: Trade-off analysis across objectives
6. **Generate Recommendation**: Optimized ecosystem configuration
7. **Human Review**: Recommendations presented for human constitutional review

## Optimization Structure

Each optimization artifact contains:

- **Optimization ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Ecosystem ID**: Ecosystem being optimized
- **Objective Weights**: Weights for each optimization objective
- **Constraints Applied**: Constraints enforced during optimization
- **Optimization Results**: Pareto-optimal ecosystem configurations
- **Trade-off Analysis**: Objective trade-off quantification
- **Recommendations**: Suggested ecosystem optimizations
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Optimization respects all constitutional constraints
- Optimization records become constitutional artifacts
- Optimization recommendations are advisory; human authority is preserved
