# Forecast Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive forecast reports summarizing forecast methodology, assumptions, scenarios, confidence distributions, decision points, risks, recalibration history, and recommendations.

## Report Structure

### Executive Summary
- High-level forecast overview
- Key findings and predictions
- Major uncertainties and risks
- Critical decision points

### Methodology
- Forecast models used
- Scenario generation methodology
- Uncertainty propagation approach
- Recalibration methodology

### Current Forecast
- Central trajectory and confidence intervals
- Scenario set with probabilities
- Key assumptions and evidence base
- Horizon-specific predictions

### Uncertainty Assessment
- Quantified uncertainty per dimension
- Dominant uncertainty sources
- Uncertainty evolution over horizon
- Known unknowns and limitations

### Decision Points
- Identified decision points and timing
- Probability, impact, and reversibility
- Decision options and branching outcomes
- Recommended decision timing

### Risk Assessment
- Identified risks and their probabilities
- Risk impact on forecast trajectories
- Risk interaction and cascading effects
- Risk mitigation recommendations

### Recalibration History
- Complete recalibration chain
- Prediction error history
- Confidence evolution over time
- Methodology improvements

### Scenario Summary
- Scenario descriptions and trajectories
- Probability-weighted outcome distribution
- Scenario signposts and indicators
- Scenario diversity assessment

### Recommendations
- Forecast-informed recommendations
- Key uncertainties requiring attention
- Suggested evidence collection priorities
- Areas for forecast methodology improvement

## Report Format

Reports are generated as deterministic constitutional artifacts with:
- Content-addressed identifier
- Complete provenance
- Fingerprint for verification
- Human-readable structure

## Constraints

- Reports must be comprehensive and transparent
- Report records become constitutional artifacts
- Uncertainty is never hidden in report presentation
