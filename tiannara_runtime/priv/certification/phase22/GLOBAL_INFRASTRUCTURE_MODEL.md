# Global Infrastructure Model

## Purpose

Represent constitutional planetary-scale infrastructure systems and their interdependencies, evolution pathways, and readiness for Kardashev stage transitions.

## Infrastructure Domains

### Energy Grid
- Generation, transmission, distribution, storage
- Grid intelligence and automated balancing
- Cross-continental interconnections
- Resilience and redundancy

### Transportation
- Surface, air, sea, and space transportation
- Logistics networks and hubs
- Autonomous transportation systems
- High-speed and mass transit

### Communication
- Global communication network
- Satellite and terrestrial connectivity
- Quantum communication infrastructure
- Bandwidth and latency evolution

### Water
- Freshwater capture, treatment, distribution
- Desalination and water recycling
- Global water management
- Water quality monitoring

### Food
- Agricultural production and distribution
- Vertical farming and controlled environment agriculture
- Global food security systems
- Sustainable agriculture practices

### Healthcare
- Universal healthcare infrastructure
- Disease surveillance and response
- Medical research and development
- Health monitoring and prevention

### Scientific Infrastructure
- Research facilities and laboratories
- Observatories and sensors
- Particle accelerators and experimental facilities
- Scientific computing infrastructure

### Manufacturing
- Distributed and centralized manufacturing
- Additive and precision manufacturing
- Industrial automation and robotics
- Manufacturing supply chains

### Computation
- Global computing network
- Distributed and cloud computing
- Specialized computing (quantum, AI)
- Computational resource sharing

### Space Infrastructure
- Launch capabilities
- Orbital infrastructure
- Lunar and planetary bases
- Deep space capabilities

## Infrastructure Interdependencies

All infrastructure systems are interdependent:

- Energy depends on communication for grid management
- Communication depends on energy for power
- Water depends on energy for treatment
- Food depends on water, energy, transportation
- Healthcare depends on energy, communication, transportation
- Computation depends on energy, manufacturing

Interdependencies are explicitly modeled and tracked.

## Infrastructure Structure

Each infrastructure assessment contains:

- **Infrastructure ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Domain**: Infrastructure domain
- **Current Capacity**: Current operational capacity
- **Target Capacity**: Required capacity for target stage
- **Interdependencies**: Dependencies on other infrastructure domains
- **Evolution Pathway**: Roadmap for capacity expansion
- **Resilience Assessment**: Robustness and redundancy evaluation
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Infrastructure interdependencies must be explicit
- Infrastructure records become constitutional artifacts
- Infrastructure planning is evidence-driven
