# Civilizational State Transitions

## Purpose

Define deterministic state transitions for civilizations — expansion, stabilization, transformation, contraction, recovery, and reorganization.

## Transition Types

### Expansion
- Growth acceleration across multiple dimensions
- New capability domain acquisition
- Infrastructure build-out phase
- Knowledge frontier expansion
- Trigger: innovation cluster, energy surplus, institutional quality

### Stabilization
- Growth rate stabilization at sustainable levels
- Optimization of existing systems
- Institutional maturation
- Infrastructure maintenance focus
- Trigger: capacity limits, diminishing returns, risk awareness

### Transformation
- Fundamental reorganization of civilizational systems
- New energy regime adoption
- Institutional restructuring
- Technological step-change
- Trigger: breakthrough innovation, existential pressure, opportunity recognition

### Contraction
- Reduction in capability or complexity
- Infrastructure consolidation
- Institutional simplification
- Knowledge domain contraction
- Trigger: resource constraints, environmental stress, systemic failure

### Recovery
- Restoration after contraction or collapse
- Infrastructure rebuilding
- Knowledge recovery and regeneration
- Institutional reconstitution
- Trigger: stabilization of decline, resource availability, external assistance

### Reorganization
- Structural reconfiguration without expansion or contraction
- Institutional redesign
- Governance reform
- Capability portfolio rebalancing
- Trigger: performance evaluation, adaptation pressure, learning

## Transition Properties

- **Trigger Conditions**: Deterministic thresholds that initiate transition
- **Transition Duration**: Expected time to complete transition
- **Transition Pathway**: Sequence of intermediate states
- **Success Factors**: Conditions for successful transition
- **Failure Modes**: How transitions can fail
- **Lineage**: Parent and successor transition records

## Constraints

- Transition conditions must be deterministic
- All transitions preserve replay lineage
- Transition records become constitutional artifacts
