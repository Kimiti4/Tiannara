# Evolution Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive reports on the state, trajectory, and continuity of civilizational evolution.

## Report Sections

- Executive Summary
- Current Epoch Assessment
- Evolution Trajectory
- Institutional Evolution Status
- Knowledge Evolution Trends
- Technological Evolution Progress
- Continuity Verification
- Long-Horizon Projections
- Replay and Archaeology
- Evolution Recommendations

## Frequency
- Standard: Per epoch transition
- Comprehensive: Per 10 epoch transitions
