# Readiness Evaluation Engine

## Purpose

Quantitatively evaluate civilization readiness for Kardashev stage transitions across multiple dimensions, producing probabilistic readiness assessments with actionable gap identification.

## Readiness Dimensions

### Scientific Readiness
- Fundamental knowledge completeness for next stage
- Research institution capacity
- Scientific workforce availability
- Discovery trajectory and acceleration

### Engineering Readiness
- Engineering capability for next-stage systems
- Manufacturing capacity for required infrastructure
- Engineering workforce and expertise
- Technology readiness levels

### Institutional Readiness
- Governance capacity for larger-scale coordination
- Institutional adaptability
- Constitutional compliance frameworks
- Regulatory and standards readiness

### Infrastructure Readiness
- Current infrastructure capacity vs. requirements
- Infrastructure gap analysis
- Infrastructure expansion capability
- Infrastructure resilience adequacy

### Governance Readiness
- Constitutional governance maturity
- Human oversight mechanisms
- Transparency and accountability systems
- Decision-making capacity at scale

### Economic Readiness
- Resource availability for transition investment
- Economic stability and resilience
- Investment capacity for long-term projects
- Economic coordination mechanisms

### Educational Readiness
- Workforce education and training adequacy
- Knowledge transfer capacity
- Educational institution capability
- Continuous learning infrastructure

### Environmental Readiness
- Environmental impact assessment of transition
- Sustainability of transition pathway
- Environmental monitoring capacity
- Remediation capability

### Technological Readiness
- Key technology availability
- Technology integration capability
- Technology risk assessment
- Innovation pipeline health

## Readiness Scoring

Each dimension receives:

- **Current Score**: Current readiness (0.0–1.0)
- **Target Score**: Required readiness for transition
- **Gap**: Target - Current
- **Confidence**: Confidence in score (0.0–1.0)
- **Trend**: Direction and rate of readiness change
- **Key Barriers**: Primary obstacles to readiness improvement

## Overall Readiness

Overall readiness remains probabilistic: a distribution rather than a single number, capturing uncertainty across all dimensions.

## Readiness Structure

Each readiness evaluation contains:

- **Evaluation ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Civilization ID**: Civilization being evaluated
- **Target Stage**: Target Kardashev stage
- **Dimension Scores**: Readiness scores per dimension
- **Overall Readiness Distribution**: Probabilistic overall readiness
- **Critical Gaps**: Largest readiness gaps
- **Recommended Focus Areas**: Priority areas for improvement
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Readiness evaluation must be quantitative and evidence-based
- Readiness records become constitutional artifacts
- Readiness uncertainty is explicitly preserved
