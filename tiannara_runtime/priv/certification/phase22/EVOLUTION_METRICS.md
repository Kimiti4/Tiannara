# Evolution Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the rate, quality, and continuity of civilizational evolution.

## Metrics

- **Evolution Rate**: Epoch transitions per time unit
- **Adaptation Rate**: Adaptive changes per epoch
- **Knowledge Growth**: Knowledge accumulation rate across epochs
- **Scientific Growth**: Discovery rate across epochs
- **Engineering Growth**: Capability expansion rate
- **Institution Stability**: Institution survival rate across epochs
- **Technological Progress**: Technology maturation rate
- **Constitutional Continuity**: Constitutional integrity preservation
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Civilizational Evolution Readiness**: Overall evolution maturity index

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constitutional Continuity | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Knowledge Growth Rate | >0%/epoch | >2%/epoch | >5%/epoch |
| Institution Stability | 0.80 | 0.90 | 0.99+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
