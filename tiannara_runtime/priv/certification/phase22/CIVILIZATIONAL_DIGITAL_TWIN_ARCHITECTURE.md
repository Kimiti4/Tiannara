# Constitutional Civilizational Digital Twin Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Civilizational Digital Twin System (CCDTS) provides Tiannara's capability to construct, maintain, synchronize, and evolve living digital twins of civilizations. Unlike traditional simulations, a Constitutional Civilizational Digital Twin continuously integrates validated observations, scientific discoveries, engineering developments, institutional evolution, and environmental changes into an evidence-driven representation of an entire civilization.

## Constitutional Principle

A Digital Twin is not reality. It is the highest-fidelity constitutional representation of reality supported by current evidence. Every Digital Twin remains evidence-driven, constitutionally governed, mathematically grounded, continuously synchronizable, replayable, and archaeologically reconstructible. Reality always has authority over the twin.

## Constitutional Hierarchy

```
Reality
  └── Observations
       └── Validated Knowledge
            └── Civilizational Ontology
                 └── Civilizational Dynamics
                      └── Digital Twin
                           └── Simulation Context
                                └── Predicted Futures
                                     └── Intervention Evaluation
                                          └── Evidence Feedback
```

Every level remains independently replayable.

## Architecture Components

### Digital Twin Model
- Complete civilization representation
- State, history, knowledge, institution, infrastructure, resource graphs

### Twin Synchronization Engine
- Evidence-driven synchronization only
- No heuristic mutation

### State Synchronization Model
- Multi-dimensional state alignment
- Uncertainty preservation

### Multi-Layer Twin Architecture
- Population, knowledge, science, engineering, infrastructure, economy, governance, environment, technology, institution layers
- Explicit layer interactions

### Reality Alignment Engine
- Agreement, divergence, missing observations, uncertainty, confidence tracking
- No forced convergence

### Twin Consistency Engine
- Logical, mathematical, causal, institution, resource, engineering, cross-layer consistency

### Intervention Simulation Model
- Temporary simulation branches
- Canonical twin never mutated

## Acceptance Criteria

- Digital Twin architecture is constitutionally specified
- Synchronization remains evidence-driven
- Reality retains authority
- Layer interactions remain deterministic
- Intervention simulations never mutate canonical twins
- Replay reconstructs complete twin evolution
- Archaeology reconstructs complete synchronization history
- No runtime implementation exists
