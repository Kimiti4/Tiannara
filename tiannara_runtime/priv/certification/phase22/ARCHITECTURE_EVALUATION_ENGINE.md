# Architecture Evaluation Engine

## Purpose

Evaluate civilization architectures across multiple dimensions, providing comprehensive assessment for human constitutional review.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### Constitutional Compliance
- Human authority preservation
- Constitutional guarantee satisfaction
- Determinism and replayability
- Transparency and auditability

### Scientific Capacity
- Knowledge generation potential
- Research institution health
- Scientific capital efficiency
- Discovery and innovation capacity

### Engineering Capacity
- Infrastructure development capability
- Engineering productivity
- Technology deployment velocity
- Engineering knowledge depth

### Human Well-being
- Projected health outcomes
- Educational attainment potential
- Economic opportunity distribution
- Cultural and scientific participation

### Resilience
- Projected Civilizational Resilience Index
- Recovery capacity
- Adaptive capacity
- Redundancy adequacy

### Sustainability
- Long-term resource renewal
- Environmental health projection
- Inter-generational equity
- Existential risk reduction

### Evolutionary Capacity
- Adaptive evolution potential
- Constitutional continuity
- Institutional evolution capacity
- Long-term capability growth

### Feasibility
- Resource requirement feasibility
- Transition path viability
- Institutional adoption capacity
- Timeline realism

## Evaluation Output

- Dimension-by-dimension scores (0.0–1.0)
- Overall architecture score
- Trade-off documentation
- Risk assessment
- Improvement recommendations
- Comparison with alternative architectures

## Constraints

- Evaluation must be multi-dimensional
- Evaluation records become constitutional artifacts
- No single dimension dominates
