# Collapse and Recovery Model

## Purpose

Model constitutional failure modes — collapse mechanisms, early warning indicators, cascade dynamics, and evidence-based recovery pathways.

## Failure Modes

### Scientific Stagnation
- Declining discovery rate
- Research institution degradation
- Knowledge fragmentation
- Loss of scientific methodology

### Infrastructure Degradation
- Infrastructure maintenance deficit
- Cascade failures from interdependent systems
- Critical infrastructure loss
- Irreversible infrastructure collapse

### Institutional Fragmentation
- Loss of institutional coordination
- Governance authority erosion
- Institutional knowledge loss
- Succession failure

### Energy Scarcity
- Energy return on investment decline
- Energy infrastructure degradation
- Transition failure between energy regimes
- Perpetual energy crisis

### Knowledge Loss
- Educational system collapse
- Archive and library destruction
- Generational knowledge transfer failure
- Methodology and skill extinction

### Environmental Stress
- Critical resource depletion
- Ecosystem service collapse
- Climate regime shift
- Irreversible environmental damage

### Governance Instability
- Constitutional violation cascade
- Decision paralysis
- Legitimacy erosion
- Governance fragmentation

## Collapse Dynamics

- Trigger events and propagation pathways
- Cascade amplification mechanisms
- Tipping points and irreversibility thresholds
- Early warning indicators with deterministic thresholds

## Recovery Mechanisms

- Evidence-based recovery pathways
- Knowledge preservation through collapse
- Infrastructure priority restoration sequences
- Institutional reconstitution mechanisms
- Constitutional continuity provisions

## Constraints

- Collapse never destroys archaeology
- Recovery must be evidence-driven
- Collapse records become constitutional artifacts
- Recovery pathways must be constitutionally governed
