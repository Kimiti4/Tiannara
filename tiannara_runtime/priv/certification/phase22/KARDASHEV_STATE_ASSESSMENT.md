# Kardashev State Assessment

## Purpose

Measure current civilization Kardashev level and evaluate readiness for progression to the next level.

## Assessment Dimensions

### Energy Capture
- Total civilization energy capture (watts)
- Energy capture per capita
- Energy capture per unit area
- Energy growth trajectory

### Energy Source Portfolio
- Source type distribution (renewable, fossil, nuclear, fusion, space-based)
- Source diversity index
- Energy return on investment per source
- Source depletion and renewal rates

### Energy Infrastructure
- Generation capacity and utilization
- Distribution network capacity
- Storage capacity
- Grid resilience and redundancy

### Transition Readiness
- Scientific knowledge for next level
- Engineering capability for next level
- Institutional readiness
- Resource availability
- Environmental constraints

## Kardashev Level Classification

- **Type 0.7**: Current human civilization (~0.73)
- **Type 0.8**: Advanced planetary energy systems
- **Type 0.9**: Near-planetary energy capture
- **Type I**: Complete planetary energy capture (~10^16 W)
- **Type I-II Transition**: Expanding beyond planet
- **Type II**: Complete stellar energy capture (~10^26 W)
- **Type II-III Transition**: Expanding beyond star system
- **Type III**: Complete galactic energy capture (~10^36 W)

## Assessment Output

- Current Kardashev level with confidence bounds
- Dimension-by-dimension readiness scores
- Critical gaps and dependencies
- Recommended next transition focus

## Constraints

- Assessment must be evidence-based
- Assessment records become constitutional artifacts
