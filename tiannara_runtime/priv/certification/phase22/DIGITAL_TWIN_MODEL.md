# Digital Twin Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional representation of a civilizational digital twin — the highest-fidelity evidence-driven representation of a civilization.

## Model Structure

Each Digital Twin contains:
- **Twin ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Parent Civilization**: Reference to ontology civilization entity
- **Current State**: Complete civilizational state at current generation
- **Historical States**: Lineage of all previous states (immutable)
- **Knowledge Graph**: Knowledge entities and relationships
- **Institution Graph**: Institution entities and relationships
- **Infrastructure Graph**: Infrastructure entities and relationships
- **Resource Graph**: Resource entities, flows, and transformations
- **Scientific State**: Scientific capital, discovery pipeline, research programs
- **Engineering State**: Engineering capital, infrastructure projects, capabilities
- **Economic State**: Economic output, capital allocation, resource flows
- **Governance State**: Governance structure, institutional health, policy state
- **Environmental State**: Environmental conditions, resource base, ecological health
- **Synchronization History**: Complete record of all synchronization events
- **Replay Root**: Content-addressed replay identifier
- **Archaeology Root**: Content-addressed archaeology identifier

## Twin Properties

- **Identity**: Unique, content-addressed, lineage-preserving
- **Fidelity**: Measured alignment with observed reality
- **Uncertainty**: Explicit bounds on all state values
- **Freshness**: Time since last synchronization
- **Completeness**: Coverage of civilization dimensions

## Twin Lifecycle

1. **Initialization**: Twin created from ontology and initial observations
2. **Synchronization**: Continuous integration of validated evidence
3. **Simulation**: Twin used as simulation base (in branches)
4. **Evolution**: Twin evolves with civilization
5. **Archive**: Historical twins preserved as constitutional artifacts

## Constraints

- Twins must be constitutionally governed
- Twin records become constitutional artifacts
- Reality always has authority over the twin
