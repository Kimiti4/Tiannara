# Forecast Comparison Model

## Purpose

Enable deterministic comparison of forecasts across multiple civilizations, alternative interventions, technology pathways, institutional architectures, governance strategies, and scientific investment strategies.

## Comparison Dimensions

### Multi-Civilization Comparison
- Forecasts for different civilizations under similar assumptions
- Comparative trajectory analysis
- Relative performance assessment

### Intervention Comparison
- Forecasts with and without specific interventions
- Intervention effect size estimation
- Intervention timing sensitivity analysis

### Pathway Comparison
- Alternative technology pathway forecasts
- Competing institutional architecture forecasts
- Different governance strategy outcomes

### Strategy Comparison
- Scientific investment portfolio comparisons
- Resource allocation strategy forecasts
- Coordination approach comparisons

## Comparison Methodology

1. **Select Forecasts**: Identify forecasts for comparison
2. **Normalize Dimensions**: Align comparable dimensions across forecasts
3. **Calculate Differences**: Quantitative difference metrics across all dimensions
4. **Assess Significance**: Statistical significance of differences given uncertainty
5. **Identify Divergence Points**: Where and when forecasts diverge
6. **Attribute Causes**: Causal analysis of forecast differences
7. **Generate Comparison**: Complete comparison artifact

## Comparison Metrics

- **Trajectory Divergence**: RMS difference between forecast trajectories
- **Outcome Distribution Overlap**: Similarity of probability distributions
- **Decision Point Alignment**: Agreement on critical decision points
- **Uncertainty Overlap**: Shared vs. unique uncertainty regions
- **Sensitivity Concordance**: Agreement on key sensitivity factors

## Comparison Structure

Each comparison contains:

- **Comparison ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Forecast IDs**: Forecasts being compared
- **Comparison Dimensions**: Dimensions used for comparison
- **Similarity Scores**: Quantitative similarity per dimension
- **Divergence Points**: Key points of forecast divergence
- **Significance Assessment**: Statistical significance of differences
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Comparisons must be fully deterministic
- Comparison records become constitutional artifacts
- Comparison methodology is documented with limitations
