# Humanity Architecture Recommendation

## Purpose

Generate the comprehensive architectural vision for humanity's long-term future, synthesizing all civilizational intelligence into a coherent proposal for human constitutional review and decision.

## Recommendation Structure

### Executive Summary
- High-level architectural vision
- Key architectural decisions
- Expected outcomes and benefits
- Risk and uncertainty summary

### Architectural Vision
- Civilization purpose and objectives
- Core architectural principles
- Long-term trajectory and destination
- Generational transition narrative

### Institution Architecture
- Complete institution portfolio recommendation
- Institution purpose, responsibilities, interfaces
- Governance structure and accountability
- Evolution pathways

### Infrastructure Architecture
- Complete infrastructure portfolio recommendation
- Capacity plans and redundancy
- Technology roadmap
- Energy transition pathway

### Scientific Ecosystem
- Research institution architecture
- Knowledge management system
- Scientific collaboration framework
- Discovery and innovation support

### Capability Development
- Target capability portfolio
- Development roadmaps
- Resource requirements
- Timeline and milestones

### Risk and Resilience
- Identified risks and mitigations
- Resilience mechanisms
- Continuity guarantees
- Adaptive capacity provisions

### Constitutional Compliance
- Constitutional alignment certification
- Human authority preservation
- Transparency and auditability
- Replay and archaeology guarantees

### Multi-Century Roadmap
- Generational phases
- Transition conditions
- Uncertainty bounds
- Adaptive revision mechanisms

## Presentation

The recommendation is presented for human constitutional review. It is an advisory proposal. Humans retain full constitutional authority to accept, modify, or reject any aspect of the recommendation.

## Constraints

- Recommendation is advisory only
- Human authority is explicitly preserved
- Recommendation records become constitutional artifacts
- Multiple alternatives may be presented
