# Growth Engine

## Purpose

Model deterministic civilization growth emerging from the interaction of knowledge, energy, engineering, institutions, education, and technology.

## Growth Drivers

### Knowledge-Driven Growth
- Knowledge enables more efficient resource utilization
- New knowledge opens new capability domains
- Knowledge compounding produces accelerating returns
- Growth depends on knowledge integration, not just accumulation

### Energy-Driven Growth
- Energy availability constrains physical growth
- Energy efficiency improvements enable growth without proportional energy increase
- Energy transitions unlock new growth regimes

### Engineering-Driven Growth
- Engineering transforms knowledge into capability
- Infrastructure enables scaling of all productive activities
- Engineering productivity determines conversion efficiency

### Institution-Driven Growth
- Effective institutions reduce coordination costs
- Institutional quality determines knowledge and resource flow efficiency
- Adaptive institutions enable sustained growth

### Education-Driven Growth
- Education determines knowledge distribution across population
- Skill development enables technology utilization
- Research training produces knowledge creators

### Technology-Driven Growth
- Technology amplifies human and institutional capability
- Technology clusters produce synergistic growth
- Technology adoption follows deterministic diffusion

## Growth Model Properties

- Growth emerges from driver interactions, not hard-coded assumptions
- No exponential growth assumption — growth rates emerge from state
- Growth can be logistic, linear, or declining depending on constraints
- Multiple growth regimes possible simultaneously in different domains

## Constraints

- Growth calculations must be fully deterministic
- Driver interaction parameters must be ontology-derived
- Growth records become constitutional artifacts
