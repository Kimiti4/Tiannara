# Digital Twin Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the fidelity, freshness, consistency, and utility of civilizational digital twins.

## Metrics

- **Reality Alignment Score**: Aggregate twin fidelity (0.0–1.0)
- **Synchronization Latency**: Average time between observation and twin update
- **Twin Fidelity**: Composite accuracy across all dimensions
- **Cross-Layer Consistency**: Fraction of consistency checks passed
- **Observation Coverage**: Fraction of dimensions with recent observations
- **Scientific Coverage**: Fraction of scientific domains represented
- **Engineering Coverage**: Fraction of engineering domains represented
- **Institution Coverage**: Fraction of institutions represented
- **Intervention Simulation Accuracy**: Predicted vs. actual outcomes
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Twin Readiness**: Overall twin maturity index

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reality Alignment Score | 0.80 | 0.95 | 0.99+ |
| Synchronization Latency | <10 gen | <3 gen | <1 gen |
| Cross-Layer Consistency | 0.90 | 0.99 | 1.00 |
| Observation Coverage | 0.70 | 0.90 | 0.99+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
