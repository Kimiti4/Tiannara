# Civilization Scoring Engine

## Purpose

Score civilization designs using constitutional metrics, producing interpretable scores that enable comparison and optimization.

## Scoring Metrics

### Scientific Capacity
- Knowledge generation potential
- Discovery infrastructure quality
- Research institution health
- Scientific capital efficiency

### Engineering Capacity
- Infrastructure development capability
- Engineering productivity
- Technology deployment velocity
- Engineering knowledge depth

### Knowledge Production
- Knowledge accumulation rate
- Knowledge diversity index
- Knowledge integration rate
- Knowledge preservation integrity

### Innovation Rate
- Innovation generation frequency
- Cross-domain innovation transfer
- Breakthrough innovation potential
- Innovation adoption velocity

### Institutional Robustness
- Institution stability index
- Institutional adaptive capacity
- Institutional diversity
- Institutional knowledge retention

### Infrastructure Quality
- Infrastructure capacity adequacy
- Infrastructure reliability
- Infrastructure efficiency
- Infrastructure lifecycle health

### Energy Security
- Energy source diversity
- Energy resilience index
- Transition readiness
- Energy efficiency

### Environmental Health
- Environmental state score
- Resource renewal rate
- Ecological diversity index
- Sustainability trajectory

### Civilizational Resilience
- Composite Civilizational Resilience Index
- Recovery capacity score
- Adaptive capacity score
- Redundancy adequacy

### Human Flourishing
- Health outcome index
- Educational attainment score
- Economic opportunity index
- Cultural participation rate

## Scoring Properties

- All scores are on 0.0–1.0 scale
- Scores are interpretable and decomposable
- Score components are traceable to civilization state
- Score confidence bounds are explicit

## Constraints

- Scoring must be fully deterministic
- Score records become constitutional artifacts
- Scores are comparable across designs
