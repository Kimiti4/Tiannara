# Energy Transition Model

## Purpose

Model civilization-scale energy transitions across successive energy paradigms, from fossil fuels through renewables, advanced nuclear, fusion, space-based energy, and ultimately Dyson-scale concepts.

## Energy Stages

| Stage | Primary Sources | Typical Threshold | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-Industrial | Biomass, animal, wind, water | <10^12 W | Low energy civilization |
| Industrial | Coal, oil, gas | ~10^13 W | Fossil fuel-based |
| Renewable | Solar, wind, hydro, geothermal | ~10^14 W | Sustainable generation mix |
| Advanced Nuclear | Fission (Gen IV+), thorium | ~10^15 W | Safe, abundant nuclear |
| Fusion | Controlled fusion | ~10^15–10^16 W | Near-limitless energy |
| Space-Based | Orbital solar, lunar power | ~10^16–10^17 W | Beyond-planet generation |
| Planetary Grid | Fully integrated planetary energy | ~10^16 W | Stage I complete |
| Stellar Infrastructure | Dyson structures, stellar harvest | ~10^26 W | Stage II capability |
| Galactic Network | Multi-stellar energy infrastructure | ~10^36 W | Stage III capability |

## Transition Constraints

All energy transitions are constrained by:

- **Engineering Feasibility**: Demonstrated or theoretically achievable engineering
- **Scientific Evidence**: Firm scientific foundation for proposed technologies
- **Environmental Sustainability**: No irreversible environmental damage
- **Constitutional Governance**: Transitions are constitutionally governed
- **Economic Viability**: Resource requirements are within civilization capacity
- **Risk Tolerance**: Transition risks are understood and acceptable

## Transition Assessment

1. **Measure Current Portfolio**: Energy source distribution and capacity
2. **Evaluate Transition Readiness**: Scientific, engineering, institutional readiness
3. **Assess Constraints**: Feasibility, sustainability, governance, risk
4. **Design Transition Path**: Optimal sequence of energy source transitions
5. **Estimate Timelines**: Horizon with uncertainty per transition phase
6. **Generate Plan**: Complete energy transition artifact

## Energy Transition Structure

Each transition plan contains:

- **Plan ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Current Primary Sources**: Current energy source distribution
- **Target Source Architecture**: Target energy configuration
- **Transition Phases**: Ordered energy transition phases
- **Infrastructure Requirements**: New infrastructure needed
- **Scientific Requirements**: Research needed
- **Engineering Requirements**: Engineering needed
- **Environmental Impact Assessment**: Sustainability evaluation
- **Risk Assessment**: Transition risks and mitigations
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Energy transitions must be evidence-driven
- Transition records become constitutional artifacts
- Sustainability constraints are non-negotiable
