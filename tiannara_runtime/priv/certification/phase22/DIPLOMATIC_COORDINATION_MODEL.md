# Diplomatic Coordination Model

## Purpose

Represent and manage diplomatic relationships between civilizations, including agreements, negotiations, shared policies, conflict resolution, mutual assistance, scientific treaties, and engineering standards.

## Diplomatic Instruments

### Agreements
- Bilateral and multilateral agreements
- Treaty frameworks and protocols
- Agreement lifecycle: negotiation, signing, ratification, implementation
- Amendment and termination provisions

### Negotiations
- Structured negotiation protocols
- Position and interest representation
- Mediation and facilitation mechanisms
- Agreement documentation

### Shared Policies
- Common policy frameworks
- Harmonized standards and regulations
- Coordinated policy positions
- Policy implementation monitoring

### Conflict Resolution
- Dispute identification and escalation
- Mediation and arbitration mechanisms
- Conflict resolution procedures
- Post-resolution relationship rebuilding

### Mutual Assistance
- Defense and security cooperation
- Emergency and disaster assistance
- Resource sharing during crises
- Technical assistance programs

### Scientific Treaties
- Research cooperation frameworks
- Data sharing agreements
- Scientific freedom protections
- Research ethics harmonization

### Engineering Standards
- Common engineering standards
- Interoperability protocols
- Safety and quality standards
- Certification and accreditation

## Diplomatic History

Diplomatic history remains fully archaeologically recoverable:

- Complete agreement lifecycle records
- Negotiation position evolution
- Relationship trajectory documentation
- Conflict resolution outcomes
- Standard evolution and harmonization

## Diplomatic Structure

Each diplomatic artifact contains:

- **Diplomatic ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Instrument Type**: Type of diplomatic instrument
- **Participating Civilizations**: Involved parties
- **Terms**: Agreement terms and conditions
- **Status**: Current diplomatic status
- **History**: Complete diplomatic interaction history
- **Compliance**: Compliance tracking and verification
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Diplomatic history is fully reconstructible
- Diplomatic records become constitutional artifacts
- Civilizational sovereignty is preserved in all diplomatic interactions
