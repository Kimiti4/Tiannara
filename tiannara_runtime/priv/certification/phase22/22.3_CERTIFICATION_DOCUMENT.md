## Certification Statement
Phase 22.3 (CCES) establishes the Constitutional Civilizational Engineering System, enabling Tiannara to architect civilizations as evidence-driven engineering systems rather than collections of independent institutions. Building upon the Civilizational Ontology (22.0), Civilizational Dynamics (22.1), and Civilizational Digital Twin (22.2), this phase defines how civilizations are intentionally designed, evaluated, and evolved while preserving constitutional governance, deterministic replay, archaeological lineage, modularity, and long-term adaptability.
