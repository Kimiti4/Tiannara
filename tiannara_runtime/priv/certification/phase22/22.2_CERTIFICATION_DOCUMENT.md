## Certification Statement
Phase 22.2 (CCDTS) establishes the Constitutional Civilizational Digital Twin System, providing Tiannara with the architectural foundation for constructing and maintaining living constitutional representations of civilizations. Built upon the Civilizational Ontology (22.0) and Civilizational Dynamics (22.1), the Digital Twin enables deterministic synchronization with validated evidence, high-fidelity simulation, intervention analysis, replay, and archaeological reconstruction while preserving constitutional governance, uncertainty, provenance, and reproducibility.
