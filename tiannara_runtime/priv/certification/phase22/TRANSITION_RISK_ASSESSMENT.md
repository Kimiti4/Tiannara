# Transition Risk Assessment

## Purpose

Identify, classify, and mitigate risks associated with Kardashev transitions across technical, economic, social, and environmental dimensions.

## Risk Categories

### Technical Risks
- Scientific knowledge gaps blocking progress
- Engineering capability limitations
- Infrastructure failure during transition
- Technology immaturity

### Economic Risks
- Resource requirements exceeding capacity
- Transition cost overruns
- Economic disruption during transition
- Investment diversion from other priorities

### Social Risks
- Social disruption from rapid change
- Inequality from energy access disparities
- Cultural resistance to transition
- Governance challenges at new scale

### Environmental Risks
- Environmental damage from infrastructure
- Ecological disruption from energy capture
- Climate impacts of large-scale construction
- Irreversible environmental change

### Existential Risks
- Catastrophic failure during critical transition
- Civilization collapse triggered by transition
- Irreversible loss of capability
- Permanent setback to lower Kardashev level

## Risk Analysis

- Probability estimation per risk
- Impact assessment across dimensions
- Risk interaction and cascade analysis
- Mitigation strategy development
- Residual risk documentation

## Constraints

- Risk assessment must be evidence-based
- Risk records become constitutional artifacts
- Existential risks are highest priority
