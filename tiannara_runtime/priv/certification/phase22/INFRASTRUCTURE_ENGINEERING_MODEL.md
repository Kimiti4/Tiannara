# Infrastructure Engineering Model

## Purpose

Engineer complete infrastructure portfolios — energy systems, transportation, communication, healthcare, manufacturing, computation, research infrastructure, agriculture, and space infrastructure — with explicit dependencies and redundancy.

## Infrastructure Systems

### Energy Systems
- Generation portfolio (diverse sources)
- Transmission and distribution grid
- Storage infrastructure
- Backup and emergency systems

### Transportation
- Ground, maritime, aviation, space transport
- Logistics and supply chain networks
- Transportation hubs and terminals
- Autonomous transport infrastructure

### Communication
- Data network infrastructure
- Satellite and space communication
- Emergency communication systems
- Quantum communication networks

### Healthcare
- Medical facilities and hospitals
- Medical supply chains
- Public health monitoring
- Biomedical research infrastructure

### Manufacturing
- Production facilities
- Additive and advanced manufacturing
- Supply chain infrastructure
- Quality assurance systems

### Computation
- Data centers and HPC clusters
- Quantum computing facilities
- Distributed computing networks
- Data storage and archival systems

### Research Infrastructure
- Observatories and telescopes
- Laboratory facilities
- Experimental facilities
- Simulation infrastructure

### Agriculture
- Food production systems
- Controlled environment agriculture
- Food processing and distribution
- Agricultural research facilities

### Space Infrastructure
- Launch facilities and spaceports
- Orbital infrastructure
- Planetary surface facilities
- Deep space infrastructure

## Engineering Principles

- Infrastructure dependencies must be explicit
- Redundancy for critical systems
- Graceful degradation under failure
- Lifecycle cost optimization
- Capacity planning for future growth

## Constraints

- Infrastructure relationships must be explicit
- Infrastructure records become constitutional artifacts
- Critical systems must have redundancy
