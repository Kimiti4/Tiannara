# Decision Point Model

## Purpose

Identify, characterize, and model critical constitutional branching events where civilization decisions fundamentally alter future trajectories.

## Decision Point Types

- Biology and medicine revolutions
- Materials science breakthroughs
- Computational capability leaps

### Engineering Discoveries
- Energy system transformations
- Manufacturing capability advances
- Infrastructure paradigm shifts
- Space technology breakthroughs

### Governance Reforms
- Constitutional restructuring events
- Institutional redesign opportunities
- Coordination framework changes
- Policy regime transitions

### Energy Transitions
- Fossil to renewable transition completion
- Advanced nuclear deployment

- Space-based energy infrastructure

### Economic Restructuring
- Resource allocation paradigm shifts
- Economic system transformations
- Global economic coordination changes

### Environmental Events
- Climate tipping points reached
- Ecological regime shifts
- Resource depletion milestones
- Environmental recovery inflection points

### Technological Revolutions
- Artificial intelligence capability thresholds
- Automation and robotics transformation
- Biotechnology and genetic engineering
- Nanotechnology and molecular manufacturing

## Decision Point Structure

Each decision point contains:

- **Decision Point ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Type**: Classification of decision point type
- **Description**: Nature of the decision and its stakes
- **Probability**: Estimated probability of occurrence within horizon
- **Impact**: Magnitude of trajectory alteration (0.0–1.0)
- **Reversibility**: Degree to which decision can be reversed (0.0–1.0)
- **Sensitivity**: Sensitivity to timing and implementation
- **Dependencies**: Prerequisite events or conditions
- **Time Window**: Period during which decision is relevant
- **Decision Options**: Alternative courses of action
- **Forecast Branching**: Forecast trajectories under each option
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Decision Point Identification

1. **Horizon Scanning**: Systematic scan for potential decision points
2. **Pattern Recognition**: Identification from historical analogues
3. **Model Detection**: Discovery through simulation and forecasting
4. **Expert Integration**: Formalized knowledge integration
5. **Ensemble Analysis**: Detection across forecast ensemble

## Decision Point Network

- Decision points form a directed graph
- Predecessor and successor relationships
- Critical path identification through decision network
- Decision point clusters and interdependencies

## Constraints

- Decision points must preserve causal lineage
- Decision point records become constitutional artifacts
- Human authority over critical decisions is preserved
