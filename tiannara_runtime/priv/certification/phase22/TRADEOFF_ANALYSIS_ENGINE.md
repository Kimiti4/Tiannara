# Tradeoff Analysis Engine

## Purpose

Explicitly evaluate conflicts between competing optimization objectives, ensuring tradeoffs remain visible and constitutionally informed.

## Key Tradeoffs

### Innovation vs Stability
- High innovation requires experimentation, which introduces instability
- High stability may suppress beneficial innovation
- Optimal balance depends on civilization context and risk tolerance

### Efficiency vs Redundancy
- Maximum efficiency minimizes buffers and redundancy
- Redundancy consumes resources but provides resilience
- Trade-off varies with disruption probability and impact

### Automation vs Human Expertise
- Automation increases throughput and consistency
- Human expertise provides judgment and adaptability
- Balance shifts with technology maturity

### Growth vs Sustainability
- Rapid growth may consume resources faster than renewal
- Sustainability constraints may limit growth rate
- Long-time horizon optimization favors sustainability

### Exploration vs Risk
- Scientific and engineering exploration carries inherent risk
- Risk avoidance limits discovery and capability growth
- Constitutional bounds define acceptable risk levels

### Security vs Openness
- Open systems enable faster knowledge flow
- Security measures reduce collaboration efficiency
- Balance depends on threat environment and constitutional values

### Specialization vs Integration
- Specialization enables depth but creates fragmentation risk
- Integration enables synthesis but requires coordination overhead
- Optimal balance shifts with knowledge accumulation

### Centralization vs Distribution
- Centralization enables coordination but creates single points of failure
- Distribution provides resilience but increases coordination costs
- Constitutional governance provides bounds

## Tradeoff Documentation

Each tradeoff analysis documents:
- Competing objectives
- Trade-off curve (achievable combinations)
- Marginal improvement cost
- Knee points (maximum marginal return regions)
- Preference boundaries
- Constitutional constraint implications

## Constraints

- Tradeoffs must remain explicit and visible
- Tradeoff records become constitutional artifacts
