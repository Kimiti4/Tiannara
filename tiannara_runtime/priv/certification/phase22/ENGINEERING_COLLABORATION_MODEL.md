# Engineering Collaboration Model

## Purpose

Coordinate inter-civilization engineering collaboration for large-scale projects, infrastructure programs, manufacturing networks, space systems, energy projects, and automation programs.

## Collaboration Forms

### Large-Scale Engineering
- Multi-civilization engineering projects
- Distributed design and construction
- Shared engineering standards
- Cross-site quality assurance

### Infrastructure Programs
- Trans-civilization infrastructure networks
- Joint infrastructure investment
- Shared infrastructure operation
- Interoperability standards

### Manufacturing Networks
- Distributed manufacturing coordination
- Specialized manufacturing allocation
- Supply chain integration
- Quality standardization

### Space Systems
- Joint launch and space operations
- Shared space infrastructure
- Collaborative exploration missions
- Space resource utilization

### Energy Projects
- Trans-civilization energy infrastructure
- Joint energy research and development
- Shared energy generation facilities
- Grid interconnection projects

### Automation Programs
- Collaborative automation development
- Shared robotics infrastructure
- Joint AI and autonomy programs
- Automation standard harmonization

## Engineering Decision Framework

Engineering decisions remain replayable and evidence-driven:

- Design decisions are documented with provenance
- Alternatives are evaluated and compared
- Trade-offs are explicitly quantified
- Engineering choices are constitutionally governed

## Collaboration Structure

Each engineering collaboration contains:

- **Collaboration ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Project Type**: Type of engineering project
- **Participating Civilizations**: Involved civilizations
- **Project Scope**: Engineering objectives and deliverables
- **Standards**: Engineering standards and protocols
- **Shared Resources**: Shared engineering assets
- **Division of Labor**: Task allocation across civilizations
- **Milestones**: Project milestones and verification
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Engineering decisions must be replayable
- Collaboration records become constitutional artifacts
- Engineering collaborations preserve civilizational autonomy
