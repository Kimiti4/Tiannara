# Resource System Ontology

## Purpose

Formally represent all resource systems within civilizations, including resource types, flows, stocks, and transformation processes, with resource flows as first-class ontology objects.

## Resource Categories

### Energy Resources
- Fossil fuels (coal, oil, natural gas)
- Nuclear fuels (fission, fusion)
- Renewable (solar, wind, hydro, geothermal)
- Space-based (solar collection, orbital power)
- Energy carriers (electricity, hydrogen, batteries)

### Material Resources
- Metals and minerals
- Rare earth elements
- Construction materials
- Advanced materials and composites
- Consumable supplies

### Food Resources
- Agricultural products
- Cultured and synthetic food
- Nutritional supplements
- Food preservation and storage

### Water Resources
- Freshwater sources
- Treated and recycled water
- Extraterrestrial water sources
- Water storage and distribution

### Land Resources
- Surface area for facilities and infrastructure
- Subsurface space for underground facilities
- Orbital and planetary surface rights
- Environmental preservation areas

### Computational Resources
- Processing capacity (CPU/GPU/quantum)
- Data storage capacity
- Network bandwidth
- Simulation and modeling cycles

### Human Expertise
- Skilled personnel by domain
- Educational capacity
- Training throughput
- Knowledge specialization

### Financial Capital
- Economic value representation
- Investment capital
- Operating budgets
- Reserve funds

### Scientific Capital
- Research equipment and instruments
- Experimental facilities
- Research data archives
- Scientific knowledge base

### Engineering Capital
- Manufacturing equipment
- Construction machinery
- Engineering tools and software
- Testing and validation facilities

## Resource Flow Model

Each resource flow contains:
- **Flow ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Source**: Origin entity or stock
- **Destination**: Target entity or stock
- **Resource Type**: Category of resource
- **Quantity**: Amount transferred
- **Rate**: Flow rate over time
- **Efficiency**: Conversion efficiency
- **Provenance**: Complete flow history

## Constraints

- Resource flows are first-class ontology objects
- Resource records become constitutional artifacts
- Resource transformations must preserve mass-energy accounting
