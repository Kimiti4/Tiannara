# Constitutional Kardashev Transition Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Kardashev Transition System (CKTS) establishes Tiannara's constitutional architecture for planning, evaluating, engineering, and guiding civilization-scale progression across successive levels of technological and civilizational capability.

Unlike Phase 22.7, which forecasts possible futures, this phase defines how a civilization intentionally progresses toward increasingly advanced stages while preserving constitutional principles, sustainability, resilience, scientific integrity, and human oversight.

This architecture prepares Tiannara for the transition into Planetary Intelligence (Phase 23).

## Constitutional Principle

Civilizational advancement is not measured solely by energy consumption.

True advancement is measured by the civilization's increasing ability to:

- discover scientific knowledge
- engineer increasingly complex systems
- preserve constitutional governance
- improve resilience
- expand sustainably
- coordinate globally
- responsibly utilize energy
- increase collective intelligence

Kardashev progression shall therefore be constitutional rather than purely energetic.

## Architecture Components

### Civilizational Progression Model
- Multi-dimensional progression tracking
- Stage transition criteria and verification
- Progression history and lineage

### Transition Pathway Engine
- Deterministic roadmap generation
- Dependency and critical path analysis
- Resource requirement estimation
- Milestone sequencing

### Capability Maturity Model
- Multi-domain capability assessment
- Current vs. target level tracking
- Capability gap analysis
- Required research and engineering mapping

### Energy Transition Model
- Energy source evolution planning
- Transition constraints and feasibility
- Energy infrastructure roadmap

### Planetary Coordination Model
- Global research, infrastructure, logistics coordination
- Decentralized coordination where appropriate
- Knowledge distribution architecture

### Global Infrastructure Model
- Energy grid, transportation, communication, water, food, healthcare
- Scientific and computational infrastructure
- Space infrastructure
- Interdependency mapping

### Readiness Evaluation Engine
- Multi-dimensional readiness scoring
- Probabilistic readiness assessment
- Readiness gap identification

## Acceptance Criteria

- Constitutional progression stages are formally specified
- Capability maturity remains deterministic
- Energy transitions remain evidence-driven
- Readiness evaluation is quantitative
- Transition planning preserves uncertainty
- Replay reconstructs every transition
- Archaeology reconstructs complete transition history
- Human constitutional governance remains authoritative
- No runtime implementation exists
