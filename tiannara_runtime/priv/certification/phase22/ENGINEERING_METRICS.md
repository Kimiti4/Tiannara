# Engineering Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the completeness, quality, and effectiveness of civilization engineering designs.

## Metrics

- **Architecture Completeness**: Coverage of civilization design dimensions
- **Institution Coverage**: Fraction of required institution types designed
- **Capability Coverage**: Fraction of required capabilities specified
- **Infrastructure Connectivity**: Infrastructure dependency mapping completeness
- **Interoperability Score**: Interface specification completeness
- **Scientific Capacity**: Total scientific capital in design
- **Engineering Capacity**: Total engineering capital in design
- **Resilience Score**: Projected Civilizational Resilience Index
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Civilizational Engineering Readiness**: Overall engineering maturity index

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Architecture Completeness | 0.70 | 0.90 | 0.99+ |
| Institution Coverage | 0.80 | 0.95 | 1.00 |
| Capability Coverage | 0.70 | 0.90 | 0.99+ |
| Interoperability Score | 0.60 | 0.85 | 0.95+ |
| Resilience Score | 0.50 | 0.75 | 0.90+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
