# Adaptive Evolution Model

## Purpose

Model how civilizations adapt through multiple mechanisms, ensuring every adaptation has constitutional justification.

## Adaptation Mechanisms

### Scientific Discovery
- New knowledge drives capability expansion
- Paradigm shifts reorganize knowledge frameworks
- Discovery opens new engineering possibilities
- Scientific adaptation preserves all prior knowledge

### Engineering Innovation
- New engineering capabilities enable infrastructure evolution
- Innovation diffuses through engineering institutions
- Engineering adaptation builds on scientific foundation
- Failed innovations documented in archaeology

### Knowledge Expansion
- Knowledge grows through discovery and synthesis
- Knowledge integration creates new understanding
- Knowledge specialization enables depth
- Knowledge generalization enables breadth

### Institutional Reform
- Institutions adapt to changing conditions
- Reform driven by performance evaluation and evidence
- Institutional memory preserved through transitions
- Failed reforms documented and learned from

### Infrastructure Growth
- Infrastructure expands to meet growing demands
- Infrastructure upgrades improve efficiency
- Infrastructure replacement addresses obsolescence
- Infrastructure decommissioning preserves knowledge

### Educational Development
- Education adapts to evolving knowledge landscape
- Curriculum evolution incorporates new discoveries
- Research training adapts to new methodologies
- Educational continuity preserved through change

### Resource Transitions
- Resource base evolves with discovery and depletion
- New resource types enable new capabilities
- Resource efficiency improves through technology
- Resource sustainability guides transition pace

### Environmental Adaptation
- Civilization adapts to environmental changes
- Environmental management improves with knowledge
- Climate adaptation plans evolve with evidence
- Ecological preservation guides adaptation

## Adaptation Principles

- Every adaptation requires constitutional justification
- Adaptation rate is evidence-driven, not forced
- Failed adaptations are preserved in archaeology
- Adaptation records become constitutional artifacts
