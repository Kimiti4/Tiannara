# Constitutional Constraint Engine

## Purpose

Enforce non-negotiable constitutional boundaries that optimization may never violate, terminating optimization paths that cross them.

## Constraint Categories

### Constitution
- All constitutional guarantees are hard constraints
- Human authority is non-negotiable
- Constitutional amendment is separate from optimization

### Mathematical Substrate
- Mathematical consistency must be preserved
- Logical contradictions are not permitted
- Determinism must be maintained

### Logic
- Causal consistency required
- No backward causality in state transitions
- Identity preservation across time

### Information Integrity
- Information must not be destroyed
- Provenance must be preserved
- Knowledge artifacts are immutable after creation

### Scientific Integrity
- Evidence must support all claims
- Unvalidated claims cannot drive optimization
- Scientific method must be respected

### Replay
- All optimization events must be replayable
- Hash chain integrity must be preserved
- Replay verification must succeed

### Archaeology
- All optimization history must be recoverable
- No optimization may erase archaeological evidence
- Decision lineage must be preserved

### Human Governance
- Humans retain final constitutional authority
- Optimization recommends — humans decide
- Human override mechanisms cannot be optimized away

## Constraint Enforcement

- Constraints are checked before every optimization step
- Violations immediately terminate the optimization path
- Violations are documented as constitutional artifacts
- Constraint violations trigger human review

## Constraints

- Constraints are non-negotiable and non-optimizable
- Constraint records become constitutional artifacts
- Constraint violations are preserved in archaeology
