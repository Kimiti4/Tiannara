# Continuity Engine

## Purpose

Guarantee continuity of critical civilizational functions through disruptions, ensuring that scientific progress, engineering capability, education, governance, knowledge preservation, and constitutional integrity are maintained.

## Continuity Domains

### Scientific Continuity
- Research program continuity through disruption
- Experimental data preservation
- Scientific personnel and knowledge protection
- Research infrastructure resilience

### Engineering Continuity
- Critical infrastructure operation maintenance
- Engineering knowledge preservation
- Maintenance capability continuity
- Engineering project continuity

### Education Continuity
- Educational system function preservation
- Knowledge transmission continuity
- Research training continuation
- Educational material preservation

### Governance Continuity
- Constitutional governance function preservation
- Decision-making authority continuity
- Administrative function maintenance
- Constitutional compliance during disruption

### Knowledge Preservation Continuity
- Knowledge archive accessibility
- Knowledge integrity verification
- Knowledge backup activation
- Knowledge recovery capability

### Infrastructure Continuity
- Critical infrastructure operation
- Infrastructure monitoring during disruption
- Rapid restoration capability
- Infrastructure knowledge preservation

### Constitutional Continuity
- Constitutional guarantee preservation
- Human authority maintenance
- Replay and archaeology continuity
- Constitutional amendment process continuity

### Replay Continuity
- Replay system operation during disruption
- Event recording during disruption
- Post-disruption replay verification
- Gap identification and documentation

### Archaeology Continuity
- Archaeological recording during disruption
- Disruption event preservation
- Recovery action documentation
- Post-disruption archaeology update

## Continuity Measurement

Each domain has measured continuity metrics: function preservation level, recovery time, capability retention, knowledge retention.

## Constraints

- Continuity must be measurable
- Continuity records become constitutional artifacts
