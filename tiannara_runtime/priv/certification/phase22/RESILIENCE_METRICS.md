# Resilience Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the robustness, recovery capacity, and adaptive capability of civilizational resilience.

## Metrics

- **Systemic Risk Coverage**: Fraction of risk domains monitored
- **Failure Detection Latency**: Time between failure onset and detection
- **Recovery Time**: Time from detection to full function restoration
- **Scientific Continuity**: Fraction of scientific programs maintained through disruption
- **Engineering Continuity**: Fraction of engineering capability maintained
- **Infrastructure Stability**: Infrastructure function preservation level
- **Institutional Stability**: Institution function preservation level
- **Redundancy Coverage**: Fraction of critical functions with redundancy
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Civilizational Resilience Index**: Composite resilience score (0.0–1.0)

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civilizational Resilience Index | 0.50 | 0.75 | 0.95+ |
| Failure Detection Latency | <5 gen | <2 gen | <1 gen |
| Recovery Time | <20 gen | <10 gen | <5 gen |
| Redundancy Coverage | 0.60 | 0.85 | 0.99+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
