# Long-Horizon Evolution Model

## Purpose

Support deterministic modeling of civilizational evolution across multiple time horizons, with uncertainty explicitly increasing with horizon distance.

## Time Horizons

- **Near-term** (10 years): High resolution, low uncertainty
- **Medium-term** (25 years): Moderate resolution, bounded uncertainty
- **Long-term** (50 years): Directional resolution, significant uncertainty
- **Multi-generational** (100 years): Broad trajectory, high uncertainty
- **Century-scale** (250 years): Visionary, very high uncertainty
- **Millennial** (500 years): Fundamental direction, extreme uncertainty
- **Civilizational** (1000+ years): Existential trajectory, fundamental uncertainty

## Horizon-Specific Properties

### Near-term (10yr)
- Detailed institutional evolution projections
- Infrastructure project pipeline
- Scientific program outcomes
- Demographic trends

### Medium-term (25yr)
- Institutional reform trajectories
- Infrastructure development programs
- Technology maturation roadmaps
- Educational system evolution

### Long-term (50yr)
- Scientific paradigm evolution
- Major infrastructure transformations
- Energy transition progress
- Institutional adaptation patterns

### Multi-generational (100yr)
- Knowledge accumulation trajectories
- Technological epoch transitions
- Environmental change impacts
- Civilizational capability evolution

### Century-scale (250yr)
- Kardashev level progression
- Fundamental scientific revolutions
- Institutional architecture evolution
- Civilizational expansion patterns

### Millennial (500yr)
- Civilizational lifespan modeling
- Existential risk assessment
- Long-term knowledge preservation
- Civilizational identity evolution

### Civilizational (1000+yr)
- Transcendence trajectory
- Multi-planet civilization potential
- Civilizational purpose evolution
- Ultimate capability boundaries

## Uncertainty Management

- Uncertainty bounds increase with each horizon level
- Projections become directional rather than precise
- Confidence intervals are explicit
- Multiple evolutionary pathways maintained

## Constraints

- Long-term evolution must be deterministic
- Uncertainty must be explicit
- Evolution records become constitutional artifacts
