# Type III Transition Planning

## Purpose

Plan the transition from Type II to Type III — galactic-scale energy capture with multi-star system civilization infrastructure.

## Type III Characteristics

- Total energy capture: ~10^36 W
- Multi-star system energy infrastructure
- Galactic civilization coordination
- Advanced energy technologies beyond current physics
- Potential Dyson sphere and Matrioshka brain scale structures

## Transition Requirements

### Scientific Prerequisites
- Fundamental physics breakthroughs (beyond Standard Model)
- Advanced energy conversion (matter-antimatter, vacuum energy)
- Faster-than-information communication or equivalent
- Exotic matter and energy manipulation

### Engineering Prerequisites
- Self-replicating interstellar construction
- Stellar engineering capabilities
- Interstellar infrastructure deployment
- Autonomous civilization-scale systems

### Infrastructure Prerequisites
- Interstellar transport infrastructure
- Multi-star communication network
- Distributed energy collection systems
- Galactic-scale computation infrastructure

### Institutional Prerequisites
- Galactic governance framework
- Interstellar constitutional extension
- Multi-civilization coordination
- Long-term institutional continuity

## Transition Phases

1. **Interstellar Access**: Reliable interstellar transport
2. **Star System Development**: Multi-star presence established
3. **Stellar Engineering**: Beginning of stellar energy capture at other stars
4. **Galactic Integration**: Coordinated galactic energy infrastructure
5. **Completion**: Full galactic energy capture achieved

## Milestones

Each phase has verifiable milestones acknowledging the extreme uncertainty at this scale.

## Constraints

- Transition must be constitutionally governed
- Type III is a visionary framework, not a precise plan
- Transition records become constitutional artifacts
