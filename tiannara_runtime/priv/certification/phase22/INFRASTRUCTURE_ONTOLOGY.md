# Infrastructure Ontology

## Purpose

Formally represent all infrastructure systems within civilizations, including their capabilities, interdependencies, and lifecycle state.

## Infrastructure Domains

### Energy Infrastructure
- Power generation (fossil, nuclear, renewable, fusion, space-based)
- Grid transmission and distribution
- Energy storage systems
- Backup and emergency systems

### Transportation Infrastructure
- Ground transportation (roads, rail, tunnels)
- Maritime and waterway systems
- Aviation and aerospace
- Space launch and transit infrastructure
- Logistics and supply chain networks

### Water Infrastructure
- Freshwater supply and treatment
- Wastewater management
- Irrigation systems
- Desalination and water recycling

### Communication Infrastructure
- Data networks and internet
- Satellite communication systems
- Deep space communication
- Emergency communication systems

### Healthcare Infrastructure
- Hospitals and medical facilities
- Medical supply chains
- Public health monitoring
- Biomedical research facilities

### Scientific Infrastructure
- Observatories and telescopes
- Laboratory facilities
- Particle accelerators and research instruments
- Simulation and computing facilities

### Manufacturing Infrastructure
- Industrial production facilities
- Additive manufacturing systems
- Precision fabrication
- Supply chain and logistics

### Agricultural Infrastructure
- Food production systems
- Controlled environment agriculture
- Food processing and distribution
- Agricultural research

### Computing Infrastructure
- Data centers and HPC clusters
- Cloud and distributed computing
- Quantum computing facilities
- Data storage and archival systems

### Space Infrastructure
- Launch facilities and spaceports
- Orbital infrastructure
- Planetary surface facilities
- Deep space infrastructure

## Infrastructure Relationships

- **Dependency**: One infrastructure depends on another
- **Redundancy**: Systems providing backup capability
- **Capacity**: Throughput and capability constraints
- **Lifecycle**: Planning, construction, operation, decommission

## Constraints

- Infrastructure relationships must be explicit
- Infrastructure records become constitutional artifacts
- Infrastructure state must be deterministically tracked
