# Kardashev Transition Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive Kardashev transition reports summarizing current civilization stage, capability maturity, readiness evaluation, transition plans, risks, and recommendations for stage progression.

## Report Structure

### Executive Summary
- Current constitutional Kardashev stage
- Transition target and rationale
- Overall readiness assessment
- Key recommendations

### Civilizational State
- Current stage assessment with multi-dimensional scores
- Energy capture and source portfolio
- Capability maturity across all domains
- Infrastructure status and capacity

### Capability Assessment
- Domain-by-domain capability maturity
- Gap analysis: current vs. target levels
- Critical capability development needs
- Dependency analysis

### Readiness Evaluation
- Dimension-by-dimension readiness scores
- Overall probabilistic readiness
- Critical readiness gaps
- Recommended focus areas for readiness improvement

### Transition Plan
- Target stage definition and rationale
- Phased transition roadmap
- Milestones with verification criteria
- Timeline with uncertainty bounds
- Resource requirements

### Energy Transition
- Current energy profile
- Target energy architecture
- Energy transition phases
- Environmental and sustainability assessment

### Infrastructure Requirements
- Required infrastructure evolution
- Interdependency management
- Capacity expansion roadmap
- Resilience requirements

### Risk Assessment
- Technical, economic, social, environmental risks
- Risk probabilities and impacts
- Mitigation strategies
- Risk monitoring recommendations

### Recommendations
- Priority actions for transition readiness
- Scientific and engineering priorities
- Institutional development needs
- Coordination improvements

## Report Format

Reports are generated as deterministic constitutional artifacts with:
- Content-addressed identifier
- Complete provenance
- Fingerprint for verification
- Human-readable structure

## Constraints

- Reports must be comprehensive and evidence-based
- Report records become constitutional artifacts
- Uncertainty is explicitly preserved in all assessments
