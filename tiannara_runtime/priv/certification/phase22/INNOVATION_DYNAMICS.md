# Innovation Dynamics

## Purpose

Model innovation as a constitutional process — never spontaneous, always emerging from identifiable sources with preserved provenance.

## Innovation Sources

### Research-Driven Innovation
- Fundamental scientific discovery creates new capability domains
- Research investment and institutional quality determine discovery rate
- Experimental validation required before innovation classification

### Engineering-Driven Innovation
- Practical problem-solving produces incremental and breakthrough innovations
- Engineering constraints drive innovation in materials, processes, and systems
- Engineering knowledge accumulation enables innovation chains

### Cross-Domain Synthesis
- Innovations at domain boundaries produce disproportionate impact
- Knowledge connectivity enables analogical transfer
- Interdisciplinary collaboration mechanisms determine synthesis rate

### Mathematics-Driven Innovation
- Mathematical discoveries enable new modeling and design capabilities
- Formal proofs provide guarantees for engineered systems
- Mathematical structure discovery opens new problem-solving approaches

### Experimentation-Driven Innovation
- Systematic experimentation explores design space
- Well-designed experiments distinguish promising from unpromising directions
- Experimental infrastructure determines exploration capacity

### Simulation-Driven Innovation
- Computational simulation accelerates design iteration
- High-fidelity simulation reduces physical prototyping requirements
- Simulation capability expands accessible design space

### Civilizational Learning
- Lessons from successes and failures propagate through institutions
- Knowledge management systems determine learning retention
- Inter-generational knowledge transfer enables cumulative innovation

## Innovation Classification

- **Incremental**: Improvement within existing paradigm
- **Architectural**: Reconfiguration of existing components
- **Radical**: New paradigm addressing existing needs
- **Transformative**: New paradigm enabling new capability domains

## Constraints

- Innovation must always be traceable to sources
- Innovation records become constitutional artifacts
- Innovation rate emerges from state, never hard-coded
