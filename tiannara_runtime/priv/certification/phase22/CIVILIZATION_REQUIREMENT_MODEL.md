# Civilization Requirement Model

## Purpose

Formally specify civilization requirements derived from constitutional values, human needs, and long-term civilizational objectives.

## Requirement Categories

### Constitutional Requirements
- Human authority preservation
- Constitutional guarantee enforcement
- Determinism and replayability
- Archaeological preservation
- Transparency and auditability

### Scientific Requirements
- Knowledge growth and preservation
- Scientific discovery capacity
- Research institution health
- Cross-domain knowledge integration
- Scientific methodology integrity

### Engineering Requirements
- Infrastructure capability and resilience
- Engineering knowledge preservation
- Technology development capacity
- Manufacturing and construction capability

### Human Well-being Requirements
- Health and longevity
- Educational attainment
- Economic opportunity
- Cultural and scientific participation
- Quality of life

### Resilience Requirements
- Civilizational robustness
- Recovery capacity
- Adaptive capability
- Redundancy adequacy

### Sustainability Requirements
- Environmental health
- Resource renewal
- Long-term viability
- Inter-generational equity

### Evolutionary Requirements
- Adaptive capacity
- Constitutional continuity
- Knowledge lineage preservation
- Institutional evolution capacity

## Requirement Properties

- **Priority**: Constitutional priority level
- **Measurability**: How requirement satisfaction is measured
- **Current Satisfaction**: Current civilization performance
- **Target Satisfaction**: Desired performance level
- **Gap**: Difference between current and target
- **Trade-off Sensitivity**: Which other requirements conflict

## Constraints

- Requirements must be constitutional-derived
- Requirement records become constitutional artifacts
