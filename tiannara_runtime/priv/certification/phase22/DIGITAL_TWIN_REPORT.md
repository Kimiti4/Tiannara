# Digital Twin Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive reports on the state, fidelity, and health of civilizational digital twins.

## Report Sections

- Executive Summary
- Twin State Overview
- Synchronization Status
- Layer-by-Layer Analysis
- Reality Alignment Assessment
- Consistency Verification Results
- Intervention Simulation Catalog
- Uncertainty and Confidence
- Replay and Archaeology
- Twin Recommendations

## Frequency
- Standard: Per generation
- Comprehensive: Per 10 generations
