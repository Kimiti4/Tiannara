# Multi-Future Scenario Architecture

## Purpose
The CMFCSE continuously generates, evolves, compares, and evaluates thousands to millions of plausible future civilizations using constitutional scientific reasoning. Rather than predicting a single future, it maintains a continuously evolving ecosystem of futures whose probabilities change as new evidence arrives.

## Scope
- Scenario generation and branching from current state
- Probability evolution with new evidence
- Long-horizon forecasting (5 to 1,000+ years)
- Uncertainty propagation and black swan detection
- Civilization pathway modeling
- Scenario comparison and recommendation support

## Constitutional Principles
- **Evidence-driven** — all scenarios grounded in available data
- **Explicitly uncertain** — never predicts certainty, maintains competing futures
- **Replayable** — every scenario decision traceable
- **Versioned** — all scenario states archived
- **Human-reviewable** — governance oversight of foresight

## Dependencies
- **CPR (25.0)** — runtime, coordinator
- **PSE (25.1)** — current planetary state
- **CGON (25.2)** — current observations
- **CPDT (25.3)** — digital twin for scenario simulation
- **CPRIE-25.4** — risk context for futures
- **CPISE (25.5)** — intervention impacts on futures
- **CPRIE-25.6** — resource constraints on futures

## Non-Goals
- Predicting the future with certainty
- Making decisions (provides foresight, not command)
- Replacing human strategic planning
