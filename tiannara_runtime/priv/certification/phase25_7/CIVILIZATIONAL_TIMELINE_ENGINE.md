# Civilizational Timeline Engine

## Purpose
Constructs coherent civilizational timelines across all scenarios — tracking how civilization evolves through technology, science, governance, infrastructure, and space expansion.

## Timeline Dimensions

### Technology Evolution
- Energy technology (combustion → renewables → fusion → post-scarcity)
- Computing (classical → quantum → post-silicon → mind upload?)
- AI (narrow → general → superintelligent?)
- Transportation (ground → air → space → interstellar?)
- Manufacturing (subtractive → additive → nanofabrication → self-replication)

### Scientific Evolution
- Physics (standard model → beyond → theory of everything?)
- Biology (genome reading → genome writing → synthetic biology → life engineering?)
- Space science (solar system → exoplanets → interstellar?)
- Consciousness science

### Infrastructure Evolution
- From regional to planetary to space-based infrastructure
- Infrastructure density, intelligence, resilience
- Megaproject completion timelines (space elevators, Dyson swarms, terraforming)

### Governance Evolution
- From nation-states to planetary governance to interstellar governance
- Institutional evolution
- Constitutional development

### Economic Evolution
- From scarcity-based to post-scarcity economies
- Resource distribution models
- Economic organization (markets, planning, hybrid)

### Space Expansion
- Orbital infrastructure
- Lunar and Martian settlements
- Asteroid mining
- Outer solar system
- Interstellar precursors

## Timeline Construction
- Each timeline is a sequence of events and states
- Events have probability distributions over timing
- Causal relationships between events are tracked
- Timelines are internally consistent

## Output
For each scenario: civilizational timeline with key events, transitions, and state descriptions at each epoch.
