# Civilization Pathway Engine

## Purpose
Integrates all evolution dimensions into coherent civilization pathway scenarios — combining technology, infrastructure, science, resources, and governance into holistic civilizational trajectories.

## Pathway Dimensions

### Kardashev Evolution
- Type 0 → Type I (planetary) → Type II (stellar) → Type III (galactic)
- Current civilization: ~Type 0.7
- Pathway to Type I and beyond
- Energy consumption growth trajectory

### Civilizational Maturity
- Survival (avoiding existential risks)
- Stability (robust institutions and systems)
- Flourishing (maximizing potential)
- Transcendence (beyond current understanding)

### Existential Risk Management
- Natural risk mitigation over time
- Technological risk management (AI, nanotech, bio)
- Resilience building
- Recovery capability

### Expansion Trajectory
- Earth-bound → Earth+orbit → lunar → Martian → asteroid → outer solar system → interstellar precursors → interstellar colonization
- Rate and form of space expansion

## Pathway Types

### Flourishing Pathways
- Successful technology management
- Global cooperation
- Sustainable resource use
- Space expansion
- Scientific and cultural flourishing

### Rebound Pathways
- Setbacks followed by recovery
- Learning from crises
- Institutional adaptation

### Plateau Pathways
- Slower growth, stable civilization
- Avoiding risks but also limiting expansion
- Sustainable equilibrium

### Collapse Pathways
- Civilizational collapse
- Potential recovery or permanent decline
- Existential risk realization

### Transcendent Pathways
- Radical transformation (AI, post-human, upload)
- Beyond current comprehension
- New forms of civilization

## Output
Holistic civilization pathway scenarios combining all evolution dimensions, pathway classification, key transitions, branching points.
