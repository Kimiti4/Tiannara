# Governance Evolution Engine

## Purpose
Models the evolution of governance systems — from current nation-state system to potential planetary and interstellar governance.

## Governance Dimensions

### Scale Evolution
- Local → national → regional → planetary → solar system → interstellar
- Sovereignty distribution models
- Institutional integration levels

### Form Evolution
- Democracy evolution (direct, liquid, AI-assisted)
- Technocratic elements
- Constitutional governance (Tiannara model)
- Global coordination mechanisms

### Constitutional Development
- Rights expansion (future generations, ecosystems, AI entities)
- Precautionary principle institutionalization
- Science-based governance
- Long-term thinking institutionalization

### Conflict and Cooperation
- Geopolitical evolution scenarios
- Conflict vs. cooperation trajectories
- Treaty and alliance evolution

### Institutional Evolution
- UN and international institution reform
- New institutions for planetary challenges
- Scientific and engineering governance bodies
- Space governance

## Key Questions
- Will humanity achieve planetary-scale governance?
- Will it be cooperative or coercive?
- Can long-term thinking be institutionalized?
- How will AI affect governance?
- How will space settlement affect governance structures?

## Output
Governance evolution scenarios, institutional timelines, constitutional development pathways, conflict/cooperation projections.
