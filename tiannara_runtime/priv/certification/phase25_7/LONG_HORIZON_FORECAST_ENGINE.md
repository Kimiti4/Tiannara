# Long-Horizon Forecast Engine

## Purpose
Supports constitutional forecasting over multiple time horizons — from operational (5 years) to civilizational (1,000+ years).

## Forecast Horizons

| Horizon | Range | Purpose |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Short-term | 5 years | Near-term planning |
| Medium-term | 10 years | Tactical planning |
| Long-term | 25 years | Strategic planning |
| Generational | 50 years | Generational planning |
| Multi-generational | 100 years | Constitutional stewardship |
| Civilizational | 250 years | Civilization trajectory |
| Deep future | 500 years | Long-term risk & opportunity |
| Transcendent | 1,000+ years | Kardashev progression |

## Horizon-Specific Modeling

### Short-Term (5-10 years)
- High confidence, many known variables
- Focus on committed trajectories and near-term decisions
- Key drivers: current policies, existing infrastructure, demographic momentum

### Medium-Term (25-50 years)
- Moderate confidence, significant uncertainty
- Focus on technology adoption cycles and infrastructure transitions
- Key drivers: technology maturation, demographic shifts, climate change

### Long-Term (100-250 years)
- Low confidence, many unknown variables
- Focus on structural trends and fundamental constraints
- Key drivers: resource constraints, climate regime shifts, technology paradigms

### Deep Future (500-1,000+ years)
- Very low confidence, speculative
- Focus on civilizational possibilities and existential risks
- Key drivers: fundamental physics limits, space expansion, civilizational evolution

## Horizon Interaction
- Short-term forecasts constrain long-term possibilities
- Long-term forecasts inform short-term priorities
- Cross-horizon consistency checks

## Output
For each horizon: scenario set with probability distributions, key drivers, confidence intervals, uncertainty characterization, dependency on shorter-horizon outcomes.
