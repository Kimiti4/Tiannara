# Scenario Governance Model

## Purpose
Governance framework for scenario generation and use — ensuring that futures are not used to justify unconstitutional actions and that foresight remains subject to human oversight.

## Governance Principles

### Human Oversight
- Scenarios inform, they do not command
- Final decisions remain with human governance
- Scenario-based recommendations are advisory
- Humans can override scenario-driven logic

### Transparency
- All scenarios are generated from explicit assumptions
- Assumptions are documented and reviewable
- Probability updates have traceable causes
- The public can see what futures are being considered

### Accountability
- Scenario generation is auditable
- Assumption choices are justified
- Scenario misuse is detectable
- Evidence of scenario influence on decisions is preserved

### Constitutional Boundaries
- Scenarios cannot recommend unconstitutional actions
- Precautionary principle applies to high-impact scenarios
- Future generations' interests are represented
- Rights are inalienable across all scenarios

## Governance Entities
- **Foresight Council** — human body overseeing scenario generation
- **Ethics Board** — reviews scenario assumptions for bias
- **Public** — access to scenario information
- **Auditors** — independent verification of scenario integrity

## Usage Boundaries
- Scenarios for planning: permitted
- Scenarios for policy: recommended with human approval
- Scenarios for rights restriction: prohibited
- Scenarios for panic/urgency manipulation: prohibited
- Scenarios for long-term investment: encouraged
