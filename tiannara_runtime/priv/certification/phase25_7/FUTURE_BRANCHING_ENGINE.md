# Future Branching Engine

## Purpose
Expands scenarios into branching futures at decision points, uncertainty points, and event points — creating a tree of possible futures.

## Branching Points

### Decision Points
- Human choices (policy decisions, investment directions, ethical choices)
- Intervention decisions (from CPISE)
- Governance decisions (new laws, treaties, institutions)

### Uncertainty Points
- Technology outcomes (does fusion become viable? When?)
- Climate sensitivity (exact degree of warming)
- Economic transitions (smooth or disruptive?)
- Discovery outcomes (do we find life? new physics?)

### Event Points
- Natural events (pandemics, volcanic eruptions, asteroid impacts)
- Technological events (AI breakthrough, energy breakthrough)
- Social events (conflicts, migrations, revolutions)

## Branching Rules

### Probability Splitting
- Each branch inherits parent probability
- Split probability across child branches based on evidence
- Ensure probabilities sum correctly

### Depth Management
- Maximum branching depth configurable
- Pruning threshold: branches below probability threshold pruned
- Focus allocation: more branches near high-probability regions

### Sensitivity Tracking
- Track which branches are most sensitive to new evidence
- Prioritize evidence collection for high-sensitivity branches

## Output
Branching tree: root (current reality) → branches at each decision/uncertainty/event point, each with probability, assumptions, evidence, and lineage.
