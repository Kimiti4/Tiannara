# Black Swan Engine

## Purpose
Explicitly models low-probability, high-impact events that could fundamentally alter civilizational trajectories — the events most planning ignores.

## Black Swan Categories

### Natural Black Swans
- Supervolcanic eruption
- Large asteroid impact
- Solar superflare
- Gamma-ray burst
- Global pandemic (beyond COVID-scale)
- Climate tipping cascade

### Technological Black Swans
- Artificial superintelligence
- Radical life extension
- Self-replicating nanotechnology
- Fusion energy breakthrough
- Faster-than-light communication/discovery
- Consciousness uploading
- Alien contact (signal or artifact)

### Social Black Swans
- Global coordination breakthrough
- Civilization collapse
- Post-scarcity economics
- Universal basic prosperity
- Global-scale conflict
- Mass migration event

### Scientific Black Swans
- Paradigm shift in fundamental physics
- Discovery of extraterrestrial life
- Understanding of consciousness
- Reversal of entropy (theoretical)

## Modeling Approach

### Identification
- Scan for known potential black swans from scientific literature
- Generate plausible novel black swans from first principles
- Crowdsource from human experts

### Characterization
- Probability estimate (even if highly uncertain)
- Impact magnitude across multiple dimensions
- Warning time
- Signatures (precursors we could detect)
- Mitigation potential

### Integration
- Inject black swans as low-probability branches in scenario tree
- Track their impact on other scenario probabilities
- Identify hedging strategies

## Output
Black swan catalog with probability ranges, impact assessments, precursor signatures, and scenario integration.
