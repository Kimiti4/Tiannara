# Technology Evolution Engine

## Purpose
Models the evolution of technology across all domains — tracking current state, projecting future developments, and modeling their civilizational impact.

## Technology Domains
- **Energy** — generation, storage, distribution, efficiency
- **Computing** — hardware, architecture, algorithms, AI
- **Manufacturing** — additive, subtractive, nanofabrication, self-assembly
- **Transportation** — ground, sea, air, space, hyperloop
- **Materials** — new materials, metamaterials, smart materials
- **Biology** — genomics, synthetic biology, bioengineering
- **Medicine** — diagnostics, therapeutics, regeneration, enhancement
- **Space** — propulsion, life support, construction, ISRU
- **Communication** — bandwidth, latency, security, quantum
- **Sensors** — resolution, coverage, capability, miniaturization

## Evolution Modeling

### S-Curve Model
- Each technology follows adoption S-curve
- Track current position on curve
- Project future position based on investment and constraints

### Technology Interaction
- How technologies enable or constrain each other
- Convergence (AI + biotech, nano + energy)
- Bottlenecks (which technologies limit others)

### Breakthrough Modeling
- Probability of breakthroughs over time
- Impact of breakthrough on scenario trajectories
- Precursor indicators for breakthrough detection

### Limits
- Fundamental physical limits (thermodynamic, computational, material)
- Economic limits (cost curves, diminishing returns)
- Social limits (adoption resistance, ethical constraints)

## Output
Technology evolution projections for each technology domain, S-curve positions, interaction maps, breakthrough probabilities, limit assessments.
