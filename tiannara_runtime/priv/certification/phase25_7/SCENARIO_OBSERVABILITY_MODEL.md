# Scenario Observability Model

## Purpose
Observability for the multi-future scenario engine — operators must understand what futures are being considered, how probabilities are evolving, and what evidence is driving changes.

## Observatory Displays

### Future Tree
- Visual representation of the scenario branching tree
- Probability displayed for each branch
- Highlighted key divergences
- Pruned branches shown faded

### Scenario Probability Distribution
- Current probabilities for all active scenarios
- Probability evolution over time
- Uncertainty ranges
- Scenario ranking

### Long-Horizon Timeline
- Civilization trajectory visualization
- Key events marked
- Probability envelopes
- Scenario divergence highlighted

### Branch Divergence
- Where do scenarios diverge?
- What drives divergence?
- How much do scenarios differ?

### Technology Evolution
- Technology S-curves
- Breakthrough probabilities
- Technology interaction maps

### Risk Evolution
- Risk profiles by scenario
- Existential risk over time
- Risk mitigation effectiveness

### Kardashev Readiness
- Energy consumption trajectory
- Type I/II/III milestones
- Comparison across scenarios

### Replay Integrity
- Replay accuracy metrics
- Last verified timestamp
- Coverage of replayable operations

## Observability Interfaces
- Real-time dashboards
- Structured log streams
- Queryable history
- Alert channels
