# Scenario Comparison Engine

## Purpose
Compares multiple future scenarios side by side to identify differences, convergences, and critical uncertainties that distinguish scenarios.

## Comparison Dimensions

### Outcome Comparison
- What does civilization look like at each horizon under each scenario?
- Compare across: technology level, energy use, population, science, governance, space presence
- Quantitative and qualitative metrics

### Trajectory Comparison
- How do scenarios unfold over time?
- Where do they diverge?
- Where do they converge?
- What events drive divergence?

### Risk Comparison
- Which scenarios face which risks?
- Existential risk probability by scenario
- Risk mitigation effectiveness

### Resource Comparison
- Resource consumption by scenario
- Sustainability outcomes
- Space resource development

### Robustness Comparison
- Which scenarios are robust to uncertainty?
- Which scenarios depend on specific assumptions?
- Which scenarios are fragile?

## Comparison Methods

### Pairwise Comparison
- Direct comparison of any two scenarios
- Difference highlighting
- Commonality identification

### Ensemble Analysis
- Statistical properties across all scenarios
- Probability-weighted outcomes
- Worst-case, best-case, expected-case

### Sensitivity Analysis
- Which assumptions most affect outcomes?
- Which scenarios change most with new evidence?
- Where should evidence collection focus?

## Output
Scenario comparison tables, divergence maps, convergence analysis, ensemble statistics, sensitivity analysis, ranking by various criteria.
