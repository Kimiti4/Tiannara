# Scenario Archaeology Model

## Purpose
Full audit trail and causal analysis for scenario generation — understanding why a scenario was generated, how its probability evolved, and what evidence influenced it.

## Archaeological Records

### Scenario Genesis Records
- Why was this scenario generated?
- What assumptions were made?
- What evidence supported it at creation?
- What was its initial probability?

### Probability Evolution Records
- How did probability change over time?
- What evidence caused each change?
- What was the magnitude of each change?
- Who or what triggered the update?

### Branching Records
- Why was this branch created?
- What decision/uncertainty/event was modeled?
- What was the branching logic?
- What alternative branches were considered?

### Scenario Retirement Records
- Why was a scenario pruned or retired?
- What evidence contradicted it?
- What probability threshold was crossed?
- Could it be revived?

### Causal Analysis
- Why did scenario evolution differ from expectations?
- Were assumptions validated or contradicted?
- What evidence was most influential?
- What could have been done differently?

## Archaeological Queries
- Trace a scenario's full lifecycle
- Show probability evolution over time with evidence events
- Compare generated scenarios at different times
- Identify which evidence was most influential
- Reconstruct the full future tree at any past time

## Output
Complete scenario history: lifecycle records, probability evolution, evidence influence, branching decisions, retirement rationale.
