# Scientific Evolution Engine

## Purpose
Models the evolution of scientific knowledge and discovery — projecting which questions will be answered, which paradigms may shift, and how science will transform civilization.

## Scientific Domains
- **Physics** — quantum gravity, dark matter, dark energy, unification
- **Cosmology** — universe origin, cosmic evolution, multiverse
- **Biology** — origins of life, synthetic life, ecosystems
- **Neuroscience** — consciousness, brain-computer interfaces, mind
- **Chemistry** — complex molecular systems, new materials
- **Earth Science** — climate, geology, oceanography, atmospheric
- **Space Science** — solar system, exoplanets, astrobiology

## Evolution Modeling

### Discovery Probability
- For each major open question, estimate probability of resolution by horizon
- Based on current research investment, methodology, and difficulty
- Factor in technological enablers (better telescopes, colliders, AI)

### Paradigm Shifts
- Identify areas where current theories face anomalies
- Model potential paradigm shifts and their implications
- Track scientific revolutions and their civilizational impact

### Feedback Loops
- Science enables technology which enables more science
- AI acceleration of scientific discovery
- Resource constraints on scientific progress

### Limits of Science
- Fundamental observability limits
- Computational irreducibility
- Unknown unknown space

## Output
Scientific evolution projections, discovery timelines, paradigm shift scenarios, capability expansion forecasts.
