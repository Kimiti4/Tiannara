# Infrastructure Evolution Engine

## Purpose
Models the evolution of planetary and space infrastructure — from current systems to future megastructures.

## Infrastructure Domains
- **Energy Infrastructure** — grid, pipelines, generation plants, storage
- **Transportation Infrastructure** — roads, rail, ports, airports, launch sites
- **Communication Infrastructure** — fiber, wireless, satellite, quantum networks
- **Water Infrastructure** — treatment, distribution, desalination, dams
- **Building Infrastructure** — housing, commercial, industrial, scientific
- **Space Infrastructure** — launch, orbital, lunar, planetary
- **Digital Infrastructure** — data centers, networks, computing facilities

## Evolution Modeling

### Current State
- Age and condition of existing infrastructure
- Maintenance backlog
- Capacity utilization

### Replacement and Upgrade
- Infrastructure lifetime modeling
- Replacement cycles
- Technology upgrade paths

### New Infrastructure
- Committed projects and timelines
- Planned developments
- Speculative megaprojects

### Megastructures
- Space elevators
- Orbital rings
- Dyson swarms/rings
- O'Neill cylinders
- Terraforming infrastructure
- Interstellar infrastructure

## Infrastructure Interactions
- Infrastructure systems are interdependent
- Failure in one can cascade
- Capacity constraints create bottlenecks

## Output
Infrastructure evolution timeline, replacement/upgrade schedules, megaproject timelines, interdependency maps, capacity projections.
