# Water Flow Engine

## Purpose
Models the planetary water cycle — natural hydrological flows, human extraction and consumption, water quality, and long-term sustainability.

## Water Sources
- **Surface water** — rivers, lakes, reservoirs
- **Groundwater** — aquifers (renewable and fossil)
- **Atmospheric** — precipitation, atmospheric water generation
- **Desalinated** — seawater and brackish water desalination
- **Recycled** — treated wastewater, industrial reuse
- **Ice** — glaciers, ice caps, polar ice (strategic reserve)

## Flow Model

### Natural Cycle
- Precipitation patterns and volumes
- Evaporation and transpiration
- Surface runoff and river flow
- Groundwater recharge
- Snowpack and glacial storage dynamics

### Human Extraction
- Municipal water supply
- Agricultural irrigation
- Industrial processes
- Energy production (cooling, hydropower, extraction)
- Mining and resource extraction

### Consumption and Return
- Consumptive use (water incorporated into products, evaporated)
- Non-consumptive use (returned to source)
- Treatment and quality change
- Wastewater discharge

### Quality
- Potable water availability
- Agricultural water quality
- Industrial water quality requirements
- Pollution and contamination tracking

## Analysis
- Water balance (supply vs. demand)
- Water stress index by region
- Groundwater depletion rates
- Drought risk assessment
- Flood risk assessment
- Water quality trends
- Infrastructure adequacy

## Output
Water flow accounts, stress indices, depletion warnings, quality assessments, infrastructure status.
