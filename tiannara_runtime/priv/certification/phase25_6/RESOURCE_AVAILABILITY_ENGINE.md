# Resource Availability Engine

## Purpose
Estimates current and future availability of each resource class — accounting for production, consumption, regeneration, depletion, and external constraints.

## Availability Dimensions

### Current Availability
- Total stock (reserves)
- Current production/extraction rate
- Current consumption rate
- Net flow (production - consumption)
- Time to depletion at current rates (non-renewables)
- Regeneration rate (renewables)

### Future Availability
- Projected production changes (new mines, farms, plants)
- Projected demand changes (population, technology, economy)
- Discovery potential (undiscovered reserves)
- Substitution potential (alternative resources)
- Technological unlock (new extraction or production methods)

### Constrained Availability
- Ecological constraints (cannot extract without ecosystem damage)
- Social constraints (local opposition, labor availability)
- Infrastructure constraints (transport, processing capacity)
- Geopolitical constraints (trade restrictions, conflict zones)
- Constitutional constraints (legal limits on extraction)

### Strategic Reserves
- Current strategic reserve levels
- Reserve adequacy (days/weeks/months of consumption)
- Reserve replenishment plans
- Reserve drawdown authorization

## Output
For each resource: current availability assessment, short/medium/long-term availability projection, constraint analysis, strategic reserve status, confidence level, uncertainty range.
