# Resource Forecasting Engine

## Purpose
Projects future resource availability, demand, and flows across multiple time horizons and scenarios.

## Forecast Horizons
- Short-term (days to months) — operational planning
- Medium-term (1-5 years) — tactical planning
- Long-term (5-25 years) — strategic planning
- Generational (25-100 years) — civilizational planning
- Multi-generational (100+ years) — constitutional stewardship

## Forecast Dimensions

### Supply Forecast
- Known reserves and production capacity
- Planned capacity additions
- Discovery probability and timing
- Technological improvement in extraction/production
- Ecological and regulatory constraints

### Demand Forecast
- Population-driven demand
- Technology-driven demand (new technologies create new resource demands)
- Economic growth-driven demand
- Intervention-driven demand (from CPISE)
- Efficiency improvement reducing demand

### Flow Forecast
- Trade and transport flows
- Transformation flows (raw material → manufactured good)
- Waste and recycling flows
- Loss and inefficiency flows

### Uncertainty
- Each forecast includes probability distributions
- Scenario-dependent forecasts (optimistic, expected, pessimistic)
- Black swan impact assessment

## Output
For each resource: multi-horizon supply/demand/flow forecasts with probability distributions, scenario comparisons, key uncertainty drivers, sensitivity analysis.
