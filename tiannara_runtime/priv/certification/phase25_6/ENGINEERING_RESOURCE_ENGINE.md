# Engineering Resource Engine

## Purpose
Models engineering and manufacturing capacity — factories, construction capability, fabrication facilities, and technical workforce.

## Engineering Resource Categories
- **Manufacturing** — production lines, assembly plants, fabrication facilities
- **Construction** — heavy equipment, construction materials, project management capacity
- **Civil Engineering** — infrastructure building and maintenance capacity
- **Aerospace** — rocket and satellite manufacturing, launch facilities
- **Energy Systems** — power plant construction, grid installation
- **Chemical/Process** — refineries, chemical plants, processing facilities
- **Advanced Manufacturing** — 3D printing, semiconductor fabrication, nanotechnology
- **Personnel** — engineers, technicians, project managers, skilled trades

## Flow Model

### Capacity
- Total engineering output capacity
- Current utilization
- Surge capacity for emergencies
- Geographic distribution

### Constraints
- Skilled labor availability
- Specialized equipment availability
- Material supply chains
- Energy requirements
- Regulatory and permitting

### Strategic Allocation
- Infrastructure maintenance vs. new development
- Current vs. future capability building
- Research-to-production pipeline
- Technology transfer and scaling

### Degradation
- Infrastructure aging and maintenance backlog
- Skill atrophy in underutilized areas
- Equipment obsolescence

## Analysis
- Engineering capacity adequacy
- Critical skill gaps
- Infrastructure maintenance health
- Technology readiness for production scaling
- Surge capacity for crisis response

## Output
Engineering resource inventory, capacity analysis, skill gap assessment, construction pipeline status, surge readiness metrics.
