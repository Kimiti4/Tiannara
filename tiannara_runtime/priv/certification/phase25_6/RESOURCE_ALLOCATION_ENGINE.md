# Resource Allocation Engine

## Purpose
Distributes available planetary resources across competing demands — balancing equity, sustainability, scientific value, engineering value, infrastructure stability, civilizational benefit, emergency response, and future preservation.

## Allocation Framework

### Demand Sources
- **Essential** — survival needs (food, water, shelter, healthcare)
- **Infrastructure** — maintenance and operation of critical systems
- **Scientific** — research, exploration, experiment
- **Engineering** — development, construction, manufacturing
- **Economic** — production, trade, commerce
- **Strategic** — defense, resilience, long-term projects
- **Emergency** — disaster response, crisis mitigation
- **Future** — reserves, investment, sustainability

### Allocation Principles
- Essential needs have first priority
- Sustainability cannot be compromised for short-term gain
- Future generations have equal standing with present
- Scientific and engineering capacity are strategic assets
- Emergency reserves must be maintained
- Allocation must be explainable and auditable

### Allocation Process
- Gather demand requests from all sectors (including CPISE interventions)
- Aggregate and prioritize using constitutional weighting
- Check against availability and constraints
- Generate allocation plan
- Assess impact
- Certify

## Output
Allocation plan: per-resource allocation across demand sources, prioritization rationale, constraint satisfaction, impact assessment, sustainability check, fingerprint.
