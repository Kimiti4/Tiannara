# Planetary Resource Intelligence Architecture

## Purpose
The CPRIE models, predicts, allocates, optimizes, and safeguards planetary resources while balancing present needs with long-term civilizational sustainability. It reasons simultaneously across science, engineering, infrastructure, economics, ecology, and future generations.

## Scope
- Resource inventory, classification, and availability
- Material, energy, water, food, and compute flow modeling
- Resource allocation and multi-objective optimization
- Forecasting with uncertainty propagation
- Resource dependency and resilience analysis
- Governance, certification, replay, and archaeology

## Constitutional Principles
- **Constitution-first** — every resource decision respects constitutional limits
- **Evidence-backed** — all assessments grounded in observation
- **Sustainable** — present needs balanced against future generations
- **Multi-generational** — horizon extends centuries, not quarters
- **Replayable, explainable, auditable** — full transparency

## Dependencies
- **CPR (25.0)** — runtime, coordinator
- **CGON (25.2)** — raw observation data for resource monitoring
- **CPDT (25.3)** — digital twin for resource flow simulation
- **CPRIE (25.4)** — risk context for resource vulnerability
- **CPISE (25.5)** — intervention strategies involving resource allocation

## Non-Goals
- Implementing ERP or logistics systems
- Direct control of physical resource flows
- Replacing human governance of resource decisions
