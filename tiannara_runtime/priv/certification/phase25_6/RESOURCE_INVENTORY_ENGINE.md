# Resource Inventory Engine

## Purpose
Maintains a comprehensive, continuously updated inventory of all planetary resources — their type, quantity, quality, location, accessibility, and status.

## Resource Categories
- **Energy** — fossil, nuclear, solar, wind, hydro, geothermal, tidal, fusion (future)
- **Water** — surface, groundwater, atmospheric, desalinated, ice
- **Food** — agricultural, livestock, marine, synthetic, preserved stocks
- **Raw Materials** — metals, minerals, aggregates, timber, biomass, rare earth elements
- **Compute** — data centers, HPC capacity, quantum compute, edge compute
- **Scientific Equipment** — telescopes, colliders, labs, sensors, satellites
- **Engineering Capacity** — factories, construction equipment, fabrication facilities
- **Manufacturing Capacity** — production lines, supply chains, assembly plants
- **Human Expertise** — skilled workforce, scientists, engineers, operators
- **Financial Capital** — sovereign funds, investment capital, currency reserves
- **Ecological Resources** — forests, fisheries, biodiversity, arable land
- **Atmospheric Resources** — carbon capacity, oxygen, atmospheric water
- **Space Resources** — orbital assets, lunar/martian resources (future)

## Inventory Record
Each inventory entry: resource ID, category, subcategory, quantity, unit, quality grade, location, accessibility score, extraction/production rate, regeneration rate (if renewable), depletion estimate, data source, confidence, fingerprint.

## Update Mechanism
- Real-time updates from CGON observation streams
- Periodic deep surveys
- Adjustment based on allocation outflows and natural inflows
- Correction based on monitoring discrepancies
