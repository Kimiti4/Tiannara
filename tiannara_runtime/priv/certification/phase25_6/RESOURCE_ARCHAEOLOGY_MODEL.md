# Resource Archaeology Model

## Purpose
Full audit trail and causal analysis for resource decisions — understanding why resources were allocated as they were, what the actual outcomes were, and what can be learned.

## Archaeological Records

### Decision Records
- Why was this allocation chosen over alternatives?
- What information was available at decision time?
- What constraints and priorities drove the decision?
- Who (human or system) authorized the decision?

### Allocation Records
- What resources were allocated to whom?
- What was the expected outcome?
- What was the actual resource flow?
- Were allocation limits respected?

### Outcomes
- What were the actual outcomes of the allocation?
- Were allocation goals achieved?
- Were there unintended consequences?
- Were forecasts accurate?

### Causal Analysis
- Why did actual outcomes differ from expected?
- Were models correct? If not, why?
- Were constraints correctly identified?
- What could have been done differently?

## Archaeological Queries
- Trace a resource from source to final consumption
- Show all allocations to a specific sector over time
- Compare projected vs. actual resource flows
- Identify patterns of allocation failure or success
- Reconstruct decision context for any past allocation

## Output
Complete resource history: allocation timeline, decision records, outcome analysis, causal analysis, lessons learned.
