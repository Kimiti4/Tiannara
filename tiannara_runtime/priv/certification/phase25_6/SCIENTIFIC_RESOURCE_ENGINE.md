# Scientific Resource Engine

## Purpose
Models scientific research and exploration resources — equipment, facilities, personnel, data, and experimental capacity.

## Scientific Resource Categories
- **Observational** — telescopes (optical, radio, X-ray, gravitational wave), satellites, sensors, monitoring networks
- **Experimental** — particle colliders, reactors, laboratory facilities, wind tunnels
- **Computational** — scientific computing, simulation clusters, data archives
- **Field** — research stations, oceanographic vessels, drilling platforms, aircraft
- **Personnel** — scientists, engineers, technicians, support staff
- **Data** — scientific datasets, knowledge bases, sample collections
- **Funding** — research budgets, grants, institutional support

## Flow Model

### Capacity and Utilization
- Facility availability and scheduling
- Instrument time allocation
- Personnel allocation across projects
- Data storage and processing capacity

### Resource Intensity
- Energy consumption per experiment/observation
- Material requirements (rare isotopes, samples, reagents)
- Compute requirements for data analysis
- Personnel time per research output

### Strategic Allocation
- Fundamental research vs. applied research
- Exploration vs. exploitation
- High-risk/high-reward vs. incremental
- Cross-disciplinary allocation
- Long-term capability building

## Analysis
- Scientific capacity adequacy
- Coverage gaps (underexplored domains)
- Equipment age and replacement needs
- Personnel pipeline health
- Research output efficiency

## Output
Scientific resource inventory, utilization analysis, coverage assessment, capacity projections, strategic allocation recommendations.
