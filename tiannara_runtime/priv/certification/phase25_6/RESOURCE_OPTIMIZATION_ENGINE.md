# Resource Optimization Engine

## Purpose
Multi-objective optimization across allocation goals — finds Pareto-optimal allocation strategies that cannot improve one objective without degrading another.

## Optimization Objectives
- **Equity** — fair distribution across regions and populations
- **Sustainability** — long-term resource base preservation
- **Scientific Value** — maximize research and discovery output
- **Engineering Value** — maximize infrastructure and capability development
- **Infrastructure Stability** — maintain critical system operation
- **Civilizational Benefit** — maximize overall human/flourishing trajectory
- **Emergency Response** — maintain surge capacity for crises
- **Future Preservation** — maintain strategic reserves and options

## Optimization Approach

### Constraint Handling
- Hard constraints: constitution, physics, ecology, minimum essential needs
- Soft constraints: preference-based, can be traded off
- Feasible region: all allocations satisfying hard constraints

### Pareto Frontier
- Enumerate or sample feasible allocation strategies
- Score each on all objectives
- Identify Pareto-dominant strategies
- Present frontier to decision-makers

### Tradeoff Analysis
- Which objectives compete most strongly?
- Where are the knee points (best marginal tradeoffs)?
- What are the costs of moving from one Pareto point to another?

## Output
Pareto frontier of allocation strategies, tradeoff characterizations, sensitivity analysis, ranked recommendations under different preference weightings.
