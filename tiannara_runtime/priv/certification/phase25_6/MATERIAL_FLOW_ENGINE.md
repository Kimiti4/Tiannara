# Material Flow Engine

## Purpose
Models the flow of physical materials through the planetary economic and industrial system — from extraction to processing, manufacturing, use, and disposal or recycling.

## Flow Categories
- **Metals** — iron, aluminum, copper, lithium, rare earths, precious metals
- **Minerals** — construction materials, industrial minerals, critical minerals
- **Biomass** — timber, agricultural fiber, bio-based materials
- **Commodities** — basic industrial inputs through global supply chains
- **Waste** — material destined for disposal, recycling, or reuse

## Flow Model

### Sources
- Primary extraction (mines, wells, harvests)
- Secondary recovery (recycling, reclamation)
- Import (from other regions or space)

### Transformations
- Processing (ore → metal, crude → refined)
- Manufacturing (materials → products)
- Construction (materials → infrastructure)
- Consumption (products → use → waste)

### Sinks
- Final consumption (materials embedded in products)
- Waste disposal (landfill, incineration, dispersal)
- Export (to other regions or space)
- Recycling loop

## Analysis
- Material flow accounting (mass balance)
- Supply chain mapping
- Critical dependency identification
- Substitution analysis
- Circularity assessment
- Bottleneck detection

## Output
Material flow accounts, supply chain maps, criticality assessments, circularity metrics, bottleneck alerts.
