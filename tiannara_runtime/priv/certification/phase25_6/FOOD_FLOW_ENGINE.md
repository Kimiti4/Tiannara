# Food Flow Engine

## Purpose
Models the planetary food system — production, processing, distribution, consumption, waste, and sustainability across all food sources.

## Food Sources
- **Crops** — grains, vegetables, fruits, oilseeds, legumes
- **Livestock** — meat, dairy, eggs, aquaculture
- **Marine** — wild catch, aquaculture, seaweed
- **Synthetic** — lab-grown meat, precision fermentation, cellular agriculture
- **Novel** — insect protein, algae, microbial protein
- **Preserved** — stored food reserves, strategic grain reserves

## Flow Model

### Production
- Agricultural land use and yield
- Input requirements (water, fertilizer, energy, labor)
- Seasonal and geographic production patterns
- Technology impact (precision agriculture, vertical farming, GMO)

### Processing
- Post-harvest handling
- Food processing and manufacturing
- Preservation and packaging
- Nutritional transformation

### Distribution
- Domestic supply chains
- International trade
- Cold chain infrastructure
- Market distribution
- Emergency food distribution

### Consumption
- Human nutrition (calories, protein, micronutrients)
- Animal feed
- Industrial food use (biofuels, biomaterials)
- Food waste at consumption stage

### Waste
- Production waste (field losses)
- Processing waste
- Distribution waste (spoilage)
- Consumption waste
- Recycling and composting

## Analysis
- Caloric and nutritional adequacy
- Food security index by region
- Supply chain resilience
- Self-sufficiency assessment
- Waste reduction potential
- Land use efficiency
- Carbon and water footprint

## Output
Food flow accounts, security indices, nutritional adequacy, waste assessment, resilience metrics.
