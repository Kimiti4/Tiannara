# Compute Resource Engine

## Purpose
Models planetary computational resources — data centers, HPC clusters, quantum computers, edge compute, and network infrastructure.

## Compute Resource Categories
- **Cloud/Data Center** — general-purpose computing capacity
- **HPC** — high-performance computing for simulation and modeling
- **Scientific** — specialized scientific computing (collider data, genome analysis)
- **Quantum** — quantum computing capacity (current and future)
- **Edge** — distributed edge computing
- **AI** — specialized AI/ML compute (GPUs, TPUs, AI accelerators)
- **Network** — bandwidth, latency, interconnection capacity

## Flow Model

### Capacity
- Total compute capacity (FLOPS, qubits, throughput)
- Utilization rates
- Reserved vs. on-demand capacity
- Geographic distribution

### Energy
- Power consumption by compute type
- Power usage effectiveness (PUE)
- Carbon intensity of compute
- Energy/compute optimization

### Allocation
- Scientific research allocation
- AI training and inference
- Simulation and modeling (CPDT, CPISE, CMFCSE)
- General computing
- Reserve capacity for emergencies

### Constraints
- Hardware availability (chip supply, rare earths for magnets)
- Cooling requirements and water usage
- Network bandwidth bottlenecks
- Skilled operator availability

## Analysis
- Compute adequacy for civilizational needs
- AI compute growth trajectory
- Scientific compute coverage
- Energy efficiency trends
- Hardware dependency risks

## Output
Compute inventory, utilization rates, allocation plan, growth projections, constraint alerts.
