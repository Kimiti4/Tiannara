# Resource Governance Model

## Purpose
Governance framework for resource decisions — ensuring constitutional compliance, human oversight, and accountability.

## Governance Principles

### Human Governance
- Final allocation decisions are made or approved by humans
- The engine provides recommendations, not commands
- Decision-makers have access to full context and analysis
- Emergency override by human authority

### Constitutional Compliance
- Every allocation must pass constitutional checks
- Sustainability constraints are non-negotiable
- Future generations' interests are represented
- Equity requirements are enforced

### Transparency
- All allocation decisions are recorded
- Reasoning and tradeoffs are documented
- Decision criteria are public
- Audit trail is complete

### Accountability
- Every decision is traceable to a responsible entity
- Decisions can be reviewed and challenged
- Unconstitutional decisions can be reversed
- Lessons from failures are preserved

## Governance Entities
- **Resource Council** — human body overseeing planetary resource strategy
- **Engine Operators** — responsible for CPRIE operation and integrity
- **Auditors** — independent verification of resource decisions
- **Public** — access to resource information and decision rationale

## Decision Levels
- **Operational** — routine allocations within approved bounds (engine automates)
- **Tactical** — medium-term allocation adjustments (engine recommends, human approves)
- **Strategic** — long-term resource strategy (human council decides, engine supports)
- **Constitutional** — resource decisions affecting constitutional principles (requires council + constitutional review)
