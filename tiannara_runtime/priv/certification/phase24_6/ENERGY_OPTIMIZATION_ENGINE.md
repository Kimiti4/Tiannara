# Energy Optimization Engine

## Purpose

Improve energy efficiency of engineered systems across their entire lifecycle.

## Energy Optimization Targets

| Target | Approach |
|--------|----------|
| Operational Energy | Reduce consumption during use |
| Manufacturing Energy | Reduce energy in production |
| Standby Energy | Reduce idle consumption |
| Thermal Management | Improve heating/cooling efficiency |
| Power Conversion | Improve conversion efficiency |
| Energy Recovery | Capture waste energy |
| Lifecycle Energy | Reduce total energy footprint |

## Optimization Properties

- Energy improvements are quantified with uncertainty
- Energy tradeoffs against cost and performance are documented
- Energy history is fully preserved
- Energy optimization is fully replayable
