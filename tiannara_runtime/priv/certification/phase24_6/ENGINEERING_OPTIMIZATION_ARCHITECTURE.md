# Engineering Optimization Architecture

## Purpose

Architect Tiannara's continuous constitutional optimization capability. The CEOCE continuously improves engineering systems throughout their entire lifecycle by discovering, evaluating, validating, and deploying constitutionally certified optimizations across performance, reliability, safety, cost, manufacturability, sustainability, maintainability, scalability, resilience, and civilizational benefit.

## Core Principles

- Optimization is multi-objective — no single objective dominates without constitutional justification
- Every optimization records its tradeoff rationale
- Optimization is performed against certified baselines
- Optimization is verified and validated before deployment
- Optimization lineage is fully preserved
- All optimization is fully replayable
