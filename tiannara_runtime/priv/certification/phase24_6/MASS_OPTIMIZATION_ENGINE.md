# Mass Optimization Engine

## Purpose

Reduce system mass while maintaining structural integrity, performance, and safety.

## Mass Reduction Approaches

| Approach | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Material Substitution | Replace with lighter materials |
| Topology Optimization | Optimize shape for minimum mass |
| Structural Optimization | Remove unnecessary material |
| Component Integration | Combine functions into single parts |
| Additive Manufacturing | Complex lightweight geometries |
| Composite Materials | High strength-to-weight ratio |

## Optimization Properties

- Mass targets are traceable to requirements
- Mass tradeoffs against cost and strength are documented
- Mass history is fully preserved
- Mass optimization is fully replayable
