# Engineering Bottleneck Engine

## Purpose

Identify performance-limiting factors across all engineering dimensions. Bottlenecks are the highest-leverage targets for optimization.

## Bottleneck Categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Performance | Throughput, speed, capacity limiters |
| Weight | Heavy components, mass drivers |
| Cost | Expensive components, processes |
| Manufacturing | Low-yield processes, long lead times |
| Reliability | High-failure-rate components |
| Safety | Safety margin limiters |
| Energy | High-energy-consumption subsystems |
| Maintenance | Frequent-maintenance components |
| Sustainability | High-environmental-impact elements |
| Infrastructure | Facility, equipment constraints |

## Bottleneck Properties

- Bottlenecks are quantified with impact metrics
- Bottleneck ranking drives optimization priority
- Bottleneck history is preserved
- Bottlenecks are fully replayable
