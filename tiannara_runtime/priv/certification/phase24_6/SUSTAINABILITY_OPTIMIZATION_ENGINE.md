# Sustainability Optimization Engine

## Purpose

Reduce the environmental impact of engineered systems across their full lifecycle.

## Sustainability Improvement Approaches

| Approach | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Material Selection | Choose sustainable materials |
| Energy Efficiency | Reduce operational energy |
| Manufacturing Emissions | Reduce production emissions |
| Waste Reduction | Minimize manufacturing waste |
| Recycled Content | Use recycled materials |
| End-of-Life Design | Design for disassembly and recycling |
| Supply Chain Sustainability | Sustainable sourcing |

## Optimization Properties

- Sustainability improvements are quantified (carbon, waste, water)
- Sustainability tradeoffs against cost and performance are documented
- Sustainability history is fully preserved
- Sustainability optimization is fully replayable
