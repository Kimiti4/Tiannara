# Civilizational Engineering Impact Model

## Purpose

Measure and optimize the civilizational benefit of engineering activities. Every optimization ultimately serves civilizational progress.

## Impact Dimensions

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Scientific Advancement | Enables future scientific discoveries |
| Engineering Capability | Expands what engineering can achieve |
| Economic Productivity | Increases economic output |
| Quality of Life | Improves human wellbeing |
| Planetary Sustainability | Reduces environmental burden |
| Resilience | Increases civilization's ability to withstand shocks |
| Knowledge Growth | Contributes to cumulative knowledge |
| Long-Term Progress | Enables future civilizational milestones |

## Impact Properties

- Impact is quantified with uncertainty
- Impact is assessed per optimization baseline
- Impact history is fully preserved
- Impact is fully replayable
- Impact drives optimization priority
