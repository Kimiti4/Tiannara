# Tradeoff Analysis Engine

## Purpose

Analyze and document explicit tradeoffs between competing optimization objectives. Every optimization records its tradeoff rationale.

## Tradeoff Representation

| Improvement | Effect on Objective A | Effect on Objective B | Effect on Objective C |
|-------------|----------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Change X | Performance ↑ (+15%) | Cost ↑ (+8%) | Weight ↓ (-12%) |
| Change Y | Performance ↑ (+5%) | Cost ↓ (-3%) | Weight → (0%) |
| Change Z | Performance ↓ (-2%) | Cost ↓ (-10%) | Weight ↓ (-20%) |

## Tradeoff Properties

- Tradeoffs are explicitly quantified
- Tradeoffs include uncertainty
- Tradeoff decisions record constitutional rationale
- Tradeoff history is fully preserved
- Tradeoffs are fully replayable
