# Cost Optimization Engine

## Purpose

Reduce manufacturing and lifecycle costs without unacceptable impact on other objectives.

## Cost Optimization Targets

| Target | Approach |
|--------|----------|
| Material Cost | Substitute materials, reduce waste |
| Labor Cost | Automate, simplify assembly |
| Manufacturing Cost | Simplify processes, reduce operations |
| Supply Chain Cost | Source locally, reduce logistics |
| Quality Cost | Reduce defects, reduce rework |
| Maintenance Cost | Improve reliability, simplify access |
| Energy Cost | Reduce energy consumption |
| End-of-Life Cost | Design for recyclability |

## Optimization Properties

- Cost reductions are quantified with confidence intervals
- Cost tradeoffs against performance and quality are documented
- Cost history is fully preserved
- Cost optimization is fully replayable
