# Bias Detection Engine

## Purpose

Detect systematic biases in scientific artifacts: confirmation bias, selection bias, measurement bias, publication bias, optimization bias, reasoning bias, and systemic bias. Bias reports become constitutional artifacts.

## Bias Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Confirmation Bias | Evidence selected to support preferred conclusion |
| Selection Bias | Sample not representative of population |
| Measurement Bias | Measurement system systematically off |
| Publication Bias | Only significant results reported |
| Optimization Bias | Model overfit to training data |
| Reasoning Bias | Logical fallacies in argumentation |
| Systemic Bias | Structural biases from the research system |

## Detection Process

1. Analyze experimental design for selection and measurement bias
2. Review evidence selection for confirmation bias
3. Check for unreported negative results
4. Evaluate reasoning for logical fallacies
5. Assess whether systemic biases may apply
6. Produce bias detection report

## Constitutional Rules

- Bias detection is independent of artifact quality
- Identified biases are preserved permanently
- Bias does not automatically invalidate findings
- Bias reports are themselves reviewable
