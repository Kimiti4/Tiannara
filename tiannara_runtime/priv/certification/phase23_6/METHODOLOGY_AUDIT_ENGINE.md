# Methodology Audit Engine

## Purpose

Review the methodological quality of scientific artifacts: experimental design, controls, variables, measurement quality, sampling, instrumentation, repeatability, and method limitations.

## Audit Dimensions

| Dimension | Evaluation |
|-----------|------------|
| Experimental Design | Are controls appropriate? Is the design matched to the question? |
| Controls | Are positive and negative controls present? |
| Variables | Are variables properly defined and measured? |
| Measurement Quality | Are measurements accurate and precise? |
| Sampling | Is sampling representative and unbiased? |
| Instrumentation | Are instruments calibrated and appropriate? |
| Repeatability | Are methods described in sufficient detail? |
| Method Limitations | Are limitations honestly acknowledged? |

## Audit Process

1. Extract methodology description from artifact
2. Evaluate experimental design against domain best practices
3. Verify control adequacy
4. Assess measurement and instrumentation quality
5. Identify methodological limitations
6. Produce methodology audit report

## Constitutional Rules

- Methods are evaluated against constitutional standards, not field conventions
- Missing method details are flagged, not assumed
- Method novelty does not excuse methodological weakness
