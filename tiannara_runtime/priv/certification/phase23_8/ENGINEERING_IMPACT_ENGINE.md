# Engineering Impact Engine

## Purpose

Forecast engineering consequences of a discovery. Engineering pathways remain explicit.

## Forecast Areas

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| New Technologies | Technologies that become feasible or practical |
| New Manufacturing | Manufacturing processes enabled or improved |
| Automation | Systems that can be automated |
| Optimization | Performance improvements in existing systems |
| Materials | New materials or material improvements |
| Robotics | Robotic capabilities enhanced or enabled |
| Energy Systems | Energy generation, storage, distribution improvements |

## Engineering Pathway Properties

- Each pathway records enabling discoveries
- Pathways include estimated development timelines
- Technology readiness levels are tracked
- Pathway dependencies and prerequisites are recorded
- Alternative engineering approaches are maintained
