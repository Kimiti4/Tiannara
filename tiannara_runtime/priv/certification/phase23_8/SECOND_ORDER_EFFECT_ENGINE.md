# Second-Order Effect Engine

## Purpose

Predict second-order effects — consequences of the direct effects of a discovery. These are effects that would not occur without the direct effects existing first.

## Examples

| Direct Effect | Potential Second-Order Effect |
|---------------|-------------------------------|
| New material discovered | Manufacturing processes adapted |
| Medical breakthrough | Healthcare infrastructure changes |
| Energy efficiency gain | Industry reorganization |
| AI capability advance | Labor market shifts |
| Agricultural yield increase | Land use changes |

## Properties

- Second-order effects are derived from the impact graph
- Each second-order effect records its parent effect
- Confidence is propagated from parent effects
- Causal chains are fully recorded
