# Societal Impact Engine

## Purpose

Architect the societal consequences of a discovery covering education, healthcare, equity, employment, governance, quality of life, and resilience.

## Forecast Areas

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Education | Changes in educational needs, methods, and access |
| Healthcare | Improvements in health outcomes, access, and quality |
| Equity | Distribution of benefits and risks across populations |
| Employment | Changes in labor markets, skills, and occupations |
| Governance | Changes in governance structures and processes |
| Quality of Life | Changes in wellbeing, longevity, and fulfillment |
| Resilience | Changes in societal resilience to shocks |

## Societal Impact Properties

- Distributional effects are explicitly modeled
- Disparity and inequality impacts are recorded
- Cultural and ethical dimensions are considered
- Long-term societal trajectory changes are identified
- All assumptions about societal behavior are recorded
