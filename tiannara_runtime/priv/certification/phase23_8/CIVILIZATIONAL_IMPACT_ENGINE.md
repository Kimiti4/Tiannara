# Civilizational Impact Engine

## Purpose

Architect long-term civilizational effects covering scientific advancement, engineering capability, automation, energy availability, knowledge growth, planetary sustainability, resilience, civilizational complexity, and Kardashev progress.

## Forecast Areas

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Scientific Advancement | Acceleration of fundamental knowledge |
| Engineering Capability | Ability to build and create |
| Automation | Self-directed systems and autonomous production |
| Energy Availability | Total energy accessible to civilization |
| Knowledge Growth | Rate of knowledge accumulation |
| Planetary Sustainability | Long-term sustainable coexistence |
| Resilience | Ability to withstand and recover from shocks |
| Civilizational Complexity | Sophistication of social, technological, and knowledge systems |
| Kardashev Progress | Movement toward higher energy utilization levels |

## Properties

- Timescales extend to centuries and millennia
- Uncertainty grows with time horizon
- Existential risk implications are flagged
- Civilizational trajectories are compared
- Assumptions about civilizational behavior are explicit
