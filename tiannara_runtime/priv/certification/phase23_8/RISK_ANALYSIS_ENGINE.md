# Risk Analysis Engine

## Purpose

Quantify constitutional risks associated with each scenario. Each risk is quantified independently without aggregation that could hide individual risk profiles.

## Risk Domains

| Domain | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Scientific | Risk of incorrect conclusions, retractions, reproducibility failures |
| Engineering | Risk of engineering failure, safety issues, unintended consequences |
| Medical | Risk to human health, side effects, ethical violations |
| Ecological | Risk to ecosystems, biodiversity, environmental damage |
| Economic | Risk of economic disruption, inequality, market failure |
| Governance | Risk to institutional integrity, policy failure, regulatory gaps |
| Infrastructure | Risk to critical infrastructure, cascading failures |
| Planetary | Risk to planetary systems, climate, resource depletion |
| Civilizational | Risk to civilization stability, longevity, existential threats |

## Risk Properties

- Each risk has a severity and likelihood (both with uncertainty)
- Risks are linked to specific causal chains
- Risk correlations are recorded
- Risk mitigation options are identified where possible
