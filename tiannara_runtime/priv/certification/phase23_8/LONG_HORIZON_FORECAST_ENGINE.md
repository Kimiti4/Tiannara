# Long-Horizon Forecast Engine

## Purpose

Support prediction across time horizons from 1 year to 1000 years while maintaining appropriate uncertainty growth.

## Time Horizons

| Horizon | Typical Resolution | Uncertainty Characteristics |
|---------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| 1 Year | Monthly/quarterly | Low uncertainty, near-term dynamics |
| 5 Years | Annual | Moderate uncertainty, trend-based |
| 10 Years | Annual | Significant uncertainty, scenario-dependent |
| 25 Years | Multi-annual | High uncertainty, structural changes possible |
| 50 Years | Decadal | Very high uncertainty, paradigm shifts possible |
| 100 Years | Decadal | Civilizational-scale changes |
| 500 Years | Century-level | Speculative, fundamental transformations |
| 1000 Years | Century-level | Deep uncertainty, civilizational evolution |

## Horizon Properties

- Uncertainty growth functions are defined for each horizon
- Confidence intervals widen with horizon
- Qualitative predictions dominate at longer horizons
- Branching scenarios increase with horizon
- Long-horizon predictions are explicitly distinguished from near-term
