# Economic Impact Engine

## Purpose

Forecast economic consequences of a discovery. Economic pathways remain explicit and quantifiable.

## Forecast Areas

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Productivity | Changes in productivity across sectors |
| Innovation | Rate and direction of innovation |
| Resource Efficiency | Improvements in resource utilization |
| Industrial Transformation | Changes in industrial structure |
| Knowledge Economy | Growth of knowledge-intensive sectors |
| Technology Diffusion | Rate and pattern of technology adoption |

## Economic Impact Properties

- Impacts are quantified in economic terms where possible
- Uncertainty is preserved in all estimates
- Distributional effects are considered
- Time horizons are explicit
- Economic models used are recorded
