# Impact Explanation Engine

## Purpose

Ensure every forecast explains itself. No opaque forecasts are permitted.

## Explanation Components

Every forecast includes:
- Why the prediction exists (triggering discovery)
- Supporting evidence for the prediction
- Complete causal chain from discovery to predicted outcome
- All assumptions made during prediction
- Uncertainty associated with the prediction
- Alternative futures that were considered

## Explanation Properties

- Explanations are human-readable
- Explanations are machine-readable for review
- Explanations link to underlying evidence
- Explanations record what was NOT predicted and why
- Explanations are preserved for archaeological reconstruction
