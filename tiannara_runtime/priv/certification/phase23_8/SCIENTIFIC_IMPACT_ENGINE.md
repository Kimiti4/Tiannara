# Scientific Impact Engine

## Purpose

Predict the scientific consequences of a discovery. How will it change the landscape of scientific knowledge?

## Forecast Areas

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Future Theories | New theories that become possible |
| Future Discoveries | Discoveries that are enabled |
| Cross-Domain Breakthroughs | Connections between previously separate domains |
| New Questions | Questions that arise from the discovery |
| Research Acceleration | How the discovery speeds up research |
| Knowledge Growth | Expansion of the scientific knowledge graph |

## Scientific Impact Properties

- Impact is linked to specific areas of the knowledge graph
- Impact estimates include confidence intervals
- Cross-domain connections are explicitly recorded
- Research acceleration is quantified where possible
- Scientific impact is distinguished from engineering or societal impact
