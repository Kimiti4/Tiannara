# Engineering Decision Engine

## Purpose

Record every engineering decision with complete context, alternatives, evidence, tradeoffs, risks, and expected outcomes. Decision lineage is permanent.

## Decision Record

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Context | What prompted the decision |
| Alternatives | What options were considered |
| Selected Option | What was chosen |
| Evidence | What evidence supports the decision |
| Tradeoffs | What was gained and sacrificed |
| Risks | What risks were identified |
| Expected Outcomes | What results are anticipated |
| Decision Maker | Who or what made the decision |
| Rationale | Complete explanation |

## Decision Properties

- Decisions are uniquely identified
- Decisions link to affected artifacts
- Decisions are versioned with change history
- Decision quality is continuously assessed
- Decision outcomes are tracked for validation
