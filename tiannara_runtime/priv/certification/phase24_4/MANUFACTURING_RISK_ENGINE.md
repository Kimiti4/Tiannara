# Manufacturing Risk Engine

## Purpose

Identify, assess, and mitigate risks across the manufacturing ecosystem.

## Risk Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Technical Risk | Process capability, quality, yield |
| Supply Risk | Material availability, supplier reliability |
| Operational Risk | Equipment failure, labor shortage |
| Quality Risk | Defects, non-conformances |
| Cost Risk | Budget overruns, material price volatility |
| Schedule Risk | Delays, bottlenecks, changeover time |
| Regulatory Risk | Compliance, certification changes |
| Environmental Risk | Emissions, waste, regulatory action |
| Geopolitical Risk | Trade policy, regional instability |

## Risk Properties

- Each risk has severity, likelihood, and detectability
- Risks are continuously updated
- Risk mitigation plans are tracked to closure
- Risk history is fully preserved
- Risk posture is observable
