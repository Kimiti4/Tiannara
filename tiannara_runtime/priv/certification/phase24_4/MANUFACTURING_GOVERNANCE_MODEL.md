# Manufacturing Governance Model

## Purpose

Govern all manufacturing activities under constitutional rules. No manufacturing activity bypasses governance.

## Governance Scope

| Domain | Governance |
|--------|------------|
| Process Selection | Must be justified and traceable to design |
| Supplier Selection | Must meet constitutional standards |
| Production Changes | Must be reviewed and approved |
| Quality Disposition | Non-conformances require documented disposition |
| Certification | Certification gates are constitutionally required |
| Sustainability | Must meet constitutional sustainability requirements |
| Safety | Manufacturing safety is constitutionally enforced |

## Governance Properties

- Governance is constitutional, not discretionary
- Governance rules are explicit and verifiable
- Governance violations are flagged and resolved
- Governance history is fully preserved
- Governance is fully replayable
