# Sustainability Engine

## Purpose

Model and optimize the environmental sustainability of manufacturing activities.

## Sustainability Dimensions

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Energy Consumption | Total energy used in manufacturing |
| Carbon Footprint | CO2 and equivalent emissions |
| Material Efficiency | Material utilization rate |
| Water Usage | Water consumed in production |
| Waste Generation | Solid, liquid, and gaseous waste |
| Chemical Usage | Hazardous material use and disposal |
| Land Use | Factory footprint and ecosystem impact |

## Sustainability Properties

- Sustainability is modeled across the full lifecycle
- Sustainability metrics are continuously tracked
- Sustainability improvements are constitutionally governed
- Sustainability data is immutable after collection
- Sustainability history is fully preserved
