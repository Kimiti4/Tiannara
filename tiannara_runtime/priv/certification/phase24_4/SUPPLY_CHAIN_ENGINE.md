# Supply Chain Engine

## Purpose

Model, evaluate, and optimize supply chains for manufacturing. Supply chains are governed as constitutional artifacts.

## Supply Chain Elements

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Raw Materials | Sources, availability, quality |
| Suppliers | Capabilities, certifications, reliability |
| Transportation | Modes, routes, costs, lead times |
| Warehousing | Storage locations, capacities |
| Inventory | Stock levels, safety stock |
| Dependencies | Supplier dependencies, single points of failure |
| Geopolitical Risk | Regional stability, trade policy |
| Resilience | Alternative sources, backup routes |

## Supply Chain Properties

- Supply chains are fully modeled as dependency graphs
- Supply chain risk is continuously assessed
- Supply chain alternatives are maintained
- Supply chain history is fully preserved
- Supply chains are fully replayable
