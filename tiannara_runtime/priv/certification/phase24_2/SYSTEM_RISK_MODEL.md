# System Risk Model

## Purpose

Architect continuous risk tracking across technical, schedule, integration, operational, manufacturing, resource, scientific, and constitutional dimensions.

## Risk Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Technical Risk | Risk that system fails to meet technical requirements |
| Schedule Risk | Risk that timeline is not met |
| Integration Risk | Risk that subsystems fail to integrate |
| Operational Risk | Risk of failure during operation |
| Manufacturing Risk | Risk in production and supply chain |
| Resource Risk | Risk of insufficient resources |
| Scientific Risk | Risk that scientific basis is incorrect |
| Constitutional Risk | Risk of constitutional violation |

## Risk Properties

- Each risk has severity, likelihood, and detectability
- Risks are continuously updated as new information arrives
- Risk mitigation plans are tracked to closure
- Risk history is fully preserved
- Risk posture is observable in the Observatory
- Risk decisions are constitutionally governed
