# Certification Visualization

## Purpose
Visualizes the certification status of all Tiannara engines and components — showing what is certified, what is pending, and what has failed certification.

## Visualization Capabilities

### Certification Overview
- Overall certification status (certified, partial, not certified)
- Certification by engine and component
- Certification by type (architecture, replay, data, etc.)
- Certification trend over time

### Certification Details
- Certificate contents per component
- Checks performed and results
- Certifying authority
- Expiration and renewal dates

### Certification History
- Certification timeline per component
- Certification events (initial, renewal, failure, revocation)
- Certification evolution
- Certification gaps and coverage

### Certification Failure Analysis
- Failed certifications with reasons
- Remediation actions taken
- Re-certification results
- Failure patterns

### Certification Comparison
- Certification status across engines
- Certification standard compliance
- Certification maturity comparison

### Certification Pipeline
- Pending certifications
- Certification in progress
- Scheduled certifications
- Certification resource allocation

## Data Sources
- All Phase 25 certification models
- Certificate registry
- Certification audit records
