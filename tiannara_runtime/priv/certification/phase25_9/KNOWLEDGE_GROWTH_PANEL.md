# Knowledge Growth Panel

## Purpose
Shows the growth of scientific knowledge over time — what we know, how our understanding evolves, and what accelerates or impedes progress.

## Displays

### Knowledge Base Growth
- Total knowledge entries over time
- Knowledge growth rate
- New knowledge by domain
- Knowledge quality metrics

### Knowledge Base Coverage
- Coverage by domain and subdomain
- Coverage gaps
- Coverage depth
- Coverage balance across domains

### Knowledge Quality
- Confidence distribution
- Evidence strength distribution
- Replication rate
- Contradiction rate

### Knowledge Evolution
- How understanding changed over time
- Theory evolution timelines
- Paradigm shift events
- Knowledge consolidation

### Knowledge Velocity
- New knowledge per period
- New knowledge by source (experiment, theory, simulation, external)
- Knowledge growth acceleration/deceleration

### Knowledge Frontier
- Current frontier of knowledge by domain
- Frontier advancement rate
- Frontier density (how much is being discovered)
- Frontier challenges

## Data Sources
- Knowledge base (all certified knowledge)
- CDCIE (Phase 25.8) — discovery records
- Phase 23 scientific intelligence
