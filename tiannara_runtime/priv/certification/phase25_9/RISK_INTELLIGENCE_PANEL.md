# Risk Intelligence Panel

## Purpose
Shows the current risk landscape — identified risks, probabilities, cascading effects, and resilience status.

## Displays

### Risk Overview
- Global risk index
- Top risks by probability and severity
- Risk distribution by domain
- Risk trend (improving, stable, worsening)

### Risk Map
- Geographic risk distribution
- Risk hotspots
- Regional risk profiles

### Cascading Risks
- Active risk propagation chains
- Cascade probability
- Downstream risk amplification
- Critical cascade nodes

### Resilience Status
- System resilience scores
- Resilience by domain and region
- Resilience trend
- Vulnerability hotspots

### Urgency Overview
- Critical risks requiring immediate attention
- High urgency risks
- Emerging risks (increasing probability)

### Risk Mitigation
- Intervention impact on risk
- Risk reduction achieved
- Remaining residual risk
- Mitigation effectiveness

## Data Sources
- CPRIE-25.4 (risk intelligence)
