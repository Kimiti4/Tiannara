# Resource Panel

## Purpose
Shows planetary resource status — inventory, availability, allocation, sustainability, and strategic reserves.

## Displays

### Global Resource Inventory
- Resource by category
- Total stock, production, consumption
- Net flow (production - consumption)
- Time to depletion (non-renewables)

### Resource Flows
- Energy flow diagram (generation → distribution → consumption)
- Material flow diagram (extraction → processing → manufacturing → use → waste)
- Water balance (supply → demand → return)
- Food system (production → distribution → consumption)

### Critical Shortages
- Resources below safety threshold
- Geographic shortage hotspots
- Projected shortage timeline
- Shortage impact assessment

### Allocation Efficiency
- Allocation vs. demand by sector
- Utilization rates
- Waste and inefficiency
- Equity of distribution

### Sustainability Index
- Renewable vs. non-renewable consumption
- Regeneration vs. depletion rates
- Environmental impact
- Long-term trend

### Strategic Reserves
- Reserve levels by resource
- Reserve adequacy (days/weeks/months)
- Reserve drawdown history
- Replenishment plans

## Data Sources
- CPRIE-25.6 (resource intelligence)
