# Theory Ecology Panel

## Purpose
Shows the ecosystem of competing theories — how theories compete, which are winning, and what evidence drives theory selection.

## Displays

### Theory Landscape
- Active theories by domain
- Theory status (proposed, tested, supported, refuted, consensus)
- Theory relationships (compatible, competing, hierarchical)
- Theory confidence levels

### Theory Competition
- Competing theories for same phenomena
- Evidence for each theory
- Prediction comparison
- Falsification status

### Theory Evolution
- How theories changed over time
- Theory splitting and merging
- Theory refinement history
- Paradigm shift events

### Theory Confidence
- Confidence distribution across theories
- Confidence trend
- Evidence strength supporting each theory
- Remaining uncertainty

### Theory Predictions
- Predictions made by each theory
- Prediction outcomes (confirmed, refuted, untested)
- Novel prediction rate
- Prediction accuracy by theory

### Theory Consensus
- Areas of scientific consensus
- Areas of active disagreement
- Consensus evolution over time
- Minority viewpoints

## Data Sources
- Knowledge base — theory records
- Scientific evolution engine (Phase 25.7)
- Phase 23 scientific intelligence
