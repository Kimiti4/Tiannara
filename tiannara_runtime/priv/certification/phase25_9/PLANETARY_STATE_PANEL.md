# Planetary State Panel

## Purpose
Shows the current state of the planet — health of Earth's systems as measured and modeled by the planetary state engine.

## Displays

### Planetary Health
- Composite planetary health index
- Trend (improving, stable, declining)
- Planetary boundaries status
- Safe operating space indicators

### Climate
- Global temperature anomaly
- CO2 concentration
- Emission rates
- Warming trajectory
- Tipping point proximity

### Water
- Freshwater availability by region
- Water stress index
- Groundwater depletion
- Drought and flood indicators

### Food
- Global food production
- Caloric adequacy by region
- Nutritional adequacy
- Food reserve levels
- Food price trends

### Energy
- Energy mix (renewable vs. fossil)
- Total primary energy supply
- Energy access by region
- Energy intensity of economy
- Carbon intensity of energy

### Biodiversity
- Species extinction rate
- Habitat loss
- Ecosystem health indices
- Protected area coverage

### Population
- Population trends
- Urbanization rate
- Demographic distribution
- Migration patterns

### Economy
- Global GDP
- Economic inequality
- Employment
- Investment in science and engineering

## Data Sources
- PSE (Phase 25.1) — planetary state
- CGON (Phase 25.2) — observations
- CPDT (Phase 25.3) — digital twin state
