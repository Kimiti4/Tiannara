# Archaeology Visualization

## Purpose
Visualizes the archaeological record — complete historical reconstruction of knowledge, theories, engineering, and civilization evolution.

## Visualization Capabilities

### Knowledge Evolution
- Knowledge base state at any past time
- Knowledge growth visualization
- Knowledge lineage (tracing ideas to origins)
- Knowledge correction history

### Theory Evolution
- Theory state at any past time
- Theory branching, merging, and replacement
- Theory lineage (tracing ideas to origins)
- Paradigm shift visualization

### Engineering Evolution
- Engineering state at any past time
- Project and program evolution
- Capability growth visualization
- Infrastructure timeline

### Civilization Evolution
- Civilizational state at any past time
- Trajectory reconstruction
- Key decision points visualization
- Alternative pathways (what-if analysis)

### Causal Analysis
- Why did something happen?
- What evidence drove decisions?
- What alternatives were considered?
- What would have changed with different decisions?

### Historical Reconstruction
- Full state reconstruction at any time
- Context and conditions at that time
- Decision rationale
- Available evidence at that time

## Navigation
- Time-based browsing
- Event-based browsing
- Domain-based browsing
- Search and query

## Data Sources
- All Phase 25 archaeology models
- Historical records and snapshots
- Decision records and rationale
