# Engineering Panel

## Purpose
Shows the state of engineering programs, technology readiness, and infrastructure development.

## Displays

### Active Programs
- Active engineering programs by domain
- Program status and progress
- Milestone completion
- Program health indicators

### Technology Readiness
- TRL for key technologies
- TRL advancement over time
- Technologies nearing deployment
- Technologies stalled at low TRL

### Manufacturing Readiness
- Manufacturing capacity utilization
- Production rates
- Quality metrics
- Supply chain health

### Infrastructure Status
- Infrastructure system health (energy, water, transport, communication)
- Maintenance backlog
- Age and condition distribution
- Planned upgrades and replacements

### Engineering Throughput
- Engineering output rate
- Projects completed per period
- Engineering resource utilization
- Bottleneck identification

### Optimization Yield
- Efficiency gains from optimization
- Resource savings
- Performance improvements
- Cost reductions

## Data Sources
- Phase 24 engineering engines
- Phase 25.6 resource intelligence
- Phase 25.5 intervention engine (engineering interventions)
- Infrastructure evolution engine (Phase 25.7)
