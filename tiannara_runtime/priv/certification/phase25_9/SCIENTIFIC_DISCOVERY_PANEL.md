# Scientific Discovery Panel

## Purpose
Shows the state of scientific discovery across all domains — what we know, what we are discovering, and what remains unknown.

## Displays

### Discoveries Today/This Period
- New discoveries
- Discovery scores and value
- Most impactful discoveries
- Discovery velocity (discoveries per unit time)

### Active Hypotheses
- Active hypotheses by domain
- Hypothesis status (untested, in_testing, supported, refuted)
- Strongest and weakest hypotheses
- Hypothesis competition (competing explanations)

### Validated Principles
- Scientifically validated principles
- Confidence levels
- Evidence strength
- Replication status

### Failed Hypotheses
- Refuted hypotheses and theories
- What was learned from failure
- Remaining open questions

### Unknown Density
- Knowledge gap density by domain
- Gap closure progress
- New gaps discovered
- Gap priority ranking

### Discovery Pipeline
- Challenges flowing through the discovery pipeline
- Bottlenecks and delays
- Resource allocation for discovery
- Predicted discoveries

## Data Sources
- CDCIE (Phase 25.8) — challenge registry and discovery records
- Knowledge base — validated and refuted knowledge
- Scientific evolution engine (Phase 25.7)
