# Civilization Panel

## Purpose
Shows the state and trajectory of civilization — resilience, capacity, sustainability, and long-term progression.

## Displays

### Resilience Index
- Composite civilizational resilience
- Resilience by dimension (robustness, redundancy, adaptability, recovery)
- Resilience trend
- Vulnerability hotspots

### Scientific Capacity
- Scientific output rate
- Research personnel and funding
- Scientific infrastructure status
- Discovery capacity index

### Engineering Capacity
- Engineering output rate
- Engineering personnel and investment
- Industrial capacity utilization
- Technology development pipeline

### Innovation Index
- Patent and publication rates
- Technology adoption rate
- R&D investment as fraction of economy
- Breakthrough frequency

### Sustainability
- Environmental sustainability index
- Resource sustainability index
- Social sustainability index
- Intergenerational equity index

### Kardashev Progression Indicators
- Total energy consumption
- Energy consumption per capita
- Growth rate of energy consumption
- Projected Type I achievement timeline
- Space-based energy fraction

### Civilization Health
- Composite civilization health index
- Health by dimension (survival, stability, flourishing, transcendence)
- Health trend
- Key threats and opportunities

## Data Sources
- CMFCSE (Phase 25.7) — civilizational trajectory
- All other Phase 25 engines for component metrics
