# Knowledge Compression Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Knowledge Compression System (CKCS) continuously transforms growing knowledge into progressively higher levels of abstraction while preserving information, provenance, replayability, and constitutional integrity.

Knowledge accumulation alone is insufficient.

Scientific intelligence depends upon discovering increasingly general principles.

## Constitutional Principle

Knowledge shall continuously evolve toward simplicity without sacrificing truth.

Compression shall preserve:

- evidence
- causality
- provenance
- uncertainty
- constitutional lineage

Compression shall never invent information.

It may only discover more general representations.

## Constitutional Compression Hierarchy

```
Observations
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Information
  ↓
Knowledge
  ↓
Patterns
  ↓
Models
  ↓
Principles
  ↓
Scientific Laws
  ↓
Engineering Insights
  ↓
Unified Understanding
```

Every transformation preserves lineage.

## Architecture Components

### Multi-Level Abstraction Model
- Formal abstraction hierarchy from observations to unified understanding
- Level-specific compression rules and fidelity requirements
- Cross-level transformation protocols

### Principle Extraction Engine
- Extraction of reusable principles from repeated evidence and shared causal structures
- Common engineering solution pattern recognition
- Cross-domain principle identification

### Law Discovery Model
- General law induction from validated principles
- Mathematical formulation of discovered laws
- Boundary condition identification

### Redundancy Elimination Engine
- Duplicate detection and merging
- Equivalent concept consolidation
- Hierarchical simplification
- Obsolete knowledge retirement

### Knowledge Generalization Model
- Broader applicability discovery
- Cross-context validation
- Generalization confidence assessment

### Cross-Domain Abstraction Engine
- Abstraction discovery linking multiple domains
- Cross-domain principle identification
- Unified understanding construction

### Compression Validation Model
- Information, evidence, provenance preservation verification
- Mathematical and scientific consistency validation
- Engineering utility assessment

## Integration Points

- **Input**: All knowledge artifacts from all phases
- **Output**: Compressed knowledge at all abstraction levels
- **Foundation**: Mathematical substrate and constitutional guarantees

## Acceptance Criteria

- Multi-level knowledge compression is constitutionally specified
- Principle extraction preserves evidence
- Scientific laws preserve provenance
- Compression validation is deterministic
- Replay reconstructs all compression stages
- Archaeology reconstructs complete abstraction history
- Human constitutional governance remains authoritative
- No runtime implementation exists
