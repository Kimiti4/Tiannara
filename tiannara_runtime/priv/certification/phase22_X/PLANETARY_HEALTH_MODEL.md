# Planetary Health Model

## Purpose

Define the planetary health assessment model — evaluating climate stability, biodiversity, water security, energy security, food security, infrastructure resilience, scientific capacity, and civilizational stability.

## Health Dimensions

### Climate Stability
- Global temperature deviation from baseline
- Climate system variability
- Extreme weather frequency and intensity
- Climate feedback loop status
- Tipping point proximity assessment

### Biodiversity
- Species richness and abundance
- Ecosystem integrity
- Habitat connectivity
- Extinction rate
- Biodiversity resilience index

### Water Security
- Freshwater availability per capita
- Water quality indicators
- Aquifer depletion rate
- Glacier and ice sheet status
- Water infrastructure resilience

### Energy Security
- Energy production vs consumption
- Renewable energy share
- Grid stability and redundancy
- Energy storage capacity
- Energy infrastructure resilience

### Food Security
- Food production vs consumption
- Agricultural resilience
- Soil health indicators
- Food distribution efficiency
- Strategic food reserves

### Infrastructure Resilience
- Critical infrastructure status
- Infrastructure age and maintenance
- Redundancy and failover capacity
- Disaster preparedness
- Infrastructure investment adequacy

### Scientific Capacity
- Research and development investment
- Scientific workforce
- Research infrastructure
- Knowledge production rate
- Scientific collaboration intensity

### Civilizational Stability
- Governance effectiveness
- Social cohesion indicators
- Conflict and cooperation metrics
- Economic stability
- Institutional health

## Health Assessment Process

1. **Indicator Collection**: Gather health indicators
2. **Baseline Comparison**: Compare against constitutional baselines
3. **Trend Analysis**: Assess improvement or degradation trends
4. **Threshold Evaluation**: Check against constitutional thresholds
5. **Risk Assessment**: Identify emerging risks
6. **Health Scoring**: Composite health score per dimension
7. **Reporting**: Health assessment as constitutional artifact

## Constraints

- Health assessment is evidence-based
- Health records become constitutional artifacts
- All assessments are replayable
