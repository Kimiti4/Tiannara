# Bottleneck Priority Engine

## Purpose

Identify civilization-scale scientific and engineering bottlenecks, analyze their impact on overall progress, and prioritize bottleneck-focused research and resource allocation.

## Bottleneck Types

### Knowledge Bottlenecks
- Missing fundamental understanding
- Unexplained phenomena blocking progress
- Incomplete theories limiting prediction
- Knowledge gaps preventing engineering

### Engineering Bottlenecks
- Missing engineering capabilities
- Infrastructure limitations
- Manufacturing constraints
- Technology readiness gaps

### Resource Bottlenecks
- Compute limitations
- Simulation capacity constraints
- Laboratory availability
- Expertise shortages

### Coordination Bottlenecks
- Cross-domain information gaps
- Interdisciplinary communication barriers
- Standards and interoperability issues
- Institutional coordination failures

## Bottleneck Analysis

1. **Identification**: Scan for limiting factors across all domains
2. **Impact Assessment**: Quantify bottleneck impact on scientific progress
3. **Causal Analysis**: Understand bottleneck causes
4. **Resolution Path**: Identify paths to resolve bottleneck
5. **Priority Determination**: Prioritize based on bottleneck severity
6. **Resource Recommendation**: Recommend resource allocation

## Bottleneck Structure

Each bottleneck analysis contains:

- **Bottleneck ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Bottleneck Type**: Knowledge, engineering, resource, coordination
- **Domains Affected**: Domains impacted by bottleneck
- **Severity**: Impact severity (0.0–1.0)
- **Urgency**: How urgently resolution is needed
- **Causes**: Underlying causes of bottleneck
- **Resolution Options**: Possible resolution approaches
- **Recommended Priority**: Priority level for resolution
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Bottleneck analysis is evidence-driven
- Bottleneck records become constitutional artifacts
- Bottleneck resolution is prioritized in resource allocation
