# Scientific Investment Model

## Purpose

Govern strategic scientific investments — resource allocations targeted at long-term discovery, engineering capability development, and civilizational benefit — with expected return tracking and risk quantification.

## Investment Components

### Investment ID
- Content-addressed identifier
- Unique across all investments

### Scientific Goal
- Clear statement of the scientific objective
- Expected discoveries
- Engineering capabilities targeted
- Civilizational benefit expected

### Allocated Resources
- Resources committed to the investment
- Timeframe for resource allocation
- Resource type breakdown

### Expected Return
- Knowledge gain estimate
- Engineering gain estimate
- Cross-domain benefit projection
- Future discovery multiplier
- Risk reduction quantification

### Risk Assessment
- Technical risk
- Scientific hypothesis risk
- Resource overrun risk
- Opportunity cost risk
- Overall risk score

### Confidence
- Confidence in expected return estimate
- Confidence in timeline
- Confidence in risk assessment

## Investment Types

### Short-Term Investment (1–5 years)
- Near-term discovery focus
- Quick engineering applications
- Low risk, moderate return

### Medium-Term Investment (5–25 years)
- Significant discovery potential
- Engineering capability development
- Moderate risk, high return

### Long-Term Investment (25–100+ years)
- Transformative discovery potential
- Foundational capability building
- Higher risk, potentially transformative return

## Investment Governance

- Investments require constitutional approval
- Investment performance is periodically reviewed
- Underperforming investments may be restructured
- Investment records are constitutional artifacts
