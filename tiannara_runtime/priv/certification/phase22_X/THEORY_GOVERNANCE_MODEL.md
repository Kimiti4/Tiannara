# Theory Governance Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional governance structure for the theory ecosystem — who decides theory lifecycle transitions, competition rules, and theory status changes.

## Governance Levels

### Constitutional Level
- Theory lifecycle stages are constitutionally defined
- Competition rules are constitutional artifacts
- Minority preservation is constitutionally guaranteed
- Premature convergence prevention is constitutionally mandated

### Scientific Governance Level
- Theory lifecycle transitions governed by scientific governance
- Competition evaluation conducted by scientific boards
- Evolution decisions governed by evidence assessment
- Specialization and merging governed by domain experts

### Human Governance Level
- Human authority overrides all theory lifecycle decisions
- Human oversight of theory retirement
- Human governance of theory status changes
- Democratic processes preserved for major theory transitions

## Governance Principles

### Evidence Sovereignty
- Evidence is the sole arbiter of theory survival
- No theory may be retired without overwhelming evidence
- No theory may dominate without continuous evidence support

### Diversity Preservation
- Minority theories are constitutionally protected
- Premature convergence is prevented
- Alternative explanations actively maintained

### Transparency
- All theory transitions are publicly documented
- Competition results are fully transparent
- Governance decisions are constitutional artifacts

### Accountability
- Every theory decision is attributed
- Decision rationale is preserved
- Appeals process is constitutionally defined

## Governance Processes

### Theory Transition Approval
1. Transition proposal with evidence
2. Scientific governance review
3. Impact assessment
4. Constitutional compliance check
5. Approval or rejection with rationale
6. Transition recording

### Competition Oversight
1. Competition design approval
2. Fairness verification
3. Result validation
4. Minority report preservation
5. Competition record as constitutional artifact

### Theory Status Appeal
1. Appeal filed with evidence
2. Governance review
3. Re-evaluation
4. Status upheld, modified, or overturned
5. Appeal record preserved

## Constraints

- Governance records are constitutional artifacts
- Human authority is always preserved
- All governance actions are replayable
