# Theory Competition Engine

## Purpose

Govern the competition among multiple scientific theories, ensuring fair, evidence-based evaluation while preserving minority hypotheses and preventing premature convergence.

## Competition Types

### Prediction Competition
- Theories compete on prediction accuracy
- Same prediction target, different predictions
- Quantitative accuracy comparison
- Prediction range comparison
- Calibration evaluation

### Experimental Competition
- Theories predict experimental outcomes
- Experiment designed to distinguish theories
- Results compared against predictions
- Falsification potential maximized

### Engineering Competition
- Theories compete on engineering utility
- Which theory enables better engineering?
- Which theory produces more reliable systems?
- Which theory leads to more innovation?

### Mathematical Competition
- Theories compete on mathematical consistency
- Internal consistency verification
- External consistency with established mathematics
- Computational tractability
- Elegance and parsimony

## Competition Rules

### Fairness
- All theories receive equal opportunity to compete
- Competition criteria are transparent
- Evidence is the sole arbiter
- No theory is excluded from competition

### Minority Preservation
- Losing theories are preserved
- Minority theories maintain constitutional status
- Competition losers are not eliminated
- Historical record of all competition outcomes

### Premature Convergence Prevention
- Competition continues even with dominant theory
- Alternative theories are actively maintained
- Periodic challenge windows for all theories
- Dominant theories face continuous re-evaluation

## Competition Structure

Each competition round contains:

- **Competition ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Competition Type**: Prediction, experimental, engineering, mathematical
- **Participating Theories**: Theories competing
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Criteria used for comparison
- **Results**: Competition outcomes
- **Winner**: Theory with strongest evidence
- **Minority Report**: Preserved losing positions
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Competition never eliminates losing theories immediately
- Competition records become constitutional artifacts
- All competitions are replayable
