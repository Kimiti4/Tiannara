# Program Health Engine

## Purpose

Assess the multi-dimensional health of research programs, providing early warning for underperforming programs and evidence-based intervention triggers.

## Health Dimensions

### Scientific Progress
- Milestone completion rate vs. plan
- Discovery rate (significant findings per unit time)
- Hypothesis validation ratio
- Knowledge accumulation trajectory

### Evidence Quality
- Reproducibility of findings
- Methodological rigor
- Data quality and completeness
- Statistical significance standards

### Experiment Success
- Experiment completion rate
- Successful validation ratio
- Failed experiment learning value
- Experiment cycle time

### Resource Efficiency
- Resource consumption vs. allocation
- Output per resource unit
- Budget adherence
- Timeline adherence

### Knowledge Growth
- New knowledge generated per period
- Principle and law extraction rate
- Cross-domain knowledge contributions
- Knowledge integration completeness

### Collaboration
- Cross-program engagement level
- External collaboration quality
- Knowledge sharing participation
- Team health and communication

### Engineering Translation
- Engineering relevance of findings
- Technology transfer rate
- Practical application development
- Engineering impact assessment

### Long-Term Value
- Anticipated future impact trajectory
- Foundational knowledge contribution
- Capability enablement potential
- Scientific legacy assessment

## Health Scoring

Each dimension receives a score (0.0–1.0) with confidence bounds.

Overall health is a weighted composite with uncertainty.

## Health States

- **Healthy**: All dimensions meeting targets
- **Warning**: One or more dimensions below threshold
- **Critical**: Multiple dimensions significantly below threshold
- **Failing**: Program not meeting core objectives

## Health-Based Interventions

- **Warning**: Increased monitoring, targeted improvement plan
- **Critical**: Resource reallocation consideration, restructuring evaluation
- **Failing**: Termination evaluation, knowledge preservation planning

## Constraints

- Health assessments are evidence-driven
- Health records become constitutional artifacts
- Health interventions preserve program lineage
