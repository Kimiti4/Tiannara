# Portfolio Rebalancing Engine

## Purpose

Govern the rebalancing of the research portfolio — increasing or decreasing priority, suspending or resuming programs, splitting or merging programs, and terminating programs — with complete lineage preservation for every change.

## Rebalancing Operations

### Increase Priority
- Evidence of higher-than-expected discovery yield
- New bottleneck identification
- Constitutional priority shift
- Strategic opportunity emergence

### Decrease Priority
- Lower-than-expected discovery yield
- Bottleneck resolution
- Supersession by better approach
- Resource reallocation need

### Suspend
- Temporary resource constraints
- Awaiting prerequisite results
- Methodology reassessment
- External factor changes

### Resume
- Prerequisite results available
- Resource availability restored
- Methodology improvements ready
- New evidence justifies continuation

### Split
- Program scope too large
- Diverging research directions
- Specialization opportunity
- Team size management

### Merge
- Overlapping program objectives
- Resource consolidation opportunity
- Interdisciplinary integration
- Synergy exploitation

### Terminate
- Scientific dead end confirmed
- Resource exhaustion
- Supersession by superior approach
- Chronic underperformance

## Rebalancing Governance

Each rebalancing operation requires:

- **Trigger**: Condition or event triggering rebalancing
- **Evidence**: Evidence supporting the rebalancing decision
- **Review**: Constitutional review of the decision
- **Approval**: Authorization for the change
- **Documentation**: Complete rebalancing record
- **Lineage Preservation**: All prior states preserved

## Rebalancing Structure

Each rebalancing record contains:

- **Rebalancing ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Operation Type**: Increase, decrease, suspend, resume, split, merge, terminate
- **Programs Affected**: Programs changed by this operation
- **Rationale**: Evidence and reasoning for rebalancing
- **Impact Assessment**: Expected impact of rebalancing
- **Approval**: Governance approval record
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Every rebalancing change preserves complete lineage
- Rebalancing records become constitutional artifacts
- All rebalancing requires constitutional governance
