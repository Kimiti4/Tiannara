# Theory Retirement Engine

## Purpose

Govern the retirement of scientific theories when they are no longer viable — ensuring that retired theories remain archaeologically accessible and their knowledge contributions preserved.

## Retirement Conditions

### Overwhelming Evidence Contradiction
- Evidence consistently contradicts the theory
- Predictive failures accumulate
- Replication failures dominate
- Anomalies cannot be resolved

### Superior Explanatory Model
- Another theory explains all evidence and more
- Superior predictive accuracy
- Broader explanatory scope
- Greater engineering utility

### Engineering Failure
- Theory repeatedly fails to enable reliable engineering
- Practical applications consistently fail
- Engineering predictions incorrect
- Alternative theory enables successful engineering

### Irrelevance
- Theory no longer addresses active research domains
- Phenomena explained by other theories
- No active research programs using the theory
- Historical significance only

## Retirement Process

1. **Retirement Assessment**: Evaluate against retirement conditions
2. **Impact Analysis**: Assess impact on dependent theories and programs
3. **Knowledge Preservation**: Catalog all knowledge contributions
4. **Archaeological Packaging**: Package theory for archaeological preservation
5. **Lineage Finalization**: Complete the evolutionary lineage record
6. **Constitutional Review**: Governance approval of retirement
7. **Retirement Recording**: Record with complete lineage

## Retirement Structure

Each retirement record contains:

- **Retirement ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Theory ID**: Theory being retired
- **Retirement Reasons**: Specific conditions met
- **Impact Analysis**: Impact on ecosystem
- **Knowledge Contributions**: Preserved knowledge
- **Archaeological Reference**: Reference to archaeology package
- **Successor Theory**: Any successor theory (if applicable)
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Historical theories remain archaeologically accessible
- Retirement records become constitutional artifacts
- All retirements are replayable
