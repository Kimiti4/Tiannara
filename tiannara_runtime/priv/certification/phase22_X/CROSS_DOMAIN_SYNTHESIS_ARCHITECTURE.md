# Cross-Domain Synthesis Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Cross-Domain Synthesis System (CCDSS) discovers, validates, and continuously expands scientific and engineering relationships that span multiple knowledge domains.

Current architectures reason well within domains.

This architecture enables Tiannara to discover principles that emerge only when multiple domains are considered simultaneously.

## Constitutional Principle

The greatest scientific discoveries frequently occur between disciplines rather than within them.

Cross-domain synthesis shall therefore preserve:

- evidence
- causality
- mathematical consistency
- provenance
- replayability
- archaeological lineage

No synthesized relationship may exist without supporting evidence.

## Architecture Components

### Domain Relationship Model
- Formal domain representation with boundaries
- Cross-domain relationship types and strength
- Relationship discovery and maintenance

### Synthesis Engine
- Shared pattern discovery across domains
- Cross-domain analogy identification
- Principle unification from multiple domains
- Engineering and scientific transfer

### Interdisciplinary Discovery Model
- Systematic cross-domain exploration
- Novel relationship prediction
- Emergent principle detection

### Principle Unification Engine
- Combining principles from multiple domains
- Unified principle formulation
- Unification scope and confidence

### Multi-Domain Validation Model
- Cross-domain evidence collection
- Reproducibility across domains
- Unified validation framework

### Knowledge Transfer Engine
- Origin to target domain transfer
- Adaptation requirements
- Validation evidence and expected benefit

### Synthesis Certification Model
- Correctness, utility, consistency, evidence, reproducibility, compliance verification
- Deterministic certification process

## Integration Points

- **Input**: All domain knowledge from Phase 22.X.2 and prior phases
- **Output**: Cross-domain relationships, unified principles, validated syntheses
- **Foundation**: CSOS v1.0 constitutional guarantees

## Acceptance Criteria

- Cross-domain synthesis is constitutionally specified
- Relationships preserve evidence
- Knowledge transfer preserves provenance
- Replay reconstructs complete synthesis
- Archaeology reconstructs synthesis history
- Human constitutional governance remains authoritative
- No runtime implementation exists
