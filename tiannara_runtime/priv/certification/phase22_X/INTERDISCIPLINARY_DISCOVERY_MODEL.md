# Interdisciplinary Discovery Model

## Purpose

Systematically explore the space of possible cross-domain relationships, predict novel connections, and detect emergent principles that arise from domain intersections.

## Discovery Strategies

### Systematic Cross-Domain Exploration
- Pairwise domain comparison across all domains
- Structural similarity computation
- Causal flow analysis between domains
- Methodology compatibility assessment

### Novel Relationship Prediction
- Extrapolate from known cross-domain patterns
- Predict likely undiscovered relationships
- Prioritize predictions by expected value
- Design targeted validation experiments

### Emergent Principle Detection
- Monitor domain intersections for novel patterns
- Detect principles not derivable from single domains
- Identify synthesis-induced understanding improvements
- Track cross-domain discovery trajectories

### Gap Analysis
- Identify missing cross-domain connections
- Assess impact of missing connections
- Prioritize gap closure efforts
- Track gap closure progress

## Discovery Process

1. **Exploration Planning**: Design discovery strategy
2. **Domain Scanning**: Systematic scan across domains
3. **Pattern Detection**: Identify potential relationships
4. **Hypothesis Formation**: Formulate cross-domain hypotheses
5. **Priority Assessment**: Rank by expected value
6. **Investigation**: Deep investigation of highest-value candidates
7. **Recording**: Record discovery with lineage
8. **Integration**: Add discovered relationship to knowledge base

## Discovery Structure

Each interdisciplinary discovery contains:

- **Discovery ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Discovery Strategy**: Strategy that produced the discovery
- **Domains Involved**: Domains connected by the discovery
- **Relationship Type**: Type of cross-domain relationship
- **Evidence**: Supporting evidence
- **Novelty Assessment**: How novel is the discovery
- **Expected Impact**: Anticipated scientific/engineering impact
- **Status**: Hypothesis, under investigation, validated, integrated
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Discoveries require evidence from all involved domains
- Discovery records become constitutional artifacts
- Novelty is assessed relative to existing cross-domain knowledge
