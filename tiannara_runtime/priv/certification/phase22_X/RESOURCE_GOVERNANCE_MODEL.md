# Resource Governance Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional governance structure for resource allocation decisions — ensuring transparency, fairness, accountability, and human authority over all resource distribution.

## Governance Levels

### Constitutional Level
- Resource allocation principles are constitutionally defined
- Constitutional minimum allocations per domain
- Reserve capacity requirements are constitutional
- Emergency redistribution protocols are constitutional

### Strategic Governance Level
- Strategic investment decisions
- Long-term resource planning
- Cross-domain resource balancing
- Major reallocation decisions

### Operational Governance Level
- Routine allocation decisions
- Resource utilization monitoring
- Efficiency improvements
- Short-term rebalancing

### Human Governance Level
- Human authority overrides all resource decisions
- Human approval of strategic investments
- Human oversight of emergency redistribution
- Democratic processes for major allocation changes

## Governance Principles

### Transparency
All resource allocation decisions, including rationale and evidence, are publicly accessible constitutional artifacts.

### Accountability
Every resource decision is attributed to a governance authority with clear responsibility.

### Fairness
Resource distributions across domains are monitored for fairness and constitutional compliance.

### Efficiency
Resource utilization efficiency is monitored and reported.

### Sustainability
Resource allocation considers long-term sustainability across generations.

## Governance Processes

### Allocation Approval
1. Allocation proposal with rationale and evidence
2. Constitutional compliance review
3. Fairness assessment
4. Approval or rejection with rationale
5. Allocation recorded as constitutional artifact

### Allocation Appeal
1. Appeal filed with evidence of unfairness or inefficiency
2. Governance review of allocation
3. Re-evaluation
4. Allocation upheld, modified, or overturned
5. Appeal record preserved

### Resource Audit
1. Periodic audit of resource utilization
2. Efficiency and fairness assessment
3. Audit report as constitutional artifact
4. Recommendations for improvement

## Constraints

- Governance records are constitutional artifacts
- Human authority is always preserved
- All governance actions are replayable
