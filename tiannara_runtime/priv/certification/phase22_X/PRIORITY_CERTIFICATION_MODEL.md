# Priority Certification Model

## Purpose

Certify that research prioritization decisions are constitutionally valid, transparent, evidence-based, and aligned with constitutional values.

## Certification Criteria

### Transparency
- Priority factors are fully documented
- Weight assignments are public
- Decision process is auditable
- Rationale is available for every decision

### Evidence-Based
- Every priority decision references supporting evidence
- Evidence quality is assessed
- Evidence provenance is preserved
- Uncertainty is quantified

### Constitutional Alignment
- Constitutional principles are respected
- Human authority is preserved
- Fairness across domains is maintained
- Sustainability considerations are included

### Reproducibility
- Priority calculations are deterministic
- Same inputs produce same priorities
- External auditors can verify decisions
- Replay confirms consistency

## Certification Process

1. **Submission**: Priority decision submitted for certification
2. **Evidence Review**: Evidence package reviewed against criteria
3. **Transparency Check**: Decision process documented and verified
4. **Constitutional Review**: Constitutional compliance verified
5. **Reproducibility Test**: Priority calculation reproduced
6. **Certification Decision**: Pass, pass with conditions, fail
7. **Recording**: Certification recorded with lineage

## Certification Outcomes

- **Certified**: Full certification
- **Certified with Conditions**: Certified with required follow-up actions
- **Failed**: Certification denied, priority decision must be revised

## Constraints

- Certification is deterministic and evidence-based
- Certification records become constitutional artifacts
- Certifications are themselves replayable
