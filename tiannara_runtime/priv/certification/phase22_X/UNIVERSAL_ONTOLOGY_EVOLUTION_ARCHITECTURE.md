# Universal Ontology Evolution Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Universal Ontology Evolution System (CUOES) governs the continuous evolution of Tiannara's universal ontology while preserving constitutional consistency, scientific correctness, mathematical rigor, replayability, provenance, and archaeological lineage.

Unlike knowledge growth, ontology evolution governs the structure of knowledge itself. The ontology becomes a living constitutional representation of reality.

## Constitutional Principle

Ontologies shall evolve through evidence rather than manual redesign. Ontology evolution shall preserve semantic consistency, mathematical consistency, causal consistency, provenance, replayability, and archaeology. Ontology evolution shall never invalidate previously certified knowledge.

## Architecture Components

### Ontology Hierarchy Model
- Reality → Scientific Domains → Subdomains → Concept Families → Concepts → Properties → Relationships → Evidence → Observations
- Every level preserves deterministic lineage

### Concept Evolution Engine
- Creation, refinement, expansion, deprecation of concepts
- Hierarchy reorganization
- Relationship discovery

### Concept Merging Engine
- Merge when definitions, evidence, mathematics converge
- Engineering usage overlaps
- Preserve ancestral lineage

### Concept Splitting Engine
- Split when multiple meanings emerge, domains diverge
- Evidence or engineering usage separates
- Each descendant preserves parent lineage

### Semantic Validation Engine
- Definition, hierarchy, relationship consistency
- Mathematical, cross-domain, backward compatibility
- Constitutional compliance

### Ontology Versioning Model
- Versioned ontology snapshots
- Change tracking across versions
- Backward compatibility preservation

### Ontology Governance Model
- Constitutional oversight of ontology changes
- Human authority preserved for structural changes

- Universal ontology evolution is constitutionally specified
- Concept evolution preserves lineage

- Replay reconstructs complete ontology evolution
- Archaeology reconstructs semantic history
- Human constitutional governance remains authoritative
