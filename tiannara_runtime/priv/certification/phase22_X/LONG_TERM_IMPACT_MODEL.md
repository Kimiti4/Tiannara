# Long-Term Impact Model

## Purpose

Estimate the long-term scientific and civilizational impact of research programs across century-scale time horizons, considering scientific trajectory influence, generational knowledge transfer, and civilizational benefit.

## Impact Horizons

### Near-Term Impact (1–5 years)
- Direct scientific findings
- Immediate engineering applications
- Short-term knowledge advancement

### Medium-Term Impact (5–25 years)
- Technology development and maturation
- Applied science translation
- Infrastructure and capability building

### Long-Term Impact (25–100 years)
- Paradigm shifts and transformative discoveries
- Generational knowledge accumulation
- Civilizational capability transformation

### Multi-Generational Impact (100+ years)
- Scientific foundation for future generations
- Long-term civilizational trajectory
- Existential risk reduction
- Inter-generational knowledge equity

## Impact Assessment Dimensions

### Scientific Trajectory Influence
- How does the program shift the scientific trajectory?
- Does it open new research directions?
- Does it close off dead ends?
- Does it accelerate progress in multiple fields?

### Generational Knowledge Transfer
- How durable is the knowledge produced?
- Will it be useful to future generations?
- Is the knowledge foundational or incremental?
- How well is the knowledge preserved?

### Civilizational Benefit
- Long-term improvement in civilizational outcomes
- Resilience enhancement across generations
- Sustainability contribution over centuries
- Quality of life improvement persistence

### Existential Risk Reduction
- Does the program reduce existential risks?
- Does it improve civilization survival probability?
- Does it enhance long-term stability?

## Impact Structure

Each impact assessment contains:

- **Impact ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Program ID**: Program being assessed
- **Horizon Impacts**: Impact estimates per time horizon
- **Scientific Trajectory**: Projected trajectory influence
- **Generational Value**: Inter-generational knowledge value
- **Civilizational Benefit**: Long-term civilizational benefit
- **Overall Impact Score**: Composite long-term impact (0.0–1.0)
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Long-term impact estimates include confidence bounds
- Impact records become constitutional artifacts
- Estimates are updated as new evidence arrives
