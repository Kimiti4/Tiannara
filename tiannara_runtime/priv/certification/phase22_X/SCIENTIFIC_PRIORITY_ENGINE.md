# Scientific Priority Engine

## Purpose

Evaluate and prioritize research programs based on multi-factor analysis including scientific importance, engineering leverage, cross-domain value, risk, feasibility, resource requirements, expected discovery yield, and constitutional alignment.

## Priority Factors

### Scientific Importance
- Fundamental knowledge advancement potential
- Paradigm shift potential
- Scientific community impact
- Long-term discovery enablement

### Engineering Leverage
- Engineering capability enablement
- Infrastructure improvement potential
- Technology development acceleration
- Practical application breadth

### Cross-Domain Value
- Number of domains benefiting
- Cross-domain discovery potential
- Interdisciplinary integration value
- Knowledge unification potential

### Risk
- Technical risk (feasibility uncertainty)
- Scientific risk (hypothesis failure probability)
- Resource risk (cost overrun probability)
- Timeline risk (schedule uncertainty)

### Feasibility
- Current scientific foundation adequacy
- Engineering capability readiness
- Resource availability
- Timeline reasonableness

### Resource Requirements
- Compute resources needed
- Human expertise needed
- Facility and equipment needs
- Time investment required

### Expected Discovery Yield
- Expected number of significant discoveries
- Expected knowledge growth rate
- Expected principle extraction potential
- Expected engineering insights

### Constitutional Alignment
- Constitutional compliance
- Human benefit alignment
- Ethical governance compatibility
- Sustainability impact

## Priority Calculation

```
Priority = w1 × ScientificImportance + w2 × EngineeringLeverage + w3 × CrossDomainValue + w4 × (1 - Risk) + w5 × Feasibility + w6 × (1 - ResourceIntensity) + w7 × DiscoveryYield + w8 × ConstitutionalAlignment
```

Weights are constitutionally governed configurable.

## Priority Re-evaluation

Priorities are re-evaluated when:
- New significant evidence arrives
- Program health changes significantly
- Resource availability changes
- Constitutional priorities are updated
- New programs are proposed

## Constraints

- Priority decisions are evidence-driven
- Priority records become constitutional artifacts
- Priority weights are constitutionally governed
