# Planetary Observation Model

## Purpose

Define the multi-layered planetary observation model — capturing observations from planetary physics through climate, atmosphere, hydrology, geology, ecology, agriculture, energy, transportation, economy, civilizations, and scientific infrastructure.

## Observation Layers

### Layer 1: Planetary Physics
- Orbital mechanics and dynamics
- Gravitational field measurements
- Magnetic field observations
- Seismic and tectonic activity
- Thermal radiation and energy balance

### Layer 2: Climate
- Global temperature measurements
- Precipitation patterns
- Atmospheric circulation
- Climate zone boundaries
- Long-term climate trends

### Layer 3: Atmosphere
- Atmospheric composition
- Greenhouse gas concentrations
- Aerosol and particulate measurements
- Ozone layer status
- Weather patterns and extremes

### Layer 4: Hydrology
- Ocean temperature and currents
- Sea level measurements
- Freshwater availability
- Ice sheet and glacier status
- Groundwater levels

### Layer 5: Geology
- Tectonic plate movements
- Volcanic activity
- Erosion and sedimentation
- Mineral resource distribution
- Geological hazard status

### Layer 6: Ecology
- Biodiversity measurements
- Ecosystem health indicators
- Species population trends
- Habitat coverage
- Ecological network integrity

### Layer 7: Agriculture
- Crop yields and productivity
- Soil health indicators
- Agricultural water usage
- Land use patterns
- Food production capacity

### Layer 8: Energy Infrastructure
- Energy production and consumption
- Grid stability and capacity
- Renewable energy deployment
- Energy storage status
- Energy distribution efficiency

### Layer 9: Transportation
- Transportation network status
- Logistics and supply chain health
- Infrastructure resilience
- Transportation emissions
- Mobility patterns

### Layer 10: Economy
- Economic output and productivity
- Resource consumption rates
- Trade and commerce flows
- Economic stability indicators
- Infrastructure investment

### Layer 11: Civilizations
- Population distribution
- Urbanization patterns
- Social stability indicators
- Governance effectiveness
- Cultural and scientific output

### Layer 12: Scientific Infrastructure
- Research capacity
- Scientific instrument network
- Data collection infrastructure
- Knowledge production rate
- Scientific collaboration patterns

## Observation Structure

Each observation contains:

- **Observation ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Layer**: Observation layer
- **Location**: Spatial reference
- **Time**: Temporal reference
- **Measured Variables**: Observed quantities
- **Sensor Sources**: Instrument references
- **Confidence**: Measurement confidence
- **Uncertainty**: Quantified uncertainty
- **Dependencies**: Prerequisite observations
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash
