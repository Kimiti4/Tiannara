# Principle Extraction Engine

## Purpose

Extract reusable scientific principles from repeated evidence, shared causal structures, common engineering solutions, mathematical similarity, cross-domain relationships, and validated scientific models.

## Principle Sources

### Repeated Evidence
- Observations consistently supporting the same conclusion
- Statistical regularities across experiments
- Reproducible phenomena
- Cross-validation across methodologies

### Shared Causal Structures
- Common causal patterns across different phenomena
- Isomorphic causal relationships
- Recurring interaction types
- Transferable causal mechanisms

### Common Engineering Solutions
- Design patterns solving similar problems
- Reusable engineering approaches
- Proven optimization strategies
- Standard engineering practices

### Mathematical Similarity
- Equations with analogous structure
- Shared mathematical frameworks
- Isomorphic formal systems
- Transferable mathematical methods

### Cross-Domain Relationships
- Principles operating in multiple domains
- Domain-independent generalizations
- Cross-domain causal patterns
- Universal regularities

### Validated Scientific Models
- Models with strong predictive accuracy
- Models with broad applicability
- Models that survived falsification attempts
- Models with mechanistic understanding

## Principle Structure

Each principle contains:

- **Principle ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Statement**: Concise formulation of the principle
- **Evidence Sources**: Evidence supporting the principle
- **Applicable Domains**: Domains where principle applies
- **Boundary Conditions**: When principle does not apply
- **Confidence**: Confidence in principle validity (0.0–1.0)
- **Mathematical Formulation**: Formal representation where applicable
- **Counterexamples**: Known exceptions or limitations
- **Provenance Chain**: Complete trace to source evidence

## Extraction Process

1. **Pattern Detection**: Identify recurring patterns in evidence
2. **Generalization Assessment**: Evaluate whether pattern is generalizable
3. **Domain Scope Determination**: Identify domains of applicability
4. **Boundary Identification**: Determine where principle breaks down
5. **Confidence Calculation**: Quantify evidential support
6. **Principle Formulation**: Express principle formally
7. **Validation**: Test against held-out evidence
8. **Integration**: Add to principle knowledge base

## Constraints

- Principles must be evidence-supported
- Principle records become constitutional artifacts
- Principle confidence must reflect evidence strength
