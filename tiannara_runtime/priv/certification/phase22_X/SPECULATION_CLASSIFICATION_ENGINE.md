# Speculation Classification Engine

## Purpose

Classify every generated scientific artifact — hypothesis, counterfactual, alternative physics model, engineering concept — into a deterministic, evidence-based speculation class that remains constitutionally separated from validated knowledge.

## Classification Levels

### Level 0: Validated
- Confirmed through multiple independent experiments
- Reproducible predictions
- Engineering utility demonstrated
- Constitutional certification complete

### Level 1: Highly Supported
- Strong experimental evidence
- Broad predictive success
- No significant contradictory evidence
- Pending final constitutional certification

### Level 2: Plausible
- Consistent with existing evidence
- No contradictions known
- Predictive power demonstrated in limited domain
- Further testing required

### Level 3: Speculative
- Some evidence or theoretical support
- Significant uncertainties remain
- Predictions not yet tested
- Requires substantial investigation

### Level 4: Highly Speculative
- Limited evidence
- Major assumptions unverified
- Predictions not clearly distinguishable
- High uncertainty

### Level 5: Counterfactual
- Known to be false in our universe
- Useful for boundary exploration
- Constitutional utility in what-if analysis
- Explicitly not intended as replacement

### Level 6: Impossible
- Contradicts established conservation laws
- Violates mathematical theorems
- Logically inconsistent
- Constitutionally classified as impossible

## Classification Criteria

### Evidence Strength
- Quantity and quality of supporting evidence
- Reproducibility of results
- Consistency across methods

### Predictive Success
- Accuracy of predictions
- Breadth of predictive range
- Novel prediction confirmation

### Mathematical Consistency
- Internal mathematical consistency
- Consistency with established mathematics
- Computational tractability

### Engineering Utility
- Demonstrated engineering application
- Reliability in practice
- Breadth of application

### Falsifiability
- Whether the claim can be empirically tested
- Whether clear falsification criteria exist
- Whether predictions distinguish from alternatives

## Classification Process

1. **Evidence Collection**: Gather all relevant evidence
2. **Criteria Evaluation**: Score against each criterion
3. **Classification Assignment**: Deterministic classification
4. **Constitutional Review**: Verification of classification
5. **Recording**: Classification documented with lineage

## Constraints

- Classification is fully deterministic
- Classification records become constitutional artifacts
- All classifications are replayable
- Classification may change as new evidence arrives
- Reclassification preserves lineage
