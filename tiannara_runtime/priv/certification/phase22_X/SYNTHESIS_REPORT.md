# Synthesis Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive cross-domain synthesis reports summarizing domain relationships, active syntheses, knowledge transfers, unified principles, validation status, and recommendations.

## Report Structure

### Executive Summary
- Cross-domain connectivity overview
- Major syntheses and unified principles
- Knowledge transfer impact
- Strategic recommendations

### Domain Relationship Map
- Domain connectivity matrix
- Relationship strength distribution
- Recently discovered relationships
- Missing relationship gaps

### Active Syntheses
- Synthesis operations in progress
- Synthesis outcomes (completed, pending, failed)
- Synthesis confidence distribution
- Cross-domain discovery status

### Knowledge Transfer Summary
- Active transfers by type
- Transfer success rate
- Adaptation requirements documentation
- Transfer impact assessment

### Unified Principles
- Unified principle inventory
- Unification type distribution (vertical, horizontal, network)
- Confidence distribution
- Recently certified unified principles

### Validation Status
- Syntheses by validation status
- Validation dimension scores
- Failed validations and causes
- Certification decisions

### Recommendations
- Priority synthesis opportunities
- High-value knowledge transfer targets
- Cross-domain discovery focus areas
- Validation methodology improvements

## Constraints

- Reports are comprehensive and evidence-driven
- Report records become constitutional artifacts
- Uncertainty is preserved in all assessments
