# Alternative Physics Model

## Purpose

Support the exploration of alternative physical models — alternative constants, symmetries, conservation laws, force interactions, and cosmological models — while maintaining explicit classification as speculative.

## Alternative Physics Dimensions

### Alternative Constants

- Modified gravitational constant
- Modified Planck constant
- Modified fine-structure constant
- Modified particle masses
- Modified coupling constants

### Alternative Symmetries

- Modified gauge symmetries
- Modified internal symmetries

### Alternative Force Interactions

- Unified force alternatives

### Alternative Cosmological Models
- Modified universe expansion
- Alternative big bang models
- Cyclic universe models
- Multiverse models
- Modified cosmic evolution

## Exploration Process

1. **Dimension Selection**: Choose which physics dimension to modify
2. **Modification Specification**: Specify the modification precisely
3. **Mathematical Formalization**: Formulate the alternative mathematically
4. **Consequence Derivation**: Derive physical consequences
5. **Observable Prediction**: Predict observable differences from reality
6. **Consistency Check**: Check internal mathematical consistency
7. **Classification**: Classify as speculative
8. **Recording**: Document with lineage

## Alternative Physics Structure

Each alternative physics model contains:

- **Model ID**: Content-addressed identifier
- **Dimension**: Physics dimension being modified
- **Standard Value**: Current accepted value
- **Alternative Value**: Proposed alternative
- **Mathematical Formulation**: Formal description
- **Derived Consequences**: Predicted consequences
- **Observable Predictions**: Empirically distinguishable predictions
- **Consistency Assessment**: Internal consistency evaluation
- **Motivation**: Why this alternative is interesting
- **Classification**: Speculative confidence
- **Fingerprint**: Deterministic content hash

## Constraints

- Every model remains explicitly speculative
- Models are constitutionally separated from validated physics
- All alternative models are replayable
