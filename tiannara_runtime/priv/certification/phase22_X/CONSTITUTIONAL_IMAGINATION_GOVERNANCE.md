# Constitutional Imagination Governance

## Purpose

Define the constitutional governance structure for the imagination system — ensuring that imagination never modifies validated knowledge, constitutional principles, scientific laws, or engineering standards without completing the constitutional validation pipeline.

## Governance Principles

### Epistemic Separation
- Imagination is advisory, validation is authoritative
- No speculative artifact automatically enters validated knowledge
- Classification is constitutionally binding
- Speculation is always distinguishable from fact

### Constitutional Protection
- Imagination shall never modify constitutional principles
- Imagination shall never modify validated scientific laws
- Imagination shall never modify engineering standards
- Without completing the validation pipeline

### Human Authority
- Human governance overrides all imagination operations
- Human approval required for knowledge migration
- Human oversight of imagination direction
- Democratic processes for imagination governance

## Governance Levels

### Constitutional Level
- Classification levels are constitutionally defined
- Validation pipeline requirements are constitutional
- Epistemic separation is constitutionally enforced
- Imagination governance principles are constitutional artifacts

### Scientific Governance Level
- Imagination direction governed by scientific priorities
- Hypothesis generation aligned with knowledge gaps
- Counterfactual exploration governed by scientific value
- Engineering innovation governed by capability needs

### Human Governance Level
- Human authority overrides any imagination operation
- Human approval for classification changes
- Human oversight of knowledge migration
- Democratic processes for imagination priorities

## Prohibited Operations

The imagination subsystem shall never:
- Modify validated knowledge without validation pipeline
- Modify constitutional principles under any circumstances
- Modify certified scientific laws without constitutional process
- Modify engineering standards without validation
- Present speculation as fact
- Bypass classification requirements

## Governance Processes

### Imagination Direction Approval
1. Direction proposal with scientific rationale
2. Constitutional compliance review
3. Resource allocation assessment
4. Approval or rejection with rationale
5. Direction recorded as constitutional artifact

### Classification Change Approval
1. New evidence supporting reclassification
2. Evidence quality assessment
3. Constitutional review
4. Approval or rejection
5. Reclassification recorded with lineage

### Knowledge Migration Approval
1. Validation evidence package submission
2. Evidence quality assessment
3. Constitutional compliance review
4. Epistemic integrity verification
5. Approval or rejection
6. Migration recorded with lineage

## Constraints

- Governance records are constitutional artifacts
- Human authority is always preserved
- All governance actions are replayable
