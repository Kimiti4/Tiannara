# Priority Evaluation Workflow

## Purpose

Define the step-by-step workflow for evaluating research programs, experiments, and scientific questions through the adaptive scientific prioritization system.

## Workflow Stages

### 1. Submission
- Research candidate submitted with description, objectives, methodology, expected outcomes, resource requirements, and dependencies
- Candidate recorded with content-addressed identifier
- Baseline metadata captured

### 2. Initial Assessment
- **Scientific Importance**: Evaluate fundamental knowledge advancement potential
- **Engineering Leverage**: Assess engineering capability enablement
- **Knowledge Gap**: Determine gap magnitude and criticality
- **Domain Alignment**: Identify primary and cross-domains affected

### 3. Discovery Leverage Analysis
- **Downstream Discovery**: Estimate downstream discovery enablement
- **Engineering Unlock**: Quantify capability unlock potential
- **Cross-Domain Benefit**: Identify cross-domain benefits
- **Uncertainty Reduction**: Assess uncertainty reduction contribution

### 4. Resource Requirement Analysis
- **Compute Requirement**: Estimate compute needs
- **Simulation Requirement**: Estimate simulation needs
- **Engineering Requirement**: Estimate engineering needs
- **Laboratory Requirement**: Estimate lab and experimental needs
- **Personnel Requirement**: Estimate human resources needed

### 5. Bottleneck Analysis
- **Bottleneck Relevance**: Does the candidate address a known bottleneck?
- **Bottleneck Impact**: How much does resolution help?
- **Alternatives Assessment**: Are there alternative approaches?

### 6. Long-Term Impact Assessment
- **Near-Term Impact**: 1–5 year projection
- **Medium-Term Impact**: 5–25 year projection
- **Long-Term Impact**: 25–100 year projection
- **Generational Impact**: 100+ year projection

### 7. Priority Calculation
- **Factor Scoring**: Score all priority factors
- **Weight Application**: Apply constitutional weights
- **Composite Priority**: Calculate composite priority score
- **Confidence Bounds**: Quantify uncertainty

### 8. Constitutional Review
- **Compliance Check**: Verify constitutional alignment
- **Ethical Governance**: Check ethical requirements
- **Human Authority**: Confirm human governance preserved
- **Fairness Assessment**: Verify cross-domain fairness

### 9. Resource Allocation
- **Availability Check**: Verify resources available
- **Marginal Return**: Calculate marginal discovery return
- **Allocation Decision**: Allocate resources if priority justifies
- **Conflict Resolution**: Resolve competing allocations

### 10. Certification
- **Evidence Package**: Compile decision evidence
- **Transparency Report**: Document decision rationale
- **Certification Request**: Submit for certification
- **Certification Result**: Record certification outcome

## Workflow Constraints

- Every stage produces a deterministic record
- Records become constitutional artifacts
- Workflow is replayable end-to-end
- Human governance may override at any stage
- Override records preserve complete lineage
