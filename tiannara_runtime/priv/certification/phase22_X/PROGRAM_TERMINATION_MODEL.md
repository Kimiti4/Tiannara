# Program Termination Model

## Purpose

Govern the termination of research programs with constitutional oversight, ensuring knowledge preservation, lineage continuity, and archaeological recovery.

## Termination Criteria

### Scientific Dead End
- Hypothesis definitively falsified
- Evidence consistently contradicts program assumptions
- Scientific approach proven infeasible

### Resource Exhaustion
- Required resources exceed civilization capacity
- Resource requirements unsustainable
- More efficient alternatives exist

### Supersession
- More promising approach discovered
- Superior program in same domain
- Technology makes program approach obsolete

### Priority Shift
- Constitutional priorities change
- Higher-priority programs require resources
- Strategic reorientation

### Chronic Underperformance
- Persistent health below critical threshold
- Repeated failure to meet milestones
- Inability to produce reproducible results

## Termination Process

1. **Termination Evaluation**: Assess against termination criteria
2. **Evidence Collection**: Gather evidence supporting termination
3. **Review**: Constitutional review of termination decision
4. **Knowledge Preservation**: Extract all knowledge from program
5. **Principle Extraction**: Derive principles from program findings
6. **Lineage Documentation**: Complete program lineage record
7. **Resource Release**: Release resources for reallocation
8. **Archival**: Archive complete program record
9. **Termination Certification**: Final certification of termination

## Knowledge Preservation on Termination

All program knowledge is preserved:
- Raw data and observations
- Processed results and findings
- Methodologies and protocols
- Failed approaches and lessons learned
- Principle and law extractions
- Complete provenance chain

## Termination Archaeology

Termination records answer:
- Why was the program terminated?
- Which criteria triggered termination?
- What evidence supported the decision?
- What knowledge was preserved?
- What principles were extracted?

## Constraints

- Termination requires constitutional review
- Termination records become constitutional artifacts
- No knowledge is discarded on termination
