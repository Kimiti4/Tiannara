# Theory Ecology Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Theory Ecology System (CTES) governs scientific theories as evolving constitutional organisms within a living ecosystem. Theories emerge, compete, evolve, specialize, merge, and retire based on evidence, predictive success, engineering utility, mathematical consistency, reproducibility, and constitutional integrity.

No theory is permanently true. No theory is immune from replacement. No theory is discarded without preserving its archaeological lineage.

## Constitutional Principle

Scientific theories are never permanently true. They are evolving explanatory models whose survival depends upon evidence, predictive success, engineering usefulness, mathematical consistency, reproducibility, and constitutional integrity.

## Architecture Components

### Theory Representation
- Theory identity, scope, assumptions, evidence, predictions, mathematical basis, engineering utility, confidence, contradictions, dependencies
- Each theory is a constitutional artifact with deterministic lineage

### Theory Lifecycle
- Observation → Hypothesis → Candidate Theory → Validated Theory → Competing Theory → Dominant Theory → Specialized Theory → Unified Theory → Historical Theory
- Every transition preserves deterministic lineage

### Theory Competition
- Parallel competition among multiple theories
- Minority preservation ensures losing theories persist
- Prediction, experimental, engineering, and mathematical competitions

### Theory Evolution
- Revision, expansion, simplification, mutation, refinement, stabilization
- Evidence-driven evolution only

### Theory Specialization
- One theory becomes multiple specialized theories
- Lineage preserved across all specializations

### Theory Merging
- Evidence convergence, prediction convergence, mathematical unification, engineering compatibility
- Merged theories preserve all ancestral lineage

### Theory Retirement
- Evidence overwhelming contradiction
- Superior explanatory model existence
- Engineering failure
- Historical theories remain archaeologically accessible

### Theory Governance
- Constitutional oversight of theory lifecycle transitions
- Human authority preserved for all theory decisions
