# Synthesis Replay Model

## Purpose

Define replay mechanisms ensuring identical reconstruction of relationship discovery, principle synthesis, knowledge transfer, validation, and certification.

## Replay Types

### Relationship Discovery Replay
- Domain mapping replay
- Cross-domain comparison replay
- Hypothesis formation replay
- Relationship discovery reconstruction

### Principle Synthesis Replay
- Principle collection replay
- Abstraction search replay
- Unification formulation replay
- Domain-specific verification replay

### Knowledge Transfer Replay
- Transfer identification replay
- Adaptation design replay
- Validation testing replay
- Transfer reconstruction

### Validation Replay
- Evidence collection replay
- Validation dimension assessment replay
- Scoring and aggregation replay
- Validation certification replay

### Certification Replay
- Submission review replay
- Evidence evaluation replay
- Decision process replay
- Certification reconstruction

## Verification Process

Standard hash chain verification against cold storage.

## Replay Requirements

- Same inputs produce identical syntheses
- Replay reconstructs complete synthesis pipeline
- All intermediate states reproduced
- Divergence detectable and reportable

## Replay Structure

Each replay contains:
- Replay ID, synthesis artifact IDs, replay type
- Timestamp, verification results, fingerprint match
- Divergence report, fingerprint

## Constraints

- Replay must produce identical hashes
- Replay records become constitutional artifacts
- Divergence triggers archaeological investigation
