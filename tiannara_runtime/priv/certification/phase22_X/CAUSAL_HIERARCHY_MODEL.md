# Causal Hierarchy Model

## Purpose

Formally define the 16 constitutional causal layers, their boundaries, internal ontologies, and inter-layer connection protocols.

## Layer Definitions

### Layer 0 — Mathematics
- **Scope**: Formal systems, logic, number theory, geometry, algebra, analysis
- **Entities**: Axioms, theorems, proofs, structures, transformations
- **Causality**: Logical implication, derivation, proof-theoretic causality
- **Evidence**: Proof, logical consistency, mathematical truth

### Layer 1 — Information
- **Scope**: Data, computation, communication, entropy, complexity
- **Entities**: Bits, algorithms, messages, codes, channels
- **Causality**: Computational causality, information flow
- **Evidence**: Algorithmic verification, information-theoretic measures

### Layer 2 — Physics
- **Scope**: Fundamental forces, particles, fields, spacetime, quantum mechanics
- **Entities**: Particles, fields, forces, energy, momentum, spacetime
- **Causality**: Physical causation, conservation laws, field equations
- **Evidence**: Experimental measurement, observational data

### Layer 3 — Chemistry
- **Scope**: Elements, molecules, reactions, bonding, thermodynamics
- **Entities**: Atoms, molecules, chemical bonds, reactions, phases
- **Causality**: Chemical causation, reaction kinetics, thermodynamic driving forces
- **Evidence**: Spectroscopic data, reaction measurements

### Layer 4 — Materials
- **Scope**: Material properties, structures, processing, performance
- **Entities**: Crystals, polymers, composites, alloys, metamaterials
- **Causality**: Structure-property relationships, processing-structure causation
- **Evidence**: Materials characterization, mechanical testing

### Layer 5 — Biology
- **Scope**: Life, evolution, genetics, cells, organisms, ecosystems
- **Entities**: DNA, proteins, cells, organs, organisms, populations, species
- **Causality**: Biological causation, evolutionary selection, genetic causality
- **Evidence**: Genomic data, experimental biology, field observation

### Layer 6 — Medicine
- **Scope**: Health, disease, treatment, prevention, physiology
- **Entities**: Pathogens, organs, physiological systems, treatments, patients
- **Causality**: Physiological causation, disease causation, treatment effect
- **Evidence**: Clinical trials, epidemiological studies, physiological measurements

### Layer 7 — Cognition
- **Scope**: Mind, perception, reasoning, learning, decision-making
- **Entities**: Neurons, neural circuits, cognitive processes, beliefs, goals
- **Causality**: Neural causation, cognitive causation, rational inference
- **Evidence**: Neuroscience data, behavioral experiments, cognitive models

### Layer 8 — Engineering
- **Scope**: Design, construction, optimization of systems and artifacts
- **Entities**: Designs, components, systems, requirements, specifications
- **Causality**: Design causation, functional causation, failure causation
- **Evidence**: Engineering tests, simulation, verification

### Layer 9 — Technology
- **Scope**: Tools, machines, infrastructure, technological systems
- **Entities**: Devices, networks, platforms, protocols, systems
- **Causality**: Technological causation, adoption causation, obsolescence
- **Evidence**: Performance data, adoption metrics, technological assessment

### Layer 10 — Institutions
- **Scope**: Organizations, laws, governance, markets, norms
- **Entities**: Organizations, constitutions, regulations, contracts, norms
- **Causality**: Institutional causation, governance effects, incentive effects
- **Evidence**: Institutional analysis, economic data, governance metrics

### Layer 11 — Civilizations
- **Scope**: Societies, cultures, economies, global systems
- **Entities**: Civilizations, nations, cultures, economies, global systems
- **Causality**: Civilizational causation, historical causality, systemic causality
- **Evidence**: Historical data, economic indicators, social metrics

### Layer 12 — Planetary Systems
- **Scope**: Climate, biosphere, geology, hydrology, orbital systems
- **Entities**: Climate systems, ecosystems, geological formations, water cycles
- **Causality**: Planetary causation, Earth system causality
- **Evidence**: Earth observation, climate data, geological records

### Layer 13 — Solar Systems
- **Scope**: Stars, planets, moons, asteroids, solar dynamics
- **Entities**: Stars, planets, moons, orbits, solar environments
- **Causality**: Stellar causation, orbital mechanics, solar-terrestrial interaction
- **Evidence**: Astronomical observation, space mission data

### Layer 14 — Galactic Systems
- **Scope**: Galaxies, star clusters, interstellar medium, galactic dynamics
- **Entities**: Galaxies, star systems, black holes, dark matter, interstellar medium
- **Causality**: Galactic causation, cosmological structure formation
- **Evidence**: Astrophysical observation, cosmological data

### Layer 15 — Universal Systems
- **Scope**: Universe, cosmology, fundamental laws, spacetime structure
- **Entities**: Universe, fundamental fields, spacetime, cosmic evolution
- **Causality**: Universal causation, cosmological causality
- **Evidence**: Cosmological observation, theoretical consistency

## Inter-Layer Connection Protocols

Each adjacent layer pair defines:

- **Upward Mapping**: How lower-layer phenomena give rise to higher-layer entities
- **Downward Mapping**: How higher-layer constraints affect lower-layer dynamics
- **Interface Invariants**: Properties preserved across the boundary
- **Emergence Rules**: When higher-layer properties cannot be reduced to lower-layer

## Constraints

- Layer boundaries are formal and documented
- Inter-layer connections preserve causal consistency
- New layers may be added via constitutional amendment
