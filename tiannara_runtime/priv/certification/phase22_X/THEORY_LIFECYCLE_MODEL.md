# Theory Lifecycle Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional lifecycle stages through which every scientific theory passes, with deterministic lineage preserved at every transition.

## Lifecycle Stages

### Observation
- Raw empirical observation
- Anomalous data point
- Measurement result
- Experimental outcome
- No explanatory model yet

### Hypothesis
- Tentative explanatory proposal
- Testable prediction
- Preliminary evidence support
- Formal statement

### Candidate Theory
- Complete explanatory framework
- Mathematical formalization
- Tested predictions
- Peer validation
- Registered as constitutional theory candidate

### Validated Theory
- Reproducible predictions
- Consistent evidence support
- Mathematical consistency
- Engineering utility demonstrated
- Constitutional validation

### Competing Theory
- Actively competing with other theories
- Participating in prediction competitions
- Experimental competitions
- Engineering competitions
- Mathematical competitions

### Dominant Theory
- Best available explanation
- Widest predictive range
- Highest engineering utility
- Strongest evidence support
- Subject to continuous challenge

### Specialized Theory
- Domain-specific specialization
- Refined for particular phenomena
- Limited scope
- Derived from parent theory

### Unified Theory
- Multiple theories merged
- Broader explanatory scope
- Unified mathematical framework
- Consolidated evidence base

### Historical Theory
- Retired or superseded
- Archaeologically preserved
- Complete evolutionary lineage
- Knowledge contributions preserved

## Transition Rules

- Every transition requires constitutional governance approval
- Transitions preserve complete deterministic lineage
- Transitions are replayable
- Human authority may block any transition
