# Compression Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive compression reports summarizing knowledge compression state, principle extraction progress, law discovery, redundancy elimination, cross-domain abstraction status, and recommendations.

## Report Structure

### Executive Summary
- Overall compression state and key metrics
- Major principles and laws discovered
- Redundancy and knowledge health status
- Strategic recommendations

### Compression Overview
- Current compression ratio and trend
- Information preservation assessment
- Abstraction level distribution
- Compression pipeline performance

### Principle Inventory
- Principles by domain and cross-domain scope
- Principle confidence distribution
- Recently extracted principles
- Principle retirement and refinement

### Law Inventory
- Scientific laws by domain
- Law confidence and prediction accuracy
- Recently discovered laws
- Law boundary condition updates

### Redundancy Status
- Redundancy detected and eliminated
- Redundancy remaining and impact
- Hierarchical simplification progress
- Obsolete knowledge retirement

### Cross-Domain Abstraction
- Cross-domain abstraction inventory
- Domain connectivity map
- Unification progress
- New cross-domain links discovered

### Validation Summary
- Compression validation results
- Any failed validations and causes
- Information preservation verification
- Provenance integrity verification

### Recommendations
- Priority compression opportunities
- Principle extraction focus areas
- Law discovery priorities
- Redundancy elimination targets
- Cross-domain abstraction opportunities

## Constraints

- Reports must be comprehensive and evidence-driven
- Report records become constitutional artifacts
- Information loss is transparently reported
