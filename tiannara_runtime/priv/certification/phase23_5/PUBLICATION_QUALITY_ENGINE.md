# Publication Quality Engine

## Purpose

Evaluate the methodological and scientific quality of publications across
multiple dimensions. Quality assessments are transparent, evidence-based,
and independent of publication venue.

## Quality Dimensions

| Dimension | Evaluation |
|-----------|------------|
| Methodology | Experimental design quality, controls, blinding |
| Reproducibility | Are methods sufficiently described to reproduce? |
| Statistical Rigor | Appropriate statistical tests, effect sizes, power |
| Sample Quality | Sample size, representativeness, selection bias |
| Citation Quality | Are cited claims actually supported by citations? |
| Engineering Relevance | Utility for engineering applications |
| Prediction Quality | Accuracy of predictions made |
| Uncertainty Reporting | Are uncertainties honestly reported? |
| Data Availability | Is underlying data accessible? |
| Conflict of Interest | Are COIs disclosed? |

## Quality Score

Each dimension receives an independent score (0–1) with confidence interval.
No single aggregate score — quality is multi-dimensional.

## Constitutional Rules

- Quality scores reference specific evidence
- Quality is revisable (e.g., if data becomes available)
- Publication venue is not a quality dimension
- Quality transparency is required
