# Scientific Credibility Engine

## Purpose

Assign constitutional credibility to publications, claims, and evidence
based on evidence quality and reproducibility, not publication venue or
author prestige.

## Credibility Factors

| Factor | Weight Basis |
|--------|-------------|
| Methodological Quality | Publication Quality Engine scores |
| Replication Count | Number of independent replications |
| Evidence Strength | Effect size, sample size, statistical power |
| Consistency | Agreement with other high-quality studies |
| Predictive Success | Accuracy of predictions made |
| Transparency | Data and method availability |
| Falsifiability | Claims are precisely stated and testable |
| Uncertainty Honesty | Uncertainties are not understated |

## Credibility Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Established | Multiple high-quality replications, strong evidence |
| Supported | Good evidence, some replication |
| Suggestive | Preliminary evidence, not yet replicated |
| Inconclusive | Insufficient evidence to assess |
| Contradicted | Strong evidence against |
| Retracted | Officially retracted |

## Constitutional Rules

- Credibility is always linked to specific evidence
- Credibility is revisable with new evidence
- Credibility cannot be inherited from venue or author
- Minority views can have high credibility
