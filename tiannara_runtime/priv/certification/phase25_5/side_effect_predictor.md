# Side Effect Predictor

## Purpose
Predicts unintended consequences and cascading effects of interventions — the most dangerous aspect of planetary interventions is what we do not anticipate.

## Prediction Categories

### Known Side Effects
- Documented in the intervention catalog
- Observed in previous deployments
- Modeled from first principles

### Unknown Side Effects (Uncertainty)
- Incomplete domain models
- Novel intervention types with no history
- Emergent system behavior

### Cascading Effects
- Intervention triggers chain reactions across domains
- Second and third order effects
- Feedback loops (amplifying or dampening)

### Tipping Points
- Interventions that push systems past thresholds
- Irreversible changes
- Regime shifts

## Methodology
- Cross-reference with all domain models in the digital twin
- Run perturbation analysis on the twin
- Check historical analogues (archaeology records)
- Apply precautionary overestimation for unknown unknowns

## Output
Side effect profile for each intervention: enumerated side effects with probability, severity, affected domain, time horizon, confidence level, and suggested mitigations.
