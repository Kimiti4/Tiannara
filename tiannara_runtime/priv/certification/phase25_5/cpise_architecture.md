# CPISE — Constitutional Planetary Intervention & Strategy Engine

## Purpose
Once planetary risks are understood (CPRIE), interventions must be designed, evaluated, and executed. CPISE is the engine that generates intervention strategies across domains, models outcomes and side effects, optimizes resource allocation, ensures constitutional compliance, coordinates staged deployment, and monitors effectiveness.

## Scope
- Strategy generation and combination from an intervention catalog
- Cross-domain intervention planning
- Constitutional constraint checking
- Outcome modeling with uncertainty quantification
- Resource allocation and multi-objective optimization
- Staged deployment planning and rollout coordination
- Monitoring, adaptation, and feedback integration

## Dependencies
- **CPR (Phase 25.0)** — runtime pipeline, coordinator, event system
- **PSE (Phase 25.1)** — planetary state for context
- **CGON (Phase 25.2)** — observation data for monitoring
- **CPDT (Phase 25.3)** — digital twin for scenario modeling
- **CPRIE (Phase 25.4)** — risk intelligence for prioritization

## Non-Goals
- Executing interventions in the real world (external action systems)
- Making final decisions (human-in-the-loop governance)
- Replacing existing intervention frameworks
