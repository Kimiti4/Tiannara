# Tradeoff Analyzer

## Purpose
Analyzes tradeoffs between competing strategies — every intervention decision involves tradeoffs and this engine makes them explicit.

## Tradeoff Categories

### Intrinsic Tradeoffs
- Effectiveness vs. cost
- Speed vs. safety
- Scale vs. reversibility
- Short-term vs. long-term impact
- Precision vs. robustness

### Cross-Domain Tradeoffs
- Benefit to one domain vs. harm to another
- Resource competition between domains
- Timing conflicts

### Equity Tradeoffs
- Benefit to one region vs. another
- Benefit to current generation vs. future generations
- Benefit to one population segment vs. another

## Analysis Methodology

### Tradeoff Matrix
- For each pair of strategies, identify which dimensions compete
- Quantify the magnitude of each tradeoff
- Flag irreversible tradeoffs

### Acceptability Assessment
- Are tradeoffs within acceptable bounds?
- Are affected parties consenting?
- Are there mitigating actions?

## Output
Tradeoff analysis for each decision: tradeoffs made, magnitude, affected stakeholders, acceptability assessment, suggested mitigations.
