# Effectiveness Monitor

## Purpose
Tracks whether interventions are achieving their desired outcomes — early detection of underperformance enables timely adaptation.

## Monitoring Approach

### Outcome Tracking
- Define measurable outcome indicators for each intervention
- Subscribe to relevant observation streams from CGON
- Compare actual outcomes to projected outcomes
- Calculate performance delta

### Time-Based Analysis
- Short-term: immediate indicators (hours to days)
- Medium-term: outcome indicators (weeks to months)
- Long-term: impact indicators (months to years)

### Attribution
- Determine how much of observed change is attributable to the intervention
- Account for confounding factors
- Estimate counterfactual (what would have happened without intervention)

### Anomaly Detection
- Detect unexpected outcomes (positive or negative)
- Flag performance outside expected bounds
- Alert when intervention is significantly underperforming

## Output
For each intervention: performance dashboard with actual vs. projected outcomes, attribution scores, anomaly flags, trend analysis, counterfactual estimate.
