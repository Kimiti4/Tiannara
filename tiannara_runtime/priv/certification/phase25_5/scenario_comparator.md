# Scenario Comparator

## Purpose
Compares multiple intervention scenarios side by side to support decision-making.

## Comparison Dimensions

### Effectiveness
- Primary outcome achievement probability
- Magnitude of intended effect
- Time to effect
- Duration of effect
- Sustainability of effect

### Cost
- Resource consumption (energy, materials, compute, funding)
- Opportunity cost
- Ongoing maintenance cost
- Reversal cost

### Risk
- Failure probability
- Side effect severity
- Irreversibility
- Unknown unknown potential

### Speed
- Time to deploy
- Time to first measurable effect
- Time to full effect

### Equity
- Distribution of benefits
- Distribution of costs
- Distribution of risks
- Impact on vulnerable populations

## Visualization
- Pareto frontier across dimensions
- Radar charts for multi-dimensional comparison
- Sensitivity tornado charts
- Risk/reward scatter plots

## Output
Structured comparison table with scores, rankings, and visualizations for each scenario across all comparison dimensions.
