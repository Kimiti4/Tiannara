# Intervention Archaeology

## Purpose
Full audit trail and causal analysis for intervention decisions — understanding why an intervention was chosen, how it was deployed, and what happened.

## Archaeological Records

### Decision Records
- Why was this intervention chosen over alternatives?
- What information was available at decision time?
- Who (human or system) made the decision?
- What constraints were considered?

### Deployment Records
- When was each deployment action taken?
- What was the state of the planet at deployment time?
- What resources were consumed?
- What were the immediate outcomes?

### Outcome Records
- What were the observed outcomes (planned and unplanned)?
- What was the counterfactual estimate?
- What adaptations were made?
- What was the final result?

### Causal Analysis
- What caused the observed outcomes?
- Were predictions accurate? If not, why?
- Were side effects anticipated? If not, why?
- What could have been done differently?

## Output
Complete intervention history for any past intervention: decision context, deployment timeline, outcomes, causal analysis, lessons learned.
