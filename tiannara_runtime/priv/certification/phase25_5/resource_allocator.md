# Resource Allocator

## Purpose
Allocates limited planetary resources across competing interventions — resources (energy, materials, compute, funding, human capital) are finite and must be allocated optimally.

## Resource Model
- **Energy** — available energy budget across sources
- **Materials** — critical materials and supply chains
- **Compute** — available computational capacity
- **Funding** — economic/financial resources
- **Human Capital** — skilled personnel and labor
- **Time** — temporal sequencing constraints
- **Infrastructure** — existing infrastructure utilization

## Allocation Approach

### Constraint-Based Allocation
- Resource budgets are hard constraints
- Interventions cannot exceed available resources
- Shared resources must be scheduled

### Optimization Goals
- Maximize total risk reduction
- Minimize total cost
- Maximize resilience improvement
- Balance across domains and regions
- Respect equity constraints

### Allocation Strategies
- **Efficiency-first** — allocate to highest return interventions
- **Balanced** — allocate across domains proportionally
- **Resilience-first** — prioritize robustness over peak efficiency
- **Equity-first** — prioritize underserved regions and populations

## Output
Allocation plan: which interventions receive which resources, timing of resource allocation, resource utilization rates, constraints that are binding, and tradeoffs made.
