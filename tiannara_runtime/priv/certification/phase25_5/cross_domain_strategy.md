# Cross-Domain Strategy Engine

## Purpose
Strategies that span multiple planetary domains — the most powerful and most dangerous interventions often cross domains. This engine identifies, models, and coordinates cross-domain strategies.

## Cross-Domain Strategy Types
- **Synergistic** — two interventions that amplify each other (e.g., renewable energy + grid modernization)
- **Sequential** — intervention in one domain enables another (e.g., governance reform → infrastructure investment)
- **Cascading** — one intervention triggers beneficial cascade (e.g., reforestation → water cycling → agriculture)
- **Mitigating** — intervention in one domain offsets side effects from another (e.g., carbon capture offsets continued fossil use during transition)

## Detection
- Scan for known cross-domain interaction patterns
- Use digital twin to simulate combined effects
- Detect emergent cross-domain opportunities from observation data

## Coordination
- Ensure cross-domain interventions are synchronized
- Identify shared resource requirements
- Detect conflicts (competing for same resources)
- Balance pace across domains to avoid bottlenecks

## Governance
- Cross-domain strategies require higher-level certification
- Conflict detection is mandatory before approval
- Cross-domain strategies are always staged with hold points
