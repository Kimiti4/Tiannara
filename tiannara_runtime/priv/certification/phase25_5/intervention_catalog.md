# Intervention Catalog

## Purpose
A registry of all possible intervention types across domains, their preconditions, expected effects, resource requirements, and constitutional implications.

## Catalog Entry Schema
Each intervention type records:
- **id** — unique identifier
- **domain** — primary domain (infrastructure, economy, health, etc.)
- **name** — human-readable name
- **description** — what the intervention does
- **preconditions** — conditions that must hold before intervention
- **expected_outcomes** — primary intended effects
- **side_effects** — known or predicted side effects
- **resource_profile** — estimated resource requirements (energy, materials, compute, funding, time)
- **constitutional_constraints** — relevant constitutional principles
- **maturity** — readiness level (concept → validated → deployed)
- **scalability** — local to global scalability assessment
- **reversibility** — whether the intervention can be undone

## Domain Coverage
- Infrastructure (repair, retrofit, new construction, decommissioning)
- Energy (generation, distribution, storage, efficiency, conservation)
- Food (agriculture, distribution, preservation, alternative sources)
- Water (treatment, desalination, distribution, conservation)
- Health (surveillance, response, prevention, capacity building)
- Economic (incentives, regulation, insurance, investment)
- Ecological (restoration, conservation, rewilding, pollution cleanup)
- Climate (mitigation, adaptation, geoengineering, carbon removal)
- Scientific (research, exploration, data collection, experiment)
- Engineering (new capability development, infrastructure hardening)
- Governance (policy, coordination, legal, institutional reform)
