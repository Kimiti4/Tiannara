# Food Security Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks to global food security.

## Risk Types

- **Crop Failure** — Drought, flood, pest, disease, heat stress.
- **Livestock Disease** — African swine fever, avian flu, foot-and-mouth.
- **Fisheries Collapse** — Overfishing, ocean warming, acidification.
- **Supply Chain Disruption** — Transportation, storage, processing failure.
- **Price Volatility** — Sudden food price spikes.
- **Land Degradation** — Soil loss, desertification, urbanization.
- **Water Scarcity** — Irrigation water shortage.
- **Fertilizer/Input Shortage** — Fertilizer, pesticide, seed shortage.
- **Trade Disruption** — Export bans, tariffs, conflict.
- **Climate Regime Shift** — Long-term changes in growing conditions.

## Assessment Methodology

- **Food Balance** — Monitor global and regional food supply vs. demand.
- **Crop Monitoring** — Satellite-based crop health monitoring.
- **Price Monitoring** — Track food prices globally.
- **Supply Chain Mapping** — Map food supply chain vulnerabilities.
- **Climate Impact** — Assess climate impacts on agricultural zones.
- **Water-Food Nexus** — Assess water availability for agriculture.

## Data Sources

- FAO food balance sheets and price data.
- WFP hunger monitoring.
- FEWS NET early warning.
- Satellite crop monitoring (MODIS, Sentinel-2).
- Agricultural market information systems.
- IPCC agricultural impact assessments.
