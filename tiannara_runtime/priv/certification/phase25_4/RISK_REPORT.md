### Causal Propagation

- Cascading risk scenarios.
- Systemic risk nodes.
- Propagation probabilities.

### Resilience Status

- Resilience trends.
- Resilience gaps.

### Priority Risks

- High risks (tier 2).
- Emerging risks.

### Certification

- Pending certifications.

### Recommendations
- Risk monitoring priorities.
- Resilience improvement suggestions.
- Intervention triggers.
- Investigation recommendations.
