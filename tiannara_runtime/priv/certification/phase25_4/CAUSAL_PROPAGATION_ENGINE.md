# Causal Propagation Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that models how risks propagate through interconnected planetary systems.

## Propagation Model

```
Root Cause
    ↓
Primary Effects
    ↓
Secondary Effects
    ↓
Infrastructure Impact
    ↓
Economic Impact
    ↓
Scientific Impact
    ↓
Civilizational Impact
    ↓
Recovery Pathways
```

## Propagation Dimensions

- **Spatial Propagation** — How risk spreads geographically.
- **Temporal Propagation** — How risk evolves over time.
- **Domain Propagation** — How risk crosses domain boundaries.
- **Cascading Propagation** — How failures trigger secondary failures.
- **Systemic Propagation** — How risk affects system-level properties.

## Propagation Mechanisms

- **Direct Causation** — A directly causes B.
- **Conditional Causation** — A causes B only under specific conditions.
- **Threshold Effects** — Risk becomes active above threshold.
- **Feedback Loops** — Amplifying or damping feedback.
- **Network Effects** — Propagation through interconnected networks.
- **Tipping Points** — Abrupt state changes after gradual change.

## Propagation Recording

Every propagation path records:
- Source risk and target risk.
- Propagation mechanism and probability.
- Time delay (how long propagation takes).
- Attenuation or amplification factor.
- Confidence in propagation assessment.
- Evidence supporting propagation link.
