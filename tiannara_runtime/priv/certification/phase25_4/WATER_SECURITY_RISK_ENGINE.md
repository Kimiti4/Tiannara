# Water Security Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks to global water security.

## Risk Types

- **Drought** — Meteorological, agricultural, hydrological, socioeconomic.
- **Flooding** — River, coastal, flash, urban.
- **Groundwater Depletion** — Aquifer overdraft, saltwater intrusion.
- **Water Pollution** — Industrial, agricultural, municipal contamination.
- **Infrastructure Failure** — Dam failure, treatment plant failure, pipeline rupture.
- **Transboundary Conflict** — Water sharing disputes.
- **Climate Change** — Altered precipitation patterns, glacier melt.
- **Sanitation Crisis** — Inadequate sanitation, waterborne disease.
- **Water Access** — Inadequate access to safe drinking water.
- **Extreme Events** — Combined drought and heatwave, flood after drought.

## Assessment Methodology

- **Water Balance** — Monitor water availability vs. demand regionally.
- **Drought Monitoring** — Use standard drought indices (SPI, SPEI, PDSI).
- **Flood Risk** — Assess flood probability and exposure.
- **Quality Monitoring** — Track water quality indicators.
- **Infrastructure Assessment** — Assess water infrastructure condition.
- **Conflict Risk** — Monitor transboundary water tensions.

## Data Sources

- GRACE satellite (groundwater, drought).
- Global runoff data centers.
- Aqueduct water risk data (WRI).
- FAO AQUASTAT.
- National water resource agencies.
- River basin commission data.
- Climate model projections.
