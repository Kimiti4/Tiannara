# Geopolitical Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks from geopolitical tensions and conflicts.

## Risk Types

- **Armed Conflict** — International war, civil war, insurgency.
- **Terrorism** — Domestic and international terrorism.
- **Sanctions** — Economic sanctions, trade embargoes.
- **Diplomatic Crisis** — Embassy closures, treaty withdrawals.
- **Nuclear Proliferation** — Nuclear weapons development, testing.
- **Displacement** — Refugee crises, internal displacement.
- **Political Instability** — Government collapse, coup, unrest.
- **Resource Conflict** — Water, energy, mineral disputes.
- **Cyber Warfare** — State-sponsored cyber attacks.
- **Disinformation Campaigns** — Information warfare.
- **Territorial Disputes** — Maritime, land, border disputes.

## Assessment Methodology

- **Conflict Indicators** — Monitor early warning indicators.
- **Political Stability** — Assess government stability and capacity.
- **Diplomatic Relations** — Track bilateral and multilateral relations.
- **Military Posture** — Monitor military deployments and exercises.
- **Economic Pressure** — Track sanctions and economic coercion.
- **Social Cohesion** — Assess social fracture lines.

## Data Sources

- Uppsala Conflict Data Program.
- ACLED conflict data.
- Fragile States Index.
- Global Peace Index.
- International crisis group reports.
- Diplomatic and state department reports.
- Media monitoring and analysis.
