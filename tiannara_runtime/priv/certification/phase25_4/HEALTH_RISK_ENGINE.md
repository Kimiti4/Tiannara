# Health Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks to global public health.

## Risk Types

- **Pandemic/Epidemic** — Emerging infectious diseases, antimicrobial resistance.
- **Non-Communicable Disease** — Rising cancer, cardiovascular, diabetes burden.
- **Environmental Health** — Air pollution, water contamination, climate health effects.
- **Malnutrition** — Undernutrition, micronutrient deficiency, obesity.
- **Healthcare System Failure** — Capacity exceeded, supply chain failure.
- **Health Inequality** — Widening health outcome disparities.
- **Mental Health Crisis** — Rising depression, anxiety, suicide rates.
- **Demographic Shock** — Population decline, aging crisis.
- **Vaccine Hesitancy** — Reduced vaccination coverage, disease resurgence.
- **Biosecurity** — Lab accident, bioweapon, engineered pathogen.

## Assessment Methodology

- **Disease Surveillance** — Monitor global disease surveillance data.
- **Epidemiological Modeling** — Model disease spread and impact.
- **Healthcare Capacity** — Assess healthcare system capacity and resilience.
- **Risk Factor Monitoring** — Track health risk factor trends.
- **Pandemic Preparedness** — Assess preparedness using GHS index and similar frameworks.

## Data Sources

- WHO Global Health Observatory.
- IHME Global Burden of Disease.
- GHS pandemic preparedness index.
- Disease surveillance networks (GOARN, FluNet, etc.).
- National health information systems.
- Pharmaceutical supply chain data.
