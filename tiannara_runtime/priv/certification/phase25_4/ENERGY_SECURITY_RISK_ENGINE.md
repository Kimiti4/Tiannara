# Energy Security Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks to global energy security.

## Risk Types

- **Supply Disruption** — Oil/gas production halt, refinery outage.
- **Grid Instability** — Blackout, frequency instability, voltage collapse.
- **Fuel Shortage** — Coal, gas, uranium, biomass shortage.
- **Infrastructure Failure** — Pipeline rupture, storage leak, transmission failure.
- **Price Spike** — Sudden energy price increases.
- **Energy Transition Risk** — Stranded assets, transition disruptions.
- **Cyber Attack** — Grid cyber attack, SCADA compromise.
- **Geopolitical Risk** — Energy weaponization, transit route closure.
- **Renewable Variability** — Extended low-wind/low-solar periods.
- **Nuclear Risk** — Nuclear accident, waste management.
- **Critical Mineral Shortage** — Lithium, cobalt, rare earth supply.

## Assessment Methodology

- **Supply Monitoring** — Track energy production and inventories.
- **Infrastructure Monitoring** — Monitor energy infrastructure health.
- **Price Monitoring** — Track energy prices across markets.
- **Grid Stability** — Monitor grid frequency, voltage, reserves.
- **Geopolitical Assessment** — Assess energy geopolitical risks.
- **Transition Risk** — Model energy transition pathway risks.

## Data Sources

- IEA energy security indicators.
- IRENA renewable energy data.
- Grid operator data.
- Energy market data.
- Geopolitical risk assessments.
- Critical mineral supply chain data.
