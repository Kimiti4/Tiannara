# Technological Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks from technological systems and failures.

## Risk Types

- **Energy System Failure** — Grid failure, nuclear accident, pipeline rupture.
- **Transportation Failure** — Aviation accident, maritime disaster, rail failure.
- **Industrial Accident** — Chemical spill, explosion, structural collapse.
- **Communications Failure** — Network outage, satellite failure, data loss.
- **Manufacturing Failure** — Quality failure, supply chain disruption.
- **Software Failure** — Critical system failure, data corruption.
- **AI Safety** — Unintended AI behavior, control loss.
- **Biotechnology Risk** — Lab escape, engineered pathogen.
- **Nanotechnology Risk** — Environmental release, health effects.
- **Space Technology Risk** — Launch failure, debris collision.

## Assessment Methodology

- **Hazard Identification** — Identify potential failure modes.
- **Failure Probability** — Estimate probability based on design, operation, history.
- **Failure Consequence** — Estimate consequences across domains.
- **Cascading Failure** — Model failure propagation through interconnected systems.
- **Safety Margin** — Assess safety margins and redundancies.

## Data Sources

- Incident databases (IAEA, ICAO, IMO, etc.).
- Industry reliability reports.
- Regulatory safety assessments.
- Engineering design specifications.
- Operational history and maintenance records.
- Near-miss reporting systems.
