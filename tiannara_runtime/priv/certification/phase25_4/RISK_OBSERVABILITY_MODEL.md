# Risk Observability Model

## Purpose

Define observability for the CPRIE.

## Observable Dimensions

| Dimension | Description |
|---|---|
| Planetary Risk Map | Geographic risk visualization |
| Global Risk Heatmap | Aggregate risk by type and region |
| Risk Timeline | Risk evolution over time |
| Emerging Risks | Newly detected risks |
| Critical Infrastructure Risks | Infrastructure-specific risks |
| Cascade Networks | Risk propagation chain visualization |
| Resilience Index | System resilience by type |
| Recovery Capacity | Recovery capability by region |
| Risk Replay | Historical risk state replay |

## Observatory Displays

- **Risk Dashboard** — Global risk overview and key indicators.
- **Risk Map** — Geographic risk layer visualization.
- **Cascade Network** — Interactive risk propagation maps.
- **Resilience Board** — System resilience dashboards.
- **Alert Console** — Risk alerts and escalations.
- **Risk Timeline** — Risk evolution over time.
- **Unknown Risk Tracker** — Unclassified risk observation tracking.
- **Priority Matrix** — Probability vs. severity matrix.

## Alerting

| Level | Condition |
|---|---|
| Info | New risk detected |
| Warning | Risk probability or severity increasing |
| Critical | High-priority risk confirmed |
| Critical | Cascading risk chain activated |
| Emergency | Imminent catastrophic risk |
| Info | Unknown risk pattern detected |
