# Constitutional Planetary Risk Intelligence Architecture

## Purpose

Define the architecture of the CPRIE — the engine that continuously identifies, models, predicts, prioritizes, and explains risks affecting planetary civilization.

## Architecture Overview

Unlike traditional risk systems focusing on isolated hazards, the CPRIE reasons across interconnected natural, technological, economic, social, scientific, and engineering systems. Its objective is early constitutional warning while preserving uncertainty, evidence, replayability, and complete causal traceability.

The CPRIE never makes policy. It produces evidence-backed risk intelligence.

## Architecture Principles

1. **Evidence-backed** — Every risk assessment grounded in validated observations.
2. **Explainable** — Every risk assessment traceable to its evidence chain.
3. **Probabilistic** — All risks expressed with explicit probability and uncertainty.
4. **Replayable** — Every risk assessment deterministically replayable.
5. **Auditable** — Complete audit trail for all risk operations.
6. **Constitution-first** — Risk assessments respect constitutional governance.
7. **Time-versioned** — Risk assessments valid for specific time windows.
8. **Human-reviewable** — All risk intelligence designed for human evaluation.

## Core Principle

Unknown risks must never be discarded.
