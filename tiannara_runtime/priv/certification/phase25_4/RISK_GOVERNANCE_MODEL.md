# Risk Governance Model

## Purpose

Define governance for the CPRIE.

## Governance Structure

```
Planetary Runtime
    ↑
Risk Intelligence Governance
    ↑
    ├── Risk Taxonomy Approval
    ├── Risk Methodology Approval
    ├── Risk Priority Standards
    ├── Resilience Standards
    └── Certification Authority
```

## Governance Functions

| Function | Description |
|---|---|
| Risk Taxonomy Approval | Risk categories and types approved here |
| Risk Methodology Approval | Risk assessment methods approved |
| Risk Priority Standards | Priority criteria and weights defined |
| Resilience Standards | Resilience assessment standards defined |
| Certification | Risk domain engine certification decisions |

## Governance Rules

1. All risk assessments must be evidence-backed.
2. Probability and uncertainty must always be explicit.
3. Unknown risks must never be discarded.
4. Risk assessments must be human-reviewable.
5. Risk methodology must be scientifically valid.
6. All risk operations must be replayable.
7. Risk governance decisions are constitutional.

## Escalation

- Critical risks automatically escalated to Planetary Council.
- High risks escalated to Engineering Council.
- Medium risks escalated to program management.
- Risk methodology disagreements escalated to governance.
