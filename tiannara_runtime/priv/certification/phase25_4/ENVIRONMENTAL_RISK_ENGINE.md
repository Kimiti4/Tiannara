# Environmental Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks to planetary environmental systems.

## Risk Types

- **Climate Change** — Temperature rise, sea level rise, extreme weather.
- **Biodiversity Loss** — Species extinction, habitat destruction.
- **Ecosystem Collapse** — Coral reef die-off, forest dieback, wetland loss.
- **Pollution** — Air, water, soil, plastic, chemical.
- **Deforestation** — Amazon, Congo, Southeast Asia forest loss.
- **Ocean Acidification** — pH decrease, marine ecosystem impact.
- **Freshwater Scarcity** — Aquifer depletion, river drying.
- **Soil Degradation** — Erosion, desertification, salinization.
- **Chemical Contamination** — Pesticides, heavy metals, PFAS, pharmaceuticals.
- **Invasive Species** — Ecological and economic impact.
- **Nitrogen/Phosphorus Cycle Disruption** — Eutrophication, dead zones.
- **Ozone Depletion** — Stratospheric ozone recovery and threats.

## Assessment Methodology

- **Planetary Boundaries** — Assess against nine planetary boundary framework.
- **Ecosystem Health** — Monitor ecosystem health indicators.
- **Tipping Point Analysis** — Identify potential system tipping points.
- **Irreversibility Assessment** — Assess irreversibility of environmental changes.
- **Cumulative Impact** — Assess combined effects of multiple stressors.

## Data Sources

- IPCC assessment reports.
- IPBES biodiversity assessments.
- NASA/ESA Earth observation data.
- UNEP environmental data.
- National environmental monitoring networks.
- Scientific literature on planetary boundaries.
