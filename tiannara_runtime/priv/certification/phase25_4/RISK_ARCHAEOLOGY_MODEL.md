# Risk Archaeology Model

## Purpose

Define archaeological analysis of the CPRIE's long-term evolution.

## Archaeology Scope

- Risk landscape evolution over time.
- Risk detection methodology improvements.
- Causal propagation model evolution.
- Resilience assessment methodology evolution.
- Risk prioritization evolution.
- Risk assessment accuracy over time.
- Unknown risk discovery history.

## Analysis Capabilities

- **Risk Landscape Evolution** — How planetary risk landscape has changed.
- **Detection Improvements** — How risk detection has improved.
- **Propagation Model Evolution** — How causal models have been refined.
- **Accuracy Trends** — How risk assessment accuracy has evolved.
- **Emerging Risk Patterns** — New risk types that emerged.
- **Near-Miss Analysis** — Events that nearly occurred.
- **False Positive/Negative Analysis** — Assessment errors over time.

## Archaeology Architecture

1. **Risk State Archive** — Immutable archive of all risk states.
2. **Risk Event Log Archive** — Complete risk event history.
3. **Model Version Registry** — History of risk model versions.
4. **Analysis Engine** — Tools for archaeological analysis.
5. **Visualization** — Temporal visualization of risk evolution.
