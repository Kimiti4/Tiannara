# Infrastructure Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks to planetary infrastructure systems.

## Infrastructure Systems

- **Transportation** — Roads, bridges, tunnels, railways, ports, airports.
- **Energy** — Power plants, transmission lines, substations, pipelines.
- **Water** — Dams, treatment plants, distribution networks, wastewater.
- **Communications** — Fiber, cellular, satellite, data centers, IXPs.
- **Buildings** — Residential, commercial, government, industrial.
- **Waste** — Collection, treatment, disposal facilities.
- **Healthcare** — Hospitals, clinics, medical supply chains.
- **Education** — Schools, universities, research facilities.
- **Government** — Administrative buildings, data centers.
- **Defense** — Military installations, early warning systems.

## Risk Dimensions

- **Structural Vulnerability** — Age, condition, design standards.
- **Operational Vulnerability** — Dependencies, single points of failure.
- **Hazard Exposure** — Natural and technological hazard exposure.
- **Systemic Importance** — Criticality to other infrastructure systems.
- **Redundancy** — Backup systems and alternatives.
- **Recovery Capacity** — Ability to restore function after failure.

## Assessment Methodology

1. **Inventory** — Catalog infrastructure assets.
2. **Condition Assessment** — Evaluate current condition.
3. **Hazard Exposure** — Map hazard zones.
4. **Vulnerability Analysis** — Assess vulnerability to each hazard.
5. **Cascading Impact** — Model failure propagation.
6. **Criticality Analysis** — Prioritize critical infrastructure.
7. **Risk Score** — Compute risk score for each asset and system.
