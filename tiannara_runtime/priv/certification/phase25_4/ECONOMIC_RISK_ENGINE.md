# Economic Risk Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that assesses risks to the global economy.

## Risk Types

- **Recession/Depression** — Global or regional economic contraction.
- **Financial Crisis** — Banking crisis, sovereign debt, currency crisis.
- **Inflation/Deflation** — Sustained price instability.
- **Trade Disruption** — Tariff wars, shipping disruption, trade route closure.
- **Supply Chain Failure** — Critical input shortage, logistics failure.
- **Labor Disruption** — Skills shortage, demographic decline, strikes.
- **Technology Disruption** — Automation displacement, industry obsolescence.
- **Resource Scarcity** — Critical mineral shortage, energy price spike.
- **Market Crash** — Stock, bond, commodity market collapse.
- **Sovereign Default** — Government debt default cascade.

## Assessment Methodology

- **Economic Indicators** — Monitor leading, coincident, lagging indicators.
- **Vulnerability Assessment** — Assess economic structure vulnerabilities.
- **Shock Simulation** — Model economic impact of various shocks.
- **Contagion Modeling** — Model financial contagion across markets.
- **Debt Sustainability** — Assess government and corporate debt sustainability.

## Data Sources

- IMF World Economic Outlook.
- World Bank development indicators.
- Central bank financial stability reports.
- Market data (stock, bond, commodity).
- Credit rating agency assessments.
- Supply chain monitoring data.
