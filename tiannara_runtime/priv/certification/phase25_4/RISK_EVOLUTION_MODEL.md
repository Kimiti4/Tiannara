# Risk Evolution Model

## Purpose

Define how the CPRIE evolves over time.

## Evolution Dimensions

| Dimension | Description |
|---|---|
| Risk Domain Addition | New risk domains added |
| Detection Improvement | Better risk detection methods |
| Propagation Refinement | Improved causal propagation models |
| Resilience Enhancement | Better resilience assessment methods |
| Priority Refinement | Improved risk priority methodology |
| Accuracy Improvement | Better risk prediction accuracy |
| Coverage Expansion | More comprehensive risk coverage |

## Evolution Process

1. **Gap Analysis** — Identify largest risk assessment gap.
2. **Methodology Design** — Design improved risk methodology.
3. **Validation** — Validate improvement against historical data.
4. **Certification** — Certify improved methodology.
5. **Deployment** — Deploy into live CPRIE.
6. **Monitoring** — Monitor improvement effectiveness.

## Evolution Priorities

- Largest risk coverage gaps addressed first.
- Highest-impact risk domains prioritized.
- Methodology improvements validated against historical events.
- New data sources evaluated for risk detection improvement.

## Evolution Records

- Every evolution step recorded in risk event log.
- Evolution effectiveness measured and reported.
- Unsuccessful evolutions analyzed.
- Evolution history available for archaeological analysis.
