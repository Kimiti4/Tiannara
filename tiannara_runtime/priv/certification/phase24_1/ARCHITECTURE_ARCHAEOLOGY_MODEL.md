# Architecture Archaeology Model

## Purpose

Reconstruct the complete history of technology architecture decisions: why technologies were proposed, why architectures changed, why subsystems evolved, why interfaces were redesigned, and the complete architectural lineage.

## Reconstructable Questions

- Why was this technology proposed?
- Why did the architecture change at a specific point?
- Why did a subsystem evolve in a particular direction?
- Why was an interface redesigned?
- Which architectural decisions proved incorrect?
- What was the complete lineage of a technology architecture?

## Archaeological Queries

| Query | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| TechnologyOrigin(tech_id) | Why and how a technology was conceived |
| ArchitectureEvolution(arch_id) | How architecture changed over time |
| SubsystemHistory(subsystem_id) | Complete subsystem decision trail |
| InterfaceRedesign(interface_id) | Why an interface was redesigned |
| DecisionImpact(decision_id) | What a decision ultimately affected |
| ArchitectureLineage(arch_id) | Complete ancestry of an architecture |

## Preservation

- All architecture events are permanently preserved
- No pruning or summarization of architectural history
- Archaeology operates on the same event log as replay
