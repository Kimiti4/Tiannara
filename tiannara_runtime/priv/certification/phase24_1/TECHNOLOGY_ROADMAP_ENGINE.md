# Technology Roadmap Engine

## Purpose

Represent technology evolution from concept through civilizational infrastructure as a constitutional roadmap.

## Roadmap Stages

```
Concept
    ↓
Prototype
    ↓
Experimental
    ↓
Validated
    ↓
Engineering Ready
    ↓
Manufacturing Ready
    ↓
Operational
    ↓
Civilizational Infrastructure
```

## Roadmap Properties

- Roadmaps are constitutional objects
- Each stage has defined entry and exit criteria
- Stage transitions require certification
- Roadmaps include estimated timelines
- Alternative roadmap paths are maintained
- Roadmap history is fully preserved
- Roadmaps are fully replayable
