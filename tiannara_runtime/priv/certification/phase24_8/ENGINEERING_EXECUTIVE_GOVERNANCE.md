# Engineering Executive Governance

## Purpose

Define the constitutional governance layer that oversees all engineering activities at the highest level.

## Governance Bodies

| Body | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| Engineering Council | Strategic engineering direction, portfolio approval |
| Constitutional Review | Constitutional compliance of engineering decisions |
| Resource Board | Major resource allocation decisions |
| Certification Authority | Final certification authority |
| Ethics Committee | Engineering ethics and safety oversight |

## Governance Processes

- Portfolio strategy review (periodic)
- Program approval and termination
- Resource conflict resolution
- Certification appeals
- Constitutional amendment recommendations
- Engineering policy development

## Governance Properties

- Governance is transparent and replayable
- All governance decisions are recorded with rationale
- Governance history is fully preserved
- Governance itself is constitutionally governed
- Governance is fully replayable
