# Engineering Risk Portfolio

## Purpose

Track and manage risks at the portfolio level, across all programs and projects.

## Portfolio Risk Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Strategic Risk | Misalignment with constitutional goals |
| Program Risk | Risk of program failure |
| Technology Risk | Risk of technology not maturing |
| Resource Risk | Risk of resource shortfall |
| Dependency Risk | Risk from program interdependencies |
| Market Risk | Risk of changing external conditions |
| Regulatory Risk | Risk of regulatory change |
| Civilizational Risk | Risk of negative civilizational impact |

## Portfolio Risk Properties

- Risks are aggregated across the portfolio
- Portfolio risk exposure is continuously quantified
- Risk correlation between programs is modeled
- Risk mitigation is prioritized by portfolio impact
- Risk history is fully preserved
- Portfolio risk is fully replayable
