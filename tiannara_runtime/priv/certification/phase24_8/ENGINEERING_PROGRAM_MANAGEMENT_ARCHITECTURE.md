# Engineering Program Management Architecture

## Purpose

Architect Tiannara's constitutional engineering program management capability. The CEPME governs thousands of simultaneous engineering programs, projects, technologies, infrastructures, verification campaigns, manufacturing initiatives, and deployment activities — transforming Tiannara into an autonomous constitutional engineering organization capable of coordinating engineering at industrial, planetary, and civilizational scales.

## Core Principles

- Every program serves constitutional goals
- Programs are governed through their full lifecycle
- Resources are allocated by constitutional priority
- Portfolio balance is continuously maintained
- All decisions are replayable and auditable
- No program bypasses constitutional governance
