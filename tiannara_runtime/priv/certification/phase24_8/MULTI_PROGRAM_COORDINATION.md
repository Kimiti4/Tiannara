# Multi-Program Coordination

## Purpose

Coordinate across multiple engineering programs to ensure coherent execution, resource sharing, and dependency management.

## Coordination Areas

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Schedule Coordination | Align schedules across dependent programs |
| Resource Sharing | Share scarce resources across programs |
| Technology Transfer | Transfer technology between programs |
| Knowledge Sharing | Share lessons learned and best practices |
| Standards Alignment | Ensure consistent standards |
| Risk Coordination | Coordinate risk mitigation across programs |
| Certification Coordination | Align certification processes |

## Coordination Properties

- Coordination decisions are constitutionally governed
- Coordination history is fully preserved
- Coordination is fully replayable
- Coordination conflicts are escalated constitutionally
