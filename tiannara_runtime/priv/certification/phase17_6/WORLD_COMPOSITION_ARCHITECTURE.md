# Phase 17.6 — Constitutional Multi-Model Composition & World Fusion: Architecture

## 1. Purpose

Compose, align, synchronize, and reason across multiple certified world models while preserving replay, evidence lineage, mathematical consistency, and constitutional governance.

## 2. Principles

1. No model exists in isolation — every model is a component of a larger representation of reality.
2. Composition must preserve replay, provenance, ownership, and mathematical consistency.
3. No composed model may invalidate the certification of its constituent models.
4. Shared variables must be resolved without ambiguity.
5. Interface conflicts must be detected and resolved deterministically.
6. Every composition must be archaeologically explainable.

## 4. Key Concepts

### ComposedWorldModel
A deterministic composition of multiple certified world models with explicit domain interfaces, shared variable mappings, and synchronization rules.

### Domain Interface
A contract between two domain models specifying which variables are shared, in which direction, and under what constraints.

### Shared Variable
A variable that exists in multiple domain models and must be resolved to a common value during composition.

### World Graph
A directed graph where nodes are world models, variables, equations, causal structures, evidence, predictions, and counterfactuals. Edges represent dependency, causality, synchronization, composition, and ownership.

### Synchronization Rule
A deterministic rule specifying how two models exchange state at each time step or event.
