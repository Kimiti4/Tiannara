# Digital Engineering Architecture

## Purpose

Architect Tiannara's constitutional digital engineering capability. The CDEE establishes the authoritative digital representation of every engineered system throughout its lifecycle — every technology exists first as a constitutional digital artifact before physical realization.

## Core Principles

- Digital twin is the authoritative source of truth
- Every model is constitutionally governed
- Models are fully replayable and traceable
- Digital artifacts are immutable after certification
- Multi-domain models are interoperable
- Lifecycle synchronization is deterministic

## Digital Twin Hierarchy

```
Scientific Principle
    ↓
System Model
    ↓
Architecture Model
    ↓
Subsystem Model
    ↓
Component Model
    ↓
Manufacturing Model
    ↓
Operational Twin
    ↓
Historical Twin
```
