# Energy Model

## Purpose

Define the energy layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Primary Energy** — Fossil fuels, nuclear, renewables.
- **Electricity** — Generation, transmission, distribution, storage.
- **Transportation Energy** — Oil products, biofuels, electricity, hydrogen.
- **Industrial Energy** — Process heat, steam, electricity.
- **Residential/Commercial** — Heating, cooling, appliances.
- **Energy Trade** — Cross-border energy flows.
- **Energy Prices** — Wholesale, retail, by fuel type.
- **Energy Infrastructure** — Plants, grids, pipelines, storage.
- **Energy Investment** — Capacity additions, grid upgrades.
- **Energy Access** — Electrification rate, clean cooking.
- **Energy Efficiency** — Intensity, efficiency by sector.
- **Energy Emissions** — CO2, CH4 from energy sector.

## Model Variables

- Energy production by source and region.
- Electricity generation capacity and output.
- Grid interconnection capacity.
- Energy consumption by sector.
- Energy intensity (energy/GDP).
- Electrification rate.
- Renewable energy share.
- Energy prices by fuel and market.
- Energy investment flows.
- Energy efficiency indices.
- Energy-related CO2 emissions.

## Data Sources

- IEA World Energy Balances.
- IRENA renewable energy data.
- BP Statistical Review.
- National energy statistics.
- Grid operator data.
- Energy market data.
- Satellite observation of energy infrastructure.

## Update Frequency

- Annual energy balances.
- Quarterly production data.
- Monthly prices.
- Real-time grid data (where available).
- Investment data: annual.
