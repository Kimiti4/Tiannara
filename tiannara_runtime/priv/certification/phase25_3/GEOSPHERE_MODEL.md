# Geosphere Model

## Purpose

Define the geosphere layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Lithosphere** — Earth's crust, tectonic plates, continental structure.
- **Topography** — Surface elevation, bathymetry, terrain features.
- **Geology** — Rock types, mineral deposits, fossil fuel reservoirs.
- **Soil** — Soil types, composition, fertility, erosion.
- **Tectonics** — Plate boundaries, fault lines, seismic activity.
- **Volcanism** — Active volcanoes, eruption history, geothermal.
- **Cryosphere** — Ice sheets, glaciers, permafrost, snow cover.
- **Geomorphology** — Landforms, erosion patterns, sedimentation.
- **Geophysical Fields** — Gravity, magnetic, electromagnetic.

## Model Variables

- Elevation (digital elevation model).
- Crustal thickness and composition.
- Soil type and properties by region.
- Mineral resource locations and estimates.
- Seismic hazard zones.
- Volcanic hazard zones.
- Ice sheet thickness and mass.
- Permafrost extent and temperature.
- Groundwater aquifer geometry and properties.

## Data Sources

- Global digital elevation models.
- Geological survey data.
- Seismic monitoring networks.
- GRACE satellite gravity data.
- Ice sheet monitoring (NASA, ESA).
- Soil survey databases.
- Mineral resource surveys (USGS, BGS).

## Update Frequency

- Topography: static (updated with major events).
- Geology: static (updated with new surveys).
- Cryosphere: monthly updates.
- Seismic activity: real-time monitoring.
- Volcanic activity: real-time monitoring.
