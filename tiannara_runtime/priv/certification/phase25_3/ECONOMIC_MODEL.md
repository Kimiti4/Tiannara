# Economic Model

## Purpose

Define the economic layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Production** — Goods and services by sector.
- **Consumption** — Household, government, investment.
- **Trade** — International and domestic.
- **Employment** — Jobs by sector, wages.
- **Prices** — Goods, services, assets, wages.
- **Finance** — Banking, credit, capital markets.
- **Investment** — Fixed capital, R&D, infrastructure.
- **Innovation** — Patents, productivity, technology adoption.
- **Inequality** — Income and wealth distribution.
- **Development** — Economic development indicators.
- **Digital Economy** — E-commerce, digital services.
- **Informal Economy** — Unregistered economic activity.

## Model Variables

- GDP by sector and region.
- Industrial production indices.
- Employment and unemployment rates.
- CPI and sectoral price indices.
- Trade volumes and values.
- Foreign direct investment flows.
- R&D expenditure.
- Patent applications.
- Gini coefficient.
- Multidimensional Poverty Index.
- Human Development Index.
- Digital economy size.

## Data Sources

- IMF World Economic Outlook.
- World Bank development indicators.
- UN national accounts.
- ILO labor statistics.
- UN Comtrade trade data.
- Central bank statistics.
- National statistical agencies.

## Update Frequency

- Quarterly GDP and trade.
- Monthly employment and prices.
- Annual comprehensive accounts.
- Real-time financial market data.
- Periodic structural surveys.
