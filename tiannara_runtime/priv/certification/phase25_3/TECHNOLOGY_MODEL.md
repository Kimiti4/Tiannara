# Technology Model

## Purpose

Define the technology layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Technology Adoption** — S-curves, diffusion rates, saturation.
- **Technology Readiness** — TRL by technology domain.
- **R&D Pipeline** — Research, development, demonstration.
- **Innovation Ecosystems** — Startups, labs, universities, corporations.
- **Technology Spillovers** — Cross-domain technology transfer.
- **Technology Standards** — Adoption and evolution of standards.
- **Digital Infrastructure** — Computing, storage, networking.
- **AI Capability** — Model performance, compute, data.
- **Biotechnology** — Genomics, synthetic biology, therapeutics.
- **Materials** — Advanced materials, nanomaterials.
- **Manufacturing Technology** — Automation, 3D printing.
- **Energy Technology** — Solar, wind, battery, fusion, hydrogen.
- **Transportation Technology** — EVs, autonomous, hyperloop.
- **Space Technology** — Launch, satellites, space manufacturing.

## Model Variables

- Technology adoption rate (by technology and region).
- Technology readiness level (TRL).
- R&D expenditure by technology domain.
- Patent counts by technology class.
- Scientific publications by technology domain.
- Technology price-performance curves.
- Technology standards adoption.
- AI training compute and model performance.
- Renewable energy levelized cost.
- Battery energy density and cost.
- Semiconductor process node.

## Data Sources

- Patent databases (USPTO, EPO, WIPO).
- Technology roadmaps (IEA, IRENA, ITU).
- Industry market reports.
- Scientific publication databases.
- Startup funding databases.
- National R&D surveys.

## Update Frequency

- Quarterly technology tracking.
- Annual comprehensive assessment.
- Continuous emerging technology scanning.
- Event-driven for key milestones.
