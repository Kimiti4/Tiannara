# Biosphere Model

## Purpose

Define the biosphere layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Terrestrial Ecosystems** — Forests, grasslands, wetlands, tundra, deserts.
- **Marine Ecosystems** — Coral reefs, seagrass, mangroves, open ocean.
- **Freshwater Ecosystems** — Rivers, lakes, wetlands.
- **Biodiversity** — Species distributions, populations, interactions.
- **Ecosystem Function** — Productivity, nutrient cycling, decomposition.
- **Land Cover** — Vegetation type, extent, change.
- **Biogeochemical Cycles** — Carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus cycles.
- **Ecological Networks** — Food webs, mutualisms, competition.
- **Ecosystem Services** — Pollination, water purification, carbon storage.

## Model Variables

- Vegetation cover by type (forest, grassland, cropland).
- Net primary productivity (NPP).
- Leaf area index (LAI).
- Species richness and abundance.
- Habitat extent and fragmentation.
- Ecosystem carbon stocks (biomass, soil).
- Fire frequency and intensity.
- Deforestation rate.
- Coral reef health index.
- Fish stock biomass.
- Pollinator abundance and diversity.

## Data Sources

- MODIS, VIIRS, Sentinel-2 satellite data.
- Global Forest Watch.
- IUCN Red List.
- Living Planet Index.
- GBIF species occurrence data.
- FLUXNET eddy covariance towers.
- Citizen science (eBird, iNaturalist).
- National biodiversity monitoring programs.

## Update Frequency

- Land cover: monthly to annual.
- Vegetation indices: 8–16 days.
- Biodiversity assessments: annual.
- Fire monitoring: daily.
- Ecosystem carbon: annual.
