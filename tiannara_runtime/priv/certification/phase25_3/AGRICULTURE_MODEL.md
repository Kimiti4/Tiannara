# Agriculture Model

## Purpose

Define the agriculture layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Crop Systems** — Major crops, rotations, management.
- **Livestock Systems** — Animal production, feed, emissions.
- **Fisheries** — Capture fisheries, aquaculture.
- **Land Use** — Cropland, pasture, shifting cultivation.
- **Soil Management** — Fertilizer, irrigation, conservation.
- **Agricultural Technology** — Mechanization, precision ag, GMOs.
- **Supply Chains** — Processing, storage, transport, markets.
- **Food Security** — Availability, access, utilization, stability.
- **Agricultural Emissions** — GHG, ammonia, nitrates.
- **Agricultural Water Use** — Irrigation, rainfed.
- **Agricultural Biodiversity** — Crop varieties, breeds.

## Model Variables

- Crop area harvested by crop type.
- Crop yield per hectare.
- Livestock numbers by type.
- Fertilizer use (N, P, K).
- Irrigation area and water use.
- Agricultural employment.
- Food production indices.
- Food price indices.
- Prevalence of undernourishment.
- Agricultural GHG emissions.
- Pesticide use.
- Organic agriculture area.
- Mechanization level (tractors per area).

## Data Sources

- FAO production and trade data.
- USDA agricultural projections.
- IFPRI agricultural research.
- WFP food security assessments.
- Satellite crop monitoring (MODIS, Sentinel-2).
- National agricultural statistics.
- Market price data.

## Update Frequency

- Annual production surveys.
- Quarterly crop forecasts.
- Monthly price monitoring.
- Real-time satellite crop monitoring.
- Food security: continuous.
