# Governance Model

## Purpose

Define the governance layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Political Systems** — Democratic, authoritarian, hybrid.
- **Government Effectiveness** — Capacity, quality, implementation.
- **Rule of Law** — Legal framework, enforcement, property rights.
- **Regulatory Systems** — Economic, environmental, social regulation.
- **International Governance** — Treaties, organizations, norms.
- **Policy Domains** — Climate, energy, trade, health, education.
- **Fiscal Policy** — Taxation, spending, debt management.
- **Monetary Policy** — Central bank, inflation, interest rates.
- **Trade Policy** — Tariffs, agreements, barriers.
- **Environmental Policy** — Protection, conservation, pollution.
- **Science Policy** — Funding, priorities, evaluation.
- **Social Policy** — Welfare, healthcare, education.
- **Security Policy** — Defense, internal security.
- **Digital Governance** — Data, AI, platform regulation.

## Model Variables

- Democracy index score.
- Government effectiveness index.
- Rule of law index.
- Corruption perception index.
- Regulatory quality index.
- Policy stringency by domain (climate, environment, trade).
- Tax revenue as % of GDP.
- Government debt as % of GDP.
- Trade tariff rates.
- Environmental policy stringency.
- R&D tax incentives.
- Social safety net coverage.
- Internet governance framework.

## Data Sources

- Worldwide Governance Indicators.
- V-Dem democracy indices.
- OECD policy indicators.
- UN treaty database.
- National policy documents.
- Think tank analyses.

## Update Frequency

- Annual governance indicators.
- Quarterly policy tracking.
- Real-time for major policy changes.
- Election-driven updates.
