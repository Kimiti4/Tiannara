# Population Model

## Purpose

Define the population layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Distribution** — Population density by region.
- **Demographics** — Age, sex, household structure.
- **Dynamics** — Birth, death, migration.
- **Urbanization** — Urban population growth, city sizes.
- **Education** — Educational attainment distribution.
- **Health** — Health status distribution.
- **Labor** — Workforce size and composition.
- **Income** — Income distribution, poverty.
- **Housing** — Housing type, quality, density.
- **Mobility** — Daily and seasonal movement patterns.

## Model Variables

- Population by administrative region.
- Population density grid (1km resolution).
- Age pyramid by region.
- Fertility and mortality rates.
- Net migration rate.
- Urban population percentage.
- Educational attainment distribution.
- Labor force participation rate.
- Income distribution (Gini, quantiles).
- Housing unit counts and types.
- Commuting patterns.

## Data Sources

- UN World Population Prospects.
- National census data.
- Demographic and Health Surveys.
- UN population division.
- GRID3 population grids.
- Facebook/Google mobility data (anonymized).
- Satellite settlement data.

## Update Frequency

- Annual demographic estimates.
- Quarterly projections.
- Real-time mobility (anonymized aggregates).
- Census-driven updates every 5–10 years.
