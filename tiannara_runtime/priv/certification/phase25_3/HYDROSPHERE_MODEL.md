# Hydrosphere Model

## Purpose

Define the hydrosphere layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Oceans** — Temperature, salinity, currents, chemistry.
- **Surface Water** — Rivers, lakes, reservoirs, wetlands.
- **Groundwater** — Aquifers, water table, recharge, depletion.
- **Precipitation** — Rainfall distribution and intensity.
- **Evapotranspiration** — Land surface water loss.
- **Runoff** — Surface and subsurface runoff.
- **Water Quality** — Chemical, biological, physical parameters.
- **Sea Ice** — Extent, thickness, drift.
- **Glaciers** — Mass balance, meltwater contribution.
- **Water Cycle** — Complete hydrological cycle.
- **Cryosphere** — Frozen water in all forms.

## Model Variables

- Sea surface temperature and salinity.
- Ocean currents (3D velocity fields).
- River discharge at major gauging stations.
- Lake and reservoir levels.
- Groundwater storage and depletion rates.
- Precipitation totals and intensity.
- Evapotranspiration rates.
- Water quality indices.
- Sea ice extent and thickness.
- Glacier mass balance.
- Snow water equivalent.

## Data Sources

- ARGO ocean buoy network.
- GRACE/GRACE-FO satellite.
- River gauging stations (GRDC).
- Satellite altimetry (Jason, Sentinel).
- Ocean color satellites (MODIS, VIIRS).
- Precipitation satellites (GPM, TRMM).
- National water resource agencies.

## Update Frequency

- Ocean: monthly to seasonal.
- Surface water: daily to monthly.
- Groundwater: monthly to annual.
- Sea ice: daily.
- Glaciers: annual.
- Water quality: varies by parameter.
