# Scientific Capability Model

## Purpose

Define the scientific capability layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Research Personnel** — Researchers, technicians, support.
- **Research Funding** — Government, corporate, philanthropic.
- **Research Output** — Publications, patents, data, software.
- **Research Quality** — Replication, methodological rigor.
- **Research Infrastructure** — Labs, observatories, supercomputers.
- **Research Networks** — Collaboration, mobility, conferences.
- **Open Science** — Open access, data sharing, preprints.
- **Science Education** — STEM pipeline, PhDs, postdocs.
- **Citizen Science** — Public participation in research.
- **Science Policy** — Funding priorities, evaluation.
- **Technology Transfer** — Commercialization of research.
- **International Collaboration** — Cross-border research.

## Model Variables

- Researchers per capita.
- R&D expenditure as % of GDP.
- Publication counts by field.
- Field-weighted citation impact.
- Research collaboration index.
- Open access publication rate.
- Replication rate by field.
- PhDs awarded by field.
- Research infrastructure index.
- Patent-to-publication ratio.
- International co-authorship rate.
- Citizen science participation.

## Data Sources

- UNESCO Science Report.
- OECD science indicators.
- Scopus, Web of Science.
- National research council data.
- Funding agency databases.
- Research institution reports.

## Update Frequency

- Annual comprehensive assessment.
- Quarterly publication metrics.
- Continuous research network monitoring.
- Periodic surveys (2–5 years).
