# Atmosphere Model

## Purpose

Define the atmosphere layer of the planetary digital twin.

## Model Scope

- **Composition** — Gases, aerosols, pollutants.
- **Temperature Profile** — Surface to stratosphere.
- **Pressure** — Atmospheric pressure distribution.
- **Wind** — Global and regional wind patterns.
- **Precipitation** — Rainfall, snowfall, hail.
- **Cloud Cover** — Cloud type, altitude, coverage.
- **Humidity** — Absolute and relative humidity.
- **Radiation** — Solar radiation, UV index.
- **Air Quality** — PM2.5, PM10, NO2, O3, SO2, CO.
- **Greenhouse Gases** — CO2, CH4, N2O, F-gases.
- **Weather Systems** — Cyclones, fronts, storms.
- **Climate Variables** — Long-term atmospheric trends.

## Model Variables

- Temperature at surface and pressure levels.
- Wind speed and direction (u, v, w components).
- Precipitation rate and accumulation.
- Cloud fraction and optical depth.
- Specific humidity.
- Surface pressure.
- Shortwave and longwave radiation fluxes.
- Pollutant concentrations.
- GHG concentrations.
- Atmospheric stability indices.

## Data Sources

- ECMWF/NOAA reanalysis.
- Weather station networks.
- Satellite observations (MODIS, VIIRS, TROPOMI, OCO-2).
- Radiosonde and aircraft data.
- Air quality monitoring networks.

## Update Frequency

- Weather: real-time to hourly.
- Air quality: hourly to daily.
- Climate variables: monthly to annual.
- GHG concentrations: weekly to monthly.
