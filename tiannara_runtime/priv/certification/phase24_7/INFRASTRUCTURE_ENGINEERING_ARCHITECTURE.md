# Infrastructure Engineering Architecture

## Purpose

Architect Tiannara's constitutional infrastructure engineering capability. The CIEE elevates Tiannara from engineering individual technologies to engineering complete infrastructures — long-lived, interconnected engineering ecosystems composed of multiple technologies, organizations, resources, environments, and operational constraints.

## Core Principles

- Infrastructures are systems-of-systems
- Every infrastructure component is independently certifiable
- Infrastructure interoperability is constitutionally governed
- Infrastructure resilience is designed in, not retrofitted
- Infrastructure dependencies are fully mapped
- Infrastructure evolution preserves complete lineage

## Infrastructure Hierarchy

```
Independent Systems
    ↓
Subsystem Networks
    ↓
Regional Infrastructure
    ↓
National Infrastructure
    ↓
Planetary Infrastructure
    ↓
Interplanetary Infrastructure
```
