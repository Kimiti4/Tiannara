# Energy Infrastructure Engine

## Purpose

Architect constitutional energy infrastructure including generation, transmission, distribution, and storage.

## Energy Infrastructure Elements

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Generation | Power plants, renewables, distributed generation |
| Transmission | High-voltage lines, pipelines, transport |
| Distribution | Local delivery networks |
| Storage | Batteries, pumped hydro, thermal, hydrogen |
| Control | Grid management, load balancing, automation |
| Markets | Energy trading, pricing, allocation |

## Properties

- Energy infrastructure is constitutionally governed
- Reliability and sustainability are primary requirements
- Energy infrastructure dependencies are fully mapped
- Energy infrastructure is fully replayable
