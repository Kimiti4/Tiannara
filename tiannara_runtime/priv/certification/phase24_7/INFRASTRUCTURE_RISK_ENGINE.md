# Infrastructure Risk Engine

## Purpose

Identify, assess, and mitigate risks across the complete infrastructure ecosystem.

## Risk Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Technical Risk | System failure, performance degradation |
| Operational Risk | Human error, process failure |
| Financial Risk | Cost overrun, funding shortfall |
| Regulatory Risk | Compliance failure, policy change |
| Environmental Risk | Pollution, resource depletion |
| Social Risk | Public opposition, equity concerns |
| Security Risk | Cyber attack, physical intrusion |
| Natural Risk | Earthquake, flood, storm, fire |
| Cascade Risk | Failure propagation across systems |
| Strategic Risk | Misalignment with constitutional goals |

## Risk Properties

- Each risk has severity, likelihood, and cascade potential
- Risks are continuously monitored and updated
- Risk mitigation plans are tracked to closure
- Risk history is fully preserved
- Risk is fully replayable
