# Theory Unification Engine

## Purpose

Merge compatible theories into more comprehensive theories that preserve
complete lineage and all supporting evidence.

## Unification Requirements

- Compatible assumptions (assumptions do not contradict)
- Compatible evidence (evidence does not conflict)
- Non-conflicting predictions (predictions agree within uncertainty)
- Shared mechanisms (overlapping causal structure)
- Shared causal structure (common underlying dynamics)

## Unification Process

1. Identify candidate theory pairs for unification
2. Verify unification requirements are met
3. Construct unified scope (union of individual scopes)
4. Merge assumptions (intersection of compatible assumptions)
5. Combine evidence (union of supporting evidence)
6. Derive combined predictions
7. Assign uncertainty (combined from both)
8. Record lineage (both theories as parents)
9. Register unified theory
10. Flag parent theories as subsumed (not deleted)

## Constitutional Rules

- No unification can discard contradictory evidence
- Unified theories must cite all evidence from both parents
- If unification introduces new assumptions, those must be explicitly stated
- Any parent theory can be independently reconstructed from the lineage
