# Governance State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the governance domain.

## State Variables

- **Political System** — System type, stability, transitions.
- **Government Effectiveness** — WGI government effectiveness index.
- **Rule of Law** — WGI rule of law index.
- **Corruption** — CPI corruption perceptions index.
- **Democracy Indicators** — V-Dem, EIU democracy indices.
- **Regulatory Quality** — WGI regulatory quality index.
- **Policy Frameworks** — Climate, energy, trade, health policy.
- **International Agreements** — Treaty participation and compliance.
- **Institutional Capacity** — Government capacity indicators.
- **Human Rights** — Human rights indicators.
- **Fiscal Policy** — Tax rates, fiscal space.
- **Trade Policy** — Tariffs, trade agreements.
- **Environmental Policy** — Environmental regulation stringency.
- **Science Policy** — Science funding, priority setting.
- **Conflict** — Conflict intensity, casualties, displacement.

## Data Sources

- Worldwide Governance Indicators.
- V-Dem, EIU democracy indices.
- Fragile States Index.
- UN treaty database.
- National policy documents.

## Update Frequency

- Annual governance indicator updates.
- Quarterly policy tracking.
- Real-time event-driven updates.

## Uncertainty

- Governance indicator measurement uncertainty.
- Policy implementation vs. adoption gap.
- Expert assessment subjectivity.
- Conflict data reporting lags.
