# Economic State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the economic domain.

## State Variables

- **GDP** — Nominal, PPP, per capita, growth rate.
- **Economic Structure** — Agriculture, industry, services share.
- **Employment** — Employment rate, by sector, informal employment.
- **Unemployment** — Total, youth, long-term.
- **Inflation** — CPI, core inflation.
- **Trade** — Exports, imports, balance, by partner and product.
- **Investment** — Gross fixed capital formation, FDI.
- **Government Finance** — Revenue, expenditure, debt, deficit.
- **Income Inequality** — Gini coefficient, Palma ratio.
- **Poverty** — Poverty headcount at various thresholds.
- **Labor Productivity** — Output per worker.
- **Economic Complexity** — Economic complexity index.
- **Digital Economy** — Digital services, e-commerce.
- **Supply Chains** — Global supply chain networks.

## Data Sources

- IMF World Economic Outlook.
- World Bank development indicators.
- UN national accounts.
- National statistical agencies.
- Central bank data.
- UN Comtrade trade data.

## Update Frequency

- Quarterly GDP and trade updates.
- Annual comprehensive economic accounts.
- Monthly inflation and employment tracking.

## Uncertainty

- GDP measurement uncertainty.
- Informal economy estimation.
- Trade data reporting lags.
- Purchasing power parity uncertainty.
