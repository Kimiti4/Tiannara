# Water State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the water domain.

## State Variables

- **Freshwater Availability** — Surface water, groundwater, ice melt.
- **Water Withdrawal** — By sector (agriculture, industry, domestic).
- **Water Consumption** — Water consumed vs returned.
- **Water Stress** — Withdrawal to availability ratio.
- **Groundwater Depletion** — Rate of depletion.
- **Reservoir Storage** — Major reservoir levels.
- **River Flow** — Major river discharge rates.
- **Water Quality** — Pollution indicators.
- **Wastewater Treatment** — Treatment capacity and coverage.
- **Access to Water** — Percentage with safe water access.
- **Access to Sanitation** — Percentage with sanitation access.
- **Virtual Water Trade** — Water embedded in traded goods.

## Data Sources

- GRACE satellite (groundwater).
- Global runoff data centers.
- National water resource agencies.
- WHO/UNICEF water and sanitation data.
- FAO AQUASTAT.
- River basin commission data.

## Update Frequency

- Monthly surface water updates.
- Quarterly groundwater assessments.
- Annual comprehensive water accounts.

## Uncertainty

- Groundwater measurement uncertainty.
- River flow estimation uncertainty.
- Water use reporting uncertainty.
- Climate projection uncertainty for water availability.
