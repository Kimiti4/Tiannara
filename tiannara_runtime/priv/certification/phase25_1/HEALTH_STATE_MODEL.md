# Health State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the health domain.

## State Variables

- **Life Expectancy** — At birth, healthy life expectancy.
- **Mortality Rates** — Infant, maternal, under-5, age-specific.
- **Disease Burden** — DALYs by cause (communicable, non-communicable, injuries).
- **Malnutrition** — Stunting, wasting, underweight, obesity.
- **Healthcare Access** — Universal health coverage index.
- **Healthcare Workforce** — Doctors, nurses, midwives per capita.
- **Vaccination Coverage** — By vaccine type.
- **Disease Incidence** — HIV, TB, malaria, NCDs.
- **Mental Health** — Prevalence of mental health conditions.
- **Risk Factors** — Tobacco, alcohol, physical inactivity, hypertension.
- **Health Expenditure** — Per capita, % of GDP.
- **Pandemic Preparedness** — GHS index and components.
- **Air Quality** — PM2.5, household air pollution.

## Data Sources

- WHO Global Health Observatory.
- IHME Global Burden of Disease.
- UNICEF, UNFPA health data.
- National health information systems.
- Disease surveillance networks.

## Update Frequency

- Annual comprehensive health assessment.
- Quarterly disease-specific updates.
- Weekly epidemic intelligence.
- Real-time outbreak tracking.

## Uncertainty

- Cause of death attribution uncertainty.
- Survey sampling uncertainty.
- Disease burden modeling uncertainty.
- Healthcare quality measurement uncertainty.
