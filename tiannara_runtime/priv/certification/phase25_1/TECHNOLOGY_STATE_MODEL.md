# Technology State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the technology domain.

## State Variables

- **Technology Readiness** — TRL by technology domain.
- **Adoption Rates** — S-curve parameters for key technologies.
- **R&D Investment** — Public and private R&D expenditure.
- **Patent Activity** — Patents granted by technology domain.
- **Technology Diffusion** — Adoption by region and sector.
- **Emerging Technologies** — Identification and tracking.
- **Technology Standards** — Adoption of key standards.
- **Digital Infrastructure** — Internet penetration, bandwidth, cloud.
- **Energy Technology** — Solar, wind, battery, hydrogen, nuclear.
- **Biotechnology** — Genomics, synthetic biology, medical tech.
- **Materials Technology** — Advanced materials, nanomaterials.
- **Manufacturing Technology** — Automation, 3D printing, robotics.
- **AI Technology** — AI capability metrics.
- **Space Technology** — Launch capability, satellites.

## Data Sources

- Patent databases (USPTO, EPO, WIPO).
- Technology adoption surveys.
- Industry reports.
- Scientific publication trends.
- Technology roadmaps.

## Update Frequency

- Quarterly technology readiness updates.
- Annual adoption rate assessments.
- Continuous emerging technology scanning.

## Uncertainty

- Technology readiness assessment subjectivity.
- Adoption rate estimation from disparate sources.
- R&D expenditure reporting lags.
- Future technology trajectory uncertainty.
