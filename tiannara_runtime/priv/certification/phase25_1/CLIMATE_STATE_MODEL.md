# Climate State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the climate domain.

## State Variables

- **Global Mean Temperature** — Surface and ocean.
- **Regional Temperature** — By region and season.
- **Precipitation** — Total, intensity, frequency.
- **Atmospheric CO2** — Concentration at Mauna Loa and global.
- **Greenhouse Gases** — CH4, N2O, SF6, F-gases.
- **Ocean Heat Content** — By depth layer.
- **Sea Level** — Global mean and regional.
- **Ice Sheet Mass** — Greenland, Antarctica.
- **Glacier Mass** — Global glacier monitoring.
- **Sea Ice Extent** — Arctic and Antarctic.
- **Extreme Weather** — Frequency of extremes.
- **Climate Oscillations** — ENSO, NAO, PDO, AMO indices.
- **Radiative Forcing** — Total and by component.

## Data Sources

- NASA GISS, NOAA, ECMWF reanalysis.
- Satellite observations (MODIS, GRACE, Sentinel).
- Weather station networks.
- ARGO ocean buoy network.
- Ice core and paleoclimate data.

## Update Frequency

- Daily weather data ingestion.
- Monthly climate variable updates.
- Annual climate assessment.

## Uncertainty

- Measurement uncertainty.
- Reanalysis model uncertainty.
- Spatial interpolation uncertainty.
- Natural variability uncertainty.
