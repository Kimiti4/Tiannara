# Energy State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the energy domain.

## State Variables

- **Primary Energy Production** — By source (fossil, nuclear, hydro, solar, wind, biomass, geothermal, ocean).
- **Electricity Generation** — Capacity, output, by source.
- **Energy Consumption** — By sector (industry, transport, residential, commercial).
- **Energy Intensity** — Energy per unit GDP.
- **Electrification Rate** — Percentage with electricity access.
- **Grid Capacity** — Generation and transmission capacity.
- **Energy Storage** — Pumped hydro, batteries, hydrogen.
- **Fuel Prices** — Oil, gas, coal, uranium.
- **Carbon Intensity** — CO2 per unit energy.
- **Energy Trade** — Imports and exports by fuel type.
- **Renewable Share** — Percentage of renewable energy.
- **Energy Efficiency** — Efficiency by sector and technology.
- **Critical Minerals** — Production and reserves for energy transition.

## Data Sources

- IEA World Energy Balances.
- IRENA renewable energy data.
- BP Statistical Review.
- National energy statistics.
- Satellite observation of energy infrastructure.

## Update Frequency

- Annual comprehensive updates.
- Quarterly production updates.
- Monthly price tracking.
- Real-time grid monitoring (where available).

## Uncertainty

- Reporting lag uncertainty.
- Informal energy use estimates.
- Renewable resource variability.
- Technology cost projection uncertainty.
