# Scientific State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the scientific capacity domain.

## State Variables

- **Research Personnel** — Researchers, technicians, FTE.
- **R&D Expenditure** — GERD as % of GDP, by sector.
- **Publication Output** — Number of publications, by field.
- **Publication Impact** — Citation rates, field-weighted.
- **Patent Output** — Patent applications, grants, by field.
- **Research Infrastructure** — Major facilities, observatories.
- **Collaboration Networks** — Domestic and international co-authorship.
- **Open Science** — Open access rates, data sharing.
- **Research Quality** — Replication rates, methodological rigor.
- **Science Education** — STEM graduates, PhDs awarded.
- **Citizen Science** — Participation and output.
- **Technology Transfer** — Spin-offs, licensing, commercialization.
- **Science Policy** — Science funding and policy frameworks.

## Data Sources

- UNESCO Science Report.
- OECD science and innovation indicators.
- Scopus, Web of Science, Nature Index.
- WIPO patent data.
- National research council data.

## Update Frequency

- Annual comprehensive assessment.
- Quarterly publication and patent updates.
- Continuous research network monitoring.

## Uncertainty

- Publication data coverage gaps.
- Citation metric biases.
- R&D data reporting lags.
- Research quality measurement uncertainty.
