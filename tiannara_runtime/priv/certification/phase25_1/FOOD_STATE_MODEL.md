# Food State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the food domain.

## State Variables

- **Crop Production** — Major crops by region.
- **Livestock Production** — Meat, dairy, eggs.
- **Fisheries Production** — Capture and aquaculture.
- **Agricultural Land** — Cropland, pasture, by type.
- **Crop Yields** — Yield per hectare by crop.
- **Fertilizer Use** — Nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium.
- **Pesticide Use** — Agricultural pesticide application.
- **Food Supply** — Calories, protein, fat per capita.
- **Undernourishment** — Prevalence of undernourishment.
- **Food Prices** — Global and regional food price indices.
- **Food Trade** — Imports and exports by commodity.
- **Food Waste** — Waste at each supply chain stage.
- **Soil Health** — Soil organic carbon, erosion.
- **Agricultural Emissions** — GHG emissions from agriculture.

## Data Sources

- FAO food balance sheets.
- World Bank food price data.
- WFP food security assessments.
- Satellite agricultural monitoring.
- National agricultural statistics.

## Update Frequency

- Annual comprehensive assessment.
- Quarterly production forecasts.
- Monthly food price monitoring.
- Real-time food security alerts.

## Uncertainty

- Production estimation uncertainty.
- Food trade data lags.
- Food waste estimation uncertainty.
- Price data representativeness.
