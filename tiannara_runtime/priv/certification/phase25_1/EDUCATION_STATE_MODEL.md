# Education State Model

## Purpose

Define the state representation for the education domain.

## State Variables

- **Enrollment Rates** — Pre-primary, primary, secondary, tertiary.
- **Educational Attainment** — Population by highest education level.
- **Literacy Rate** — Youth and adult literacy.
- **Learning Outcomes** — PISA, TIMSS, PIRLS scores.
- **School Infrastructure** — Schools, classrooms, facilities.
- **Teacher Workforce** — Teachers, student-teacher ratio, qualifications.
- **Education Expenditure** — Per student, % of GDP.
- **Gender Parity** — Enrollment and attainment by gender.
- **Digital Access** — Internet access in schools.
- **Early Childhood Education** — Enrollment rate.
- **Vocational Education** — TVET enrollment.
- **Tertiary Education** — University enrollment, graduation rates.
- **Research Output** — PhDs awarded, publications.

## Data Sources

- UNESCO education statistics.
- OECD education indicators.
- PISA, TIMSS, PIRLS learning assessments.
- National education ministries.
- World Bank education data.

## Update Frequency

- Annual enrollment and attainment updates.
- Periodic learning assessment cycles (3–5 years).
- Quarterly education policy tracking.

## Uncertainty

- Enrollment data reporting lags.
- Learning assessment sampling uncertainty.
- Attainment estimation from surveys.
- Education quality measurement uncertainty.
