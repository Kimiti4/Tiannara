# Multi-Discipline Engineering Integration

## Purpose

Define the architecture for coordinating engineering across all scientific and engineering disciplines.

## Disciplines Coordinated

- Mechanical Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Electronics Engineering
- Software Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence Engineering
- Robotics Engineering
- Materials Engineering
- Energy Engineering
- Communications Engineering
- Manufacturing Engineering
- Infrastructure Engineering
- Systems Engineering
- Governance Engineering
- Scientific Research

## Integration Model

Each discipline operates autonomously within constitutional constraints but coordinates through:

1. **Interface Specifications** — Standardized interfaces between disciplines.
2. **Dependency Graphs** — Cross-discipline dependency tracking.
3. **Shared Models** — Digital twin models accessible across disciplines.
4. **Design Reviews** — Constitutional multi-discipline review gates.
5. **Conflict Resolution** — Automated cross-discipline conflict detection.

## Multi-Discipline Synchronization

- **Requirements Synchronization** — System requirements decomposed across disciplines.
- **Design Synchronization** — Interdependent designs aligned.
- **Test Synchronization** — Cross-discipline test plans coordinated.
- **Integration Synchronization** — Subsystem integration across discipline boundaries.

## Constitutional Constraint

No discipline operates outside constitutional governance. All cross-discipline interfaces are deterministic and replayable.
