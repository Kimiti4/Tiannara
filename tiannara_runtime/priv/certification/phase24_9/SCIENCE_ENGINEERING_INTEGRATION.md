# Science-Engineering Integration

## Purpose

Define the deterministic bridge between validated scientific knowledge (Phase 23) and constitutional engineering (Phase 24).

## Integration Pipeline

```
Scientific Discovery (Phase 23)
      ↓
Validation & Certification
      ↓
Knowledge Transfer
      ↓
Engineering Requirements
      ↓
Feasibility Assessment
      ↓
Engineering Program Initiation
      ↓
Constitutional Approval
      ↓
Engineering Execution (Phase 24)
```

## Knowledge Transfer Contract

Scientific runtime provides:
- Validated theories and models
- Empirical evidence
- Uncertainty bounds
- Reproducibility records
- Constitutional certification

Engineering runtime accepts:
- As authoritative input
- Subject to engineering feasibility constraints
- With preserved uncertainty propagation

## Feedback Loop

Engineering observations flow back to science:
- Anomalous behavior → scientific investigation
- Unexplained phenomena → new research
- Engineering constraints → scientific question refinement
- Operational data → model validation

## Constitutional Gate

No science→engineering transition occurs without constitutional certification that:
- The science is validated.
- The engineering application is constitutional.
- The civilizational benefit is assessed.
