# Engineering-Manufacturing Integration

## Purpose

Define the deterministic bridge between engineering design (24.0–24.3) and manufacturing intelligence (24.4).

## Integration Pipeline

```
Engineering Design (24.0–24.3)
      ↓
Design for Manufacturing Assessment
      ↓
Manufacturability Evaluation (24.4)
      ↓
Process Selection
      ↓
Production Planning
      ↓
Supply Chain Configuration
      ↓
Quality Assurance Planning
      ↓
Manufacturing Execution
      ↓
Verification (24.5)
```

## Key Integration Points

- **DFM Feedback** — Engineering designs receive manufacturability constraints from 24.4.
- **Material Selection** — Engineering materials matched to manufacturing capabilities.
- **Tolerance Analysis** — Design tolerances validated against manufacturing precision.
- **Test Integration** — Manufacturing test points aligned with verification requirements.

## Certification Gate

No engineering design proceeds to manufacturing without:
- DFM certification.
- Supply chain readiness.
- Quality plan certification.
- Constitutional compliance.
