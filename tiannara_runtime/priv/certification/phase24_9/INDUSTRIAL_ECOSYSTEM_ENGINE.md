# Industrial Ecosystem Engine

## Purpose

Architect and manage the relationships among all industrial participants within the engineering civilization.

## Ecosystem Participants

| Participant | Role |
|---|---|
| Factories | Production of engineered artifacts |
| Supply Chains | Material and component flow |
| Research Centers | Applied research and development |
| Engineering Organizations | Design and systems engineering |
| Infrastructure Operators | Operation of large-scale systems |
| Universities | Education and fundamental research |
| Governments | Regulation and policy |
| Industrial Partners | Collaborative industrial activity |
| Autonomous Laboratories | Automated scientific research |

## Ecosystem Architecture

1. **Participant Registry** — Every participant registered with constitutional identity.
2. **Capability Catalog** — Each participant declares capabilities.
3. **Dependency Graph** — Inter-participant dependencies mapped.
4. **Contract Registry** — Formal agreements between participants.
5. **Performance Monitoring** — Participant performance tracked.

## Coordination Model

- **Market Mechanisms** — Constitutional resource allocation.
- **Direct Contracts** — Bilateral participant agreements.
- **Constitutional Oversight** — Governance of ecosystem health.
- **Dispute Resolution** — Constitutional arbitration.

## Ecosystem Health Metrics

- Participant diversity
- Supply chain resilience
- Innovation rate
- Production capacity
- Quality metrics
- Constitutional compliance rate
- Ecosystem stability index
