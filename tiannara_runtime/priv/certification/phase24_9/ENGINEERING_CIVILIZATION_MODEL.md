# Engineering Civilization Model

## Purpose

Model the engineering civilization as a unified, evolving, constitutional entity.

## Civilization Architecture

```
Scientific Discovery → Engineering → Manufacturing → Infrastructure → Deployment → Operations → Continuous Evolution
                                    ↓
                            Constitutional Governance
                                    ↓
                            Civilizational Benefit
```

## Civilization Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Continuous scientific discovery |
| Engineering | Constitutional engineering execution |
| Production | Manufacturing at civilizational scale |
| Infrastructure | System-of-systems at civilizational scale |
| Operations | Sustained reliable operations |
| Evolution | Continuous self-improvement |
| Governance | Constitutional oversight |
| Resilience | Ability to withstand and recover from disruptions |

## Civilization Metrics

- **Engineering Capability Index** — Aggregate measure of engineering civilization capacity.
- **Civilizational Engineering Benefit** — Quantified benefit to civilization from engineering.
- **Engineering Sustainability** — Long-term sustainability of engineering activities.
- **Constitutional Compliance** — Degree of compliance with constitutional principles.
- **Replay Integrity** — Completeness and accuracy of replay capability.
- **Evolution Velocity** — Rate of engineering civilization improvement.

## Civilization States

- **Formative** — Engineering civilization being established.
- **Operational** — Engineering civilization fully operational.
- **Evolving** — Engineering civilization actively improving.
- **Resilient** — Engineering civilization maintaining stability through disruptions.
- **Transcendent** — Engineering civilization achieving new capability levels.

## Long-Term Vision

The engineering civilization continuously:
- Expands engineering capabilities.
- Integrates new scientific discoveries.
- Evolves infrastructure at civilizational scale.
- Maintains constitutional governance.
- Preserves knowledge across millennia.
- Generates measurable civilizational benefit.
