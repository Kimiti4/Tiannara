# Reputation Engine

## Purpose

Architect reputation based on validated discoveries, engineering success, review quality, reproducibility, collaboration quality, and constitutional compliance. No popularity metrics.

## Reputation Factors

| Factor | Measurement |
|--------|-------------|
| Validated Discoveries | Count and impact of validated discoveries |
| Engineering Success | Successful engineering applications |
| Review Quality | Quality of peer reviews conducted |
| Reproducibility | Track record of reproducible outputs |
| Collaboration Quality | Effectiveness in past collaborations |
| Constitutional Compliance | Adherence to constitutional principles |

## Reputation Properties

- Reputation is multi-dimensional
- Reputation is evidence-based, not popularity-based
- Reputation is revisable with new evidence
- Reputation factors are transparent and decomposable

## Constitutional Rules

- Reputation cannot be gamed through popularity
- Low-reputation participants retain full scientific rights
- Reputation is independently verifiable
- Reputation discrimination is constitutionally prohibited
