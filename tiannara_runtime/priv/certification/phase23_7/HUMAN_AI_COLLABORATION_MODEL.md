# Human-AI Collaboration Model

## Purpose

Architect constitutional collaboration between human researchers and Tiannara. Humans remain collaborators, not supervisors of truth.

## Collaboration Modes

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Human Review | Humans review Tiannara's discoveries |
| Human Experimentation | Humans conduct physical experiments |
| Human Engineering | Humans build engineered systems |
| Constitutional Approval | Humans approve constitutional changes |
| Human Feedback | Humans provide qualitative feedback |
| Scientific Partnership | Humans and AI co-design research |

## Human Participant Rights

- Humans receive full attribution for contributions
- Humans can challenge any Tiannara conclusion
- Humans can propose new research directions
- Humans can participate in peer review
- Humans have access to all replay and archaeology systems

## Constitutional Boundaries

- Human authority does not override evidence
- Human intuition is a suggestion, not a proof
- Human decisions affecting the knowledge base are recorded
- Human errors are corrected constitutionally, not silently
