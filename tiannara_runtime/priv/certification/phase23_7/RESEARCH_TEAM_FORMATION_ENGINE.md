# Research Team Formation Engine

## Purpose

Form research teams from single investigators to civilization-scale collaborations. Every team is constitutionally represented.

## Team Scales

| Scale | Size | Characteristics |
|-------|------|-----------------|
| Single Investigator | 1 | Independent research |
| Small Group | 2–5 | Focused collaboration |
| Large Collaboration | 6–50 | Multi-institution coordination |
| Cross-Disciplinary Team | Varies | Multiple domain experts |
| Planetary-Scale | 50–1000 | Global research coordination |
| Civilization-Scale | 1000+ | Multi-instance, multi-institutional |

## Team Structure

Each team records:
- Team ID
- Research problem
- Member list with roles
- Team formation rationale
- Governance model
- Communication model
- Task allocation plan
- Collaboration timeline
- Expected outputs
- Formation timestamp

## Formation Process

1. Receive research problem and participant rankings
2. Determine required team scale
3. Select participants based on capability matching
4. Assign roles (lead, contributor, reviewer, advisor)
5. Define governance and communication model
6. Register team constitutionally
