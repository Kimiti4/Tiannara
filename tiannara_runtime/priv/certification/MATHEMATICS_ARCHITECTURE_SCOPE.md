## 1. Three-Layer Architecture

Tiannara's knowledge architecture consists of three constitutionally distinct layers.

### Layer 1 — Epistemic Substrates

These define **how reasoning works**. They are not research domains. They are the formal infrastructure that every higher layer depends on.

| Substrate | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Mathematics** | Formal language for structure, proof, optimization, and symbolic reasoning |
| **Logic** | Rules for valid inference, consistency, and entailment |
| **Information** | Theory of entropy, communication, compression, evidence, and knowledge representation |

**Constitutional rule**: No research domain may redefine or bypass an epistemic substrate. All higher reasoning must be traceable to these foundations.

---


## 2. The Mathematical Substrate's Ten Core Services

After Phase 16.X, Mathematics is no longer a module or a domain. It is the **formal reasoning kernel** — the universal language that every higher subsystem uses.

Every major subsystem depends on the substrate rather than implementing its own mathematical logic.

### Service 1 — Universal Mathematical Representation

Every subsystem represents mathematical objects in a common internal form.

Canonical representations for:

- Scalars, vectors, matrices, tensors
- Functions and operators
- Sets and graphs
- Probability distributions
- Optimization problems
- Differential equations
- Constraint systems

**Contract**: Any subsystem may serialize a mathematical object to the canonical form and deserialize it back. Canonicalization is deterministic and fingerprintable.

---

### Service 2 — Symbolic Reasoning Engine

Expression manipulation, not just numerical computation.

Capabilities:

- Simplify equations
- Rearrange formulas
- Substitute variables
- Factor expressions
- Differentiate and integrate
- Solve symbolic systems
- Manipulate tensor equations

**Contract**: `SymbolicEngine.simplify/1`, `.expand/1`, `.factor/1`, `.substitute/3`, `.differentiate/2`, `.integrate/2`, `.solve/2`

---

### Service 3 — Proof Infrastructure

Constitutional verifier for mathematical claims.

Supported strategies:

- Direct proofs
- Proof by contradiction
- Mathematical induction
- Constructive proofs
- Computational proofs

**Contract**: `ProofEngine.build_proof/4`, `.verify_proof/1`, `.replay_proof/1`, `.fingerprint/1`

---

### Service 4 — Conjecture Generation

Continuous mathematical discovery:

- What pattern exists?
- Can this be generalized?
- Does this theorem have a converse?
- Is there a simpler formulation?

**Contract**: `ConjectureEngine.generate_conjecture/2`, `.replay_conjecture/1`, `.research_queue/1`

### Service 5 — Constraint Solving

Every subsystem delegates constraint problems to the substrate.

Domains served:

- Robotics: motion planning, kinematics
- Governance: policy optimization

**Contract**: Deferred to Phase 17+. The symbolic and proof engines provide the foundation.

## 4. Constitutional Rules

### Rule 1 — No bypass

No subsystem may implement its own mathematical logic. All mathematical operations must go through the substrate.

### Rule 2 — No redefinition

No subsystem may redefine mathematical primitives (types, operations, axioms). Mathematics is frozen.

### Rule 3 — Determinism

Every mathematical operation must be deterministic and replayable. Non-determinism is a constitutional violation.

### Rule 4 — Auditability

Every mathematical artifact must be archaeologically explainable. Origin, assumptions, dependencies, and lineage must be recoverable.

### Rule 5 — Migration

Structural changes to the mathematical substrate may only occur through constitutional migration (Phase 21+). No modifications to frozen mathematics.

## 5. Long-Term Role

By Phase 20, the Mathematical Epistemic Substrate functions as an **operating system for formal reasoning**.

Higher-level systems do not implement mathematics themselves. They **request services**:

- Prove a theorem
- Solve a constrained optimization

- Find an invariant
- Canonicalize an expression
- Replay a proof
- Fingerprint an artifact

At that point, mathematics has evolved from being a subject of study into Tiannara's **formal reasoning kernel**: the universal computational language that underpins every scientific, engineering, and governance capability in the system.
