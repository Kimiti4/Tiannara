# Unknown Prioritization Engine

## Purpose

Rank scientific unknowns by their importance for strategic decision-making. Not all unknowns are equally worth pursuing.

## Ranking Factors

| Factor | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Scientific Importance | How fundamental is this unknown to a field? |
| Engineering Leverage | How many engineering advances depend on this? |
| Uncertainty | How much does uncertainty impede progress? |
| Societal Benefit | What is the potential benefit to society? |
| Planetary Impact | What is the potential benefit to planetary systems? |
| Civilizational Impact | What is the long-term civilizational benefit? |

## Ranking Properties

- Rankings are recomputed as knowledge changes
- Each rank records its component factor scores
- Rankings are explainable and decomposable
- Unknowns with low ranks are not discarded — they remain available for exploration
- Ranking disagreements between factors are highlighted
