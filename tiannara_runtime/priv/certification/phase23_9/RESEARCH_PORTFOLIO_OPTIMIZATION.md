# Research Portfolio Optimization

## Purpose

Allocate research effort across different types of science to maximize long-term civilizational benefit.

## Research Categories

| Category | Description | Typical Allocation |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Basic Science | Fundamental understanding without immediate application | 20-30% |
| Applied Science | Knowledge directed toward practical problems | 20-30% |
| Engineering | Building systems based on scientific knowledge | 15-25% |
| Validation | Reproducing and verifying existing results | 10-15% |
| Replication | Independent confirmation of discoveries | 5-10% |
| Exploratory Research | High-risk, unconventional approaches | 5-10% |
| High-Risk/High-Reward | Potential breakthrough but high failure probability | 5-10% |

## Optimization Properties

- Portfolio allocation is driven by constitutional goals
- Allocation is dynamic — it changes with discovery landscape
- Allocation decisions are fully explainable
- Alternative portfolio models are maintained
- Portfolio balance metrics are continuously tracked
