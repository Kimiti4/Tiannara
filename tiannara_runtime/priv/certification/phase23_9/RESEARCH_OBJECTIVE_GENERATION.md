# Research Objective Generation

## Purpose

Generate concrete research objectives from high-level strategic plans. Translate grand challenges into actionable scientific questions.

## Generation Process

```
Grand Challenge
        → Research Missions
                → Research Programs
                        → Research Objectives
                                → Scientific Questions
                                        → Hypotheses
                                                → Experiments
```

## Properties

- Each objective traces back to a specific strategic goal
- Objectives are actionable and measurable
- Competing objectives are noted with trade-off analysis
- Objectives include estimated resource requirements
- Objective success criteria are defined
