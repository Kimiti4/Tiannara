# Strategic Research Planning

## Purpose

Architect long-term scientific roadmaps with multi-decade planning horizons and dynamic reprioritization as discoveries reshape the landscape.

## Planning Hierarchy

| Level | Horizon | Update Frequency |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Civilizational Goals | Century+ | Constitutional amendment |
| Grand Challenges | 25-50 years | Decadal review |
| Research Missions | 10-25 years | Major discovery trigger |
| Research Programs | 5-10 years | Annual review |
| Research Objectives | 1-5 years | Quarterly |
| Scientific Questions | Months to years | Continuous |

## Roadmap Properties

- Roadmaps are fully deterministic from constitutional goals
- Each roadmap level links to the levels above and below
- Roadmap changes are recorded with rationale
- Alternative roadmaps are maintained when uncertainty is high
- Roadmaps are fully replayable and archaeologically traceable
