# Strategy Explanation Engine

## Purpose

Ensure every strategic decision is self-explanatory. No opaque strategy decisions are permitted.

## Explanation Components

Every strategic decision includes:
- Why the decision was made (triggering conditions)
- What alternatives were considered
- What evidence supports the decision
- What assumptions were made
- What uncertainty surrounds the decision
- What the expected outcomes are
- What would cause the decision to be reversed

## Properties

- Explanations are human-readable
- Explanations are machine-verifiable
- Explanations link to underlying evidence
- Explanations record what was NOT chosen and why
- Explanations are preserved for archaeological reconstruction
