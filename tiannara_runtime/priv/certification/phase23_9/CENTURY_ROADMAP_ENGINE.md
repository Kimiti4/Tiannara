# Century Roadmap Engine

## Purpose

Generate and maintain century-scale strategic roadmaps that guide Tiannara's research prioritization across generational timescales.

## Roadmap Structure

| Horizon | Scope | Key Outputs |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| 10 Years | Near-term research objectives | Prioritized research programs, resource plans |
| 25 Years | Generation-scale missions | Grand challenge milestones, technology forecasts |
| 50 Years | Multi-generation programs | Paradigm shift targets, capability roadmaps |
| 100 Years | Century-scale strategy | Civilizational capability goals, Kardashev milestones |

## Roadmap Properties

- Roadmaps are derived from constitutional civilizational goals
- Roadmaps are updated as discoveries change the landscape
- Alternative century paths are maintained for uncertainty
- Roadmap convergence events are tracked
- Historical roadmaps are preserved for archaeological comparison
