# Strategy Archaeology Model

## Purpose

Reconstruct the complete history of strategic decisions: why certain research directions were chosen, why priorities shifted, which opportunities were missed, and how strategy evolved over time.

## Reconstructable Questions

- Why was this grand challenge prioritized over others?
- Why did the research portfolio shift at a specific time?
- Which opportunities were identified but not pursued?
- Which strategic assumptions later proved incorrect?
- How did strategy evolve in response to a major discovery?
- What was the complete strategic lineage of a research program?

## Archaeological Queries

| Query | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| StrategyEvolution(time_range) | How strategy changed over a period |
| DecisionRationale(decision_id) | Why a specific decision was made |
| AbandonedOpportunities | Opportunities identified but never pursued |
| AssumptionFailureHistory | Assumptions that failed |
| DiscoveryResponse(discovery_id) | How strategy changed after a discovery |
| ProgramLineage(program_id) | Complete history of a research program |

## Preservation

- All strategic events are permanently preserved
- No pruning or summarization of strategic history
- Archaeology operates on the same event log as replay
