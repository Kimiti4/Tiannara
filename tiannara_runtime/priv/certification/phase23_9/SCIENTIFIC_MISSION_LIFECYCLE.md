# Scientific Mission Lifecycle

## Purpose

Represent the complete lifecycle of a scientific mission from proposal through completion and civilizational benefit realization.

## Lifecycle Stages

```
Mission Proposed
        ↓
Mission Approved
        ↓
Research Programs
        ↓
Experiments
        ↓
Knowledge
        ↓
Technology
        ↓
Civilizational Benefit
        ↓
Mission Complete
```

## Stage Properties

| Stage | Description | Exit Criteria |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Proposed | Mission submitted for constitutional review | Approval or rejection |
| Approved | Mission accepted, resources allocated | Research program activation |
| Research Programs | Active research execution | Knowledge production |
| Knowledge | Discoveries and theories produced | Technology development |
| Technology | Engineered systems based on knowledge | Civilizational deployment |
| Civilizational Benefit | Tangible benefit to civilization | Measured impact |
| Complete | Mission finished, lessons archived | Final report certified |

## Lifecycle Governance

- Missions can be paused, reprioritized, or redirected at any stage
- Mission changes are constitutionally reviewed
- Complete mission history is preserved for archaeology
- Mission completion triggers strategy update
