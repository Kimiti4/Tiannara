# Scientific Civilization Metrics

## Purpose

Define and track metrics that measure the quality, effectiveness, and civilizational value of Tiannara's research strategy.

## Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Research Strategy Quality | How well does strategy serve constitutional goals? |
| Unknown Resolution Velocity | Rate at which unknowns are converted to knowledge |
| Grand Challenge Completion | Progress toward grand challenge milestones |
| Discovery Leverage | Average downstream impact of discoveries |
| Scientific Opportunity Capture | Ratio of pursued to identified opportunities |
| Portfolio Balance | Distribution across research categories vs. targets |
| Civilizational Research ROI | Civilizational benefit per unit research investment |
| Strategic Adaptation Rate | Speed of strategy adjustment to new discoveries |
| Long-Term Research Stability | Consistency of long-running research programs |
| Knowledge Graph Growth | Rate and quality of knowledge expansion |

## Properties

- Metrics are computed deterministically
- Metrics are published for observatory display
- Metrics drive strategic review and adjustment
- Metric trends are more important than absolute values
- All metrics include uncertainty
