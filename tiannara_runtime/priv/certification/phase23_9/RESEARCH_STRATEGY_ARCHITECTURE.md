# Research Strategy Architecture

## Purpose

Architect Tiannara's constitutional autonomous research strategy capability. CARSE answers not "how do we do science well?" but "what science should humanity pursue next, and why?" — managing the evolution of entire scientific agendas over decades and centuries.

## Strategic Hierarchy

```
Civilizational Goals
        ↓
Scientific Grand Challenges
        ↓
Research Missions
        ↓
Research Programs
        ↓
Research Objectives
        ↓
Scientific Questions
        ↓
Hypotheses
        ↓
Experiments
        ↓
Discoveries
        ↓
Strategy Update
```

## Core Components

| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| Strategic Research Planning | Long-term scientific roadmaps, multi-decade planning |
| Grand Challenge Engine | Manage grand challenges (fusion, cancer, AGI safety, etc.) |
| Unknown Prioritization Engine | Rank unknowns by importance, leverage, uncertainty, benefit |
| Research Portfolio Optimization | Allocate across basic/applied/validation/exploratory/high-risk |
| Discovery Dependency Graph | Represent downstream leverage chains |
| Scientific Opportunity Engine | Search for emerging areas, neglected fields, paradigm shifts |
| Constitutional Resource Planning | Distribute compute, engineering, lab capacity by priority |
| Scientific Mission Lifecycle | Mission proposed → approved → programs → knowledge → benefit → complete |
| Century Roadmap Engine | Century-scale strategic roadmaps |
| Dynamic Reprioritization Engine | Adjust strategy as discoveries change the landscape |

## Constitutional Governance

- Strategy is always subordinate to civilizational constitutional goals
- No strategy can violate constitutional principles
- All strategic decisions are replayable and explainable
- Strategy adapts to new discoveries without destabilizing ongoing research
