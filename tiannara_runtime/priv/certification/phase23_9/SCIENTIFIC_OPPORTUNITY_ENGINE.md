# Scientific Opportunity Engine

## Purpose

Continuously search for emerging research areas, neglected fields, interdisciplinary opportunities, paradigm shifts, and technology inflection points.

## Search Targets

| Target | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Emerging Research Areas | Fields that are growing rapidly or showing new promise |
| Neglected Fields | Important areas that are receiving insufficient research attention |
| Interdisciplinary Opportunities | Problems at the intersection of multiple fields |
| Paradigm Shifts | Fundamental changes in how a field understands its subject |
| Technology Inflection Points | Technologies approaching breakthrough thresholds |
| Reproducibility Crises | Fields where foundational results need re-examination |
| Funding Discrepancies | Areas where importance and resource allocation diverge |

## Opportunity Properties

- Opportunities are ranked by strategic importance
- Each opportunity records its evidence base
- Opportunities include estimated resource requirements
- Opportunities are linked to specific grand challenges
- False opportunities (dead ends) are recorded archaeologically
