# Grand Challenge Engine

## Purpose

Architect management of scientific grand challenges that span decades and require coordinated research campaigns across multiple domains.

## Managed Grand Challenges

| Challenge | Domains | Typical Horizon |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| Controlled Fusion | Physics, materials, engineering | 25-50 years |
| Cancer | Biology, medicine, computation | 25-50 years |
| AGI Safety | AI, ethics, governance | 10-25 years |
| Climate Resilience | Climate science, engineering, policy | 25-50 years |
| Quantum Materials | Physics, chemistry, materials science | 25-50 years |
| Interstellar Propulsion | Physics, engineering, materials | 50-100 years |
| Planetary Sustainability | Ecology, energy, agriculture, economics | 50-100 years |
| Fundamental Physics | Particle physics, cosmology, mathematics | 50-100 years |

## Challenge Lifecycle

- Challenge proposed with evidence of importance and feasibility
- Challenge accepted when constitutional benefit threshold is met
- Challenge decomposed into research missions
- Progress is tracked and reported
- Challenge completion triggers certification and civilizational benefit review
