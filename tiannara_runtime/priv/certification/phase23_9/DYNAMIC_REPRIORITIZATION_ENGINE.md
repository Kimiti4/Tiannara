# Dynamic Reprioritization Engine

## Purpose

Continuously adjust research strategy as new discoveries, external events, and changing conditions reshape the scientific landscape. Reprioritization must balance responsiveness with stability.

## Reprioritization Triggers

| Trigger | Response |
|---------|----------|
| Major Discovery | Re-evaluate related research programs, reallocate resources |
| Grand Challenge Breakthrough | Advance mission stage, reassign resources |
| External Crisis | Redirect resources to crisis-relevant research |
| Paradigm Shift | Restructure affected research programs |
| Reproducibility Failure | Increase validation allocation |
| Opportunity Emergence | Create new research program |
| Resource Constraint | Rebalance portfolio |

## Stability Principles

- Ongoing experiments are not preempted without strong justification
- Reprioritization is proportional to the triggering event's significance
- All reprioritization decisions are recorded with rationale
- Reprioritization is replayable and auditable
