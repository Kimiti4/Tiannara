## What CSOS Is

CSOS v1.0 is the first certified Constitutional Scientific Operating System. It is a deterministic, replayable, evidence-first, constitutionally governed operating system for autonomous scientific and engineering evolution.

Unlike conventional operating systems that manage hardware resources, CSOS manages:
- **Constitutional integrity** — Invariants that govern all system evolution
- **Scientific discovery** — Autonomous hypothesis, experiment, theory, and validation
- **Engineering production** — Autonomous requirements through verification
- **Knowledge preservation** — Permanent, replayable knowledge graph
- **Archaeological record** — Complete, immutable history of every operation
- **Generational evolution** — Constitutional generation transitions with full replay

## Constitutional Guarantees

1. **Deterministic replay** — Every operation produces identical output for identical input
2. **Mathematical integrity** — All proofs, rewrites, and canonicalizations are preserved
3. **Scientific reproducibility** — Every experiment, hypothesis, and theory is reproducible
4. **Engineering traceability** — Every artifact is traced to its evidence chain
5. **Governance accountability** — Every decision is recorded and replayable
6. **Evidence-first reasoning** — No claim accepted without verifiable evidence
7. **Archaeological reconstructibility** — The entire OS can be reconstructed from cold storage
8. **Human constitutional authority** — Human override is preserved at all times
9. **Safe future evolution** — All evolution proceeds within constitutional bounds
