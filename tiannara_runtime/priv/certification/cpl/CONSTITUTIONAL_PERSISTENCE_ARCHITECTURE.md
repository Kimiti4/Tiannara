# Constitutional Persistence Layer Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Persistence Layer (CPL) is a cross-cutting constitutional subsystem that guarantees Tiannara remains a continuously operating constitutional scientific organism regardless of unexpected shutdown, power failure, operating system crash, hardware failure, process termination, runtime restart, or network interruption.

The objective is not continuous uptime. The objective is **continuous constitutional continuity**.

No scientific knowledge shall be lost. No engineering lineage shall disappear. No replay chain shall become invalid. No archaeology shall become incomplete. No experiment shall silently restart.

Every interruption becomes an immutable constitutional event.

## Constitutional Principles

The Constitutional Persistence Layer shall:
- Preserve constitutional continuity
- Preserve scientific continuity
- Preserve engineering continuity
- Preserve replay
- Preserve archaeology
- Preserve provenance
- Preserve determinism

The CPL shall never:
- Modify scientific conclusions
- Modify experiments
- Modify discoveries
- Modify certification

It only preserves.
