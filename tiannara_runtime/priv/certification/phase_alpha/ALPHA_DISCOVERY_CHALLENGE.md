# Tiannara Discovery Challenge — Stage Alpha

## Constitutional Alpha Research Program (CARP-1)

### Mission
Validate that Tiannara functions as a continuously operating constitutional scientific organism under months-long sustained execution. Not a product launch — a constitutional scientific validation program.

### Duration
1–3 months continuous operation.

### Participants
- Chief Constitutional Systems Architect
- Tiannara itself (all Phases 20–25)

### Objectives
1. Validate the complete discovery pipeline end-to-end
2. Measure observatory metrics under sustained load
3. Stress-test constitutional governance under continuous operation
4. Tune scoring, prioritization, and routing
5. Measure replay integrity over months of state changes
6. Demonstrate checkpoint recovery under failure scenarios
7. Produce measurable evidence of scientific and engineering capability
8. Establish baseline metrics for all constitutional domains

### Constitutional Principles
- **Evidence-first** — every conclusion traced to data
- **Replayable** — every decision reconstructible
- **Deterministic** — same inputs produce same outputs
- **Constitutional** — all actions respect constitutional limits
- **Measurable** — every claim backed by metric
- **Preservative** — zero knowledge loss under any failure

### Constraints
- Continuous execution for months
- Checkpoint recovery at any point
- Deterministic replay of all operations
- Constitutional governance at all times
- Resource limits enforced
- All failures survivable with zero knowledge loss

### Expected Outcomes
- Thousands of hypotheses generated and evaluated
- Verified engineering artifacts produced
- Complete replay integrity demonstrated
- Observatory telemetry for months of operation
- Measurable knowledge growth
- Demonstrated failure recovery
- Exit criteria met for Stage Beta readiness

### Exit Criteria (Summary)
- ≥99.9% replay integrity
- Zero unrecoverable failures
- Automatic recovery verified
- Memory plateau demonstrated
- Thousands of hypotheses evaluated
- Discovery pipeline reproducible
- Evidence lineage complete
- Months of telemetry collected
- Stable constitutional governance
