# Verification, Validation & Qualification Architecture

## Purpose

Architect Tiannara's constitutional engineering assurance capability. The CVVQE ensures every engineered system satisfies its constitutional requirements before deployment — governing verification ("build the system right"), validation ("build the right system"), qualification ("prove readiness"), and certification ("authorize deployment").

## Core Principles

- Every requirement maps to objective evidence
- Verification is independent from design
- Validation is independent from verification
- Qualification is progressive and constitutional
- All evidence is immutable after certification
- Every activity is replayable and archaeologically reconstructable
- No deployment without certification

## Assurance Hierarchy

```
Requirements
    ↓
Verification Planning
    ↓
Test Design
    ↓
Evidence Collection
    ↓
Requirement Verification
    ↓
Operational Validation
    ↓
Qualification
    ↓
Certification
    ↓
Deployment Approval
```
