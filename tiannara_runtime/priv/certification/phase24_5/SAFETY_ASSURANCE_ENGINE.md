# Safety Assurance Engine

## Purpose

Ensure every engineered system is constitutionally safe. Safety is non-negotiable and independently assured.

## Safety Activities

| Activity | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Hazard Analysis | Identify potential hazards |
| Risk Assessment | Assess severity and likelihood of each hazard |
| Safety Requirements | Define safety requirements to mitigate hazards |
| Safety Design | Design safety features and interlocks |
| Safety Verification | Verify safety requirements are met |
| Safety Validation | Validate safety in operational environment |
| Safety Certification | Certify system as safe for deployment |

## Safety Properties

- Safety is independently assured from design
- Safety evidence is immutable after certification
- Safety incidents are fully investigated
- Safety improvements are constitutionally governed
- Safety history is fully preserved
- Safety is fully replayable
