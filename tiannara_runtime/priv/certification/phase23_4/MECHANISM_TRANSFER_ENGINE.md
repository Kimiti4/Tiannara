# Mechanism Transfer Engine

## Purpose

Transfer causal, physical, chemical, biological, computational, social, and
economic mechanisms across domains while preserving their evidence chains
and constitutional validation.

## Transferable Mechanisms

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Biological | Evolutionary selection, homeostasis, feedback inhibition |
| Engineering | Control loops, error correction, redundancy |
| Physical | Diffusion, wave propagation, conservation laws |
| Chemical | Reaction kinetics, catalysis, equilibrium |
| Computational | Sorting, search, optimization, learning |
| Social | Coordination, game theory, network effects |
| Economic | Market clearing, resource allocation, incentives |

## Transfer Process

1. Identify a mechanism in the source domain
2. Abstract the mechanism to its essential causal structure
3. Map the abstraction to the target domain
4. Validate semantic and causal compatibility
5. Adapt variables, constraints, and scales
6. Register the transferred mechanism
7. Certify and integrate

## Constitutional Rules

- Mechanisms remain linked to source evidence
- Transferred mechanisms are provisional until validated in context
- Mechanisms include known boundary conditions
- Mechanism lineage is fully preserved
