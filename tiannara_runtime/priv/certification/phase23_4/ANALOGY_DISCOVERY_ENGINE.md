# Analogy Discovery Engine

## Purpose

Discover structural, causal, functional, and mathematical analogies between
domains. Analogies are treated as evidence — not proof — and require
constitutional validation before transfer.

## Analogy Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Structural | Domains share underlying relational structure |
| Causal | Domains share causal mechanism patterns |
| Functional | Components in different domains serve equivalent functions |
| Mathematical | Domains share equations or mathematical structures |
| Optimization | Domains share optimization landscapes or objectives |
| Control | Domains share feedback or regulation mechanisms |
| Evolutionary | Domains share selection or adaptation dynamics |

## Analogy Structure

Each analogy records:
- Source domain elements
- Target domain elements
- Mapping between elements
- Analogy type(s)
- Structural alignment score
- Evidence supporting the analogy
- Uncertainty of the analogy
- Known disanalogies (where the analogy breaks down)

## Constitutional Rules

- Analogies are hypotheses, not facts
- All analogies require independent validation
- Disanalogies are as important as analogies
- Failed analogies remain in archaeological record
