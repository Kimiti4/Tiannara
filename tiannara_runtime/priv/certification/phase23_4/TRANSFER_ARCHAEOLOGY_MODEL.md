# Transfer Archaeology Model

## Purpose

Reconstruct the complete history of cross-domain knowledge transfer: why
transfers occurred, why they failed, how domains became connected, which
principles spread, and how scientific understanding unified.

## Reconstructable Questions

- Why did this transfer occur when it did?
- What discoveries enabled the transfer?
- Why did a transfer fail or succeed?
- How did two domains become connected?
- Which principles spread across the most domains?
- What transfer caused a paradigm shift in a domain?
- What alternative transfers were considered and rejected?

## Archaeological Queries

| Query | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| TransferLineage(id) | Full ancestry of a transfer, including alternatives |
| DomainConnectionGraph(domain) | All connections to/from a domain |
| PrincipleDiffusion(principle_id) | How a principle spread across domains |
| TransferImpact(transfer_id) | What changed as a result of this transfer |
| FailedTransferHistory(domain) | All failed transfer attempts involving this domain |
| CrossDomainInfluence(from, to) | How one domain affected another through transfer |

## Preservation

- All transfer events are permanently preserved
- No pruning or summarization of transfer history
- Archaeology operates on the same event log as replay
