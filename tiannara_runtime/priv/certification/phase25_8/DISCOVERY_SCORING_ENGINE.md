# Discovery Scoring Engine

## Purpose
Scores the value and impact of discoveries resulting from challenge resolution — measuring scientific return on investment.

## Scoring Dimensions

### Scientific Impact
- Paradigm change magnitude (how much does the discovery change existing understanding?)
- Knowledge gap closure (how much uncertainty did the discovery resolve?)
- Theory unification (does the discovery connect previously separate domains?)
- Predictive power increase (how much better can we predict phenomena?)
- Reproducibility and robustness

### Methodological Impact
- New experimental technique or capability
- New analytical method
- New computational approach
- Improved measurement precision

### Engineering Impact
- New engineering capability enabled
- Bottleneck removed
- Efficiency improvement
- Risk reduction

### Citation and Adoption
- Adoption by other researchers and engineers
- Integration into standard practice
- Derivative discoveries enabled

### Efficiency
- Resources consumed vs. value generated
- Time to discovery
- Collaboration efficiency

## Scoring Methodology
- Quantitative scores where possible (citation counts, error reduction, efficiency gains)
- Qualitative scores where needed (paradigm impact, novelty)
- Expert assessment integration
- Sensitivity analysis

## Output
For each solved challenge and discovery: multi-dimensional impact score, resource efficiency score, comparative analysis (similar discoveries), value-for-resources assessment.
