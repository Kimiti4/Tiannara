# Challenge Governance Model

## Purpose
Governance framework for challenge management — ensuring challenges are identified, classified, routed, and prioritized with appropriate human oversight.

## Governance Principles

### Human Oversight
- Challenge priority is recommended, not commanded
- Major reclassifications require human approval
- Challenge abandonment requires human decision
- Value assessments are reviewed by humans

### Transparency
- Challenge registry is publicly accessible
- Classification rationale is documented
- Priority factors are explicit
- Routing decisions are traceable

### Inclusivity
- Anyone can submit a challenge
- Challenge identification is not limited to automated sources
- Diverse perspectives in challenge prioritization
- Global representation in challenge value assessment

### Accountability
- Every challenge decision is traceable
- Challenge evolution is auditable
- Incorrect classifications are correctable
- Lessons preserved for future decisions

### Constitutional Boundaries
- Challenges must respect constitutional principles
- No challenges involving prohibited research
- Ethical review for sensitive challenges
- Precautionary principle for high-impact experiments

## Governance Entities
- **Challenge Council** — human body overseeing challenge strategy
- **Domain Review Boards** — scientific and engineering expert review
- **Ethics Board** — reviews challenges for ethical implications
- **Public** — challenge submission and visibility

## Decision Levels
- **Operational** — routine classification and routing (engine automates)
- **Tactical** — priority adjustments and resource allocation (engine recommends, human approves)
- **Strategic** — challenge portfolio direction (human council decides)
- **Constitutional** — challenges affecting constitutional principles (special review required)
