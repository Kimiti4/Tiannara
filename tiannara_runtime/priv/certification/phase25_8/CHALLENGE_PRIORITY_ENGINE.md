# Challenge Priority Engine

## Purpose
Assigns priority to challenges based on scientific value, engineering value, civilizational value, urgency, and dependencies.

## Priority Factors

### Scientific Value
- Contribution to fundamental knowledge
- Paradigm shift potential
- Breadth of impact across disciplines
- Enabling power (unlocks other science)
- Novelty (how much is already known)

### Engineering Value
- Bottleneck removal impact
- Capability enablement
- Efficiency improvement
- Risk reduction for engineering systems
- Technology readiness advancement

### Civilizational Value
- Risk reduction (existential and catastrophic)
- Sustainability improvement
- Quality of life impact
- Equity and justice implications
- Future generation benefit

### Urgency
- Risk-driven urgency (from CPRIE-25.4)
- Resource-driven urgency (from CPRIE-25.6)
- Opportunity window (time-limited chance)
- Dependency pressure (blocking other challenges)

### Dependency Position
- Prerequisite challenges get higher priority
- High-dependency challenges (block many others) prioritized
- Critical path challenges flagged

## Priority Computation
- Multi-factor weighted score
- Sensitivity analysis (how priority changes with weights)
- Transparency: priority rationale is fully documented

## Output
Ranked challenge queue, priority scores with component breakdown, priority rationale, sensitivity analysis.
