# Knowledge Gap Engine

## Purpose
Maintains a structured map of what is not known — the inverse of the knowledge base, systematically tracking missing knowledge.

## Knowledge Gap Types

### Empirical Gaps
- Phenomena not yet observed
- Measurements not yet taken
- Regions not yet explored
- Conditions not yet tested

### Theoretical Gaps
- Phenomena without explanatory theory
- Theories without mathematical formulation
- Frameworks with contradictions
- Unexplained anomalies

### Methodological Gaps
- Phenomena we cannot yet measure
- Computations we cannot yet perform
- Experiments we cannot yet conduct
- Models we cannot yet build

### Integration Gaps
- Disciplines not yet connected
- Theories not yet unified
- Scales not yet bridged
- Time horizons not yet linked

## Gap Representation
Each gap: domain, description, importance (scientific/civilizational), related challenges, prerequisites to close, estimated difficulty, priority, status.

## Gap Map
- Multi-dimensional map of knowledge gaps
- Gaps connected by dependencies
- Gap closure tracked over time
- Gap evolution visualization

## Sources
- Challenge registry (unsolved challenges = knowledge gaps)
- Scientific literature (explicitly stated unknowns)
- Expert elicitation
- Automated anomaly detection in knowledge base

## Output
Knowledge gap map, gap closure priorities, gap dependency graph, gap evolution over time, gap discovery rate (new gaps identified per period).
