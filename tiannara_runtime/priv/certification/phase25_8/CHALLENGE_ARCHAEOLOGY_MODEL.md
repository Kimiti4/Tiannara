# Challenge Archaeology Model

## Purpose
Full audit trail and causal analysis for challenge decisions — understanding why a challenge was classified a certain way, routed to particular domains, or prioritized over alternatives.

## Archaeological Records

### Challenge Genesis
- How was this challenge identified?
- What observation, theory, or problem triggered it?
- Who or what identified it?
- What was the initial formulation?

### Classification History
- How did classification change over time?
- What evidence drove reclassification?
- Who authorized changes?

### Routing History
- Why was it routed to specific domains?
- Were alternative routes considered?
- What capabilities were available at routing time?
- How did routing change as capabilities evolved?

### Priority History
- How did priority change over time?
- What events drove priority changes?
- What was competing for priority position?

### Lifecycle History
- Complete transition record
- Rationale for each transition
- Authorizing entity
- Alternative transitions considered

### Outcome Analysis
- Was the challenge solved? How?
- What discoveries resulted?
- Were predictions about value accurate?
- What would have been different with alternative approaches?

## Archaeological Queries
- Trace challenge from identification to resolution
- Show priority evolution with context
- Compare routing decisions at different times
- Analyze why challenges were deprioritized
- Reconstruct decision context for any challenge transition

## Output
Complete challenge history: full lifecycle trace, decision records, priority evolution, routing evolution, outcome analysis, lessons learned.
