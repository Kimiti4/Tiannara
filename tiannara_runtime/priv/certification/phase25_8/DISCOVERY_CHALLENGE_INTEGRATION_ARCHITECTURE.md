# Discovery Challenge Integration Architecture

## Purpose
The CDCIE transforms the Tiannara Discovery Challenge into the permanent external driver of scientific and engineering evolution. It continuously ingests unanswered scientific questions, engineering bottlenecks, planetary anomalies, technological opportunities, and civilizational problems — closing the loop between the external world and Tiannara's internal constitutional evolution.

## Scope
- Challenge ingestion and classification
- Scientific and engineering routing
- Multi-domain orchestration
- Challenge lifecycle management
- Knowledge gap representation
- Discovery scoring and civilizational value assessment

## Constitutional Principles
- **Never discarded** — solved challenges remain permanent scientific artifacts
- **Evidence-backed** — every challenge grounded in observation or theory
- **Versioned** — challenges evolve as knowledge grows
- **Traceable** — full provenance from question to discovery
- **Replayable, auditable, explainable**

## Dependencies
- **CPR (25.0)** — runtime, coordinator
- **CGON (25.2)** — observations that generate challenges
- **CPDT (25.3)** — digital twin for experimental scenarios
- **CPRIE-25.4** — risk-driven challenges
- **CPISE (25.5)** — intervention-driven challenges
- **CPRIE-25.6** — resource-driven challenges
- **CMFCSE (25.7)** — future-scenario-driven challenges

## Non-Goals
- Implementing benchmarks or test suites
- Replacing the scientific method
- Making autonomous research decisions
