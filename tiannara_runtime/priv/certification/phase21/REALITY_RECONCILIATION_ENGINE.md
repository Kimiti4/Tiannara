# Reality Reconciliation Engine

## Purpose

Reconcile theories and models across domains, evaluating compatibility and preserving contradictions until evidence provides resolution.

## Reconciliation Outcomes

### Compatible
Theories are logically consistent and mathematically coherent.
- Can be directly integrated into unified model
- No conflict requiring resolution
- Integration proceeds without modification

### Incomplete
Theories partially overlap but neither fully covers the relevant domain.
- Both preserved with documented overlap
- Integration identifies gaps in each
- Additional evidence needed for completeness

### Competing
Theories offer alternative explanations for the same phenomena.
- Both preserved with evidence support documented
- Confidence distribution computed
- Distinguishing experiments identified when possible

### Contradictory
Theories make incompatible predictions about the same phenomena.
- Both preserved until evidence resolves
- Contradiction explicitly documented
- Resolution experiments prioritized
- Constitutional review may be required

### Dependent
One theory depends on another for its foundation.
- Dependency explicitly recorded
- Foundational theory integrity verified
- Dependency chain documented

### Superseded
A theory has been replaced by a more complete successor.
- Original theory preserved in archaeology
- Supersession rationale documented
- Evidence supporting replacement recorded

## Reconciliation Process

### Step 1: Theory Pair Analysis
- Compare each theory pair across domain boundaries
- Evaluate logical compatibility
- Check mathematical consistency
- Assess experimental evidence overlap

### Step 2: Classification
- Assign reconciliation classification
- Document evidence supporting classification
- Record classification rationale

### Step 3: Integration or Preservation
- Compatible theories → integrate into unified model
- Contradictory theories → preserve both with evidence
- Competing theories → preserve with confidence distribution

## Contradiction Preservation

- Contradictions are never suppressed
- All contradictions have documented evidence basis
- Resolution attempts are deterministic and evidence-driven
- Long-standing contradictions flagged for strategic attention

## Constraints

- Reconciliation must be deterministic and evidence-based
- Contradictions must be explicitly documented
- Resolution must only occur through new evidence
- Reconciliation records become constitutional artifacts
