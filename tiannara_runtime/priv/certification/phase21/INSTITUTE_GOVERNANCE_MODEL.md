# Institute Governance Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional governance framework for each research institute within the federation, ensuring autonomous operation within constitutional bounds.

## Institute Constitution

Every institute possesses:

### Charter
- Founding purpose and scientific mandate
- Domain specialization and boundaries
- Constitutional rights and responsibilities
- Governance structure specification

### Scientific Mandate
- Primary research domains
- Secondary research interests
- Prohibited research areas (if any)
- Mandatory collaboration obligations

### Knowledge Portfolio
- Current knowledge assets
- Active research programs
- Completed discoveries
- Pending peer review items

### Resource Budget
- Allocated scientific capital
- Compute and simulation resources
- Engineering and laboratory resources
- Personnel and expertise inventory

### Mission Queue
- Active and pending missions
- Priority-ordered execution list
- Dependency satisfaction status
- Resource allocation schedule

### Discovery History
- All discoveries made by the institute
- Provenance and attribution records
- Peer review outcomes
- Impact assessments

## Governance Processes

### Strategic Planning
- Institute-level research strategy
- Long-term capability development
- Collaboration and competition strategy

### Resource Management
- Internal resource allocation
- Cross-institute resource sharing
- Capital efficiency optimization

### Quality Assurance
- Internal review processes
- Reproducibility verification
- Constitutional compliance monitoring

## Autonomy Guarantees

- Institutes select their own research programs
- Institutes allocate internal resources independently
- Institutes choose collaboration partners
- Institutes manage their mission queues
- No institute may direct another institute's research

## Constitutional Constraints

- All institute decisions must be deterministic
- No institute may violate CSOS v1.0
- Institutes must participate in federation governance
- Institutes must share discoveries through exchange protocol
- Institute autonomy is bounded by constitutional guarantees
