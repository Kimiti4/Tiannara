# Interdisciplinary Mission Model

## Purpose

Specify the architecture for generating, managing, and evaluating research missions that span multiple scientific domains, ensuring interdisciplinary collaboration preserves provenance and constitutional governance.

## Collaboration Types

### Physics ↔ Engineering
- Experimental apparatus design
- Measurement technique development
- Physical theory validation through engineered systems

### Chemistry ↔ Materials
- Novel material synthesis
- Chemical property prediction
- Structure-function relationship discovery

### Medicine ↔ Computation
- Biomedical modeling
- Drug discovery simulation
- Diagnostic algorithm development

### Energy ↔ Ecology
- Sustainable energy systems
- Environmental impact assessment
- Ecological engineering

### Cognition ↔ Robotics
- Cognitive architecture implementation
- Perception-action loops
- Autonomous decision systems

### Mathematics ↔ Every Domain
- Formal modeling across domains
- Mathematical structure discovery
- Computational method development

## Collaborative Mission Structure

Every interdisciplinary mission contains:
1. Primary domain mission component
2. Secondary domain mission component
3. Integration mission component
4. Cross-domain dependency graph
5. Collaboration protocol specification
6. Provenance record

## Provenance Preservation

- Each discipline's contribution must be independently traceable
- Integration decisions must be documented and replayable
- Cross-domain insights must be attributed to originating domains
- Collaboration artifacts become constitutional records

## Domain Weighting

Interdisciplinary missions may weight domain contributions differently:
- Balanced: Equal contribution from each domain (0.5/0.5)
- Lead-Support: Primary lead with secondary support (0.7/0.3)
- Collaborative: Multiple domains with varying weights

## Constraints

- Every interdisciplinary mission must have at least two domains
- Provenance must be preserved for each domain contribution
- Integration points must be explicitly defined
- Cross-domain dependencies must be documented
- Interdisciplinary missions must have explicit collaboration protocols
