# Mission Validation Architecture

## Purpose

Define the validation framework for constitutional research missions, ensuring every mission has objective, measurable, and reproducible success criteria.

## Validation Components

### Validation Criteria
- Hypothesis to be tested
- Prediction to be verified
- Observable outcome specification
- Measurement methodology
- Statistical significance requirements

### Evidence Requirements
- Type of evidence needed (experimental, observational, theoretical, computational)
- Evidence quantity thresholds
- Evidence quality standards
- Reproducibility requirements

### Success Thresholds
- Minimum knowledge gain for success
- Required confidence level
- Falsification criteria
- Partial success definitions

### Failure Conditions
- Threshold for mission failure
- Conditions requiring mission termination
- Criteria for mission restructuring

## Validation Plan Structure

Every validation plan contains:
1. Validation question
2. Measurement protocol
3. Expected outcomes
4. Success criteria
5. Failure conditions
6. Reproducibility protocol
7. Independent audit requirements

## Reproducibility Requirements

- Experimental results must specify exact methodology for independent replication
- Computational results must provide complete input/output specifications
- Theoretical results must provide full derivations
- Observational results must document collection methodology

## Independent Audit Requirements

- High-impact missions require independent validation
- Validation methodology must be auditable by external reviewers
- Audit criteria must be specified in advance
- Audit results become constitutional artifacts

## Determinism

Validation plans must be deterministically generated from mission specifications. No learning, no optimization, no randomness.

## Constraints

- Every mission must have at least one validation criterion
- Success thresholds must be quantitative where possible
- Failure conditions must be clearly specified
- Reproducibility requirements must be achievable
- Validation plans must be independently auditable
