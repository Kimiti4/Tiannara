# Future Selection Model

## Purpose

Evaluate and rank future world models to support constitutionally governed selection of civilizational trajectories.

## Selection Criteria

### Scientific Progress
- Predicted knowledge growth trajectory
- Theory unification potential
- New discovery frontiers opened
- Instrumentation and observational capability expansion

### Civilizational Resilience
- Robustness to disruption scenarios
- Adaptive capacity
- Risk mitigation effectiveness
- Knowledge preservation guarantees

### Risk Exposure
- Existential risk probability
- Systemic failure probability
- Irreversible damage probability
- Knowledge loss probability

### Constitutional Alignment
- Preservation of human authority
- Adherence to constitutional guarantees
- Transparency and auditability
- Determinism and replayability

## Selection Process

- Each future scored on all criteria
- Scores weighted by constitutional priorities
- Composite score computed with uncertainty
- Futures ranked by composite score
- Top futures presented with trade-off analysis

## Selection Output

- Ranked list of future world models
- Score breakdown per criterion per future
- Uncertainty bounds on all scores
- Trade-off documentation between top futures
- Recommendation with explicit uncertainty

## Constraints

- Selection must be evidence-driven
- Multiple criteria must be considered
- Uncertainty must be preserved in output
- Selection records become constitutional artifacts
