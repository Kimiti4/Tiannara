# Opportunity Discovery Engine

## Purpose

Detect scientific opportunity regions within the knowledge frontier through deterministic discovery mechanisms, transforming frontier gaps into prioritized scientific opportunities.

## Discovery Mechanisms

### Knowledge Graph Discontinuities
- Detect structural gaps in knowledge graph connectivity
- Identify domains where knowledge paths break
- Measure frontier curvature at domain boundaries
- Score discontinuity severity (0.0–1.0)

### Repeated Contradictions
- Track experimental results that contradict theory
- Identify theoretical inconsistencies within paradigms
- Detect cross-domain contradictions
- Accumulate contradiction frequency and severity

### Unexplained Observations
- Catalog observations lacking theoretical explanation
- Measure anomaly significance against established models
- Track observation reproducibility and precision
- Score explanatory gap urgency

### Theory Incompleteness
- Identify domains where theory provides partial explanations
- Detect boundary conditions where theories break down
- Measure theory coverage of experimental regimes
- Score incompleteness severity

### Mathematical Inconsistencies
- Detect inconsistencies between mathematical frameworks
- Identify unproven conjectures blocking progress
- Find gaps in formal derivation chains
- Score mathematical barrier significance

### Engineering Limitations
- Identify engineering bottlenecks limiting experimentation
- Detect measurement precision barriers
- Find simulation accuracy limitations
- Score engineering constraint severity

### Simulation Divergence
- Detect regimes where simulations diverge from reality
- Identify model extrapolation beyond validated domains
- Find parameter sensitivity causing instability
- Score simulation credibility gaps

### Cross-Domain Conflicts
- Detect incompatible models between domains
- Identify phenomena requiring multi-domain resolution
- Find domain boundary tensions
- Score cross-domain resolution urgency

## Discovery Determinism

All discovery mechanisms must produce identical output given identical frontier state. No randomness, no learning, no optimization.

## Opportunity Formation

When a discovery mechanism identifies a frontier gap, an opportunity is formed containing:
- Scientific question
- Domains involved
- Knowledge gap characterization
- Discovery mechanism type
- Evidence supporting the gap
- Estimated difficulty range

## Constraints

- All discovery mechanisms must be fully deterministic
- Each opportunity must have at least one discovery mechanism
- Evidence must be sufficient to justify the opportunity
- Multiple mechanisms may identify the same opportunity
- Discovery records become constitutional artifacts
