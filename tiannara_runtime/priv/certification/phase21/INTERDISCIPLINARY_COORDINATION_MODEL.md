# Interdisciplinary Coordination Model

## Purpose

Coordinate research across scientific institutes and domains, ensuring interdisciplinary collaboration preserves provenance, respects constitutional governance, and maximizes cross-domain discovery.

## Coordination Domains

### Physics ↔ Mathematics
- Theoretical physics relies on mathematical foundations
- Mathematical structures discovered through physical insight
- Common coordination: formal theory development

### Engineering ↔ Materials
- Engineering requirements drive materials discovery
- Materials science enables new engineering capabilities
- Common coordination: experimental apparatus design

### Medicine ↔ Chemistry
- Chemical compounds enable medical treatments
- Medical requirements guide chemical synthesis
- Common coordination: drug discovery missions

### Energy ↔ Ecology
- Energy systems impact ecological balance
- Ecological constraints shape energy research
- Common coordination: sustainable systems design

### Robotics ↔ Computation
- Robotics depends on computational intelligence
- Computational models enable robotic autonomy
- Common coordination: autonomous systems development

### Governance ↔ Economics
- Governance structures shape economic systems
- Economic models inform governance design
- Common coordination: civilizational systems design

### Cognition ↔ All Domains
- Cognitive architectures applicable across domains
- Domain-specific cognition models
- Common coordination: intelligence theory

## Coordination Lifecycle

### Step 1: Cross-Domain Opportunity Identification
- Detect opportunities requiring multiple domains
- Validate interdisciplinary necessity
- Assign lead and supporting domains

### Step 2: Collaboration Protocol Definition
- Define communication mechanisms
- Specify shared resource requirements
- Establish coordination milestones

### Step 3: Synchronized Execution
- Execute coordinated work packages
- Share intermediate results
- Synchronize at defined milestones

### Step 4: Integration and Publication
- Integrate cross-domain results
- Certify interdisciplinary discoveries
- Archive coordination provenance

## Provenance Preservation

- Every interdisciplinary result records contributions from each domain
- Attribution maintained across all integration steps
- Cross-domain insights traceable to originating institutes
- Constitutional review validates interdisciplinary governance

## Constraints

- Every interdisciplinary mission must have a lead institute
- Coordination protocols must be defined before execution
- Cross-domain results must attribute all contributing domains
- No interdisciplinary mission may bypass constitutional governance
