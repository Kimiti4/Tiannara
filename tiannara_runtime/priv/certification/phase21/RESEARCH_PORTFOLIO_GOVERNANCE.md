# Constitutional Research Portfolio Governance (Phase 21.1)

## Mission

Design the Constitutional Research Portfolio Governance System (CRPG). The objective is to enable the Constitutional Autonomous Research Civilization (CARC) to autonomously manage, prioritize, expand, suspend, retire, and evolve research programs using constitutional governance. This phase transforms scientific discovery from isolated research into coordinated civilization-scale research planning.

## Constitutional Principle

Research resources are finite. Scientific opportunity is effectively infinite. The civilization must therefore allocate attention, computational resources, scientific capital, engineering effort, and long-term planning according to constitutional priorities rather than local optimization.

## Portfolio Hierarchy

```
Scientific Civilization
    ↓
Research Portfolio (12 categories)
    ↓
Research Programs (long-term objectives)
    ↓
Research Missions (bounded efforts)
    ↓
Projects → Experiments → Discoveries
```

Every layer remains independently replayable.
