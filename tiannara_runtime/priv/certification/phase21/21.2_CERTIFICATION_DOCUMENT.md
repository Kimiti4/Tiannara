## Certification Statement

Phase 21.2 (Constitutional Scientific Opportunity Management) establishes the constitutional framework for maintaining a continuously evolving map of humanity's knowledge frontier and proactively identifying the most valuable scientific opportunities. The architecture provides 8 deterministic discovery mechanisms, 10-dimension prioritization, 8-dimension information gain estimation, 6-stage governance lifecycle, cross-domain opportunity detection with provenance preservation, and complete replay/archaeology reconstruction of the knowledge frontier and all opportunity decisions.
