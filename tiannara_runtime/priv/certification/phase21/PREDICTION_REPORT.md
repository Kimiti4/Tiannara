# Prediction Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive reports on the state, accuracy, and health of the predictive civilization modeling system.

## Report Sections

- Executive Summary
- Future World Inventory
- Scenario Families
- Branch Activity
- Intervention Analysis
- Risk Registry
- Confidence Trends
- Forecast Accuracy
- Replay and Archaeology
- Predictive Recommendations

## Frequency
- Standard: Per generation
- Comprehensive: Per 10 generations
