# Institute Specialization Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional framework for institute specialization, ensuring each institute develops deep expertise in its domains while maintaining awareness of broader scientific context.

## Institute Types

| Institute | Primary Domain | Secondary Domains |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | Formal mathematics, proofs | Computation, Physics |
| Physics | Fundamental physics | Materials, Engineering |
| Chemistry | Chemical systems | Materials, Medicine |
| Medicine | Human health | Chemistry, Biology |
| Engineering | Engineered systems | Physics, Materials |
| Computation | Computing theory | Mathematics, Robotics |
| Cognition | Cognitive architectures | Computation, Philosophy |
| Robotics | Autonomous systems | Engineering, Cognition |
| Energy | Energy systems | Physics, Engineering |
| Agriculture | Food systems | Ecology, Chemistry |
| Ecology | Environmental systems | Agriculture, Energy |
| Governance | Societal systems | Economics, Sociology |
| Aerospace | Space systems | Engineering, Physics |
| Materials | Material science | Chemistry, Engineering |
| Philosophy | Foundational questions | Mathematics, Cognition |
| Economics | Economic systems | Governance, Sociology |
| Sociology | Social systems | Governance, Economics |
| Linguistics | Language systems | Cognition, Computation |
| Architecture | Designed environments | Engineering, Ecology |
| Cross-Domain Integration | Interdisciplinary synthesis | All domains |

## Specialization Depth

Each institute maintains:
- **Core Expertise**: Deep knowledge in primary domain
- **Working Knowledge**: Competent understanding in secondary domains
- **Awareness**: Basic familiarity with all other domains
- **Collaboration Network**: Active partnerships with complementary institutes

## Specialization Boundaries

- Institutes may not claim exclusive domain ownership
- Multiple institutes may research the same domain
- Specialization is competency-based, not territory-based
- Cross-domain institute exists to prevent domain isolation

## Constraints

- Every institute must have a primary and secondary domain
- Institute specialization must be publicly declared
- Institutes may evolve specialization over time
- Specialization changes require federation notification
- No specialization may create constitutional monopolies
