## Certification Statement

Phase 21.5 (Constitutional Research Institute Federation) establishes the constitutional framework for organizing the Autonomous Research Civilization into a federation of 20 specialized constitutional research institutes. The architecture enables autonomous institute governance, inter-institute coordination, provenance-preserving discovery exchange, evidence-based constitutional peer review, healthy scientific competition alongside collaboration, federation-wide priority coordination, and complete replay/archaeology reconstruction of all institutional interactions. Tiannara transforms from a single coordinated research organization into a resilient federation of autonomous institutes operating under a shared constitutional framework.
