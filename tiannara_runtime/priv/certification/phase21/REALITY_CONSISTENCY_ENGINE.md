# Reality Consistency Engine

## Purpose

Measure and monitor consistency across all aspects of the unified reality model, detecting inconsistencies that require reconciliation.

## Consistency Dimensions

### Logical Consistency
- Internal logical coherence of each theory
- No contradictions within a single theory's formal structure
- Deductive closure verification
- Consistency of logical consequences with empirical evidence

### Mathematical Consistency
- Mathematical structures internally consistent
- Formal proofs verified within each mathematical framework
- Cross-framework mathematical compatibility
- Computational consistency of numerical models

### Causal Consistency
- Causal direction preserved across all domains
- No causal cycles in the hierarchy
- Causal relationships consistent with temporal ordering
- Intervention predictions consistent across domains

### Experimental Consistency
- Theory predictions match empirical observations
- Experimental results reproducible within bounds
- Measurement precision consistent across experiments
- Statistical consistency of evidence aggregation

### Simulation Consistency
- Simulation results reproduce when re-run
- Simulation fidelity bounds documented
- Extrapolation beyond validated regime flagged
- Cross-simulation consistency verified

### Cross-Domain Consistency
- Domain theories make compatible predictions at boundaries
- Cross-domain implications validated
- Shared mathematical foundations consistent
- Interdisciplinary predictions reconciled

### Engineering Consistency
- Engineering models consistent with physical laws
- Performance predictions match measured behavior
- Safety bounds consistent with theory
- Engineering approximations bounded and documented

## Consistency Scoring

Each dimension scored 0.0–1.0:
- **0.0–0.3**: Inconsistent — action required
- **0.3–0.7**: Partial consistency — monitoring
- **0.7–0.9**: Largely consistent — minor issues
- **0.9–1.0**: Fully consistent — verified

## Inconsistency Handling

- Inconsistencies trigger reconciliation
- Multiple inconsistencies may indicate deeper structural issues
- Persistent inconsistencies escalate to civilizational strategy
- All consistency scores become constitutional artifacts

## Constraints

- Consistency measurements must be deterministic
- Consistency does not imply correctness
- Inconsistency detection must be continuous
- Consistency records become constitutional artifacts
