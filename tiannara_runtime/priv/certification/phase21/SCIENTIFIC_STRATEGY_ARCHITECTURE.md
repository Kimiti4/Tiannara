# Constitutional Scientific Strategy Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Scientific Strategy System (CSSS) enables the Autonomous Research Civilization to construct, maintain, evaluate, and continuously evolve long-horizon scientific roadmaps spanning decades of research. Rather than optimizing individual missions or portfolios, the civilization develops strategic scientific trajectories that maximize long-term knowledge generation and engineering capability.

## Strategic Hierarchy

```
Civilizational Vision
    ↓
Scientific Strategy
    ↓
Research Roadmaps
    ↓
Strategic Objectives
    ↓
Research Portfolios
    ↓
Scientific Programs
    ↓
Research Missions
    ↓
Experiments
    ↓
Discoveries
```

## Architecture Components

### Strategic Planning Layer
- Formulates long-term scientific strategy from civilizational vision
- Constructs research roadmaps spanning decades
- Defines strategic objectives with evidence-based justification
- Coordinates across all research institutes

### Trajectory Modeling Layer
- Models alternative scientific trajectories toward strategic objectives
- Evaluates required discoveries, theories, mathematics, and engineering
- Estimates uncertainty reduction, risk, and civilizational impact
- Trajectory selection is evidence-based and deterministic

### Priority Allocation Layer
- Ranks strategic objectives using multi-dimension evaluation
- Allocates scientific capital across roadmaps
- Coordinates portfolio alignment with strategic goals
- Balances long-term and short-term priorities

### Dependency Roadmapping Layer
- Constructs knowledge dependency DAGs across strategic objectives
- Identifies critical path discoveries and bottlenecks
- Maps theory-to-engineering dependency chains
- Ensures acyclic dependency structure

### Civilization Alignment Layer
- Ensures all research institutes contribute to strategic objectives
- Alerts on misalignment between strategy and execution
- Coordinates cross-institute strategic collaboration
- Monitors strategic coherence across the ecosystem

## Constitutional Constraints

- All strategic decisions must be evidence-derived
- Roadmaps must preserve replay and archaeology roots
- Trajectory evaluation must be deterministic
- Dependency graphs must remain acyclic
- Strategy must preserve constitutional continuity
- Human constitutional authority retains strategic override

## Integration Points

- **Input**: Phase 21.6 (ecosystem state), Phase 21.2 (knowledge frontier), Phase 21.1 (portfolios)
- **Output**: Strategic objectives for Phase 21.3 (mission generation) and Phase 21.4 (orchestration)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Scientific strategy is constitutionally defined
- Roadmaps remain deterministic
- Strategic trajectories preserve provenance
- Knowledge dependency graphs remain acyclic
- Alignment across institutes is measurable
- Replay reconstructs every strategic decision
- Archaeology reconstructs long-term scientific evolution
- No runtime implementation exists
