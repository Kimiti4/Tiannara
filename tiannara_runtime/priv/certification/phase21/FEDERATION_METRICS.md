# Federation Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the health, diversity, productivity, and constitutional compliance of the research institute federation.

## Metrics

### Institute Count
- Total active institutes
- Institutes per domain
- New institute formation rate
- Institute closure rate

### Institute Diversity
- Domain coverage
- Specialization distribution
- Research approach diversity
- Size distribution

### Cross-Institute Collaboration
- Active collaborations
- Collaborations per institute
- Cross-domain collaboration ratio
- Collaboration success rate

### Discovery Exchange Rate
- Artifacts exchanged per generation
- Exchange volume by type
- Exchange latency
- Cross-institute discovery propagation

### Peer Review Throughput
- Submissions per generation
- Reviews completed per generation
- Average review duration
- Acceptance rate

### Scientific Capital Distribution
- Capital per institute
- Capital inequality (Gini index)
- Capital efficiency by institute
- Capital flow between institutes

### Federation Productivity
- Total discoveries per generation
- Cross-institute discovery ratio
- Knowledge frontier advancement rate
- Engineering artifact production

### Replay Stability
- Federation replay pass rate
- Hash match percentage
- Replay failure count
- Root hash convergence

### Archaeology Completeness
- Complete federation archaeology records
- Archaeology coverage ratio
- Independent reconstruction success rate
- Archaeology gap count

### Institutional Resilience
- Institute survival rate
- Crisis recovery time
- Adaptation rate
- Redundancy coverage

### Civilization Knowledge Growth
- Total knowledge graph size
- Knowledge growth rate
- Knowledge diversity index
- Knowledge frontier coverage

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active Institutes | 10 | 20 | 30+ |
| Cross-Institute Collaboration Ratio | 30% | 50% | 70%+ |
| Discovery Exchange Rate | 50/gen | 200/gen | 500+/gen |
| Peer Review Throughput | 20/gen | 50/gen | 100+/gen |
| Scientific Capital Gini | <0.40 | <0.30 | <0.20 |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |

## Recording

All metrics must be deterministically computable from federation artifacts. Metrics become part of the constitutional record and must be replayable.
