# Multi-Domain World Model

## Purpose

Construct integrated world models that span multiple domains of reality, creating coherent multi-layer representations of physical, biological, social, and engineered systems.

## World Model Layers

### Layer 1: Physical World
- Fundamental physics and cosmology
- Planetary systems and geology
- Climate and atmospheric science
- Oceanography and hydrology

### Layer 2: Biological Systems
- Ecosystems and biodiversity
- Organism physiology
- Genetics and evolution
- Biochemical networks

### Layer 3: Human Civilization
- Social structures and institutions
- Economic systems and markets
- Cultural and knowledge systems
- Governance and legal frameworks

### Layer 4: Engineering Infrastructure
- Physical infrastructure networks
- Energy generation and distribution
- Transportation and logistics
- Communication systems

### Layer 5: Autonomous Systems
- Robotic and AI systems
- Automated production
- Autonomous logistics
- Self-regulating infrastructure

### Layer 6: Scientific Institutions
- Research organizations
- Knowledge production systems
- Scientific communication networks
- Epistemic infrastructure

### Layer 7: Knowledge Systems
- Formal mathematical structures
- Scientific theories and models
- Computational simulations
- Knowledge graphs and ontologies

## Layer Interaction

- Upper layers depend on lower layers
- Lower layers constrain upper layers
- Cross-layer feedback documented
- Emergent properties at layer boundaries

## World Model Construction

### Integration Process
1. Collect domain reality models
2. Map cross-domain interactions
3. Identify layer dependencies
4. Construct integrated world model
5. Validate consistency across layers

### Consistency Requirements
- Each layer internally consistent
- Adjacent layers consistent at boundaries
- Cross-layer causal chains valid
- Emergent properties documented

## Constraints

- All layers must be evidence-based
- Layer dependencies must be acyclic
- Cross-layer interactions must be documented
- World model must be replayable
- World model records become constitutional artifacts
