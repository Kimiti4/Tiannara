# Multi-World Forecast Engine

## Purpose

Generate and maintain multiple competing future world models, representing diverse possible trajectories for civilization's evolution.

## Scenario Families

### Baseline Continuation
- Current trends continue without major disruption
- Incremental scientific and engineering progress
- Standard institutional evolution

### Accelerated Scientific Progress
- Breakthrough discoveries compress timelines
- Theory unification accelerates
- Engineering capability grows rapidly

### Technological Transformation
- Revolutionary engineering capabilities emerge
- Autonomous systems transform infrastructure
- New scientific frontiers open

### Climate Adaptation
- Climate-driven changes reshape priorities
- Adaptation and mitigation research dominates
- Ecological engineering advances

### Energy Transformation
- Energy systems fundamentally restructured
- New energy sources enable expanded capability
- Resource constraints relieved

### Exploration Expansion
- Significant expansion beyond Earth
- Space-based research infrastructure
- New observational capabilities

### Unexpected Discovery
- Paradigm-shifting discovery restructures science
- New domains of reality revealed
- Current theories fundamentally revised

### Disruption Scenarios
- Major disruptions: resource constraints, systemic failures
- Resilience and recovery research
- Institutional adaptation

## Forecast Generation

### Step 1: Scenario Definition
- Define scenario family parameters
- Specify key assumptions and drivers
- Document scenario rationale

### Step 2: World Construction
- Project current reality model forward under scenario assumptions
- Apply scenario-specific trends and events
- Generate future world state

### Step 3: Confidence Assignment
- Assign evidence-weighted confidence to each scenario
- Document confidence rationale
- Maintain uncertainty bounds

### Step 4: Ensemble Maintenance
- Add new scenarios as evidence warrants
- Prune unsupported scenarios
- Adjust confidence distributions

## Constraints

- No future is privileged without evidence
- All scenarios must be derived from current reality model
- Scenario generation must be deterministic
- Forecast records become constitutional artifacts
