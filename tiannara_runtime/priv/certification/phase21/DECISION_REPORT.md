# Decision Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive reports on the state, quality, and constitutional compliance of civilizational decision-making.

## Report Sections

- Executive Summary
- Decisions Made
- Future Selection Rationale
- Intervention Plan Status
- Priority Allocation Overview
- Constitutional Compliance
- Replay and Archaeology
- Civilization Recommendations

## Frequency
- Standard: Per generation
- Comprehensive: Per 10 generations
