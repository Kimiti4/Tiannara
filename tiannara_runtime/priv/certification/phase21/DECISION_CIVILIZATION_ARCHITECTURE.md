# Constitutional Scientific Decision Civilization Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Scientific Decision Civilization (CSDCS) is the final component of the Constitutional Applications research pipeline. It transforms predictive insights from CPCMS into constitutionally governed decisions, closing the loop from observation through prediction to action — always preserving human authority.

## Constitutional Principle

The Scientific Decision Civilization advises. Human civilization decides. All decisions are constitutionally bounded, evidence-informed, and fully transparent.

## Decision Hierarchy

```
Predictive Civilization Model (CPCMS)
    ↓
Decision Civilization Model (CSDCS)
    ↓
Future Selection → Decision Comparison → Strategic Planning → Priority Allocation
    ↓
Constitutional Governance → Human Authority Interface
    ↓
Civilizational Action
    ↓
Outcome Observation → Evidence Feedback
```

## Architecture Components

### Civilizational Decision Engine
- Selects among competing future worlds
- Compares intervention strategies across futures
- Generates constitutionally bounded decision recommendations

### Future Selection Model
- Evaluates futures against constitutional values
- Scores futures on scientific progress, resilience, risk
- Presents ranked futures with uncertainty bounds

### Strategic Intervention Planning
- Transforms decisions into intervention plans
- Maps interventions to institutes and programs
- Schedules phased implementation

### Priority Allocation Engine
- Allocates research priorities across domains
- Distributes resources among institutes
- Balances short-term and long-term objectives

### Constitutional Governance Model
- Ensures all decisions respect constitutional bounds
- Preserves human authority in all critical decisions
- Provides transparency and audit trails

## Integration Points

- **Input**: Phase 21.9 (predictive models), Phase 21.5 (federation)
- **Output**: Constitutional decisions for civilizational action
- **Foundation**: CSOS v1.0 constitutional guarantees

## Acceptance Criteria

- Decisions are constitutionally bounded
- Human authority is preserved
- Future selection is evidence-driven
- Intervention planning is deterministic
- Priority allocation is reproducible
- Replay reconstructs every decision
- Archaeology reconstructs decision evolution
- No runtime implementation exists
