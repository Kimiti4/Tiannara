# Discovery Frontier Engine

## Purpose

Manage the active discovery frontier — the set of scientific opportunities currently being pursued — tracking frontier velocity, coverage, and bottlenecks across the research civilization.

## Frontier State

The discovery frontier consists of three opportunity classes:

### Active Opportunities
- Opportunities currently being investigated through active missions
- Resource-allocated and scheduled for execution
- Regular progress tracking and milestone monitoring

### Pending Opportunities
- Prioritized opportunities awaiting resource availability
- Dependency-satisfied but not yet scheduled
- Queue ordered by priority score

### Completed Opportunities
- Opportunities where knowledge gap has been closed
- Discoveries integrated into established knowledge
- Archaeological record finalized

## Frontier Metrics

### Frontier Velocity
- Opportunities processed per generation
- Knowledge gap closure rate
- Frontier layer advancement speed
- Discovery-to-integration cycle time

### Frontier Coverage
- Fraction of frontier actively pursued
- Domain coverage distribution
- Priority tier coverage completeness
- Opportunity-to-mission conversion rate

### Bottleneck Detection
- Resource constraints limiting frontier progress
- Dependency chains blocking opportunity advancement
- Domain-specific constraints
- Cross-domain coordination gaps

## Frontier Evolution

The frontier evolves through:
1. New opportunities discovered by Opportunity Discovery Engine
2. Active opportunities transitioning to completed
3. Pending opportunities promoted to active
4. Completed discoveries advancing the knowledge frontier

## Determinism

Frontier state transitions must be fully deterministic given opportunity priorities and resource availability.

## Constraints

- Every opportunity must be in exactly one frontier class
- Frontier velocity must be measurable and bounded
- Bottlenecks must be identifiable and reportable
- Frontier state must be fully replayable
- Frontier records become constitutional artifacts
