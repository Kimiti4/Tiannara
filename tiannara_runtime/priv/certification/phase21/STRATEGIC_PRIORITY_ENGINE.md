# Strategic Priority Engine

## Purpose

Rank and allocate strategic objectives using deterministic prioritization, ensuring civilization-scale scientific capital is directed toward the highest-value long-term goals.

## Prioritization Dimensions

### Civilizational Benefit (Weight: 0.20)
- Long-term humanity benefit
- Existential risk reduction
- Quality of life improvement
- Sustainability contribution

### Scientific Importance (Weight: 0.18)
- Fundamental knowledge advancement
- Theory unification potential
- Paradigm shift potential
- Knowledge frontier breadth

### Engineering Leverage (Weight: 0.15)
- Engineering capability multiplier
- Infrastructure advancement
- Instrumentation improvement
- Technology readiness progression

### Knowledge Dependency Reduction (Weight: 0.12)
- Prerequisite knowledge closure
- Bottleneck discovery impact
- Critical path acceleration
- Dependency chain shortening

### Cross-Domain Influence (Weight: 0.12)
- Domains affected per objective
- Unification potential
- Methodology export
- Interdisciplinary discovery enablement

### Long-Term Sustainability (Weight: 0.10)
- Research continuity maintenance
- Institutional knowledge preservation
- Generational knowledge transfer
- Civilizational resilience

### Expected Scientific Capital Return (Weight: 0.08)
- Knowledge value per capital unit
- Discovery density per investment
- Engineering artifact production
- Long-term capital generation

### Strategic Urgency (Weight: 0.05)
- Window of opportunity
- Competitive advantage potential
- Timeliness of discovery
- Precedent establishment

## Composite Priority Score

```
Priority = Σ(weight_i × score_i) for all dimensions
```

## Priority Classification

| Score Range | Priority Level | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0.80–1.00 | Critical | Immediate strategic action |
| 0.60–0.79 | High | Expedited roadmap creation |
| 0.40–0.59 | Medium | Standard strategic planning |
| 0.20–0.39 | Low | Monitor and reassess |
| 0.00–0.19 | Background | Strategic awareness only |

## Constraints

- Weights must sum to 1.0
- All dimension scores must be deterministically computed
- Priority scores must be fully replayable
- No stochastic prioritization methods
- Priority records become constitutional artifacts
