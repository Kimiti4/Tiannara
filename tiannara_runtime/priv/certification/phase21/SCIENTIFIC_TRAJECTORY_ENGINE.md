# Scientific Trajectory Engine

## Purpose

Model alternative scientific trajectories toward strategic objectives, enabling evidence-based comparison and selection of optimal research paths.

## Trajectory Components

Each trajectory contains:
- **Required Discoveries**: Ordered set of knowledge milestones
- **Required Theories**: Theoretical advances needed
- **Required Mathematics**: Formal structures required
- **Required Engineering**: Engineering capabilities needed
- **Expected Uncertainty Reduction**: Knowledge gain per milestone
- **Risk Profile**: Identified risks with evidence basis
- **Civilizational Impact**: Expected benefit estimation
- **Capital Requirement**: Total resource need
- **Time to Completion**: Estimated duration range

## Trajectory Types

### Direct Trajectory
- Shortest path to objective
- Highest risk from knowledge gaps
- Maximum dependency on pristin discoveries

### Incremental Trajectory
- Sequential milestone progression
- Lower per-step risk
- Longer total duration

### Parallel Trajectory
- Simultaneous pursuit of multiple approaches
- Risk diversification
- Higher resource demand

### Exploratory Trajectory
- Deliberate uncertainty investigation
- Knowledge gathering before commitment
- Pre-decisional and reversible

### Transformative Trajectory
- Targets paradigm-shifting discoveries
- High risk, high reward
- May bypass incremental path

## Trajectory Comparison

### Evaluation Dimensions
- Probability of success within horizon
- Expected information gain
- Resource efficiency (gain per capital unit)
- Risk-adjusted return
- Cross-domain benefit
- Long-term adaptability

### Selection Algorithm
- Evaluate all candidate trajectories
- Score each dimension deterministically
- Compute composite trajectory score
- Rank and present recommendations
- Preserve all alternatives for archaeology

## Constraints

- All trajectory modeling must be deterministic
- Trajectories must preserve complete lineage
- Comparison must be evidence-based with uncertainty bounds
- All alternatives must be archaeologically preserved
- Trajectory selection records become constitutional artifacts
