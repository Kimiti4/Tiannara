# Institute Retirement Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional framework for retiring research institutes, ensuring knowledge is preserved, lineage is maintained, and the ecosystem remains healthy.

## Retirement Conditions

### Mission Completion
- Institute's founding scientific mandate is fulfilled
- All primary objectives achieved
- No remaining core questions in domain
- Successor capability exists elsewhere in federation

### Scientific Redundancy
- Another institute covers the domain equally or better
- Distinctive capability no longer unique
- Resource consolidation would improve efficiency
- Diversity not significantly impacted

### Domain Integration
- Institute's domain absorbed by broader field
- Domain boundaries no longer scientifically meaningful
- Cross-domain integration renders specialization obsolete
- Knowledge integrated into civilization knowledge base

### Successor Institute Established
- New institute explicitly formed from this institute's work
- Knowledge and personnel transferred
- Legacy preserved in successor charter
- Retirement enables fresh institutional evolution

### Inactivity
- No active missions for 20 consecutive generations
- No discoveries for 10 consecutive generations
- No collaborations for 10 consecutive generations
- Federation council confirms inactivity

## Retirement Process

### Step 1: Retirement Assessment
- Evaluate against retirement conditions
- Knowledge preservation inventory
- Resource reallocation plan
- Diversity impact analysis

### Step 2: Knowledge Preservation
- Complete knowledge graph export
- Discovery archive certification
- Methodology and tool preservation
- Lineage documentation

### Step 3: Resource Reallocation
- Scientific capital redistribution
- Personnel reassignment plan
- Infrastructure transfer
- Resource efficiency assessment

### Step 4: Constitutional Certification
- CSOS v1.0 compliance verified
- Knowledge preservation confirmed
- Replay and archaeology finalization
- Retirement recorded in constitutional record

### Step 5: Closure
- Institute status set to Retired
- Charter archived in perpetuity
- Knowledge legacy accessible to all institutes
- Ecosystem state updated

## Knowledge Preservation Guarantee

- All discoveries remain accessible after retirement
- Methods and tools remain available
- Knowledge graph contributions persist
- Lineage preserved for future archaeology
- No knowledge is lost through retirement

## Constraints

- Retirement must never destroy knowledge
- Retirement must be evidence-based
- Resource reallocation must be fair
- Lineage must be preserved in perpetuity
- Retirement records become constitutional artifacts
