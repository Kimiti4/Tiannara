# Federation Governance Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional governance framework for the federation of research institutes, coordinating global priorities, resource balancing, and cross-domain initiatives while preserving institute autonomy.

## Federation Governance Structure

### Federation Council
- Representatives from each institute
- Rotating council leadership
- Constitutional decision-making authority
- Global priority coordination

### Priority Coordination
- Civilization-wide research priorities
- Long-horizon initiative planning
- Emergency research mobilization
- Constitutional mandate enforcement

### Resource Balancing
- Cross-institute resource distribution
- Fair share guarantees enforcement
- Strategic reserve allocation
- Capital rebalancing decisions

### Cross-Domain Integration
- Interdisciplinary initiative coordination
- Knowledge frontier gap detection
- Cross-domain program sponsorship
- Integration of discoveries across domains

### Long-Horizon Initiatives
- Multi-generational research programs
- Civilizational capability building
- Existential risk research
- Generational knowledge preservation

### Emergency Research
- Crisis detection and assessment
- Rapid resource mobilization
- Priority override (constitutionally bounded)
- Post-emergency recovery coordination

## Governance Principles

- Federation cannot override individual institute autonomy on internal matters
- Federation decisions require constitutional compliance verification
- All governance decisions are deterministic and replayable
- Council representation must be proportional and fair
- Governance records become constitutional artifacts

## Decision Making

- Strategic decisions: 2/3 council majority
- Constitutional decisions: Unanimous + CSOS verification
- Emergency decisions: Federation council chair + constitutional review
- Operational decisions: Simple majority

## Constraints

- Federation governance cannot violate CSOS v1.0
- Institute autonomy is constitutionally protected
- All governance decisions must be replayable
- Governance archaeology must be complete
- Emergency powers are bounded by constitutional limits
