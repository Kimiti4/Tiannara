# Intervention Evaluation Engine

## Purpose

Model and evaluate constitutional interventions across future worlds, providing evidence-based assessment of expected outcomes and risks.

## Intervention Families

### Scientific Investment
- Research capital allocation increases
- New institute formation
- Mission portfolio expansion
- Strategic research programs

### Engineering Investment
- Infrastructure development programs
- Instrumentation projects
- Simulation capability expansion
- Engineering education programs

### Policy Changes
- Priority reallocation
- Collaboration incentives
- Resource redistribution
- Constitutional adjustments (within bounds)

### Infrastructure Development
- Compute capacity expansion
- Experimental facility construction
- Data storage and archival
- Communication network enhancement

### Educational Expansion
- Knowledge dissemination programs
- Researcher training initiatives
- Cross-domain education
- Public science engagement

### Collaborative Programs
- Cross-institute initiatives
- International scientific cooperation
- Interdisciplinary research programs
- Knowledge sharing infrastructure

## Evaluation Dimensions

- **Expected Benefit**: Projected positive outcomes (0.0–1.0)
- **Expected Risk**: Projected negative outcomes (0.0–1.0)
- **Uncertainty Reduction**: How much uncertainty the intervention resolves
- **Scientific Leverage**: Knowledge gain per unit investment
- **Civilizational Resilience**: Long-term robustness improvement
- **Implementation Feasibility**: Likelihood of successful execution

## Intervention Comparison

- All interventions evaluated across all future worlds
- Scores aggregated with uncertainty bounds
- Trade-offs explicitly documented
- Ranked by risk-adjusted expected benefit

## Constraints

- Intervention evaluation must be deterministic
- All scores must have uncertainty bounds
- Multiple futures must be evaluated
- Intervention records become constitutional artifacts
