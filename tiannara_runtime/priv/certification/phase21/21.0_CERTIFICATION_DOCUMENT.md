## Certification Statement

Phase 21.0 (Constitutional Autonomous Research Civilization) establishes the constitutional architecture for the first Autonomous Research Civilization built upon the Tiannara Constitutional Scientific Operating System. The architecture transforms Tiannara from a scientific operating system into a permanent scientific institution capable of coordinating large-scale, multi-domain research through deterministic governance, evidence-first reasoning, mathematical rigor, reproducible knowledge growth, and complete archaeological traceability. This phase operates as a CSOS extension — it inherits all CSOS 1.0 constitutional guarantees without modifying the foundational baseline.
