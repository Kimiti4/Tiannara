# Constitutional Autonomous Research Civilization Architecture (Phase 21.0)

## Mission

Phase 21 begins Era II of Tiannara. Phases 1–20 constructed the Constitutional Scientific Operating System (CSOS). Phase 21 uses that operating system to create the first Constitutional Autonomous Research Civilization (CARC).

The objective is not merely to automate research, but to architect an institution capable of continuously generating, prioritizing, executing, validating, preserving, and expanding scientific knowledge across all research domains while remaining governed by constitutional principles.

## Constitutional Principle

Research is a constitutional process. Scientific progress emerges through evidence, reproducibility, governance, collaboration, and continual refinement rather than isolated discovery. Every research decision must remain evidence-first, mathematically grounded, replayable, archaeologically reconstructible, and constitutionally governed.

## Architecture Layers

```
Layer 1: Civilization Governance
  Scientific Council, Engineering Council, Mathematics Council, Human Oversight
  Constitutional governance, policy enforcement, capital allocation

Layer 2: Research Institutes
  20 constitutional institutes covering all research domains
  Institute charters, governance councils, capital accounts

Layer 3: Research Programs
  Long-term scientific objectives within each institute
  Mission statements, dependencies, capital allocation, priority

Layer 4: Research Missions
  Bounded scientific efforts with defined lifecycle
  Proposal → Approval → Execution → Validation → Integration → Archive

Layer 5: Discovery Portfolios
  Categorized portfolios across the civilization
  Foundational Science, Applied Science, Engineering, High-Risk, etc.

Layer 6: Scientific Capital
  Civilization-wide capital governance and reinvestment
  Reproducibility, impact, novelty, cross-domain influence
```

## Key Properties

- Civilization operates through constitutional governance — no institute governs itself
- All research is deterministic, replayable, and archaeologically preserved
- Scientific capital governs resource allocation constitutionally
- Cross-domain collaboration preserves provenance
- Knowledge growth is measurable and constitutionally protected
