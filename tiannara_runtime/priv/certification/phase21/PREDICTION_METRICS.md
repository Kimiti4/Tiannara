# Prediction Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the accuracy, diversity, and health of the predictive civilization modeling system.

## Metrics

- **Forecast Accuracy**: Historical prediction vs. outcome comparison
- **Prediction Calibration**: Confidence calibration accuracy
- **Scenario Diversity**: Number of active distinct future scenarios
- **Branch Stability**: Average branch lifetime
- **Risk Coverage**: Fraction of identified risks with mitigation plans
- **Intervention Effectiveness**: Predicted vs. actual intervention outcomes
- **Uncertainty Resolution**: Rate of uncertainty reduction through evidence
- **Long-Horizon Consistency**: Cross-horizon prediction coherence
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Predictive Civilization Readiness**: Overall predictive maturity index

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active Future Worlds | 3 | 8 | 20+ |
| Forecast Accuracy | 0.60 | 0.75 | 0.90+ |
| Calibration Error | <0.15 | <0.10 | <0.05 |
| Risk Coverage | 0.50 | 0.75 | 0.95+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
