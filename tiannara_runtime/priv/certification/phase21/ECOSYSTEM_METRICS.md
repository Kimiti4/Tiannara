# Ecosystem Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the health, diversity, adaptability, and constitutional compliance of the scientific ecosystem.

## Metrics

### Scientific Diversity Index
- Composite diversity across all dimensions
- Shannon index per dimension
- Overall ecosystem diversity score (0.0–1.0)
- Diversity trend (improving/stable/declining)

### Institution Count
- Total active institutes
- Institute formation rate (per 10 generations)
- Institute retirement rate
- Net institute growth

### Collaboration Density
- Actual collaborations / possible collaborations
- Average collaboration strength
- Collaboration distribution (per institute)
- Cross-domain collaboration ratio

### Knowledge Exchange Rate
- Discoveries exchanged per generation
- Exchange volume by artifact type
- Cross-institute knowledge propagation speed
- Knowledge retention rate

### Institute Evolution Rate
- Transitions per generation
- Evolution type distribution
- Transition success rate
- Average institute lifetime

### Scientific Capital Distribution
- Capital per institute (Gini index)
- Capital efficiency by institute
- Capital flow volume between institutes
- Capital concentration risk

### Cross-Domain Connectivity
- Cross-domain collaboration edges
- Bridge institute identification
- Cross-domain discovery rate
- Domain boundary permeability

### Civilizational Adaptability
- Adaptation frequency
- Adaptation success rate
- Average adaptation completion time
- Reversal rate (if any)

### Replay Stability
- Ecosystem replay pass rate
- Hash match percentage
- Replay failure count
- Root hash convergence

### Archaeology Completeness
- Complete ecosystem archaeology records
- Archaeology coverage ratio
- Independent reconstruction success rate
- Archaeology gap count

### Long-Term Ecosystem Resilience
- Ecosystem survival through paradigm shifts
- Recovery from incorrect theories
- Institutional stability during crises
- Knowledge retention across generations

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scientific Diversity Index | 0.60 | 0.75 | 0.90+ |
| Active Institutes | 10 | 20 | 30+ |
| Collaboration Density | 0.20 | 0.35 | 0.50+ |
| Knowledge Exchange Rate | 50/gen | 200/gen | 500+/gen |
| Institute Evolution Rate | 1/10gen | 3/10gen | 5+/10gen |
| Capital Gini Index | <0.40 | <0.30 | <0.20 |
| Cross-Domain Connectivity | 20% | 35% | 50%+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |

## Recording

All metrics must be deterministically computable from ecosystem artifacts. Metrics become part of the constitutional record and must be replayable.
