# Institute Formation Model

## Purpose

Define the constitutional framework for creating new research institutes within the federation, ensuring each new institute has clear purpose, evidence basis, and complete lineage.

## Formation Triggers

### Emergence of a New Scientific Discipline
- A novel domain achieves sufficient maturity
- Knowledge frontier shows distinct cluster of activity
- At least 10 missions active in the domain
- Critical mass of discoveries requiring dedicated institution

### Persistent Cross-Domain Activity
- Sustained collaboration across 3+ domains over 10+ generations
- Discovery rate exceeds single-institute capacity
- Unique interdisciplinary methodology developed
- Dedicated institute would accelerate progress

### Knowledge Graph Modularity
- Knowledge graph analysis reveals natural domain boundary
- Internal coherence exceeds cross-boundary connections
- Modularity metric exceeds threshold (0.7)
- Independent institute justified by graph structure

### Scientific Critical Mass
- Research activity in domain reaches threshold:
  - 50+ active missions
  - 10+ discoveries per generation
  - 100+ active researchers (or equivalent compute)
- Dedicated coordination required

### Civilizational Necessity
- Existential risk identified requiring dedicated research
- Constitutional mandate for new institute
- Human constitutional authority directive
- Emergency research requiring permanent institution

## Formation Process

### Step 1: Proposal
- Evidence dossier compiled
- Proposed charter drafted
- Resource requirements estimated
- Constitutional compliance pre-checked

### Step 2: Federation Review
- Federation council evaluates proposal
- Impact on existing institutes assessed
- Diversity impact analyzed
- Resource availability verified

### Step 3: Constitutional Certification
- CSOS v1.0 compliance verified
- Constitutional invariants confirmed
- Replay and archaeology roots initialized

### Step 4: Institute Charter
- Official charter issued
- Domain mandate established
- Resource budget allocated
- Replay and archaeology roots set

### Step 5: Launch
- Institute becomes active
- Initial programs and missions seeded
- Collaboration partners identified
- Ecosystem state updated

## Lineage Preservation

Every institute formation records:
- Parent institute(s) or origin context
- Formation trigger and evidence
- Charter evolution from founding
- Complete lineage chain

## Constraints

- Every institute must have a clear formation trigger
- Formation must be evidence-based
- Institute must have independent viability
- Lineage must be complete and replayable
- Formation records become constitutional artifacts
