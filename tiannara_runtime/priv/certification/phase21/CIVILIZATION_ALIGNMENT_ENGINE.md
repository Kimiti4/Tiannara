# Civilization Alignment Engine

## Purpose

Ensure all research institutes, portfolios, mission generation, scientific capital, and engineering priorities are aligned with strategic objectives, detecting and correcting misalignment continuously.

## Alignment Dimensions

### Institute Alignment
- Each institute's research portfolio mapped to strategic objectives
- Institute mission contributions measured against strategy
- Cross-institute strategic collaboration tracked
- Institute-level strategic awareness verified

### Portfolio Alignment
- Portfolio allocation consistency with strategic priorities
- Strategic objective coverage across portfolios
- Resource allocation alignment with strategic importance
- Portfolio rebalancing guided by strategy

### Mission Generation Alignment
- Mission objectives traceable to strategic goals
- Knowledge dependency satisfaction strategy-driven
- Cross-domain mission alignment with strategic trajectories
- Mission priority influenced by strategic urgency

### Scientific Capital Alignment
- Capital allocation aligned with strategic priorities
- Strategic reserve allocation for long-horizon objectives
- Capital flow toward critical path discoveries
- Capital efficiency measured against strategic return

### Engineering Priority Alignment
- Engineering projects aligned with strategic milestones
- Infrastructure development supporting strategic objectives
- Instrumentation investment guided by strategic needs
- Simulation capability built for strategic roadmap

## Alignment Monitoring

### Alignment Metrics
- Fraction of institute research mapped to strategic objectives
- Portfolio-strategy correlation score
- Mission-to-strategy traceability percentage
- Capital-strategy alignment ratio

### Misalignment Detection
- Institute activity outside strategic objectives flagged
- Portfolio allocation inconsistent with priorities detected
- Mission generation diverging from strategy identified
- Capital flows misaligned with strategic needs reported

### Rebalancing Actions
- Strategic rebalancing recommendations per institute
- Portfolio reallocation proposals
- Mission reprioritization guidance
- Capital redistribution recommendations

## Constraints

- Alignment monitoring must be continuous and deterministic
- Misalignment detection must trigger constitutional notification
- Rebalancing recommendations must be evidence-based
- Alignment decisions preserve institute autonomy within strategy bounds
- Alignment records become constitutional artifacts
