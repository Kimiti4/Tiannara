# Federation Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive reports on the state and performance of the research institute federation.

## Report Sections

### Executive Summary
- Active institutes and domains
- Federation productivity overview
- Key collaboration metrics
- Notable discoveries and reviews

### Institute Registry
- Current institute roster
- Institute specializations
- Institute health indicators
- New and closing institutes

### Collaboration Network
- Active collaboration map
- Collaboration type distribution
- Cross-domain collaboration summary
- Resource sharing overview

### Discovery Exchange
- Exchange volume by artifact type
- Exchange provenance verification
- Knowledge propagation metrics
- Attribution integrity report

### Peer Review Status
- Submissions under review
- Completed reviews this period
- Review outcomes distribution
- Pending reviews and bottlenecks

### Competition Landscape
- Active competitive domains
- Competing research approaches
- Competitive discovery rate
- Conflict resolution status

### Federation Governance
- Recent council decisions
- Priority coordination status
- Resource balancing actions
- Emergency responses (if any)

### Scientific Capital
- Capital distribution report
- Capital efficiency by institute
- Capital flow analysis
- Strategic reserve status

### Replay and Archaeology
- Replay verification timestamp
- Replay pass/fail status
- Archaeology coverage
- Pending archaeology gaps

### Constitutional Compliance
- Federation compliance verification
- Autonomy violation report (if any)
- Pending constitutional review
- Compliance trend

## Report Generation

Reports must be deterministically generated from federation state. No interpretation, no adjustment, no learning. Reports become constitutional artifacts.

## Frequency
- Standard: Per generation
- Comprehensive: Per 10 generations
- Audit: On constitutional review request
