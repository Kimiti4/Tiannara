# Decision Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the quality, constitutionality, and effectiveness of civilizational decisions.

## Metrics

- **Decision Quality**: Outcome vs. predicted outcome comparison
- **Constitutional Compliance Rate**: Fraction of decisions passing constitutional verification
- **Human Review Ratio**: Fraction of decisions requiring human confirmation
- **Decision Timeliness**: Average time from evidence to decision
- **Intervention Success Rate**: Planned vs. actual intervention outcomes
- **Allocation Efficiency**: Resource utilization vs. allocation
- **Risk Reduction Achievement**: Actual risk reduction vs. projected
- **Replay Stability**: Target 99.9%
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Target 99%
- **Decision Civilization Readiness**: Overall decision maturity index

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Constitutional Compliance | 95% | 99% | 100% |
| Decision Quality | 0.60 | 0.75 | 0.90+ |
| Intervention Success | 0.50 | 0.70 | 0.85+ |
| Allocation Efficiency | 0.60 | 0.80 | 0.95+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
