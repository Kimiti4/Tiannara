# Interdisciplinary Collaboration (Phase 21.0)

## Purpose

Define the constitutional framework for interdisciplinary collaboration across research institutes. Collaboration must preserve provenance — every contribution must be traceable to its source.

## Collaboration Architecture

Collaboration connects institutes through shared missions, programs, and discoveries:

```
Institute A (Physics) ←→ Institute B (Engineering)
    │                         │
    ├── Shared Mission         ├── Shared Mission
    ├── Cross-domain discovery ├── Cross-domain discovery
    └── Joint capital pool     └── Joint capital pool
```

## Established Collaboration Pathways

| Domain Pair | Collaboration Type | Typical Outputs |
|-------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Physics ↔ Engineering | Applied physics | Engineered systems from physical principles |
| Chemistry ↔ Materials | Molecular materials | New materials from chemical synthesis |
| Medicine ↔ Biology | Biomedical | Treatments and therapies |
| Computation ↔ Robotics | Autonomous systems | Intelligent robots |
| Energy ↔ Ecology | Sustainable energy | Environmental impact assessment |
| Governance ↔ Economics | Policy economics | Constitutional economic models |
| Architecture ↔ Logistics | Built environment | Infrastructure planning |
| Cognition ↔ Computation | Artificial intelligence | Cognitive architectures |
| Mathematics ↔ Physics | Theoretical physics | Mathematical models of physical systems |
| Aerospace ↔ Engineering | Space systems | Orbital and flight systems |

## Provenance Preservation

Every cross-domain collaboration must preserve provenance:
1. Each contribution is content-addressed with source institute
2. Collaboration agreements are recorded as constitutional contracts
3. Joint discoveries track contribution ratios
4. Capital from joint discoveries is split deterministically
5. Full replay chain preserves collaboration history

## Collaboration Lifecycle

```
Proposal → (Institutes agree on scope and terms)
Agreement → (Constitutional contract recorded)
Execution → (Joint mission or program)
Discovery → (Joint discovery with contribution tracking)
Capital Settlement → (Deterministic capital split)
Archive → (Complete collaboration history preserved)
```
