# Institutional Evolution Engine

## Purpose

Define deterministic rules for institutional evolution within the scientific ecosystem, ensuring all transitions are constitutional, evidence-driven, and fully reconstructible.

## Evolution Transition Types

### Expansion
Institute broadens its scientific mandate or domain coverage.
- Trigger: Successful cross-domain discoveries
- Evidence: Demonstrated capability in new domain
- Constitutional check: Mandate expansion within bounds

### Specialization
Institute narrows focus to deepen expertise.
- Trigger: Significant discoveries in sub-domain
- Evidence: Comparative advantage analysis
- Constitutional check: No domain abandonment without coverage

### Merger
Two or more institutes combine into a single entity.
- Trigger: Sustained collaboration, overlapping mandates
- Evidence: Collaboration history, synergy analysis
- Constitutional check: Diversity impact assessment

### Division
A single institute splits into two or more entities.
- Trigger: Internal specialization divergence, critical mass
- Evidence: Sub-domain independence analysis
- Constitutional check: Both entities viable independently

### Transformation
Institute changes its fundamental structure or mandate.
- Trigger: Scientific paradigm shift
- Evidence: New paradigm requires institutional change
- Constitutional check: Core identity preservation

### Retirement
Institute ceases operations after fulfilling its mission.
- Trigger: Mission completion, redundancy, domain integration
- Evidence: Mission completeness verification
- Constitutional check: Knowledge preservation guaranteed

### Successor Formation
A new institute forms from the legacy of a retired one.
- Trigger: Retirement decision with ongoing relevance
- Evidence: Continued scientific value
- Constitutional check: Lineage preservation

## Transition Requirements

Every institutional transition requires:
1. Evidence-based trigger verification
2. Constitutional compliance assessment
3. Knowledge preservation plan
4. Lineage and provenance update
5. Replay root update
6. Archaeology record creation

## Constraints

- All transitions must be fully deterministic
- Evidence must be sufficient and verifiable
- Knowledge must never be lost during transitions
- Lineage must be preserved across all transitions
- Every transition becomes a constitutional artifact
