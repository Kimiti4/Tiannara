# Civilizational Decision Engine

## Purpose

Select, compare, and recommend constitutional decisions by evaluating multiple future worlds and their predicted outcomes.

## Decision Process

### Step 1: Future World Evaluation
- Receive future world models from CPCMS
- Score each future against constitutional values
- Evaluate predicted scientific progress, civilizational resilience, risk exposure
- Assign composite scores with uncertainty bounds

### Step 2: Decision Identification
- Identify decision points where civilization can influence trajectory
- Map each decision to specific future branches
- Document decision alternatives with predicted outcomes

### Step 3: Decision Comparison
- Compare decision alternatives across all futures
- Compute risk-adjusted expected utility per alternative
- Rank alternatives with explicit trade-off documentation

### Step 4: Recommendation Generation
- Generate constitutionally bounded recommendations
- Present uncertainty ranges for all recommendations
- Clearly separate evidence from interpretation

## Decision Scoring Dimensions

- **Scientific Advancement**: Rate of knowledge growth (0.0–1.0)
- **Civilizational Resilience**: Long-term robustness (0.0–1.0)
- **Risk Reduction**: Decrease in existential risk (0.0–1.0)
- **Constitutional Alignment**: Adherence to constitutional values (0.0–1.0)
- **Resource Efficiency**: Optimal use of scientific capital (0.0–1.0)
- **Uncertainty Resolution**: Knowledge gain per decision (0.0–1.0)

## Constraints

- Decisions must be constitutionally bounded
- All scores must include uncertainty bounds
- Multiple futures must be considered
- Decision records become constitutional artifacts
