# Resource Coordination Engine

## Purpose

Coordinate allocation of civilization-scale research resources across concurrent missions, ensuring fair distribution, prevention of starvation, and constitutional compliance.

## Resource Types

### Compute Clusters
- Simulation processing capacity
- Data analysis throughput
- Model training resources
- Storage allocation

### Simulation Environments
- Physics simulation availability
- Chemistry simulation capacity
- Biological simulation platforms
- Complex systems modeling

### Mathematical Engines
- Theorem proving resources
- Symbolic computation capacity
- Numerical analysis throughput
- Formal verification systems

### World Models
- Environmental model access
- Economic simulation capacity
- Social system models
- Planetary digital twin resources

### Scientific Capital
- Research funding allocation
- Equipment procurement budget
- Personnel assignment
- Material resources

### Engineering Environments
- Laboratory access scheduling
- Experimental apparatus allocation
- Prototyping facilities
- Testing infrastructure

### Working Memory
- Knowledge graph capacity
- Discovery integration bandwidth
- Cross-domain information sharing
- Civilization memory allocation

## Allocation Principles

### Fair Share
- Every institute receives minimum guaranteed resources
- Resources distributed proportionally to priority
- No institute may be starved indefinitely

### Constitutional Priority
- Emergency research receives priority allocation
- Long-horizon research receives minimum guarantee
- Cross-domain research receives coordination bonus

### Efficiency
- Resources allocated where information gain is highest
- Idle resources redistributed within bounded time
- Overallocation prevented through admission control

## Starvation Prevention

- Minimum resource guarantee per institute per scheduling cycle
- Maximum consecutive cycles without allocation: 3
- Starvation detection triggers constitutional review
- Priority boost for starved missions

## Determinism

Resource allocation must be deterministic given identical inputs. No load-based decisions, no competitive bidding, no market mechanisms.

## Constraints

- Every mission with eligible status must receive resources within bounded time
- No mission may be allocated resources exceeding its requirement
- Resource allocation records become constitutional artifacts
- Replay must reconstruct identical allocation decisions
