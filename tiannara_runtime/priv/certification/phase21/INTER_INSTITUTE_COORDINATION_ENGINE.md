# Inter-Institute Coordination Engine

## Purpose

Coordinate research activities, resource sharing, and collaborative programs across autonomous institutes while preserving institutional independence and constitutional governance.

## Coordination Mechanisms

### Collaboration Agreements
Formal agreements between institutes for joint research programs:
- Shared objectives and milestones
- Resource contribution ratios
- Attribution and credit terms
- Duration and termination conditions

### Joint Programs
Multi-institute research programs:
- Program governance structure
- Institute roles and responsibilities
- Shared dependency management
- Integrated milestone tracking

### Resource Sharing
Cross-institute resource allocation:
- Compute cluster time sharing
- Simulation environment access
- Engineering facility scheduling
- Expertise exchange programs

### Knowledge Transfer
Structured knowledge sharing:
- Discovery publication to federation
- Mathematical result distribution
- Methodology sharing
- Tool and technique transfer

### Crisis Coordination
Emergency response across institutes:
- Rapid resource mobilization
- Priority override for civilizational threats
- Temporary autonomy limitations (constitutionally bounded)

## Coordination Lifecycle

### Step 1: Opportunity Assessment
- Identify coordination opportunity
- Assess potential value
- Evaluate institute fit

### Step 2: Agreement Formation
- Define coordination scope
- Specify terms and conditions
- Constitutional compliance check

### Step 3: Active Coordination
- Execute coordinated activities
- Track progress against milestones
- Resolve coordination conflicts

### Step 4: Completion
- Deliverable acceptance
- Attribution and credit assignment
- Archaeological registration

## Constraints

- All coordination decisions must be deterministic
- No institute may be forced into coordination
- Coordination terms must be explicit and replayable
- Resource sharing must be fair and proportional
- Coordination records become constitutional artifacts
