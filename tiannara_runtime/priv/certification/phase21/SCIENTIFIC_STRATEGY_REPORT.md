# Scientific Strategy Report

## Purpose

Generate comprehensive reports on the state, progress, and health of the civilization's scientific strategy system.

## Report Sections

### Executive Summary
- Strategic objective overview
- Roadmap progress summary
- Alignment status
- Key strategic decisions

### Strategic Objectives
- Current objectives and status
- Recently completed objectives
- Newly proposed objectives
- Objective dependency map

### Roadmap Status
- Active roadmaps and milestones
- Roadmap completion tracking
- Milestone delivery report
- Critical path status

### Trajectory Analysis
- Current trajectory selections
- Alternative trajectories preserved
- Trajectory revision history
- Trajectory confidence assessment

### Priority Distribution
- Strategic priority scores
- Priority tier distribution
- Recent priority changes
- Priority revision rationale

### Alignment Assessment
- Institute alignment scores
- Portfolio alignment evaluation
- Capital alignment verification
- Misalignment alerts

### Horizon Coverage
- Active objectives per horizon
- Horizon coherence verification
- Near-term to long-term balance
- Horizon gap detection

### Resource Allocation
- Strategic capital distribution
- Capital efficiency by objective
- Resource bottleneck identification
- Capital recommendation

### Progress Toward Goals
- Knowledge frontier advancement
- Engineering capability growth
- Critical discovery milestones
- Theory unification progress

### Replay and Archaeology
- Replay verification timestamp
- Replay pass/fail status
- Archaeology coverage
- Pending archaeology gaps

### Strategic Recommendations
- Recommended priority adjustments
- Proposed new strategic objectives
- Alignment improvement suggestions
- Horizon rebalancing proposals

## Report Generation

Reports must be deterministically generated from strategy state. Reports become constitutional artifacts.

## Frequency
- Standard: Per generation
- Comprehensive: Per 10 generations
- Strategic Event: On major strategy revision
