# Constitutional Scientific Opportunity Management Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Scientific Opportunity Management System (CSOMS) maintains a continuously evolving map of humanity's knowledge frontier and uses constitutional processes to determine which unknowns should be investigated first. Rather than waiting for research requests, the Autonomous Research Civilization proactively discovers where the greatest opportunities for scientific progress exist.

## System Purpose

Transform the global state of unknown knowledge into a prioritized, constitutionally governed map of scientific opportunities.

## Architecture Components

### Knowledge Frontier Layer
- Maintains a layered representation of humanity's knowledge state
- Tracks: Established Knowledge, Incomplete Knowledge, Unresolved Questions, Unknown Relationships, Unknown Structures, Unknown Laws, Unknown Unknowns
- Every frontier element possesses identity, lineage, evidence, uncertainty, dependencies, and scientific importance

### Opportunity Discovery Layer
- Detects scientific opportunity regions through deterministic mechanisms
- Identifies unresolved contradictions, missing theories, mathematical gaps, and engineering bottlenecks
- Discovers cross-domain opportunities at knowledge frontier boundaries

### Information Gain Layer
- Estimates expected information gain across 8 dimensions
- Computes composite gain scores deterministically
- Provides uncertainty ranges for all estimates

### Prioritization Layer
- Ranks opportunities by expected knowledge value
- Applies constitutional priority weights
- Produces deterministic, replayable rankings

### Governance Layer
- Reviews and certifies discovered opportunities
- Integrates opportunities into portfolio governance
- Manages opportunity lifecycle from identification through completion

## Opportunity Categories

Foundational Mathematics, Foundational Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Engineering, World Models, Computation, Robotics, Energy, Agriculture, Governance, Cross-Domain, Civilizational, Long-Horizon

## Integration Points

- **Input**: Global scientific knowledge state, unresolved questions, contradictions
- **Output**: Prioritized scientific opportunities for Phase 21.3 (Mission Generation)
- **Constitutional Foundation**: Phase 20 (CSOS v1.0)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Knowledge frontier is constitutionally represented
- Scientific opportunities are deterministic
- Information gain is measurable
- Cross-domain opportunities preserve provenance
- Opportunity governance is constitutionally defined
- Replay reconstructs every opportunity
- Archaeology reconstructs frontier evolution
- No runtime implementation exists
