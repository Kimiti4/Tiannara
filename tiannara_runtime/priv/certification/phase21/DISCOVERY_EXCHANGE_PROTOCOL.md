# Discovery Exchange Protocol

## Purpose

Define the constitutional protocol for exchanging discoveries, knowledge, and artifacts across institutes while preserving provenance, attribution, and evidence integrity.

## Exchangeable Artifacts

### Mathematical Proofs
- Complete formal proofs
- Theorem statements and derivations
- Mathematical framework extensions
- Computational method proofs

### Scientific Theories
- Theoretical formulations
- Predictive models
- Explanatory frameworks
- Paradigm proposals

### Experimental Results
- Raw experimental data
- Analysis methodology
- Reproducibility protocols
- Instrument calibration records

### Engineering Artifacts
- Instrument designs
- Infrastructure specifications
- Software implementations
- Hardware schematics

### Simulation Models
- Model specifications
- Calibration parameters
- Validation results
- Model limitations

### World Models
- Environment models
- System dynamics
- Causal relationship maps
- Predictive simulations

### Knowledge Graphs
- Domain knowledge structures
- Relationship maps
- Discovery provenance chains
- Knowledge frontier updates

## Exchange Protocol

### Step 1: Artifact Preparation
- Format and certify artifact
- Compute artifact hash
- Attach provenance metadata

### Step 2: Publication
- Submit to federation exchange
- Notify relevant institutes
- Record in discovery registry

### Step 3: Verification
- Recipient institutes verify integrity
- Check hash consistency
- Validate provenance chain

### Step 4: Integration
- Incorporate into recipient knowledge base
- Update dependency satisfaction
- Record exchange in archaeology

## Provenance Preservation

- Every artifact records originating institute
- Derivative works maintain attribution chain
- Exchange events are timestamped deterministically
- All exchanges are fully replayable

## Constraints

- No artifact may be exchanged without provenance
- Attribution must be preserved across all derivatives
- Exchange must be atomic and verifiable
- Artifact integrity must be hash-verifiable
- Exchange records become constitutional artifacts
