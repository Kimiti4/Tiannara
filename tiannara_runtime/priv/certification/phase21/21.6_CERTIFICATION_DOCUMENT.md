## Certification Statement

Phase 21.6 (Constitutional Scientific Ecosystem Evolution) establishes the constitutional framework for enabling the Autonomous Research Civilization to continuously reorganize itself as scientific knowledge grows. Institutions become living scientific organisms that form, specialize, merge, divide, transform, retire, and spawn successors through constitutional mechanisms. Collaboration networks evolve dynamically. Scientific diversity is measured, monitored, and preserved. The ecosystem adapts at civilization scale while constitutional foundations remain frozen. Complete replay and archaeological reconstruction ensure every evolutionary decision is traceable, verifiable, and reconstructible by future generations.
