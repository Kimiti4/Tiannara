# Federation Archaeology Model

## Purpose

Define the archaeological framework for reconstructing the evolution, interactions, and decisions of the research institute federation across generations.

## Archaeological Questions

### Institute Origin Questions
- Why was an institute created?
- What scientific gap did it fill?
- Why was its specialization chosen?
- How did its charter evolve?

### Collaboration Questions
- Why did institutes collaborate?
- What triggered the collaboration?
- How were resources shared?
- What discoveries resulted?

### Discovery Exchange Questions
- Why was a discovery transferred?
- Which institutes received it?
- How was attribution maintained?
- What derivative discoveries emerged?

### Peer Review Questions
- Why was peer review requested?
- Which institutes conducted the review?
- What evidence was examined?
- What was the review outcome?

### Competition Questions
- Which institutes competed?
- What was the competitive domain?
- How was competition resolved?
- What knowledge advances resulted?

### Evolution Questions
- How did institutional relationships evolve?
- Which institutes merged or split?
- How did federation governance adapt?
- What systemic improvements emerged?

## Archaeological Record Structure

Every federation archaeology record contains:
1. Generation context and federation state
2. Institute registry and charters
3. Active collaboration records
4. Recent discovery exchanges
5. Peer review outcomes
6. Governance decisions
7. Competition records
8. Evolution markers

## Independent Reconstruction

Federation archaeology must be independently reconstructible from cold storage:
- All interactions derivable from deterministic processes
- Rationale reconstructible from inputs and parameters
- Missing data detectable and reported
- Contradictions flagged for investigation

## Constraints

- Every generation must have a federation archaeology record
- Archaeology must answer all defined questions
- Records must be independently reconstructible
- All institutional interactions must have documented rationale
- Archaeology must span federation origin through civilization impact
