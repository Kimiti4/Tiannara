Phase 21.4 (Constitutional Scientific Program Orchestration) establishes the constitutional framework for coordinating civilization-scale scientific activity across thousands of concurrent research missions. The Autonomous Research Civilization becomes capable of deterministic scheduling, multi-mission coordination, global dependency management, fair resource allocation, cross-domain synchronization, execution balancing, and complete replay/archaeology reconstruction of all orchestration decisions.
