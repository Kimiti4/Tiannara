## Certification Statement

Phase 21.3 (Constitutional Autonomous Research Mission Generation) establishes the constitutional framework for transforming prioritized scientific opportunities into fully specified, constitutionally governed research missions. The Autonomous Research Civilization becomes capable of deterministic mission synthesis, acyclic dependency management, multi-dimensional feasibility assessment, validation planning with independent audit requirements, interdisciplinary collaboration with complete provenance preservation, and full replay/archaeology reconstruction of every mission generation decision.
