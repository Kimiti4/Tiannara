# Priority Allocation Engine

## Purpose

Allocate constitutional priorities across research institutes, scientific domains, and programs based on strategic decisions.

## Allocation Dimensions

### Domain Allocation
- Scientific capital per research domain
- Institute capacity per domain
- Strategic priority weighting
- Interdisciplinary requirement assessment

### Institute Allocation
- Budget and resource distribution
- Mission assignment by expertise
- Collaboration incentives
- Infrastructure investment

### Temporal Allocation
- Short-term priorities (1–5yr)
- Medium-term investments (5–25yr)
- Long-term foundations (25–100yr)
- Multi-generational commitments

### Balance Objectives
- Domain diversity preservation
- Institute specialization vs. generalization
- Risk diversification
- Knowledge gap closure

## Allocation Process

- Receive strategic decisions from decision engine
- Map decisions to domains, institutes, programs
- Compute resource requirements per allocation
- Balance allocations against constitutional objectives
- Generate prioritized allocation plan

## Constraints

- Allocations must be evidence-informed
- Constitutional priorities must be respected
- Resource limits must be observed
- Allocation records become constitutional artifacts
