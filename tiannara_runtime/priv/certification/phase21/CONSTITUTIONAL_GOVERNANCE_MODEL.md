# Constitutional Governance Model

## Purpose

Ensure all civilizational decisions respect constitutional bounds, preserve human authority, and maintain full transparency.

## Governance Principles

### Human Authority Preservation
- All critical decisions require human confirmation
- Human override capability is always available
- Civilizational recommendations are advisory only
- Human authority is never algorithmically circumvented

### Constitutional Bounds
- Decisions must not violate constitutional guarantees
- All decisions must be auditable and replayable
- Decision rationales must be fully transparent
- Unconstitutional decisions are automatically rejected

### Transparency Requirements
- Full decision provenance documentation
- Complete evidence chain preservation
- Uncertainty disclosure for all recommendations
- Trade-off documentation for all alternatives

### Oversight Mechanisms
- Constitutional peer review for major decisions
- Decision impact monitoring and reporting
- Periodic constitutional compliance audit
- Human review triggers for critical decisions

## Governance Process

- Decisions generated by decision engine within constitutional bounds
- Constitutional compliance verified automatically
- Critical decisions flagged for human review
- Human confirmation required for irreversible decisions
- All decisions recorded with full provenance

## Constitutional Guarantee Enforcement

| Guarantee | Enforcement Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Human Authority | Human confirmation required for critical decisions |
| Determinism | All decisions are replayable |
| Transparency | Full provenance documentation |
| Evidence | All decisions linked to evidence chain |
| Preservation | Knowledge and decisions never destroyed |
| Auditability | Complete audit trail for every decision |

## Constraints

- Human authority is non-negotiable
- Constitutional compliance is automated
- All decisions must be fully documented
- Governance records become constitutional artifacts
