# Strategy Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the performance, coverage, and health of the civilizational scientific strategy system.

## Metrics

### Strategic Objective Coverage
- Total strategic objectives
- Objectives per domain
- Objectives per horizon tier
- Objective completion rate

### Roadmap Completion
- Roadmaps in progress
- Roadmaps completed
- Milestone completion percentage
- Milestone delivery rate

### Knowledge Dependency Reduction
- Dependency nodes resolved
- Critical path length reduction
- Bottleneck discoveries completed
- Dependency graph depth

### Trajectory Stability
- Trajectory revision frequency
- Average trajectory lifetime
- Revision cause distribution
- Trajectory divergence rate

### Scientific Capital Growth
- Total strategic capital allocated
- Capital efficiency per roadmap
- Capital per strategic objective
- Capital return on strategy

### Civilizational Information Gain
- Strategic discoveries per generation
- Knowledge frontier advancement toward objectives
- Theory unification progress
- Engineering capability growth

### Cross-Domain Alignment
- Institute-strategy alignment score
- Portfolio-strategy correlation
- Mission-strategy traceability
- Capital-strategy alignment

### Research Horizon Coverage
- Horizon tiers with active objectives
- Horizon depth distribution
- Horizon coherence score
- Long-term to short-term balance

### Replay Stability
- Strategy replay pass rate
- Hash match percentage
- Replay failure count
- Root hash convergence

### Archaeology Completeness
- Complete strategy archaeology records
- Archaeology coverage ratio
- Independent reconstruction success rate
- Archaeology gap count

### Civilizational Scientific Readiness
- Strategic preparedness index
- Knowledge frontier readiness
- Engineering capability readiness
- Institutional strategic alignment

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic Objectives Active | 5 | 15 | 30+ |
| Roadmap Completion Rate | 50% | 70% | 90%+ |
| Institute Alignment Score | 0.60 | 0.80 | 0.95+ |
| Trajectory Stability | 0.50 | 0.70 | 0.85+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |

## Recording

All metrics must be deterministically computable from strategy artifacts. Metrics become part of the constitutional record and must be replayable.
