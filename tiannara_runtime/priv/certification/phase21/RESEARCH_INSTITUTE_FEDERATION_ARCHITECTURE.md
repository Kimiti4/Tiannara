# Constitutional Research Institute Federation Architecture

## Overview

The Constitutional Research Institute Federation (CRIF) organizes the Autonomous Research Civilization into specialized constitutional research institutes that cooperate, compete, exchange knowledge, and collectively advance civilization-scale scientific discovery. Rather than a single monolithic research organization, Tiannara becomes a federation of autonomous institutes operating under a shared constitutional framework.

## Federation Principles

- **Constitutional Autonomy**: Every institute operates independently within constitutional bounds
- **Scientific Diversity**: Diverse research approaches strengthen civilization resilience
- **Provenance Preservation**: All inter-institute interactions maintain complete traceability
- **Cooperative Competition**: Institutes compete scientifically while cooperating on shared goals
- **Evidence-First Governance**: All federation decisions based on reproducible evidence

## Federation Hierarchy

```
Constitutional Autonomous Research Civilization
    └── Research Federation
            └── Research Institutes
                    └── Scientific Programs
                            └── Mission Groups
                                    └── Research Missions
                                            └── Projects
                                                    └── Experiments
                                                            └── Discoveries
```

## Architecture Components

### Layer 1: Institute Autonomy
- Each institute possesses its own charter, mandate, portfolio, budget, and mission queue
- Institutes make independent research decisions within constitutional bounds
- Institute identity is preserved across all interactions

### Layer 2: Inter-Institute Coordination
- Collaboration agreements and joint programs
- Resource sharing across institute boundaries
- Knowledge and discovery exchange protocols

### Layer 3: Constitutional Peer Review
- Independent verification of discoveries across institutes
- Evidence and mathematical verification
- Consensus-driven publication framework

### Layer 4: Federation Governance
- Global priority coordination
- Resource balancing across institutes
- Long-horizon initiative management
- Emergency research coordination

### Layer 5: Discovery Exchange
- Mathematical proofs and theories
- Experimental results and engineering artifacts
- Simulation models and knowledge graphs

## Integration Points

- **Input**: Phase 21.0–21.4 (CARC, CRPG, CSOMS, CARMGS, CSPOS)
- **Output**: Federated research execution across autonomous institutes

## Acceptance Criteria

- Architecture defines complete federation pipeline
- Institute autonomy is constitutionally guaranteed
- Inter-institute interactions preserve provenance
- Peer review is deterministic and evidence-based
- No runtime implementation detail is specified
