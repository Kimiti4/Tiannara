# Strategy Archaeology Model

## Purpose

Define the archaeological framework for reconstructing the strategic reasoning, evidence, and decisions that shaped the civilization's long-term scientific direction.

## Archaeological Questions

### Selection Questions
- Why was this strategic objective selected?
- What evidence justified the choice?
- Which alternatives were considered?
- Why were other objectives deprioritized?

### Roadmap Questions
- Which evidence justified the roadmap structure?
- Why were specific milestones chosen?
- What dependency reasoning guided sequencing?
- How were resource estimates derived?

### Trajectory Questions
- Which discoveries altered the trajectory?
- Why was the trajectory revised?
- What new evidence changed the path?
- Which assumptions proved incorrect?

### Priority Questions
- Why were priorities revised?
- What dimension scores changed?
- Which new information affected weighting?
- How did priority reshuffling affect execution?

### Evolution Questions
- How did scientific strategy evolve over time?
- Which paradigm shifts restructured strategy?
- How did civilizational learning inform strategy?
- What external factors influenced strategic direction?

### Impact Questions
- How did civilization progress toward each objective?
- Which strategic decisions proved most valuable?
- What discoveries resulted from strategic focus?
- How did strategy adapt to unexpected discoveries?

## Archaeological Record Structure

Every strategy archaeology record contains:
1. Generation context and strategic state snapshot
2. Current roadmaps and their justification
3. Active strategic objectives and rationale
4. Recent strategic decisions and evidence
5. Alignment assessment and adjustments
6. Priority shifts and their causes
7. Civilization progress markers against strategic goals

## Independent Reconstruction

Strategy archaeology must be independently reconstructible from cold storage.

## Constraints

- Every generation must have a strategy archaeology record
- Archaeology must answer all defined questions
- Records must be independently reconstructible
- All strategic decisions must have documented rationale
- Archaeology must span strategy origin through civilizational impact
