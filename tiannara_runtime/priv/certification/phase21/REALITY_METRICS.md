# Reality Metrics

## Purpose

Define quantitative metrics for measuring the completeness, consistency, and health of the unified reality model.

## Metrics

- **Integrated Theories**: Total theories in unified model
- **Reality Model Completeness**: Fraction of observable phenomena covered
- **Cross-Domain Consistency**: Average consistency score across all dimensions
- **Known Contradictions**: Active contradictions documented
- **Resolved Contradictions**: Contradictions resolved through evidence
- **Knowledge Compression**: Unified representation efficiency
- **Causal Coverage**: Fraction of phenomena with complete causal chain
- **Scientific Confidence**: Evidence-weighted confidence across all theories
- **Replay Stability**: Reality replay pass rate
- **Archaeology Completeness**: Archaeology coverage ratio
- **Unified Reality Readiness**: Overall model maturity index

## Target Thresholds

| Metric | Minimum | Target | Excellence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Integrated Theories | 50 | 200 | 500+ |
| Model Completeness | 0.40 | 0.70 | 0.90+ |
| Cross-Domain Consistency | 0.60 | 0.80 | 0.95+ |
| Replay Stability | 99% | 99.9% | 99.99% |
| Archaeology Completeness | 95% | 99% | 100% |
