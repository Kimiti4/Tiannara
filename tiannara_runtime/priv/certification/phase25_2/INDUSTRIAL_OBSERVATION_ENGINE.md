# Industrial Observation Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that ingests observations from industrial systems and infrastructure.

## Observation Sources

- Manufacturing facilities (production rates, energy use, emissions).
- Energy infrastructure (power plants, refineries, pipelines).
- Transportation systems (traffic, logistics, shipping).
- Communication networks (bandwidth usage, connectivity).
- Water and waste treatment facilities.
- Mining and resource extraction operations.
- Construction and infrastructure projects.
- Supply chain and logistics networks.
- Industrial IoT sensor networks.

## Ingestion Pipeline

1. **Industrial Connection** — Establish secure connection to industrial data source.
2. **Protocol Adaptation** — Translate industrial protocols to standard format.
3. **Data Acquisition** — Receive real-time or batch industrial data.
4. **Normalization** — Normalize units, scales, and naming conventions.
5. **Validation** — Check against operational ranges and historical patterns.
6. **Anomaly Detection** — Detect unusual operational patterns.
7. **Observation Creation** — Create structured industrial observations.

## Security

- All industrial connections authenticated and encrypted.
- Industrial data classified by sensitivity.
- Proprietary data access controlled.
- Industrial source certification verified.
- Data usage terms enforced.
