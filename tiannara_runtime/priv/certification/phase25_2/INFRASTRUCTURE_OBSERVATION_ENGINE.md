# Infrastructure Observation Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that ingests observations from civil infrastructure systems.

## Observation Sources

- Transportation infrastructure (roads, bridges, tunnels, rails, ports, airports).
- Energy infrastructure (transmission lines, substations, pipelines).
- Water infrastructure (dams, reservoirs, treatment plants, distribution).
- Communication infrastructure (fiber, towers, data centers).
- Buildings and urban infrastructure.
- Waste management systems.
- Public facilities (hospitals, schools, government buildings).
- Defense and security infrastructure.
- Space infrastructure (launch sites, satellite ground stations).

## Ingestion Pipeline

1. **Infrastructure Monitoring** — Connect to infrastructure monitoring systems.
2. **Structural Health Data** — Ingest structural monitoring (strain, vibration, corrosion).
3. **Operational Data** — Ingest operational status (traffic, load, throughput).
4. **Maintenance Data** — Ingest maintenance records and inspection reports.
5. **Damage Detection** — Ingest damage and incident reports.
6. **Validation** — Cross-validate with satellite and sensor data.
7. **Observation Creation** — Create structured infrastructure observations.

## Infrastructure Health Metrics

- Structural integrity index.
- Remaining useful life estimates.
- Maintenance backlog.
- Capacity utilization.
- Vulnerability to hazards.
- Resilience indicators.
