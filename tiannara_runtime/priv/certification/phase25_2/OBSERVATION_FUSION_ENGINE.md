# Observation Fusion Engine

## Purpose

Define the engine that fuses multiple observations into unified constitutional evidence.

## Fusion Scope

Fusion across:
- **Physical** — Combining physical measurements from different instruments.
- **Scientific** — Integrating scientific findings from multiple studies.
- **Engineering** — Fusing engineering sensor data.
- **Economic** — Combining economic indicators.
- **Environmental** — Integrating environmental monitoring data.
- **Societal** — Fusing social and demographic observations.
- **Infrastructure** — Combining infrastructure health data.
- **Digital** — Fusing digital activity data.

## Fusion Methods

- **Weighted Average** — Combine measurements weighted by confidence.
- **Kalman Filter** — Temporal fusion with state estimation.
- **Bayesian Fusion** — Probabilistic fusion with prior knowledge.
- **Ensemble Methods** — Combine multiple model outputs.
- **Expert Consensus** — Formal methods for combining expert judgments.
- **Hierarchical Fusion** — Fusion at multiple spatial/temporal scales.

## Fusion Properties

- **Deterministic** — Same inputs always produce same fusion output.
- **Confidence-Aware** — Fusion preserves and propagates confidence.
- **Uncertainty-Preserving** — Fusion preserves uncertainty information.
- **Replayable** — Fusion can be deterministically replayed.
- **Auditable** — Fusion evidence and method fully recorded.

## Fusion Records

Every fusion operation records:
- Source observations (which observations were fused).
- Fusion method and version.
- Fusion parameters.
- Output evidence (fused result).
- Confidence and uncertainty of fused result.
- Provenance chain linking output to inputs.
