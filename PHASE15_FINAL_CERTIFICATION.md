## Why Certification Is Withheld (Not “Failure”)

Phase 15 has successfully completed **constitutional implementation and synthetic constitutional validation** (mechanistic correctness under frozen contracts, deterministic replay, statistical validation, and adversarial robustness).

Final constitutional certification remains withheld because the remaining gates require **empirical discovery evidence** and **independent evidence-only certification audit outputs** that are not yet present.

Certification is intentionally withheld to preserve Tiannara’s evidence-first constitutional methodology: **implementation is not evidence; evidence is not certification; certification requires independent reconstruction from immutable artifacts.**

## Remaining Certification Gates (Fail Closed / Pending)

The following gates are still required for final certification:

1. **Empirical Discovery Campaigns (PENDING)**
   - Required: real-world discovery against empirical datasets with external ground-truth comparisons (not synthetic workloads).

2. **Discovery Closure Test / Evidence-Only Reconstruction (PENDING)**
   - Required: reconstruction of conclusions solely from immutable ledger + evidence + certificates + statistics—without runtime state.

3. **Independent Executable Evidence-Only Audit (PENDING)**
   - Required: independent executable evidence-only audit that reproduces discoveries/theories/knowledge graph/capital and emits an audit certificate from artifacts alone.

4. **Authorized Discovery Certificate Signing (PENDING)**
   - Required: authorized-signature artifacts for final discovery certification manifests/certificates (freeze artifacts alone are not sufficient).

5. **Long-Horizon Empirical Outcome Comparison (PENDING)**
   - Required: prediction-vs-outcome comparisons against observed outcomes (synthetic long-horizon stability does not satisfy empirical correctness).

## Certification Reconsideration Rule

Certification may be reconsidered **only from immutable campaign outputs and independent auditor evidence-only reconstruction outputs** (including the required audit/certificate artifacts). Narrative reports alone are insufficient.
