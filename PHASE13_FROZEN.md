## Phase 13 Architecture

### Stages Implemented

1. **Stage 3 - Method Evolution** (✅ Frozen)
   - Evaluates research methods for improvement opportunities
   - Generates MethodEvolutionResult canonical transactions
   - Predicts improvement impact before adoption

2. **Stage 4 - Institution Adaptation** (✅ Frozen)
   - Each institution evaluates method improvements
   - Runs simulations and pilots
   - Makes adoption decisions with complete rollback plans
   - Generates InstitutionAdaptationResult canonical transactions

3. **Stage 5 - Civilization Adaptation** (✅ Frozen)
   - Coordinates cross-institution improvement spread
   - Evaluates transferability across institutions
   - Makes civilizational decisions (universal/selective/experimental/reject)
   - Generates CivilizationAdaptationResult canonical transactions

4. **Stage 6 - Recursive Evolution** (✅ Validated)
   - Executes multi-generational cycles (100+ generations)
   - Tracks immutable GenerationHistory records
   - Demonstrates longitudinal improvement trends
   - Proves causation through A/B testing (Phase 13.5)
