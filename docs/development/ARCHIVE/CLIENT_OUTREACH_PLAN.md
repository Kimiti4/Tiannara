## Discovery Call Script (15 minutes)

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for taking the time! I'd love to learn about [Company] and the challenges you're facing with AI/decision-making."

### Questions (8 min)
1. "What are your biggest pain points with current AI systems?"
2. "How do you handle complex reasoning tasks today?"
3. "What would success look like for you in 6 months?"
4. "Have you tried other AI solutions? What worked/didn't work?"
5. "What's your timeline for implementing new AI capabilities?"

### Pitch (3 min)
"Based on what you've shared, here's how Tiannara could help..."
[Customize based on their pain points]

### Next Steps (2 min)
"I'd recommend [consulting package/API trial/custom demo]. Would you like to:"
- Schedule technical deep-dive?
- Start free API trial?
- Get custom proposal?

## Objection Handling

### "We already use [Competitor]"
**Response**: "That's great! Many of our clients use multiple AI systems. Tiannara complements existing tools by handling the complex reasoning tasks they struggle with - especially those requiring cross-domain knowledge or causal understanding. Can I show you where we add unique value?"

### "Too expensive"
**Response**: "I understand budget is a concern. Let's talk ROI: Our clients typically see 60-80% reduction in AI development costs and 40-85% faster decision-making. For a $5K consulting engagement, most clients save $50K+ in the first quarter. Would a pilot project make sense to prove the value?"

### "We need to think about it"
**Response**: "Absolutely, this is an important decision. What specific questions do you still have? I'm happy to provide more details, case studies, or arrange a technical demo with your team."

### "Send me more information"
**Response**: "Happy to! I'll send over our documentation and case studies. When would be a good time to follow up? Also, would it help to schedule a brief demo so you can see it in action?"

## Tips for Success

1. **Personalize Every Email** - Mention specific challenges they face
2. **Focus on Value** - Talk about their problems, not your features
3. **Be Persistent** - Most sales require 5+ touchpoints
4. **Track Everything** - Know what's working and double down
5. **Ask for Referrals** - Happy clients know other potential clients
6. **Provide Value First** - Share resources before asking for sale
7. **Follow Up Promptly** - Strike while interest is hot
8. **Listen More Than Talk** - Understand their needs deeply
