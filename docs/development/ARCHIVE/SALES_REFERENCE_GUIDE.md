## 🎯 Qualification Questions

### For Starter Prospects:
1. "Are you building a new AI product or improving an existing one?"
2. "What's your timeline for launch?"
3. "How many API calls per day do you expect?"
4. "What's your monthly budget for AI tools?"

**Green flags**: 
- Building something new
- Launching in <3 months
- <166 calls/day
- Budget: $50-200/month

---

### For Professional Prospects:
1. "How many teams are using AI in your organization?"
2. "What's your current AI infrastructure cost?"
3. "Do you have performance monitoring in place?"
4. "What uptime do you require?"

**Green flags**:
- 2+ teams using AI
- Spending $5K+/month currently
- No good monitoring
- Need 99%+ uptime

### For Enterprise Prospects:
1. "What regulatory requirements do you face?"
2. "Do you need custom model training?"
3. "What's your current AI spend annually?"
4. "Do you require on-premise deployment?"

**Green flags**:
- Regulated industry (finance, healthcare, legal)
- Proprietary data needs
- Spending $100K+/year on AI
- Compliance requirements
