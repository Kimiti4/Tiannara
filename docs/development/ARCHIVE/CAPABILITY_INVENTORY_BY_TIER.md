## 🎯 Choosing the Right Tier

### Choose Starter If:
- You're a developer or small team (1-5 people)
- You need quick wins and automation
- Budget is limited (<$600/year)
- Tasks are relatively simple
- Cloud-only deployment is fine

### Choose Professional If:
- You're a growing company (10-50 people)
- You need production-ready systems
- Team collaboration is important
- You want advanced analytics
- Budget allows $2,400/year

### Choose Enterprise If:
- You're a large organization (50+ people)
- You have regulatory requirements
- You need custom infrastructure
- Mission-critical reliability is essential
- Budget allows $12,000+/year

---

## 🚀 Upgrade Path

**Starter → Professional**: When you hit API limits or need team features  
**Professional → Enterprise**: When you need compliance, custom models, or on-premise  

Each tier builds on the previous one—no capabilities are lost when upgrading.
